arXiv:2611.27507v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quantum-optimization benchmarks can show reduced energy while concealing local loss of weighted covariance. We combine encoding checks with exact structural references to select informative circuit diagnostics for static geographic coverage instances. The executed 24-qubit surrogate has infeasible ground states, whereas a faithful 51-qubit slack encoding preserves its 13 support-graph components and remains exactly simulable blockwise. At the executed angles, the total ideal optimum probability is 5.47imes10^{-20}, making optimum counts uninformative at the executed budget of 10^5 shots. A product distribution with the measured bit marginals accounts for about 90% of the observed energy reduction. In three fixed-circuit jobs, the global weighted-covariance ratio is approximately 0.50, while a five-variable block has ratios between 0.12 and 0.28 and contributes only 3.7% of the ideal global denominator. Simultaneous bands resolve its deficit relative to the global ratio under an exploratory conditional counting model. The exact block reference thus reveals a local discrepancy concealed by the aggregate, without identifying an error mechanism or establishing device stability. The procedure links benchmark structure to the resolution and limitations of its observables.
