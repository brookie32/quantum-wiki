arXiv:2611.09779v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quantum technologies based on individually controlled atoms are among the most promising platforms for scalable quantum computing, owing to their favorable coherence properties and controllability. Nonetheless, achieving reliable and high-fidelity gate operations remains challenging due to the sensitivity of these systems to noise and hardware imperfections. In this work, we introduce a comprehensive software toolset designed to model atomic quantum systems with high precision and simulate qubit gate dynamics. By integrating quantum optimal control and benchmarking techniques with automatic differentiation for efficient and accurate gradient-based optimization, the toolset enables significant improvements in gate performance. We demonstrate its capabilities through detailed case studies involving both neutral atoms and trapped ions, highlighting its effectiveness in enhancing quantum gate fidelities, and providing valuable insights for experimental implementation.
