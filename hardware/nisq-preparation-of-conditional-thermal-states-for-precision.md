arXiv:2611.33461v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The ability to prepare thermal states on gate-based quantum computers is an important building block for quantum computation, both with regard to the implementation of certain classes of quantum algorithms as well as to the study of quantum thermodynamics in and out of equilibrium. A useful generalization of the standard notion of a thermal state which appears naturally in the study of quantum work distributions is the conditional thermal state, which is conditioned on the use of an accessible pointer basis associated with a measurement apparatus. Among other uses, it has been proven that conditional thermal states can outperform ordinary thermal states in quantum thermometry. In this work we demonstrate the preparation of conditional thermal states of few-qubit systems implemented on IonQ's cloud-based quantum computers across a range of temperatures. To validate their performance advantage in thermometry, we measure the sub-quantum Fisher information (a lower bound on the quantum Fisher information) of the prepared states, which can be computed via a constant-depth quantum circuit without tomography.
