arXiv:2611.18249v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The Fer expansion writes the propagator of a time-dependent Hamiltonian as a product of unitary exponentials whose error decreases doubly exponentially with the number of factors, while the Magnus expansion improves by one order at a time. Its convergence, however, was guaranteed only below a radius of about 2.6058, smaller than the Magnus radius pi. We establish a guaranteed convergence threshold of about 6.2106, or 1.98,pi, for a qubit. This is almost twice the optimal universal Magnus threshold pi; the bound on a single Fer step is sharp, while the exact convergence radius remains open.
