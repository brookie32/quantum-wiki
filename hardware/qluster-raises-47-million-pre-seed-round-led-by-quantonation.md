Qluster, a Paris-based photonic quantum computing startup, has secured €4.7 million in pre-seed funding. Led by Quantonation, the investment will be used to establish an R&D laboratory and expand its teams. Qluster's technology utilizes continuous-variable cluster states with the goal of eliminating cryogenic infrastructure and simplifying data-center integration, aiming for an intermediate-scale quantum system by 2032 and a large-scale fault-tolerant universal processor by 2036.
