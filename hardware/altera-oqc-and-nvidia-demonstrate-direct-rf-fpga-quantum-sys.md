Altera, OQC, and NVIDIA showcased a quantum system controller reference design at SC26, utilizing an Altera Agilex 9 Direct RF FPGA. This design integrates real-time FPGA control with an OQC superconducting processor and NVIDIA GPUs via NVIDIA NVQLink, simplifying quantum control stacks by reducing the need for discrete components. The system enhances qubit control, calibration, and machine learning readout classification, with a general release planned for January 2027.
