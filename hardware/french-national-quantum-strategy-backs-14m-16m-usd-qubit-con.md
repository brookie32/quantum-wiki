The French National Quantum Strategy has invested €14 million in the Qubit Control 2030 (QC2030) consortium, led by VIQTHOR. This initiative aims to develop integrated control and readout systems for quantum computers, replacing traditional copper wiring with photonic and superconducting interconnects. The goal is to significantly reduce the cost and size of quantum computing hardware, with demonstrators planned for 1,000 qubits by 2028 and 10,000 qubits by 2030.
