arXiv:2610.27639v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Treating the arrow of time as a controllable quantum variable rather than a fixed external parameter is a central challenge in quantum physics. Recently, Bloch~ite{Bloch2025} proposed the theoretical concept of a time qubit, a two-level control space H_T=span{ket{+},ket{-}} that selects between opposite Hamiltonian evolutions hat{H}_{pm}=hat{H}_0pmhat{V}, but this paradigm has remained experimentally unrealized. Here we formulate the quantum-optical theory of a single-photon time qubit---uncovering quantum-vacuum control and complementarity of the arrow of time---and experimentally probe and validate its predictions using phase-resolved single-photon interferometry from two coherently seeded entangled nonlinear biphoton sources~ite{Lee2018,Yoon2021}. First, we show theoretically and verify experimentally that a controlled pi pump--seed phase inversion during pair creation from the quantum vacuum flips the stimulated displacement generator hat{V}o -hat{V} on top of the spontaneous two-mode squeezing background hat{H}_0, reversing the stimulated evolution time [hat{U}_{stim,-}(t)=hat{U}_{stim,+}(-t)] and enabling universal single-qubit SU(2) control and postselection-free first-order tomography on the temporal Bloch sphere. Second, we prove that spontaneous idler vacuum fluctuations record complete which-branch (temporal-direction) information whereas the shared coherent seed erases it without cascading or aligning the idler beams, establishing an exact wave--particle complementarity law |m{r}|=V=|alpha|^2/(1+|alpha|^2) for the purity of the arrow of time and enabling direct first-order measurement of the Loschmidt time-reversal echo.
