arXiv:2603.06466v4 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Equational reasoning about circuits underpins quantum-circuit optimisation and verification. In the qubit CNOT-dihedral fragment, phase polynomials and layered normal forms support a complete equational theory. We develop the corresponding theory for prime-dimensional qudits, where basis labels, value controls, and phase exponents share prime-field arithmetic. We first describe reversible affine circuits over F_d as transformations x -> Ax + b, with an affine normal form extending Lafont's linear normal form by translations. Adjoining finite-angle diagonal phases by polynomial degree yields linear, quadratic, and cubic calculi. Their binomial-basis identities expose the mixed diagonal gates forced by affine transport. These calculi have unique phase-affine normal forms, and the resulting equational theories are complete: semantic equality coincides with derivable equality.
