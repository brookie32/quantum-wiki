arXiv:2511.11508v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We present an approach to compute time-optimal control of a quantum system which combines quantum brachistochrone and Lax pair techniques and enables efficient investigation of large-scale quantum systems. We illustrate our method by finding the quantum speed limit for a single-particle excitation in a nearest-neighbor-coupled qubit lattice with switchable couplings and fixed sum of their squares. We obtain the solution for a finite system with up to 10 000 qubits and for the effectively infinite lattice closed into ring. As another application of our method, we derive the quantum speed limit for a lattice with time-independent couplings.
