arXiv:2608.18421v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We study complete orthogonal product bases (OPBs) of an n-qubit system via the formal-matrix formalism for multiqubit OPBs. To each OPB formal matrix we associate an edge-colored complete multigraph, and we prove that two formal matrices are equivalent if and only if their multigraphs are isomorphic, thereby reducing the classification of OPBs to graph isomorphism. Via subcube partitions and a lemma of Tarsi, we upper bound the number of variables by 2^n - 1. We also study lower and upper bounds on the number a_n of equivalence classes of n-qubit OPBs, by showing that inom{a_{n-1}+1}{2} le a_n le B_{2^{n-1}}^n, where B_m denotes the number of partitions of an m-element set. These bounds yield the asymptotic behavior a_n = 2^{2^{n+o(n)}}. Finally, we obtain a two-phase algorithm that decides whether two OPB formal matrices are equivalent, together with its correctness proof and complexity analysis. The complexity is exponential in the worst case.
