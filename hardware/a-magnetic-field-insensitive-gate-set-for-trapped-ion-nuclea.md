arXiv:2609.37318v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We outline a complete set of operations for manipulating nuclear spin qubits of trapped-ions stored in high magnetic fields such as required for Penning trapping, where the nuclear and electron spins are largely decoupled due to the dominance of the external field Hamiltonian. The reduced nuclear magnetic moment results in insensitivity to external magnetic fields compared to the use of an electron spin, but also makes the nuclear spin hard to manipulate on fast timescales. To maintain speed, we propose a two-qubit phase gate technique which utilizes the electron spin, but for which the qubit remains protected from magnetic fields through retaining the nuclear encoding. This method works for magnetic-field insensitive qubits at lower fields as well as for both microwave and laser field gradients.
