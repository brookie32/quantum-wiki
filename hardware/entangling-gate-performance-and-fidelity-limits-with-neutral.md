arXiv:2605.19245v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Neutral-atom entangling gates are commonly analyzed with a single effective Rydberg-pair state, but near Forster resonances the pair manifold contains resonantly coupled interaction channels that change both the control landscape and the achievable fidelity. We develop a two-eigenstate model for this regime and show that when allowing for coupling to both pair states in the resonance, the gate fidelity is bounded by Fleq 1-(pi/2)/(Vau_R), for interaction strength V and Rydberg lifetime au_R. We construct a gate protocol that saturates this bound in the large-Rabi-frequency limit, improving the existing fidelity limit by approximately 40%. We also evaluate common gate protocols near Forster resonances and find that retaining the exchange dynamics increases predicted fidelities by up to two orders of magnitude over earlier treatments.
