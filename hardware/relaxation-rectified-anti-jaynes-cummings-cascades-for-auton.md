arXiv:2608.22298v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We propose an autonomous scheme for stabilizing prescribed Fock states in a Kerr cavity by rectifying anti-Jaynes--Cummings interactions with auxiliary-qubit relaxation. Full Lindblad simulations demonstrate steady states dominated by the Fock states |1rangle through |4rangle, accompanied by pronounced Wigner negativity. An analytical birth--death model captures the steady-state populations and identifies the operating regime for stabilization. These results establish auxiliary-qubit relaxation as a resource for autonomous reservoir engineering and provide a route to nonclassical bosonic-state preparation in a single Kerr cavity and, through a boundary reservoir, in a Kerr-cavity chain.
