arXiv:2611.33776v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We show a Structure Theorem for an arbitrary separable quantum system ((imHleqaleph_0)) that is coupled, through a symmetric bidirectional phase interface, to a self-sustaining phase-oscillator environment. Under the assumptions of (U(1)) covariance, instantaneous Lindblad bounded positivity, and the existence of an entrainable periodic attractor, the reduced generator is forced into a universal operator structure consisting of a state-dependent coherent recoil together with single-quantum feedback. Positivity further imposes a sharp dimensional dichotomy: purely dissipative gain saturation is forbidden in finite dimensions by trace-norm contraction, so coherent feedback is mandatory for qubits and qudits, whereas unbounded ladder algebras permit purely dissipative saturation. Expanding the general feedback functionals to leading order in the coupling recovers a canonical polynomial master equation that remains completely positive and trace-preserving in every dimension and combines additively with ordinary linear Lindblad channels. An accompanying Inverse Coupling Theorem shows that an autonomous periodic orbit can be sustained only when the interaction manifold has non-trivial fundamental group (pi_1(M)=Z), thereby isolating the sinusoidal (U(1))-covariant phase coupling as the generic mechanism. The resulting implementation is realized explicitly for a single qubit, a bipartite two-qubit system, and a bosonic Fock space.
