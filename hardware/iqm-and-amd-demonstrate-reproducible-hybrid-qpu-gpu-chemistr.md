IQM Quantum Computers and AMD have successfully demonstrated a hybrid quantum-classical computational chemistry workflow. This workflow utilizes IQM's 20-qubit Garnet processor and AMD Instinct GPUs with ROCm software to perform molecular ground-state energy calculations. The collaboration provides a multi-vendor solution for supercomputing centers and is available as an open-source Jupyter notebook for further development.
