arXiv:2607.16645v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We explore coherent ergotropy extraction in quantum batteries from a resource-geometric point of view. For an initial state rho, we quantify the coherent extraction process by the coherent ergotropy E_c(rho) and the coherent extraction distance D_c^{rm ext}(rho) between the active state sigma_rho and the passive state P_rho. This defines the geometric power capacity Pi_c(rho)=E_c(rho)/D_c^{rm ext}(rho), which measures the coherent ergotropy released unit minimal unitary distance. We prove that, for any driving Hamiltonian satisfying |V_t|leqnu, the actual coherent discharging power is bounded by P_c^{rm ext}(rho;V_t)leq nuPi_c(rho), showing that Pi_c(rho) is a capacity under unit driving norm rather than the power of a particular protocol. General bounds on Pi_c(rho) are derived by combining relative entropy bounds on coherent ergotropy with geometric bounds on the coherent extraction distance. We also formulate coherence measure induced bounds and protocol-corrected capacities involving the effective speed of a given Hamiltonian. Qubit and qutrit examples demonstrate that Pi_c(rho) captures a resource-geometric feature of coherent discharging beyond coherent ergotropy or coherence measures alone.
