arXiv:2611.14295v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Qubit probes record the relative orientation of neighboring, initially uncorrelated Ising spins through local interactions. Each probe encodes one bond label in two states with overlap 1-p. The resulting probe record is fully separable. A receiver gets one copy and guesses whether two distant spins point in the same or opposite directions. On the square lattice, the best readout with separable measurement effects approaches success probability 1/2 as the spin separation grows, for 0<ple p_0=1-1/sqrt2. This includes all local operations with classical communication. The same conclusion holds for every measurement whose effects remain positive under partial transposition across every cut of the probes. For p_*=0.29181le ple1, a joint readout of the same record maintains a success probability above 1/2 by a positive amount independent of distance. The overlap [p_*,p_0] gives a strict separation between the readouts. A record that is created locally and contains no entanglement can therefore hold long-range information that only measurements with entangled effects can read.
