A new qubit design has been shown to eliminate **98.4 % of leakage errors** that occur during measurement, dramatically improving readout fidelity and reducing noise for fault‑tolerant quantum processors. The approach modifies the qubit’s control architecture to suppress unwanted transitions while maintaining rapid, high‑speed measurement. This advancement is expected to enhance overall circuit reliability and accelerate progress toward scalable quantum computing.
