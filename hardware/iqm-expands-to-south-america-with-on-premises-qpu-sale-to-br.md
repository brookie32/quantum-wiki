IQM Quantum Computers is expanding into South America by selling an on-premises IQM Spark system to Brazil's Eldorado Research Institute, set for deployment in Q1 2027. This marks the first quantum computer acquisition by a private research institution in Brazil. The system, combined with cloud access to IQM's European 54-qubit QPU, will enable Eldorado to collaborate with universities, startups, and industrial partners in various sectors to develop quantum algorithms and explore hybrid HPC-AI-quantum workflows. This deal contributes to IQM's significant order backlog and global commercial growth.
