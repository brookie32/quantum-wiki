arXiv:2607.24521v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We investigate thermal quantum correlations in two coupled superconducting spin qubits described by an effective Andreev spin-qubit Hamiltonian with spin-orbit interaction. Using Local Quantum Fisher Information (LQFI) and Local Quantum Uncertainty (LQU), we examine the effects of superconducting phase difference, tunneling amplitude, spin-orbit coupling, and temperature. Our results show that quantum correlations decrease with increasing temperature but are enhanced by stronger tunneling and spin-orbit interaction. The superconducting phase induces periodic variations in the correlations through its modulation of the effective exchange couplings. We further demonstrate that larger energy gaps between the ground and first excited states suppress thermal excitations and enhance the stability of quantum correlations. A comparison with negativity reveals the distinct behaviors of these correlation measures, while LQFI is consistently greater than or equal to LQU in the investigated parameter regime. These findings clarify the mechanisms governing thermal quantum correlations and highlight the roles of phase engineering, spin-orbit interaction, and tunneling in protecting quantum resources in superconducting hybrid systems.
