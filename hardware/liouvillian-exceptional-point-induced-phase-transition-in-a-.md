**Liouvillian exceptional-point-induced phase transition in a single Kerr-cat qubit** (Communications Physics, 14 Oct 2026). The authors construct a driven‑dissipative Kerr‑cat qubit and identify a Liouvillian exceptional point (LEP) that triggers a non‑Hermitian phase transition: the system’s dynamics switch abruptly from underdamped oscillations to overdamped relaxation. This transition is evident in real‑time Wigner function evolution and Bloch‑sphere trajectories, where the formation of interference fringes provides a distinct phase‑space signature; Wigner negativity confirms that the observed critical behavior retains genuine quantum nonclassicality.
