arXiv:2609.23221v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In the spirit of the Brown-Susskind conjecture, proven in ite{haf} and ite{li}, we study the growth of the dimension of the set of n-qubit unitaries which can be obtained as a product of a fixed number of 2-qubit gates from a fixed set of pairs of qubits. We show that this dimension increases strictly when one more pair is added, for at least some choice of the 2-qubit pairs at each step.
