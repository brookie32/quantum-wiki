Mitsubishi Electric and OptQC are expanding their strategic partnership through investments and R&D collaborations. OptQC secured JPY 7 billion in Series A2 funding, bringing its total funding to over JPY 20 billion, which will support the development of a 10,000-qubit optical processor and a new Tokyo R&D facility. Mitsubishi Electric, through its ME Innovation Fund, participated in OptQC's funding round and is also leading two national quantum hardware control projects.
