arXiv:2606.04721v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: This study presents an analytical investigation of entanglement quantification in three-qubit pure states using the Acin canonical representation. A global concurrence measure is constructed from the one-qubit-versus-rest bipartite concurrences of the three-qubit system. A closed-form expression for this measure is derived in terms of the canonical parameters of a general three-qubit pure state. Its behavior is then examined for representative product, biseparable, GHZ-type, and W-type states. The measure is further compared with the three-tangle and tripartite negativity to examine their respective sensitivities to different forms of multipartite entanglement. The results show that the global concurrence remains sensitive to bipartite entanglement in biseparable states, whereas tripartite negativity and the three-tangle vanish when genuine tripartite entanglement is absent. These results demonstrate the complementary information provided by the different measures in characterizing entanglement in three-qubit pure states.
