arXiv:2608.18839v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We consider a fundamental problem of mixedness testing: Given n copies of an N-qubit state rho, determine whether rho = I_d/d or |rho-I_d/d|_1 geq arepsilon with high probability, where d = 2^N. In particular, we focus on performing this task in the practical setting of single-qubit measurements, where measurements are prepared independently on each qubit. We provide a nearly complete picture of single-qubit mixedness tesing by showing n = widetilde{Theta}left(sqrt{10}^N/arepsilon^2right). To establish our lower bound, we introduce a new measurement-dependent lower bound framework for adaptive single-copy state certification. For the upper bound, we present a randomized Pauli basis measurement protocol, which relies on a new primitive for computationally efficient uniformity testing of correlation-concentrated distributions on the Boolean hypercube.
