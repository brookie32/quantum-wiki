GlobalFoundries has finalized a 375 million CHIPS Act award to expand its Quantum Technology Solutions (QTS) unit in Malta, New York. This funding will scale domestic quantum hardware manufacturing, utilizing GF's 22FDX process for cryogenic CMOS and advanced packaging. The agreement establishes a secure domestic foundry ecosystem for various quantum computing architectures, reinforcing U.S. microelectronics supply chains.
