A new study benchmarked nine cloud-accessible quantum processors, revealing a significant gap between the number of physical qubits and the size of entangled GHZ states achievable without error mitigation. The research, which utilized standard remote cloud workflows, found that large superconducting processors only maintain multipartite entanglement across a small fraction of their physical registers. This highlights the challenges in achieving high-fidelity multipartite entanglement in current quantum hardware.
