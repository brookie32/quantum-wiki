IonQ has raised its full-year 2026 revenue outlook to 450 million–460 million, incorporating financial contributions from SkyWater Technology after its acquisition on July 31, 2026. This revised outlook is due to SkyWater's operations accelerating physical QPU manufacturing throughput, with 256-qubit processor wafers fabricating three times faster and delivering 12 times more wafer lots every six months.
