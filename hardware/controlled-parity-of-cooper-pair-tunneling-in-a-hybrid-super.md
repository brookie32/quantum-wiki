This research demonstrates control of the parity of Cooper pair tunneling (whether odd or even numbers of Cooper pairs tunnel) in a hybrid superconducting qubit called a harmonic parity qubit, which combines aluminum-oxide tunnel junctions with a gate-tunable nanowire junction in a SQUID configuration.   Controlling this parity opens applications in superconducting technology , and  previous approaches required complex multi-junction or all-hybrid architectures.
