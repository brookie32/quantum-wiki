arXiv:2609.21900v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In this paper, we resolve the quantum KKL conjecture of Montanaro and Osborne ite{MO2010} for quantum Boolean functions on the n-qubit hypercube. More precisely, for every self-adjoint unitary T, we bound the largest L_2-influence from below by a constant multiple of Var(T)log(n)/n. The proof heavily depends on upper and lower commutator estimates on the Hilbert space.
