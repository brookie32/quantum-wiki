Quobly and CEA-Leti are strengthening their collaboration through the FAMES Pilot Line, providing Quobly access to advanced processing tools for prototyping 3D integration and RF control layers for next-generation quantum processing units (QPUs). This partnership, alongside STMicroelectronics, aims to scale Quobly's QSOI® architecture, which integrates silicon spin qubits directly onto a 300 mm FD-SOI CMOS substrate. Quobly plans to offer initial cloud availability for its Alloy Pioneer processor family by late 2026, targeting large-scale, fault-tolerant silicon architectures by 2032.
