Alice & Bob is partnering with CEA to integrate its cat qubit software into High-Performance Computing (HPC) environments, aiming to develop hybrid supercomputing workflows and algorithms for Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing by 2030. This collaboration will see Alice & Bob's software integrated into Eviden's Bull Qaptiva™ platform, leveraging CEA's expertise for hardware-agnostic execution across supercomputers and cloud-hosted quantum processors. They will also jointly research many-body quantum physics problems to apply cat qubit technology to materials science, chemistry, and industrial physics.
