German quantum computing startup SAXON Q announced the commercial release of the SXQ128 (128 qubits) and SXQ512 (512 qubits), marking the first diamond-based nitrogen-vacancy center quantum processors to exceed ten physical qubits.   The systems operate entirely at room temperature without requiring cryogenic refrigeration, vacuum infrastructure, or specialized cleanroom facilities.   Target applications include quantum convolutional neural networks, complex material research, variational algorithms, and quantum chemistry simulations.
