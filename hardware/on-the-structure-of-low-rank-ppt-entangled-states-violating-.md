arXiv:2610.19905v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Bound-entangled states with positive partial transpose (PPT) can exhibit quantum steering and Bell nonlocality, disproving the long-standing Peres conjecture and its stronger steering counterpart. These counterexamples, obtained through explicit constructions or numerical searches, raise the question of whether they share a common structural origin. We address this question in the low-rank regime by asking whether known counterexamples can be obtained, up to invertible local filtering, from the complement of an unextendible product basis (UPB). We find that the answer depends crucially on the local dimension. In 3otimes3, the rank-four structure of PPT entangled states implies that both the steerable PPT state of Moroder extit{et al.} and the Bell-nonlocal PPT state of Vertesi and Brunner can be obtained from UPB-complement states under invertible local filtering. For three qubits, in contrast, rank-four states that are PPT across every bipartition are separable across each cut. Moreover, comparison of the product-vector structure of the relevant kernels shows that the known three-qubit Bell-nonlocal PPT states cannot be obtained by invertible local filtering from the complement of any three-qubit UPB. Going beyond rank four, we further show that a known rank-eight three-qutrit state, supported on the complement of a UPB and PPT across every bipartition, is genuinely multipartite entangled. These results show that UPBs provide a unifying structural origin for important low-rank PPT entangled states in 3otimes3, but that nonlocal bound entanglement is not, in general, a UPB phenomenon.
