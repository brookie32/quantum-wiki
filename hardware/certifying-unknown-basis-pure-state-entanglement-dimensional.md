arXiv:2601.11040v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We introduce a basis-independent algorithm that only uses single-copy measurements to certify the Schmidt number of 2n-qubit bipartite pure states. Assuming the local system dimension is d = 2^n, the Schmidt number hi is O(1), and the non-zero Schmidt coefficients are bounded below by Omega(1), our protocol achieves a total sample complexity of widetilde{O}(d^2hi^2) with only widetilde{O}(hi^4) measurement settings. This work establishes a measurement-setting-efficient approach to high-dimensional entanglement certification.
