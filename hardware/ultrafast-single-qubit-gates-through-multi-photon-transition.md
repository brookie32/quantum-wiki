Ultrafast single‑qubit gates were demonstrated on a transmon qubit using double recursive Derivative Removal by Adiabatic Gate (DRAG) pulse shaping, which eliminates unwanted multi‑photon transitions beyond nearest‑neighbor levels. The resulting X and X/2 gates achieve leakage errors ≤ 2 × 10⁻⁵ and fidelities > 99.98% with pulse durations of 6.8 ns. An amplified leakage error sequence was introduced, enabling direct measurement of leakage down to 10⁻⁶, and benchmarking across multiple durations confirms the method’s applicability to other quantum platforms.
