arXiv:2610.36356v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We investigate the probability that a conditioned quantum trajectory exceeds an ensemble-derived quantum speed limit (QSL) velocity under continuous measurement-based feedback. Using the finite-time average fidelity velocity, we derive an upper bound on the exceedance probability in terms of its mean and variance. We further obtain state-independent bounds from uniform constraints on the fidelity drift and diffusion, including an exponential concentration bound. For a feedback-controlled qubit, we derive explicit model-specific bounds and show that they are nontrivial in a short-time regime. We also identify a finite-time regime in which the exceedance probability vanishes exactly due to the restricted support of the conditioned dynamics. Monte Carlo simulations confirm the analytical bounds and illustrate their tightness.
