High‑fidelity readout of hole spin qubits in germanium double quantum dots was enabled by combining a radiofrequency charge sensor with a magnetically tuned double‑latching scheme that adiabatically maps the \(|\downarrow\downarrow\rangle\) state to the only non‑blockaded configuration. By mapping out spin–to–charge conversion and relaxation as functions of magnetic field orientation and strength, the authors minimized error rates by operating at low fields. Integrating these optimizations yields a single‑shot readout fidelity of 97 % for single‑qubit state preparation and measurement while preserving control over both spins during readout.
