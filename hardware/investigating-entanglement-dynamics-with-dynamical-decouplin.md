arXiv:2610.02453v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We study the exact entanglement dynamics of two qubits coupled to a common bosonic reservoir through a pure dephasing interaction. We ask how this dynamics changes when the qubits and the reservoir already share correlations before the qubit state is prepared. In that case the system and reservoir first interact for a long time and settle into a joint equilibrium state. Only afterward is the qubit pair projected onto the desired state by a local measurement. For an Ohmic spectral density we obtain the exact reduced two qubit dynamics. Using the Wootters concurrence we show that initial correlations always speed up entanglement sudden death relative to the factorized case, and that this gap grows quadratically with the coupling strength in the weak-to-moderate coupling regime. Finally we show that a symmetric sequence of periodic {pi} pulses applied to both qubits extends the entanglement lifetime close to a factor of seven over the range of pulse numbers studied.
