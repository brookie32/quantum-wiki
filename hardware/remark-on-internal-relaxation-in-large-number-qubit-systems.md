arXiv:2611.21681v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: For a time t_{c} of calculation in an N qubit quantum computer that is much shorter than the intrinsic Heisenberg time t_H, t_cll t_H, the dynamics of the system is effectively irreversible and the couplings between the qubits give rise to a relaxation time scale au_r. If t_Hgg t_c >sim au_r one looses quantum coherence exponentially fast. Since t_H scales exponentially with N there is a threshold N_c such that for Ngg N_c one must be aware of possible intrinsic relaxation during calculation. As an order of magnitude estimation we find N_capprox ln(au_r/T) where T is a typical system specific oscillation time of a single qubit. au_r can be estimated as the typical inverse transition rate between two parts of the system, one of which is treated as an environment of the other.
