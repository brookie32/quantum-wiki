PsiQuantum was founded on a simple premise: useful quantum computing requires a million-qubit, fault-tolerant system. We chose photonics for its scaling advantages and spent nearly a decade building an ultra-low-loss silicon photonics platform to make that path possible. That platform is foundational to our path to useful quantum computers. Now, beginning with Optical Circuit Switching, it can help power the next generation of AI infrastructure. Media
