arXiv:2611.27034v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We derive uncertainty relations for quantum channels based on the generalized Wigner-Yanase skew information. For three channels, we obtain a product lower bound by optimizing over permutations of commutator and anticommutator pairings. For an arbitrary number of channels, we establish a lower bound on the sum of squared uncertainties and construct a hierarchy of bounds using the partial Cauchy-Schwarz inequality. These bounds are invariant under changes of Kraus representation for a fixed operator basis. The first two levels of the hierarchy coincide with the elementary arithmetic-geometric mean bound, while the intermediate levels generally depend on the ordering of the operator basis. Examples involving three and four qubit channels demonstrate the bounds and compare their performance with an earlier pairing inequality under fixed channel representations.
