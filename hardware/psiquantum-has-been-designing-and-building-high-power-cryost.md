PsiQuantum has been designing and building high-power cryostats in a datacenter form-factor since 2020. Our design leverages the higher operating temperature of photonic qubits to pack hundreds of modules into a single cabinet, advancing beyond the traditional "chandelier." Our fourth generation cryogenic cabinets bring a more compact, modular design while maintaining the same 60W cooling capacity at 2 K.
