Classiq has upgraded its Qmod quantum programming language and compiler, allowing the Shor's algorithm for a 2048-bit RSA-scale integer to compile in under ten seconds. The improvement addresses a bottleneck in quantum software by preserving classical control structures during synthesis and enabling symbolic indexing of quantum qubit arrays.
