arXiv:2504.15276v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We introduce a 0.611-approximation algorithm for Quantum MaxCut and a frac{1+sqrt{5}}{4} approx 0.809-approximation algorithm for the EPR Hamiltonian of [arXiv:2209.02589]. A novel ingredient in both of these algorithms is to partially entangle pairs of qubits associated to edges in a matching, while preserving the direction of their single-qubit Bloch vectors. This allows us to interpolate between product states and matching-based states with a tunable parameter.
