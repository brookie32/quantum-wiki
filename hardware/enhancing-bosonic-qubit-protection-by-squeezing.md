Researchers show that applying quadrature‑squeezing to bosonic qubits—quantum logical states encoded in a harmonic oscillator’s high‑dimensional Hilbert space—enhances protection against decoherence. By redistributing quantum uncertainty, squeezing reduces the impact of unwanted measurements on noise‑sensitive quadratures, thereby strengthening error‑correction performance within these codes. This approach leverages principles from precision metrology to mitigate environmental back‑action that would otherwise collapse superpositions.
