A dual-channel qubit protection system uses "dual-rail" technology that includes error correction built in, improving qubit quality while reducing the physical qubits needed for logical qubits.   The qubit is encoded by a single microwave photon shared between two superconducting cavities, giving qubits both classical zero/one states and superpositions plus a third state to detect photon loss, with error correction built in.   Direct error detection allows identification of where and when qubit errors occur, making error correction easier and potentially enabling practical quantum error correction with current superconducting circuit coherence times.
