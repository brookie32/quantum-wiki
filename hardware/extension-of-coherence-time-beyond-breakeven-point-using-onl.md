In November 2026 the authors reported an autonomous quantum error‑correction (QEC) protocol that uses only engineered drives and dissipation to repeatently restore a photon‑loss corrupted logical qubit.  By transferring loss‑induced errors to ancilla qubits which are subsequently reset, they applied the scheme to a binomial code encoded in a long‑lived bosonic mode and achieved a logical‑qubit coherence time 1.04× that of the best underlying physical qubit—surpassing the breakeven point without measurement‑based feedback.  The method is derived from quantum channel simulation, is theoretically generalizable to arbitrary recovery maps, and demonstrates drives/dissipation alone can extend coherence beyond current limits.
