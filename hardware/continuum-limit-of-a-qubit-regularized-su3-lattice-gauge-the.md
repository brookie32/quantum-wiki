arXiv:2603.01215v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We show that a simple qubit-regularized SU(3) lattice gauge theory on a plaquette chain admits a continuum limit with massive gauge-invariant excitations, providing a minimal toy model of strong interactions without quarks. By mapping the plaquette-chain Hamiltonian to the three-state quantum clock model in a magnetic field, we demonstrate that the theory can be tuned to a continuum limit governed at short distances by the Z_3 parafermion conformal field theory, which serves as the ultraviolet fixed point. A small relevant magnetic perturbation then drives the system to a massive continuum quantum field theory in the infrared, featuring relativistic particles. In this continuum theory we compute two universal ratios: the mass ratio of the lightest massive particles with opposite charge conjugation, m^{-}/m^{+} = 1.459(2), and sqrt{sigma}/m^{+} = 0.2648(2), where sigma denotes the string tension between a static quark and antiquark.
