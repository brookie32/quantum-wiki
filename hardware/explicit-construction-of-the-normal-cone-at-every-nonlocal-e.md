arXiv:2611.27413v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The set of quantum correlations Q_{2222}, in the simplest Bell scenario with two parties performing two binary measurements each, is an object of fundamental interest both in quantum foundations and for device-independent applications. A nonlocal extremal quantum behaviour in this set can maximize many different Bell expressions, yet these expressions are not all equally useful in a practical experiment. Here, firstly, we give an explicit characterization of every Bell expression maximized by any given nonlocal extremal quantum behaviour in this Bell scenario. This determines the full normal cone at every such behaviour and makes it possible to optimize over all Bell tests with the same ideal behavior. We then derive an exact variational formula for the quantum bound of an arbitrary Bell expression in this scenario. As an example illustrating the construction, we derive the normal cone for a family of extremal quantum behaviours of importance in device-independent randomness certification, from Phys. Rev. Lett. 129, 150403, 2022. Finally, we explore the consequences of this characterization for device-independent randomness generation in practical experiments. Specifically, for any given detection efficiency eta in [0.75,0.98], we construct Bell tests and two-qubit experiments that certify more single-round min-entropy from one Bell score than any CHSH score can certify with the same detector efficiency, even when the quantum devices used for the CHSH test are optimized. The results provide a systematic way to choose Bell tests for a desired quantum behaviour and to improve their performance for device-independent applications under experimental imperfections.
