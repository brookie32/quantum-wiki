QuTech and TU Delft researchers received a six-year ERC Synergy Grant for their UniQ project to scale semiconductor spin-qubit processors. The project aims to overcome wiring and control bottlenecks by co-integrating spin qubits and cryo-CMOS electronics, enabling simplified control and increased qubit connectivity.
