Silicon spin qubits are scalable for quantum computing, but parallel high‑fidelity control is hindered by microwave‑driven a.c.–Stark shifts that degrade gate fidelity. The authors introduced a scalable mitigation protocol using pairwise phase calibrations on shared control lines, achieving X and Y gate fidelities above 99.99% (up to 99.999%) for each qubit. These high fidelities are maintained during simultaneous operation of up to three qubits and remain above the fault‑tolerant threshold (>99.9 %) even when all five qubits in the array are operated concurrently.
