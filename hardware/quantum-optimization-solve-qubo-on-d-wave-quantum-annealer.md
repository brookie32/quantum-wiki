Quantum annealers are devices that solve optimization problems submitted as Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) problems.   To solve a problem using D-Wave architecture, it must be expressed as a QUBO formulation, and then the logical problem is embedded in the quantum annealer by mapping logical variables to qubits.   D-Wave annealers are explicitly built for optimization and sampling problems, so optimization problems tend to run faster on them than classical computers.
