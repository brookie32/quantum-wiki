arXiv:2509.12703v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Quantum state tomography (QST) is a central task in quantum information, and its efficiency is commonly characterized by sample complexity. Although collective measurements on multiple copies achieve optimal performance, they are difficult to implement on near-term devices, motivating the study of single-copy approaches. Here, we introduce an ancilla-free single-copy QST protocol based on logarithmic-depth local circuits on an n-qubit system. For rank-r states in dimension d=2^n, our protocol achieves trace-norm error epsilon using O(dr^2log d/epsilon^2) copies, matching the single-copy lower bound up to a logarithmic factor. For full-rank mixed states, it removes this logarithmic overhead and achieves the optimal scaling O(d^3/epsilon^2), with nearly optimal classical runtime for explicit matrix output. These results show that sample-optimal QST can be realized using experimentally accessible shallow-depth measurements.
