arXiv:2610.03658v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In this note, we consider tomography of n-qubit quantum states |psi rangle that are promised to have stabilizer rank k i.e., admit a stabilizer decomposition of |psirangle = sum_{i=1}^k c_i |phi_irangle where each |phi_irangle is a stabilizer state. We give a tomography protocol for such states up to trace distance arepsilon > 0 running in extsf{poly}(n,2^k,1/arepsilon) time and thus can handle k=O(log n) efficiently. This follows from first showing that the stabilizer extent xi(|psirangle), which is the minimal ell_1 norm over the coefficients {c_i} of any stabilizer decomposition of |psirangle, can be bounded as xi(|psirangle) leq 2^{O(k)} and improving upon the previous best bound of k^{O(k)}. The protocol is then obtained by utilizing a tomography protocol for states with bounded stabilizer extent.
