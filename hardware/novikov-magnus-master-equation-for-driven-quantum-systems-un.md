arXiv:2611.09876v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We introduce a modular framework for deriving time-local quantum master equations for driven systems subject to temporally correlated classical noise. The construction combines the Donsker-Furutsu-Novikov relation with the Magnus expansion: Functional response closes the noise average, while nested commutators retain the noncommuting chronological action of noise-induced perturbations along the controlled quantum trajectory. For zero-mean Gaussian noise, we derive the resulting Novikov-Magnus quantum master equation through fourth order in the noise strength. The same response hierarchy suggests a natural extension beyond Gaussian noise, with connected higher-order noise correlations weighting higher functional derivatives of the stochastic map. For a driven qubit under Ornstein-Uhlenbeck and band-limited 1/f noise, the second- and fourth-order maps remain CPTP throughout the perturbative regime and accurately reproduce stochastic simulations. Their diamond-norm errors are comparable to those of the cumulant-based pseudo-Lindblad master equation, with a modest fourth-order improvement for Ornstein-Uhlenbeck noise.
