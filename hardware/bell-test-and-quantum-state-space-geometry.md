arXiv:2611.07450v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We explore a minimal phenomenological model of coupling between the geometry of the classical spacetime and the quantum state space. Assuming a simple structure of the combined manifold, bilinear interaction between the corresponding geometric scalars and the simplest case of the two-qubit entanglement dynamics, we derive an illustrative upper bound on the geometric coupling constant from the requirement of the Clauser-Horne-Shimony-Holt (CHSH) inequality violation over a finite time interval.
