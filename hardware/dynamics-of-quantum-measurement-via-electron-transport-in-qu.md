arXiv:2510.05348v2 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Measurement of a charge qubit via point contacts with complex internal structures is considered. In this context, a fully formalized derivation of the many-body wave function method is presented, together with the corresponding master equations for point contacts possessing an arbitrary number of internal states. The focus is placed on the current noise power spectrum and its dependence on the qubit dynamics and the point contact parameters.
