Universal Quantum, a UK-based hardware developer specializing in trapped-ion quantum processors, has successfully closed a 100 million Series A funding round. This investment will fuel their international expansion, including operations in Europe, Singapore, the U.S., and Japan, and support their 77 million contract with the German Aerospace Center. Their technology utilizes modular silicon chiplets connected by micro-electric ion-shuttling links, operating at a mild cryogenic temperature of 70 Kelvin, which reduces error rates and avoids complex sub-Kelvin cooling systems.
