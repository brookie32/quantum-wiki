NVIDIA announced that it has successfully decoded errors in cat‑qubits on GPU hardware using its NVQLink platform. The achievement demonstrates reliable logical error correction for this type of quantum bit, indicating progress toward scalable, fault‑tolerant quantum computing integrated with classical processors. This milestone showcases the potential for high‑performance quantum–classical synergies on commercial GPUs.
