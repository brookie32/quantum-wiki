Using a trapped‑ion processor that couples qubit gauge fields to vibrational modes encoding matter, the authors implement a resource‑efficient \(\mathbb{Z}_2\) lattice gauge theory and carry out its real‑time evolution with a hybrid digital–analog protocol. They confirm Gauss’s law on a single link and extend the system to a quasi‑two‑dimensional loop where interference between dynamical gauge fields reproduces the Aharonov–Bohm effect, demonstrating charge–flux coupling in the simulator. This establishes a scalable route for quantum simulations of higher‑dimensional lattice gauge theories.
