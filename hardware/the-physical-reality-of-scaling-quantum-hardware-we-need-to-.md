Scaling quantum hardware requires overcoming significant challenges related to cryogenic infrastructure, including packaging density, wiring complexity, heat load, and mechanical precision in component fabrication.   Critical dependencies for quantum materials and components—including superconducting materials, specialized photonic wafers, and isotopically enriched silicon—often flow through foreign suppliers, creating supply chain vulnerabilities.   Specialized cryogenic components like ion pumps and pulse tubes present single points of failure in quantum system architectures.
