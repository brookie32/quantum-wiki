arXiv:2611.08161v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We construct a primitive gate set for the digital quantum simulation of the 648-element discrete subgroup of SU(3), sb. The five algorithmic primitives are the inversion, group multiplication, trace, group Fourier transform, and kinetic-phase gates, for which we provide qubit and qutrit--qubit decompositions. For a fiducial shear-viscosity calculation on a 10^3 spatial lattice with 50 time steps and a total synthesis-error budget of 10^{-8}, the estimated fault-tolerant costs are 2.3--6.8imes10^{10} T gates in the qubit architecture and 1.3--5.9imes10^{12} R gates in the qutrit--qubit architecture.
