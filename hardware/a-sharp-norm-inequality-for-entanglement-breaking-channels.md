arXiv:2609.27906v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: For a channel Phi on M_d written in the generalised Bloch parameterisation r mapsto Ar+c, we prove that entanglement breaking implies |A|_*^2 + frac{d(d-1)}{2}|c|^2 le (d-1)^2, where |dot|_* denotes the nuclear norm. The bound is attained in every dimension by the completely dephasing channel, and at d=2 by an explicit family with cneq0. The qubit case reads |A|_*^2+|c|^2le1 and extends to full rank a rank-two condition of Ruskai. The proof sharpens Ruskai's bound |A|_*le1 by retaining the POVM completeness relation sum_k f_k=0, which her argument discards; this recentres A from a second moment into a cross-covariance, and is the entire content of the improvement.
