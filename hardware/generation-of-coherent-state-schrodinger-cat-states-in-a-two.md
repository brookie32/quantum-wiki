arXiv:2611.25323v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We investigate the generation of coherent-state Schrodinger cat states using a two-photon Jaynes-Cummings model (JCM) implemented in a Josephson-junction quantum circuit. We consider a specific superconducting flux-qubit implementation and estimate the resulting two-photon coupling constant. We numerically examine three types of noise: magnetic flux noise with 1/f fluctuations, energy relaxation associated with the flux-qubit T_{1} decay, and photon loss in the bosonic mode obtained by quantizing the phase variable of the dc SQUID used for qubit readout. We find that photon loss has a particularly significant effect on cat-state fidelity. We also investigate the time evolution of the probability-amplitude distribution in the Pegg-Barnett phase-state basis for the l-photon JCM (l=1,2,3,4), starting from a coherent bosonic state. Finally, we assume that the bosonic mode is initially prepared in a displaced thermal state and then decoupled from the heat bath. We study the subsequent unitary evolution to determine how the initial temperature affects the fidelity and Wigner function of the generated cat states.
