IBM has linked cryogenic quantum modules as a step toward a fault‑tolerant system expected by 2029. The approach integrates multiple module units with cold control electronics to increase qubit count while improving coherence and reducing error rates. This development is part of IBM’s broader roadmap for scalable, high‑performance quantum computers in the next decade.
