arXiv:2611.08040v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We develop an effective thermodynamics for a qubit whose energy gap depends inversely on a volume-like control parameter, Delta(V)=Delta_0/V, where V is a spectral-control coordinate rather than a literal mechanical volume. Starting from the canonical Gibbs state, we derive a complete thermodynamic structure from the Helmholtz potential and its Legendre and stability properties, including the fundamental relation S(U,V), a generalized pressure conjugate to V, the equation of state U=PV, the first law, and the associated Maxwell relations. Reversible adiabats preserve the Gibbs populations and satisfy TV=const. and PV^2=const., corresponding to an effective exponent gamma=2. The resulting effective Carnot cycle attains the Carnot efficiency, eta_{Carnot}=1-T_C/T_H, whereas the Otto cycle yields eta_{Otto}=1-V_{min}/V_{max} and remains strictly sub-Carnot at finite work output. We identify the source of Otto irreversibility exactly: entropy is produced entirely during the isochoric thermalizations, and the cycle entropy production quantifies the informational mismatch between the adiabatically transported states and the reservoir-selected Gibbs states through the sum of the corresponding quantum relative entropies. Finally, we show that unitary-generated nonpassivity can act as a nonequilibrium battery, producing an apparent efficiency above Carnot when its preparation cost is omitted. At the same time, complete work accounting restores the thermodynamic bound.
