arXiv:2611.13658v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We consider exact preparation of the uniform quantum state supported on an arbitrary nonempty interval of integer labels in an n-qubit register. For every 0leq a<a+Mleq2^n, we give an ancilla-free circuit using only one-qubit gates and singly controlled one-qubit gates. In the logical gate model specified in the paper, the direct construction has exact worst-case gate count 5n-9 for ngeq3; its exact maxima for n=1 and n=2 are 1 and 3, respectively. A worst-case lower bound of Omega(n) establishes asymptotic optimality in the register width. In addition, the gate complexity admits an instance-dependent bound O(log_2 M+epsilon), where epsilon is an offset-dependent parameter defined in terms of binary Hamming weights. We also derive a reflected construction and a classical procedure that selects the lower-count exact circuit. The direct construction admits an O(n) logical-depth upper bound. We also treat bitwise-XOR images of intervals and arithmetic progressions with power-of-two spacing.
