IBM has announced that it will link cryogenic control modules directly to its superconducting qubit chips in order to scale fault‑tolerant quantum computers. By operating the electronics at millikelvin temperatures, the modules can be positioned closer to the qubits, reducing signal latency and improving coherence. This integration is a key step toward building larger, more reliable quantum processors.
