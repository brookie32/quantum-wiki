Xanadu and Bluefors have formed a multi-million dollar partnership to develop a modular cryogenic infrastructure for utility-scale quantum data centers. Their joint effort focuses on replacing large, centralized cryoplants with compact, mass-manufacturable cryogenic modules designed for high-density detector arrays operating near 2 Kelvin. This collaboration aims to address cooling bottlenecks and provide a scalable, standardized cryogenic solution for fault-tolerant photonic quantum processors.
