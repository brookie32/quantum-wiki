Quantinuum and The University of Western Australia (UWA) have partnered to advance Australia's quantum-AI-HPC ecosystem. This collaboration grants UWA access to Quantinuum's 98-qubit Helios trapped-ion system and focuses on co-designing hybrid workflows for critical Australian sectors like minerals, energy, agriculture, and healthcare. The agreement also includes plans for a university-wide quantum computing applications center and workforce development through Quantinuum's global Q-Net network.
