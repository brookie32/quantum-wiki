arXiv:2610.36172v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Classical simulation is essential for developing and testing quantum circuits, but the exponential growth of Hilbert space challenges conventional digital processors. Here, we introduce a classical optical quantum simulation architecture that treats gates and batches of input states as independent tensor dimensions rather than separate simulation instances, while mapping eligible tensor network contractions onto the same processor. Using co-designed multidimensional encoding and readout across spatial and wavelength degrees of freedom, we experimentally realize high-order tensor-matrix multiplication in a single propagation and process all computational-basis inputs for each 4-qubit gate. Using a physics-based optical propagation model, we further numerically evaluate the architecture for the Deutsch algorithm and for the tensor network of all 128 target instances of a 7-qubit Grover algorithm. These results establish a unified framework for gate-state batch and tensor network simulation, providing a route to exploit optical concurrency across quantum simulation tasks.
