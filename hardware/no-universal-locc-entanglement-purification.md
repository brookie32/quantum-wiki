arXiv:2611.17809v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Entanglement purification aims to obtain high-fidelity entangled states from noisy entangled states. This task becomes particularly challenging when the available input states are finite in number, only partially characterized, and may not be identical. Whether there exists a universal purification protocol applicable to such inputs has therefore been a longstanding question. This motivates the study of universal n-to-1 entanglement purification. Although no such LOCC protocol exists for the full set of two-qubit entangled states, it has remained open whether this no-go result extends to higher-dimensional bipartite states and multipartite states. In this work, we resolve this question by proving that, for any fixed finite local dimensions, no n-to-1 LOCC protocol is universal for all bipartite entangled states or all multipartite entangled states. We further show that the bipartite and multipartite no-go results persist under several natural extensions and establish an analogous no-go theorem for coherence purification under strictly incoherent operations.
