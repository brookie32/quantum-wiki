Algorithmiq and IBM Quantum have successfully executed 40-qubit discrete-time quantum walks (DTQWs) on irregular protein-protein interaction networks, overcoming limitations of conventional dense encodings. They used a sparse single-excitation encoding and novel gate operations to significantly reduce circuit depth. This advancement was applied to a biomedical case study, where quantum walks helped prioritize an asthma risk gene more effectively than classical methods.
