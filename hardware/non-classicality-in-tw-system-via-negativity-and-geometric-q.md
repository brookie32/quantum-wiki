arXiv:2610.27404v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We perform a phenomenological study of non-classical correlations in the mixed-dimensional qubit-qutrit system realized by leading order ppo tW production at sqrt{s}=13 TeV in the leptonic final state. The two missing neutrinos are reconstructed with conditional normalizing flows trained on simulated detector-level objects, and the joint spin density matrix rho_{tW}inH_2otimesH_3 is obtained in a generalized Bloch representation from the angular distributions of the leptonic decay products. Non-classical correlations are quantified using the Peres--Horodecki negativity and geometric quantum discord. We quantify the correlations at different region of m_{tW} at the reconstruction level within the Standard Model and the impact of two reducible backgrounds viz. tar{t} and WWj processes, tagged using boosted decision trees. We further study the behavior of correlations in presence of anomalous chromomagnetic and chromoelectric dipole moments of the top quark. The sensitivity reach of the quantum observables along with polarization and spin correlation parameters are reported for current LHC and future HL-LHC scenario.
