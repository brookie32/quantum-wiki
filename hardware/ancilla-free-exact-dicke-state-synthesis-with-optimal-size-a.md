arXiv:2611.27640v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We construct exact circuits for n-qubit Dicke states of any Hamming weight k using O(n) single-qubit gates and CNOTs in O(log n) depth without ancillary qubits. The gate count and depth are asymptotically optimal for every 0<k<n. We compress symmetric blocks to free output qubits for weight-dependent phases. A sign correction followed by a collective rotation produces a state whose overlap with the target is at least a positive constant, so exact amplitude amplification takes O(1) steps. For balanced Dicke states (k=lfloor n/2rfloor), the logarithmic depth disproves the Omega(k) depth lower bound conjectured by Yuan and Zhang (2025).
