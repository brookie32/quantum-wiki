Gold nanoclusters are being explored by Delta Gold Technologies, Penn State, and the University of Toronto as a potential qubit platform designed to address quantum computing's stability-scalability trade-off.   Research has demonstrated approximately 40% spin-polarized photon emission from sub-1-nanometer gold nanoclusters and gram-quantity synthesis under accessible lab conditions, offering advantages over complex fabrication requirements of conventional qubit systems.   However, the technology remains at the research stage rather than commercial deployment, and it is too early to confirm that gold nanoclusters have definitively solved the stability and scalability challenge.
