arXiv:2605.22603v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Local Markovian noise cannot bring entanglement back, but it can bring magic back. Local dissipation can push a state out of the stabilizer polytope as well as in. Under independent amplitude damping, the n-qubit state alpha|0^nrangle+eta|1^nrangle with 0<alpha<eta loses its magic for a finite interval. The coherence between its ground and fully excited configurations keeps falling throughout. Magic returns when the fully excited population falls below that coherence. The damping probabilities for entanglement death and magic rebirth obey gamma_e+gamma_+=1 for every nge2. Magic returns to the register just as its reservoirs become entangled. For a sufficiently small ground-state amplitude the register is already fully separable at rebirth. Every proper marginal is a stabilizer mixture throughout, yet a joint parity measurement followed by Clifford decoding can concentrate the reborn magic onto one qubit. With thermal relaxation and dephasing, we find a range in which magic loss is permanent for every isolated qubit, yet two qubits exposed to the same noise can regain it.
