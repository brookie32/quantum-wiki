arXiv:2608.15464v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Lesniewski and Ruskai conjectured that the contraction coefficient of every monotone Riemannian metric under a unital stochastic map equals the Hilbert--Schmidt contraction on the traceless subspace. We disprove the conjecture with an explicit entanglement-breaking qutrit channel induced by a doubly stochastic 3imes3 matrix. A faithful diagonal state and a commuting traceless tangent give, simultaneously for every normalized monotone metric, the exact lower bound eta^{Riem}_{kappa}(Phi_K)ge 8896/20007>(62+2sqrt{61})/225=Lambda_2(Phi_K^{agger}Phi_K). The counterexample is entirely classical on a maximal abelian subalgebra. A theorem of Hiai and Ruskai establishes the conjectured identity for all unital qubit maps, so dimension three is minimal among full matrix algebras.
