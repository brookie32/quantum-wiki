arXiv:2611.22640v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The Quantum Rabi Model (QRM), consisting of a single qubit coupled to a harmonic oscillator, provides a paradigmatic platform for exploring critical phenomena investigated for their applications in quantum technologies. In the superradiant phase of the QRM with coupling strength above the critical point, the bosonic ground state exhibits temporally stable quantum non-Gaussian features. However, the quantum non-Gaussian features of such superradiant ground states can be fragile against loss during preparation. Here, we show that persistent quantum non-Gaussian features can instead be realized below the critical point through weak periodic qubit modulation. We analyze an adiabatic ramp protocol in which the modulation amplitude is gradually increased, allowing the system to approximately follow the instantaneous Floquet state. When the modulation period is tuned close to Poincare-Birkhoff resonances, the resulting state exhibits pronounced quantum non-Gaussian features in the directly measurable Wigner function with a complex phase-space structure that is considerably more robust against loss than that of the superradiant ground state. The protocol of adiabatic ramps provides an attractive route for generating long-lived quantum non-Gaussian effects below the critical point.
