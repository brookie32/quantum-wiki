arXiv:2611.07835v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Electric-dipole (E1) transition rates underlie applications across many areas of physics and demand more accurate wavefunctions than ground-state energies do. We report the first calculation of E1 transition rates on a D-Wave quantum annealer, for neutral Li, Li-like Be, neutral Na, and Na-like Mg. We use a zooming-and-sigma-annealing scheme with floating-point encoding within the Quantum Annealer Eigensolver (QAE) algorithm, which yields ground-state eigenvectors accurate to the chosen three decimal places. Treating the lowest even-parity (^2S_{1/2}) and odd-parity (^2P_{1/2}, ^2P_{3/2}) states as separate angular-symmetry sectors gives QAE access to excited atomic configurations for transition-rate calculations. Our results reproduce relativistic configuration-interaction values obtained classically to within numerical precision, demonstrating that the QAE framework extends to a broader class of atomic properties on near-term quantum hardware.
