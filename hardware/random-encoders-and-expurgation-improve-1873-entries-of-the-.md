arXiv:2611.08744v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: I report 192 explicit binary stabilizer codes [[n,k,d]] with 53le nle 254 and 5le dle 13 whose minimum distance exceeds the lower bound listed in Grassl's code tables at the same (n,k): 161 codes by one and 31 by two. Through the standard propagation rules they raise 1,873 entries of the table. For 190 of the codes, a random Clifford encoder is followed by expurgation: every logical operator of weight below the target distance is enumerated, and a few logical operators are promoted to stabilizers so that each low-weight one anticommutes with at least one of them. The other two are obtained by puncturing a code from the table and repairing it in the same way. All 192 distances are exact: the lower bound is certified by exhaustive meet-in-the-middle enumeration of up to 4imes10^{13} Pauli operators, and the upper bound by an explicit logical operator of weight d. A first-moment count explains the results. Expurgated random codes have about eight more logical qubits than a typical random code of the same length and distance, so they beat the table wherever its best known codes are no better than random. The largest gains, up to nine logical qubits, are at dge9 and ngtrsim170, where the table often gives a lower bound without an explicit code. The same count predicts improvements at 619 further pairs (n,d). The search was run by AI agents. The codes, their certificates and an independent checker are public.
