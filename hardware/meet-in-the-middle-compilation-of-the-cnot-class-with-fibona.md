arXiv:2610.37552v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Compilation of two-qubit Fibonacci anyon gates is complicated by leakage (ell) from the four-dimensional computational sector to the fifth state of the fusion space. We construct a GPU pipeline to find low-leakage representatives of the [ext{CNOT}] class on different subsets of the braid group. We exhaustively explore the full positive five-generator alphabet up to L=21 with minimal leakage ellsim10^{-3} and error d_{[ext{CNOT}]}sim 10^{-8} on it. To reach larger lengths, the search space is successively narrowed to three-generator, boundary-sigma_3, and palindromic braids, and by considering only the Garside left normal form. These optimizations allow us to reach L=72. For L=69, a braid with ellsim10^{-10} and d_{[ext{CNOT}]}sim 10^{-10} is found. In addition to directly enumerating optimal braids, we proved that ellinmathbb Z[arphi], and any nonzero leakage obeys an exponential lower bound in the braid length, and obtained the limit laws F_{3,infty}(ell)=ell^2 and F_{5,infty}(ell)=ell^4 for our sets. Using asymptotics for the distribution function F, we showed that for sufficiently long braids, the asymptotics of exhaustive search are better than the asymptotics of random sampling for finding low-leakage braids.
