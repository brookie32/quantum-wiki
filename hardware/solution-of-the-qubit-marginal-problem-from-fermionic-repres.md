arXiv:2611.18503v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Whether local reduced states are compatible with a global quantum state---the quantum marginal problem---is central to quantum information and simulation. Here we present a solution to the ensemble marginal problem for qubits based on the representability theory of fermions. Encoding each qubit as one fermion in two orbitals, we prove that the representable qubit marginals form an exposed face of the set of N-representable fermionic reduced density matrices, selected by the vanishing of every site-occupation variance. Complete hierarchies of fermionic representability conditions therefore transfer exactly to qubits. Applications to frustrated Heisenberg and transverse-field Ising rings show that the (2,3)-positivity conditions are exact at the Majumdar--Ghosh point and accurate to about 0.5% at criticality. Because the conditions hold for any quantum state, they also provide a framework for mitigating measurement errors on qubit-based quantum devices.
