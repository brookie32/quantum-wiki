arXiv:2611.17216v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Absolutely maximally entangled (AME) states are pure multipartite states with maximally mixed reductions on every set of at most half the parties. We construct explicit families in local dimension 2q, twice an odd prime power q, using an established coupling of qubits and q-level systems. The q-level states remove interference between branches, so the qubit states need maximally mixed reductions only on average. This gives AME(9,2q) and AME(11,2q) for every odd prime power qgeq5, AME(13,2q) for qgeq17, AME(15,2q) for qgeq9, and AME(17,2q) for qgeq67. For every odd Ngeq3, the same coupling gives AME(N,2q) under the sufficient condition qgeqmax{N,2^{N-2}} on the odd prime power q. Within this coupling, the smallest such q must grow exponentially with N. Combined with known inputs and products, these families complete the nine-party classification: for integer dgeq2, an AME(9,d) state exists if and only if dgeq3. Choosing the branch pairing also gives AME(15,2p) states with a cyclic shift of the parties compensated by local unitaries, for every prime pequiv1pmod{15}.
