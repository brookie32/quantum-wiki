arXiv:2603.29754v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Understanding how coherent driving and quantum dissipation cooperatively control nonequilibrium energy transfer is challenging in driven open quantum systems. Here, we develop a driven quantum master equation (dQME) that uses the eigenstates of the full driven Hamiltonian in the rotating frame and retains the driving phase in the system-reservoir interactions. This approach includes driving effects in both coherent evolution and dissipative exchange processes. We apply this approach to a coupled-qubit charger-battery system and dissipative energy transfer through one and two driven qubits. For a driven coupled-qubit battery, weak driving produces three peaks in stored energy near the central and two side resonances, whereas strong driving merges these peaks into a broad central peak. Ergotropy and charging efficiency are suppressed at the weak-driving resonances as battery coherence decreases. The central suppression of charging efficiency persists under strong driving. For dissipative energy transfer, the driven single-qubit model reveals three energy exchange channels set by the driving frequency and the dressed-state energy gap. Interqubit coupling produces additional current resonances, while strong driving broadens the frequency range of enhanced energy currents. We hope that the dQME may provide a common framework for understanding coherent energy charging and dissipative energy transfer.
