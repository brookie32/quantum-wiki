DRDO has partnered with Zero mK India to indigenously develop a 20 mK dilution refrigerator, crucial for quantum computing and defense. This marks the first high-value project under the expanded ₹500-crore TDF scheme, aiming to reduce import dependency and strengthen India's National Quantum Mission. The project will establish a domestic supply chain for sub-kelvin cryogenic hardware.
