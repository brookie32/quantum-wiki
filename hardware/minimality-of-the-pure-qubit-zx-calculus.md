arXiv:2608.14872v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The ZX calculus is a graphical language for reasoning about quantum processes. In this paper, we develop a minimal pure-qubit ZX calculus based on the work of Vilmart [arXiv:1812.09114], Backens, Perdrix, and Wang [arXiv:1709.08903], and Stoltz [arXiv:2606.12383]. This resolves a problem that has remained open for nearly a decade, since completeness was first proved. Specifically, we show that (I_r) is derivable and establish the necessity of (B) and (I_g), yielding two complete and minimal rulesets.
