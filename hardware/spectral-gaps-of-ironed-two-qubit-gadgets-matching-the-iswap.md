arXiv:2607.28521v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We prove that every ironed two-qubit gadget whose KAK-derived parameter satisfies a=5/9 has, on the complete graph K_n with ngeqslant 5, the same second-moment spectral gap as the iSWAP gadget. The central step is a representation-theoretic localisation theorem: the largest strictly negative eigenvalue of the associated mathfrak S_n-invariant operator always occurs in the highest-spin SU(2) summand. A local positive-semidefinite decomposition separates every spin sector except the two highest. This settles a conjecture of Kong, Li, and Liu.
