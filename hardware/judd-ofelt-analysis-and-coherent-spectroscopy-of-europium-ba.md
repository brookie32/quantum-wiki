arXiv:2610.13664v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Rare-earth-ion based molecular crystals are emerging as a promising platform for quantum information applications, combining long optical coherence times with the chemical tunability of coordination chemistry. Here we present a comprehensive optical characterisation of twelve Eu^{3+}-based molecular complexes, with a focus on the coherent ^{5}ext{D}_{0} longrightarrow ^{7}ext{F}_{0} transition at sim580 nm. Judd-Ofelt analyses are performed across all complexes, providing insights into ligand-field environments, local point-group symmetries, and the resulting branching ratios of the coherent transition. We find that lower point-group symmetry (C_1, C_2) yields higher branching ratios of the ^{5}ext{D}_{0} longrightarrow ^{7}ext{F}_{0} transition, exceeding 1% in several complexes. Optical inhomogeneous linewidths measured at 4.2 K reveal narrow linewidths for some complexes of below ten GHz, indicative of a highly ordered crystalline environment. Optical coherence measurements of one of the complexes, 5-Eu, yields an optical coherence time of T_{ext{2}}^{ext{opt}} = 2.3~mus at 4.2 K, comparable to the best-performing molecular systems reported to date. Systematic characterisation and quantitative comparison will be key to find structure-property relations guiding rational design of optimised complexes for quantum information applications.
