arXiv:2611.27660v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We look for pure state entanglement monotones that may serve as certificates of a strong form of genuine multipartite entanglement, that we refer to as irreducible entanglement: a D-partite state (with Dgeq 3) is irreducibly D-partite entangled if it cannot be decomposed as a product of states that are at most (D-1)-partite entangled. We start by establishing a no-go result proving that no reasonable genuine multipartite entanglement entropy can simultaneously obey LOCC-monotonicity and subadditivity. After relaxing the first condition to LO-monotonicity, we provide a simple method generating genuine multipartite entanglement monotones with the desired properties. Focusing on a subfamily of these monotones, we show that they are able to detect any form of genuine tripartite entanglement in three-qubit systems.
