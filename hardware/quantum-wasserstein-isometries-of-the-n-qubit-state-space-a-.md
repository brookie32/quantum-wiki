arXiv:2602.08628v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We determine the isometry group of the n-qubit state space with respect to the quantum Wasserstein distance induced by the so-called symmetric transport cost for all n in N. It turns out that the isometries are precisely the Wigner symmetries, that is, the unitary or anti-unitary conjugations.
