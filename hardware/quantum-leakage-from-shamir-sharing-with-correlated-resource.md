arXiv:2610.06350v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We bound local quantum leakage from Shamir sharing uniformly over prime fields and permitted share-dependent channels. The adversary receives all outputs and a reference to a resource independent of the secret and sharing randomness. For independent device-reference pairs at fixed output dimension, we determine the exact exponent of the worst-case trace distance for two chosen secrets at every fixed reconstruction rate above one half, including its dependence on entanglement within each pair. For unentangled one-qubit leakage, this improves the sufficient rate of about 0.7334 proved by Batra et al. Above one half, we also determine the exact exponent when an arbitrarily entangled support is tensored with single-device states elsewhere. Every fixed support fraction below the reconstruction rate gives exponential security, while a reconstruction set admits a known attack. Exponential security extends to fixed finite-memory processes with primitive transfer channels, fixed stationary chains satisfying upper operator mixing, and canonical primitive matrix product states, allowing arbitrary finite adversary extensions of the device marginal. For one-qubit outputs, fully separable resources and independent pairs whose joint output-reference states are positive under partial transpose for every share admit rates slightly below one half.
