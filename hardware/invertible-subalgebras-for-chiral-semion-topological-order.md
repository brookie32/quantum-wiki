arXiv:2610.18953v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Chiral topological order is incompatible with strictly two-dimensional local commuting-projector Hamiltonians on finite-dimensional tensor-product Hilbert spaces. Invertible subalgebras (ISAs) provide an alternative framework. We construct an ISA supporting chiral semions, together with its anti-semion commutant, on a square lattice with one qubit per edge. We give explicit non-Pauli generators for both algebras and prove that together they generate all local operators on the Hilbert space. Starting from the Z_4 toric code, we condense the boson e^2m^2 and identify the semion and anti-semion algebras. We then decompose each Z_4 qudit into two qubits and use a finite-depth circuit to disentangle one qubit per edge fixed by the condensation constraints. Pumping the semion ISA along the z direction defines an explicit three-dimensional non-Clifford quantum cellular automaton (QCA). We show that pumping eight times is equivalent to a translation followed by a finite-depth quantum circuit with ancillary qubits. We also give an explicit finite-depth circuit relating this construction to the semion QCA previously obtained from topological quantum field theory. A similar condensation construction yields a U(1)_4 ISA on a square lattice. Together with the semion and earlier odd-prime ISAs, finite stacking realizes every pointed Witt class of bosonic Abelian anyon theories. Finally, we extend both constructions to all spatial dimensions d=4k{-}2, with one qubit or Z_4 qudit on each (2k{-}1)-cell for the semion or U(1)_4 ISA, respectively.
