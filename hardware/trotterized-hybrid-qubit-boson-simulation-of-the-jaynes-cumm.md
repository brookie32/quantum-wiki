arXiv:2611.32895v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: The Jaynes-Cummings-Hubbard (JCH) model describes the interaction of light and matter in coupled optical cavities and provides a framework for studying their quantum dynamics. In this work, we design a Trotterized quantum circuit to investigate the real-time dynamics of the JCH model using Bosonic Qiskit and a Matrix Product State (MPS) backend. We simulate single-polariton transport and benchmark site occupations against exact diagonalization results. We then extend the simulations to the two-excitation sector under a hard-core photon constraint, examining excitation transport and symmetry properties. Finally, we study the dependence of bipartite entanglement entropy on the inter-cavity photon-hopping strength. The results illustrate how hybrid qubit-boson circuits, simulated with matrix product states, can be used to study light-matter lattice dynamics. This work provides a numerical benchmark for extending hybrid simulation approaches to more complex interacting and open light-matter systems.
