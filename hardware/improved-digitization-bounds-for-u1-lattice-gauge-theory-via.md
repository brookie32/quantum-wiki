arXiv:2611.26720v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Simulating lattice gauge theories on a digital quantum computer or using tensor network methods requires a finite-dimensional approximation of the gauge field, a process often called digitization. Controlling the resulting error is essential for assessing the accuracy and resource requirements of these simulations. In this work, we derive a digitization error bound for low-lying eigenstates of the Kogut--Susskind Hamiltonian for pure U(1) gauge theory and translate it into a bound on time-evolution error. Compared with existing bounds, our result achieves exponentially improved dependence on the inverse lattice spacing 1/a and evolution time T, together with improved dependence on the inverse coupling 1/g. The key idea is to identify a small parameter in the Kogut--Susskind Hamiltonian and apply tools from semiclassical analysis. Specifically, we adapt Agmon's method to establish exponential decay of the error with the truncation threshold. These results sharpen estimates of the local Hilbert space dimension and qubit resources needed to simulate gauge theories near the continuum limit, at weak coupling, and over long times.
