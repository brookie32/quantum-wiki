arXiv:2611.24473v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Entangled states that are positive under partial transposition (PPT) are the main obstruction to a simple description of entanglement. We study three basic questions about them within a class of multiqubit states, the mixtures, with real but possibly negative weights, of products of equatorial spin coherent states of several groups of qubits: which PPT entangled states belong to the class, how robust their entanglement is to white noise, and how small their rank can be. In this class each question becomes a question about a truncated trigonometric moment problem. Every such state is PPT across every bipartition, and it is fully separable exactly when an associated multi-level Toeplitz matrix is the moment matrix of a positive measure on the torus. We answer the three questions exactly in the following cases. For three qubits, every rank-four PPT entangled state with nonzero Lorentz invariant belongs to the class after invertible local operators, which proves a working hypothesis of Garberg, Irgens and Myrheim and yields a complete set of invariants for this seven-dimensional family; among the states with vanishing invariant, exactly a one-parameter family belongs to it. For every three-qubit state built from an unextendible product basis, the white-noise threshold for full separability is lambda^*=1-2/(1+sqrt{1+s}) with a local-unitary invariant sin(0,1], and lambda^*=3-2sqrt2 for the Shifts basis; a witness in closed form detects every entangled state of this family with eight local Pauli settings. For two groups of qubits we construct extremal states of minimal rank, and for three groups we determine the minimal rank of entanglement within the class in several configurations.
