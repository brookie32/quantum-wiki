arXiv:2610.28453v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Trapped-ion, neutral-atom, and circuit QED quantum computers naturally enable coupled qubit-boson simulations. Boson-fermion interactions arise in quantum field theory, where renormalization is required to obtain finite results. We study one of the simplest examples of a coupled fermion-boson system, the static Yukawa model, on the QSCOUT trapped-ion system. We use similarity renormalization to express the same physics in terms of effective particles described by different values of the renormalization parameter s. This enables us to map data taken in the window of bosonic occupancy that is experimentally accessible to larger occupancies of distinct effective particles. We use this to study strongly coupled, high-occupancy regimes despite limited readout ranges. Using a trapped-ion qubit-boson device on Sandia National Laboratories' QSCOUT platform, we demonstrate the time evolution of the static Yukawa model.
