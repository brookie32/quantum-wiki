arXiv:2512.16192v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: For block-diagonal quantum states with prescribed block weights and dimensions, we determine the minimum von Neumann entropy excess at each trace-norm distance from the blockwise-pure states. The one-block extremal is the classical reverse-Fano profile; the new problem is its constrained assembly when the block weights and dimensions cannot be redistributed. The resulting value function Phi_{q,n}(D) is attained on the full admissible interval and is the lower envelope of finitely many rank-lattice edges. Every minimizing distance allocation has at most one block between consecutive integer ranks, and the active edge can switch nonmonotonically with D. We also determine the lower convex envelope of Phi_{q,n} explicitly: it is the polygonal curve through synchronized rank vertices and admits a one-parameter dual representation. The exact nonconvex envelope differs from this convex surrogate by at most q_iamondlog(5/4), with a sharper stage-dependent bound, and the constant is optimal. For binomial charge-sector weights the uniform gap is O(L^{-1/2}). The framework yields a symmetry-sector purity certificate; in a three-qubit phase-damping example the fixed-weight certificate remains nontrivial where the universal classical entropy-error envelope is already vacuous.
