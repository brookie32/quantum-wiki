arXiv:2610.30618v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Set coherence and set imaginarity are two important quantum resources defined by sets of states. We investigate tuples of qubit states under vanishing higher-order Bargmann invariants and show that their set coherence and set imaginarity are each related to whether they contain a pair of orthogonal states. Specifically, when the relevant higher-order Bargmann invariants vanish, the tuple contains a pair of orthogonal states if and only if removing some state makes the remaining tuple incoherent. Moreover, when maximally mixed states are excluded, the same vanishing conditions imply that the tuple is imaginarity-free if and only if it contains a pair of orthogonal states. As a consequence, under these conditions, a tuple of single-qubit states has imaginarity precisely when every tuple obtained by deleting one of its states is coherent.
