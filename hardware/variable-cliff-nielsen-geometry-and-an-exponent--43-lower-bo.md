arXiv:2609.03323v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Let D=2^n and M=3n+9inom n2. We study the right-invariant one-step-cliff metric d_Q on operatorname{PU}(D), with unit penalty on Pauli weights one and two and penalty Q on all higher weights. If M/Q_Do0 and MQ_D^{3/4}/D^2o0, then, for every fixed 00 yields a Haar-typical lower bound of order D^{4/3}M^{-2/3} for the corresponding infinite-cliff Carnot--Caratheodory distance, outside an e^{-Omega(D^2)} exceptional set. The infinite-cliff diameter therefore has exponential lower rate at least 4/3, disproving Brown's exponent-one conjecture. The same estimate gives a fixed-error no-ancilla two-qubit circuit lower bound of the same order.
