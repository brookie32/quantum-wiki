arXiv:2611.22068v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We propose an analog-memory-based arbitrary waveform generator for cryogenic qubit control that separates low-speed waveform programming from high-speed waveform generation. Waveform samples are stored as capacitor voltages and sequentially accessed through buffered switching networks, enabling repeated, non-destructive readout without high-speed digital-to-analog conversion. A cryogenic prototype demonstrated Gaussian-shaped waveform generation at 14 K with a sampling rate of 40 Msps using a programming rate of 50 ksps. No measurable voltage decay was observed over 2 s at cryogenic temperatures. A complementary room-temperature prototype achieved 160 Msps. The architecture offers a potential route toward lower-power cryogenic waveform generation.
