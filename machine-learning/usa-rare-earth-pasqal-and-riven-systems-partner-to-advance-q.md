USA Rare Earth, Pasqal, and Riven Systems are collaborating to develop quantum machine learning (QML) models for separating rare earth elements. This partnership combines Pasqal's quantum computing, Riven's autonomous chemistry lab, and USA Rare Earth's resources to optimize chemical separation, aiming to reduce supply chain dependencies and improve efficiency in processing critical minerals.
