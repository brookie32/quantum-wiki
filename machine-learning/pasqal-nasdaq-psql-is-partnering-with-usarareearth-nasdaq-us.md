🤝Pasqal (Nasdaq: PSQL) is partnering with @USARareEarth (Nasdaq: USAR) and @rivensystems to apply quantum machine learning to rare earth separation. How the collaboration works: Riven's self-driving lab runs thousands of automated experiments and generates chemical training data. Pasqal's Neutral Atom QPU benchmarks quantum machine learning models against classical ones, each trained on that data, to support the search for new separation molecules. The work will target real feedstocks, including the Round Top deposit in Texas. Full announcement: https://www.pasqal.com/newsroom/usa-rare-earth-pasqal-and-riven-systems-partner-to-advance-next-generation-technologies-for-critical-mineral-production/
