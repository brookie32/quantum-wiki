Zapata Quantum has announced an open‑challenge program designed to audit and verify quantum research produced by artificial intelligence systems. The initiative invites academic and industrial participants to test AI‑generated protocols, algorithms, and data for correctness and robustness, aiming to establish reliable benchmarks in the emerging field of AI‑assisted quantum science. By fostering transparent verification procedures, Zapata seeks to accelerate trustworthy adoption of AI tools within quantum research workflows.
