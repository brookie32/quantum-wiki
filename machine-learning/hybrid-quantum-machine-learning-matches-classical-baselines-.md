Hybrid quantum machine learning (QML) systems have recently achieved performance that matches leading classical machine‑learning baselines on benchmark tasks. Despite this progress, studies indicate that a clear, general advantage of hybrid QML over traditional methods remains elusive, as gains are still highly problem‑dependent and not yet robust across diverse applications. Consequently, ongoing research focuses on identifying specific domains where quantum advantages may emerge.
