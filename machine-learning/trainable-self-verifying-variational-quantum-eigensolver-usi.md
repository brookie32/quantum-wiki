**Trainable, Self‑Verifying Variational Quantum Eigensolver Using Adiabatic Warm Starts (APS Journals)**  
This paper introduces a hybrid quantum–classical algorithm for estimating ground‐state energies that begins from adiabatically prepared warm starts rather than random initial states. The method trains circuit parameters and includes an in‑silico self‑verification step, using variational inequalities to detect incorrect solutions without requiring auxiliary hardware. Demonstrations on small molecular Hamiltonians show reduced circuit depth and improved convergence relative to conventional VQE approaches.
