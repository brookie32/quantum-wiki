Zapata and the University of Maryland have announced an AI‑driven quantum‑design challenge, inviting researchers to develop quantum algorithms that come with formal, machine‑checked proofs of correctness. The competition aims to spur advances in reliable quantum software by providing a platform for rigorous verification. Participants can submit solutions via the challenge’s dedicated web portal and receive feedback based on automated proof-checking tools.
