A recent systematic review evaluated studies that apply quantum machine learning (QML) to medical image analysis, finding that the technology is not yet ready for clinical use. The review highlighted gaps in large‑scale benchmarking, data availability, and algorithmic robustness, indicating that further research and development are required before QML can be safely integrated into healthcare workflows.
