arXiv:2611.22389v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Recent advances in artificial intelligence and quantum technologies have driven the emergence of quantum machine learning, yet imperfections in near-term quantum devices remain a major obstacle for achieving practical quantum advantage. In the absence of fault-tolerant machines, ensemble learning, which combines multiple imperfect quantum devices, may unlock new quantum computing applications. Here, we introduce two ensemble-learning algorithms, namely Bagging and AdaBoost, for enhancing the accuracy of nonlinear regression problems. While Bagging trains variational quantum regressors independently, AdaBoost employs a sequential training strategy and achieves significantly better prediction accuracy. By incorporating systematic feature selection into our ensemble learning algorithms, we enhance their predictive power for highly nonlinear functions. We further improve prediction accuracy through a feature-to-qubit mapping strategy based on repeated feature encoding. The performance of our ensemble-based algorithms improves with both the number of regressors in the ensemble and the size of each regressor. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach using four benchmarking functions with varying degrees of nonlinearity by relying on a feature pool limited to first- and second-order polynomial terms.
