Zapata Solutions announced a new partnership with QuEra Technologies to jointly develop practical quantum computing use cases. The collaboration combines Zapata’s software expertise with QuEra's quantum hardware platform, aiming to create scalable solutions for industries such as finance, logistics, and materials science. Both companies plan to accelerate the delivery of cloud‑based quantum services that can be integrated into existing enterprise workflows.
