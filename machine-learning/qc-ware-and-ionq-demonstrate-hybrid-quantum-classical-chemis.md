QC Ware and IonQ demonstrated a practical hybrid quantum‑classical chemistry workflow by integrating IonQ’s trapped‑ion quantum processors with QC Ware’s software platform. The combined system ran quantum calculations for molecular Hamiltonians while classical post‑processing handled error mitigation and result interpretation, showcasing accelerated simulation of complex chemical systems. This joint demonstration highlights the feasibility of using commercial quantum hardware to augment traditional computational chemistry workflows.
