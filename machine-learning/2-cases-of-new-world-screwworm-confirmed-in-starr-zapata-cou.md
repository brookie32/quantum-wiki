Two instances of the New World screw‑worm (Cochliomyia hominivorax) have been confirmed in Starr and Zapata counties. The sightings represent a reappearance of this invasive fly species in Texas after having been absent for many years, raising concerns over potential livestock infestations. Environmental agencies are monitoring the situation to assess risks and implement control measures if necessary.
