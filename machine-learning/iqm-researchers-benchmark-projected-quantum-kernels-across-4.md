IQM Researchers have successfully benchmarked projected quantum kernels across 45 qubits using a cross-device referee-challenger protocol, demonstrating a significant advantage over classical models in quantum machine learning tasks. Their study, conducted on IQM Emerald and Garnet processors, highlights the effectiveness of adaptive Pauli twirling in mitigating errors and achieving near noise-free accuracy. The findings suggest a promising path for quantum advantage in 2D quantum dynamics, moving beyond classically simulable systems.
