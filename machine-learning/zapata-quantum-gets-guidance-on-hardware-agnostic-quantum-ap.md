Zapata Quantum has announced it will receive strategic guidance on building hardware‑agnostic quantum applications.  The effort aims to develop frameworks and tools that allow quantum software to run seamlessly across multiple processor types, reducing vendor lock‑in for developers.  This initiative is expected to accelerate the adoption of quantum solutions by simplifying software portability between different quantum hardware platforms.
