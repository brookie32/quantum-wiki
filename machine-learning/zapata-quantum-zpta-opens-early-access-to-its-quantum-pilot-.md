Zapata Quantum (Ticker ZPTA) has announced early access to its new Quantum Pilot cloud platform, enabling customers to experiment with and run quantum workloads in a dedicated environment before full launch. The pilot offers a controlled preview of the company’s quantum-as-a-service offerings, aimed at accelerating adoption among developers and enterprises. Zapata expects the program to help validate integration scenarios and gather feedback ahead of broader deployment.
