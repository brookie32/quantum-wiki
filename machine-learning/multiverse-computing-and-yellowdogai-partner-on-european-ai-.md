The Canadian HPC startup **Multiverse Computing** has partnered with the AI‑compute firm **YellowDog.ai** to deliver accelerated artificial‑intelligence workloads on European supercomputing infrastructure. The collaboration will leverage YellowDog.ai’s hyper‑accelerated GPU‑based platform to accelerate deep learning, scientific computing, and high‑performance data analytics across Multiverse’s cloud‑edge services in Europe. This partnership aims to speed up AI research and industrial deployment within the region.
