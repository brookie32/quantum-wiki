VideoAmp announced the appointment of Andrea Zapata as its Senior Vice President of Agency Partnerships. In this role, Zapata will oversee the company’s relationships with media and marketing agencies, leveraging her experience to expand VideoAmp’s partnership network. The hire signals VideoAmp’s continued focus on strengthening its agency‑partner ecosystem.
