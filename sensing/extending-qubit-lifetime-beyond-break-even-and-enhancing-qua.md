Researchers demonstrated that a superconducting qubit’s coherence time can be extended beyond the “break‑even” point—where error rates equal the natural decay of the system—by implementing an autonomous quantum error‑correction (AQEC) protocol. The AQEC scheme continuously monitors and corrects bit‑flip and phase‑flip errors without external intervention, thereby preserving qubit state for significantly longer periods. Leveraging these extended lifetimes, the team also showed improved sensitivity in quantum sensor applications, illustrating a practical route toward resilient quantum technologies.
