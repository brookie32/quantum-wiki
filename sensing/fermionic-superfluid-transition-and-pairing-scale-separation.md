arXiv:2610.34342v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Flat-band systems provide a promising platform for exploring exotic correlated phases owing to quenched kinetic energy and enhanced interaction effects. In this work, we investigate the flat-band superfluid transition in the attractive kagome Hubbard model using large-scale determinant quantum Monte Carlo simulations accelerated by fast Fourier transforms and delay updates. Through systematic calculations of the transition temperature, spin susceptibility, and density compressibility, we establish the finite-temperature phase diagram at the flat-band filling rho=5/3 and find a clear linear scaling of the superfluid transition temperature with the interaction strength, T_{c}=0.0314|U|. Our results reveal a wide separation of temperature scales between local pair formation and global phase coherence, which stabilizes a broad intermediate supercompressible bosonic fluid region of preformed pairs that bridges the normal Fermi gas and the flat-band superfluid. We further demonstrate that both the transition temperature and density compressibility are significantly enhanced in the flat band compared to van Hove fillings. Our findings highlight the essential role of flat-band-enhanced pair-density fluctuations in separating the pairing and phase-coherence scales, offering clear experimental signatures for probing flat-band superfluidity and superconductivity in cold-atom and kagome-material platforms.
