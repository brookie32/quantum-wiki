arXiv:2611.17948v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: How precisely can an observer estimate a parameter related to a quantum field? This question is answered for quantum-mechanical systems using the SLD--Bures--Fisher metric and the quantum Cramer--Rao bound. However, local algebras in relativistic quantum field theory are of type III: they carry no trace and normal states have no density matrices, so the SLD quantum Fisher information and the quantum Cramer--Rao bound cannot be defined in the usual way. We define the SLD (Bures) quantum Fisher information on arbitrary von Neumann algebras through Uhlmann's transition probability and prove a quantum Cramer--Rao bound for collective measurements. We also show that the bound is sharp, and equals the largest classical Fisher information obtainable by measurements in the region.
