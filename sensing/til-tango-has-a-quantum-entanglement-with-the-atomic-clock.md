Quantum entanglement is used to enhance atomic clock precision by creating quantum correlations between groups of atoms, which allows clocks to overcome standard quantum measurement limitations.   Researchers use entanglement to make atoms behave collectively like a team, reducing signal noise and achieving better precision without requiring more atoms.
