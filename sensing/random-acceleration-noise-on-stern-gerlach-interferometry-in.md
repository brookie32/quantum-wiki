arXiv:2602.21288v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We analyze decoherence in a one-loop Stern--Gerlach--type matter-wave interferometer for a massive nanoparticle embedded with a nitrogen vacancy (NV)-centered nanodiamond evolving under an effective harmonic-oscillator dynamics in a magnetic-field gradient. We assume that the Stern-Gerlach interferometer is subjected to an acceleration ec{a} external to the system, which is at an angle heta_0 with respect to the direction of the superposition. For a one loop interferometer, we quantify dephasing from two noise channels: fluctuations in the external acceleration elta a(t) and fluctuations in the tilt angle elta heta(t). At the level of the action, we treat these two external noise as stochastic inputs, compute the resulting stochastic phase difference between the interferometer arms, and obtain the dephasing rate Gamma. We obtain the transfer function of the interferometer for each of the noise sources. We also show explicit results of constraints on the power spectral density of the noise sources for an interferometer that produces a superposition size of Delta xsim 1nm of a nanodiamond of mass m=10^{-15}~kg by considering white noise statistics and imposing a coherence target Gamma auleq 1, where ausimeq 0.015~s. We find sqrt{S_{aa}}lesssim O(10^{-11})~m,s^{-2,Hz^{-1/2}} if we take the external acceleration, a=0~{rm ms^{-2}} and heta_0=0^irc (along the direction of the superposition), and sqrt{S_{hetaheta}}lesssim O(10^{-10})~rad,Hz^{-1/2} for a=g= 9.81~m,s^{-2} and heta_0=90^irc (superposition direction is perpendicular to the Earth's gravity). We have also found an operating regime where the acceleration noise can be minimized by either varying heta_0 or a for a fixed set of other experimental parameters.
