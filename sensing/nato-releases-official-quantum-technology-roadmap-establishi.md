NATO has released its Quantum Technology Roadmap, effective July 2026, to implement its 2023 Quantum Technology Strategy. The roadmap outlines Lines of Effort in quantum computing, sensing, and communications, with target milestones to accelerate technology adoption and maintain defense interoperability among its 32 member nations. Key initiatives include quantum sensing for PNT in GNSS-denied environments and the Quantum Zero Beacon Project for secure communications.
