arXiv:2610.22574v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Here we report atomic structure calculations and experimental observations of the two-photon 6s^2;^1S_0 rightarrow 6s23s;^1S_0 transition in Yb. The unique level structure of Yb places the 6s6p;^1P_1 state's energy at approx!49.7% of the ionization energy, which facilitates two-photon Doppler-free excitation of the 6s23s;^1S_0 Rydberg state from the 6s^2;^1S_0 ground state at a relatively low optical intensity. This J= 0 rightarrow J=0 transition is immune to first-order Zeeman and electric quadrupole shifts, has reduced sensitivity to higher-order Zeeman shifts, and is further predicted to have relatively low sensitivity to density shifts. Using configuration interaction theory, we compute relevant properties of the 6s23s;^1S_0 Rydberg state for sensing, including the C_6 coefficient, dc polarizability, blackbody radiation (BBR) shift, and BBR depopulation rate. As a first experimental step, we demonstrate two-photon Doppler-free spectroscopy of the 6s^2;^1S_0 rightarrow 6s23s;^1S_0 transition. From our experimental spectra, we estimate the natural linewidth (lifetime) of the 6s23s;^1S_0 state to be 90 kHz (1.8 mus). We believe this transition, or other 6s^2;^1S_0 rightarrow 6sns;^1S_0 transitions in Yb with napprox23, may find use in a variety of sensing applications, potentially including a two-photon clock, electrometer, or BBR thermometer.
