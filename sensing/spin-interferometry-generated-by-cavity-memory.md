arXiv:2611.18215v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We report the control and observation of coherent spin interferometry generated by cavity memory using an ensemble of nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers coupled to a microwave cavity. The abrupt switch-on and switch-off of a detuned drive produce transient fields at the cavity frequency that supply the two spin rotations for Ramsey interferometry, observed using both microwave and fluorescence readout. Spectrally scanning the spin profile across the cavity and drive frequencies distinguishes cavity-generated Ramsey interference from conventional Rabi dynamics. Temporal weighting of the reflected microwave signal enables spectrally selective population readout at a single probe frequency. By programming the phase and amplitude of the drive envelope, we extend the same mechanism to more quantum control sequences, including spin-echo and Rabi control.
