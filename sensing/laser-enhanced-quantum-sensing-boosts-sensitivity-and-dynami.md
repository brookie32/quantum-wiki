A laser‑threshold magnetometer incorporating nitrogen‑vacancy (NV) centers in diamond achieves 100 % optical contrast and outputs up to 50 mW by integrating the NV ensemble into an optical cavity, where magnetic fields shift the lasing threshold. The device reaches a photon‑shot‑noise sensitivity better than 400 fT/√Hz for all vector components while preserving a wide dynamic range, improving super‑linearly with contrast rather than sacrificing linewidth. This technique enables highly sensitive magnetometry outside shielded environments and is suited to applications such as magnetoencephalography, navigation, and magnetic anomaly detection.
