arXiv:2512.02976v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Multipartite quantum states saturating the Heisenberg limit of sensitivity typically require full-body interactions to be prepared. On the other hand, experimentally practical Hamiltonians often involve few-body correlators only. Here, we study the metrological performances under this constraint, using tools derived from the quantum Fisher information. Our work applies to any encoding generator, also including a dependence on the parameter. For moderate system sizes up to hundred spins (even), we find that symmetric ground states of Hamiltonians with significantly few-body interactions exhibit an average quantum Fisher information that agrees with that of Haar-random symmetric states and displays Heisenberg-like scaling sensitivity. We also discuss the interaction requirements to generate metrologically useful Haar-random symmetric eigenstates. Finally, we establish a tradeoff between the Hamiltonian's gap, which quantifies preparation hardness, and the quantum Fisher information of the corresponding ground state.
