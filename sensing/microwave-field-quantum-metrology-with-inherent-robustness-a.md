The study demonstrates that an ensemble of Rydberg atoms can be used for microwave‑field quantum metrology with a single‐shot precision of (217 ± 8) µV cm⁻¹ for a 160‑ns pulse, exploiting their large dipole moments. By harnessing inter‑atomic dipolar interactions, the authors implement an error‑prevention protocol that protects sensitivity against conventional detection losses, achieving inherent robustness that combines sensing with quantum information processing. This approach enables accurate field measurement in regimes where detection efficiency typically limits quantum advantage.
