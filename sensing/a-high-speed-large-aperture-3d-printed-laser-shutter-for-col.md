arXiv:2610.27709v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Cold atom experiments require optical shutters with rapid and repeatable actuation. Existing solutions often involve high cost, limited aperture diameter and slow transitioning. We present a 3D-printable shutter design featuring a large aperture of 4.5 mm diameter, low-cost assembly, and robust performance. The device achieves switching times of 399pm8~mus with delays of 2.7pm0.2 ms for a fully illuminated aperture, with four different shutters demonstrating repeatable performance. We measure the shutter speed to be 7.0pm0.1 m/s. This approach offers an accessible, scalable solution for cold atom and other optical experiments requiring multiple shutters with precise light control and avoids the need to resize or precision-align a beam through an aperture.
