Physicists at OIST have demonstrated that the collective spin of electrons in nitrogen‑vacancy (NV) centres within a macroscopic diamond can exert enough force to move the crystal toward an external magnet when illuminated by a green laser, causing spin state transitions and red photoluminescence. The experiment shows that a purely quantum mechanical effect—spin reorientation—can produce a measurable classical displacement of a heavy object, opening prospects for ultra‑precise spin‑based sensors capable of probing gravitational phenomena and testing the limits of quantum mechanics.
