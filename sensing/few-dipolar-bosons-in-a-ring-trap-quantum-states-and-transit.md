arXiv:2611.22562v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The degenerate dipolar Bose gas has attracted great research interest for studying novel quantum states of matter due to the interplay between short-range contact and long-range dipole-dipole interactions. The superfluid, quantum droplet, and supersolid states, along with the transition between them, have been widely studied using the extended mean-field theory, which relies on the Lee-Huang-Yang correction to approximately account for the effect of quantum fluctuation. In this work, we examine the system of a few dipolar bosons trapped in a ring potential employing the exact diagonalization method, which can yield the full quantum nature of the system. By examining the quantum correlation and coherence properties of the system, we reveal a rich variety of quantum states (including Wigner crystal, superfluid, quantum droplet, and the mixed states of them) and their formation mechanisms. Furthermore, we show that transitions between these quantum states can be induced by tuning the interaction strength and particle number. The findings of this work can provide valuable insights into the dipolar gas at the microscopic quantum level, and may also find applications in the fields such as quantum sensing and quantum information.
