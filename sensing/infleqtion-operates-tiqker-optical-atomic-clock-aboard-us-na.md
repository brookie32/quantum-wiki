Infleqtion successfully demonstrated its Tiqker optical atomic clock aboard a U.S. Navy vessel during the Maritime Big Play Pacific military exercise in October 2026. The device provided stable positioning, navigation, and timing capabilities for two weeks in a marine environment, proving its resilience against GPS-degraded or jammed conditions. This marks a significant step towards quantum-based PNT for naval operations, despite integration challenges for broader fleet adoption.
