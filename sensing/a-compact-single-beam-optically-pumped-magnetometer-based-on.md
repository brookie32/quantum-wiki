arXiv:2610.32795v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We demonstrate a compact single-beam optically pumped magnetometer (OPM) employing a pure 87Rb microfabricated vapor cell with 500 Torr buffer gas. The residual magnetic field is actively minimized to below 400 pT through a combination of mu-metal magnetic shielding and internal compensation coils. We systematically characterize the OPM response as a function of laser detuning and optical intensity, and derive the magnetic-field sensitivity from the signal slope and noise amplitude spectral density. A best sensitivity of 11 fT/Hz^1/2 is achieved near the resonance transition at an optimal optical intensity, using a small cell volume of only 0.027 cm^3. Furthermore, a miniaturized OPM prototype was developed using a folded flexible printed circuit board (FPCB) to integrate the vapor cell, a compact heater, and magnetic-field coils into a cubic sensing head. Two coil configurations patterned directly on the FPCB were investigated. Under optimized operating conditions, the OPM prototype achieved a sensitivity of 66 fT/Hz^1/2. These results demonstrate that high-buffer-pressure single-beam OPMs can achieve excellent sensitivity, establishing a pathway toward compact and scalable arrays.
