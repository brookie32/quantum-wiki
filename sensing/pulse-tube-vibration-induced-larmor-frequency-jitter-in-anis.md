arXiv:2611.27267v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We investigate the role of pulse-tube mechanical vibrations towards spin frequency noise in a cryogen-free dilution refrigerator. Our system comprises of a low mode-volume superconducting resonator coupled to 50 ppm ext{Er}^{3+}:ext{CaWO}_4 spins with highly anisotropic g-factors. By measuring single-shot spin echoes using narrow-bandwidth three-pulse sequences, we directly observe a vibration induced stochastic signal resulting from the spin's Larmor frequency moving into and out of the detection bandwidth. The sensitivity to these vibrations is substantially suppressed when a) the external magnetic field is aligned along the symmetry c-axis and b) spin temperature is modestly increased to 60~mK. We also benchmark the system as a quantum sensor for angular fluctuations, extracting a noise floor of 350 nanodegrees.
