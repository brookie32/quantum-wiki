arXiv:2608.22737v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We investigate the duty-cycle-dependent response of a pulse-width-modulated Bell--Bloom atomic magnetometer. A Bloch-equation model is developed to describe the pump-on and pump-off spin dynamics, leading to analytical transient solutions and a periodic steady-state description. An extended free-induction-decay fitting method is introduced to calibrate the effective relaxation rates W and W_0, which are then used to predict the modulated atomic response. The model agrees with time-domain waveform measurements, fast virtual lock-in analysis, and practical hardware lock-in measurements in the near-resonant operating regime. We show that maximizing the signal amplitude does not generally optimize the magnetometer sensitivity, because the lock-in slope also depends on the detuning-dependent phase response. The results provide a practical framework for duty-cycle and pump-rate optimization in PWM-modulated atomic magnetometers.
