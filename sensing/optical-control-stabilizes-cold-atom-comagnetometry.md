Researchers showed that customized laser fields can extend the coherence time of two co‑trapped ytterbium isotopes in a cold‑atom comagnetometer, suppressing magnetic‑field noise by more than thirty thousand times. This optical control technique markedly improves the precision and stability of comagnetometry measurements.
