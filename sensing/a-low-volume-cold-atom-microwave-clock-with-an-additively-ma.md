arXiv:2610.35588v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We present the performance of a compact microwave interrogated atomic clock based on laser cooled ^{87}rm{Rb} atoms. The clock utilizes a micro-fabricated grating magneto-optical trap (gMOT) to cool and trap atoms, and an additively manufactured microwave cavity for microwave interrogation. We demonstrate a signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) of 370 at a 1 Hz bandwidth and mid-term instability of 6.7imes10^{-14} at an averaging time of au=3 imes 10^{4} s. In addition, a low cost mu-metal foil has been implemented on the vacuum chamber to reduce the quadratic Zeeman shift which was previously limiting stability beyond au = 10 s.
