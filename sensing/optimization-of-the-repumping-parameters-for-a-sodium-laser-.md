arXiv:2608.08983v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: A sodium laser guide star operated as a mesospheric magnetometer modulates a 589 nm laser at the local Larmor frequency and usually diverts a fraction of its power to a repumping light that recovers atoms lost to the dark ground state.The polarization, read out for the most strongly driven velocity group, calls for 2.8 times the flux optimal fraction, and a shot noise figure of merit combining the two observables for 2 times, beyond the range commercial guide star lasers provide.
