arXiv:2611.17552v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: As quantum sensing techniques continue to push the frontier of detection of ultra-weak signals, one may wonder if a sophisticated quantum strategy could be used to enable measurement of spectral distortions in the cosmic microwave background. Using quantum Fisher information techniques, we prove that the optimal strategy is simple frequency-binned intensity measurement. This proof strategy also enables us to provide numerical estimates for the information-theoretic minimum observation time required to observe the mu- and y-distortions expected from LambdaCDM cosmology. As case studies, we compare these results to the PIXIE and SPECTER mission concepts, and show that they are within a few orders of magnitude of this fundamental limit.
