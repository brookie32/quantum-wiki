We’re proud to see our portfolio company @PasqalQuantum successfully list on Nasdaq under the ticker PSQL, marking a significant milestone and a new chapter in the company’s growth and commercialization of neutral-atom quantum computing. Since our initial investment in 2023, Pasqal has continued to translate Nobel Prize-winning research into practical quantum computing solutions while expanding its global footprint. Saudi Arabia has become a central part of that journey. Pasqal has established its regional headquarters in the Kingdom, continues to grow its local team, and has worked with Aramco to deploy Middle East’s first commercial quantum computer providing quantum computing as a service platform, demonstrating its long term commitment to building local capabilities and advancing the Kingdom’s quantum computing ecosystem. Pasqal’s journey reflects what we look for in deep technology: breakthrough innovation with a clear path to commercialization, localization, and real-world impact. Congratulations to Wasiq Bokhari, Pasqal’s founders and leadership, and the entire team on this landmark achievement. We look forward to what’s next for them.
