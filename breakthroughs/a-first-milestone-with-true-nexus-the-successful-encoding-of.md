A first milestone with True Nexus: the successful encoding of selected protein structures on neutral-atom quantum hardware. Working with Pasqal's neutral-atom quantum processors, we've targeted selected protein structures tied to gelation, the process that turns liquids into gels and gives food its structure and texture. It's one of the most valuable and least understood properties in the food and biotech economy, and it has long resisted computational design. The commercial value of a protein rests on how it behaves, not on its sequence. That behavior has mostly been discoverable through slow trial and error at the bench. This milestone is a first step toward making that functionality computationally accessible. The work is delivered through the @McitGovSa in support of the @SaudiVision2030, applying quantum and AI to life sciences and biotechnology. "This is the moment protein functionality stops being a mystery and starts becoming designable. Together with Pasqal, we are bringing the chemistry that governs how proteins behave onto neutral-atom quantum hardware, opening a new path to understand, predict and ultimately design protein functionality. This can change what is possible for the future protein economy," said Dominik Grabinski, CEO of True Nexus.
