In collaboration with HyperLight, Xanadu achieved a nearly 3x reduction in optical switch loss, reducing to only 7 mdB. Achieving ultra-low-loss switch performance is a major step toward utility-scale quantum computing, but could offer value beyond quantum as well. We believe our technology could contribute to achieving higher speed, less energy intensive data centers for AI and communications.
