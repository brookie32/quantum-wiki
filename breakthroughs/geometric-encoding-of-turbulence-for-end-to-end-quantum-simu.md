arXiv:2508.05346v4 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Simulating multiscale turbulence in engineering remains challenging. Any quantum advantage for such simulations requires efficient initial-state preparation. Yet exact loading of an arbitrary classical snapshot requires exponential quantum resources. We introduce "turbuloscope", a physics-informed geometric method that generates statistically representative turbulent initial states in a gate-based register from a parameterized Pauli-spinor multiscale distribution. Scale invariance renders conditional rotations approximately affine in Gray-code space, enabling linear-depth, ancilla-free preparation. The generalized Madelung transform and Hopf fibration map the encoded Pauli spinor to fluid observables and vortex tubes. The qubit count scales logarithmically with the Reynolds number, yielding an exponential reduction in preparation overhead relative to full-grid classical spectral synthesis. We classically emulate a 30-qubit preparation circuit and generate a turbulent field over one billion grid points at a scale-inferred Reynolds number of 35,000, exhibiting Kolmogorov's five-thirds energy spectrum, tangled vortex tubes, and intermittency. This method provides scalable initial states for quantum simulation of turbulence and multiscale systems.
