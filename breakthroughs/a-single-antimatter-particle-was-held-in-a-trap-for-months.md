In October 2026 researchers at Northwestern University, led by Gerald Gabrielse, successfully confined a single positron for several months using electromagnetic “invisible walls” in an ion‑trap experiment. The positrons were produced via the beta‑positive decay of radioactive sodium and then slowed and cooled with a cryogenic system and tungsten crystal before capture. By measuring the trapped positron’s behavior over time, the team aims to determine its intrinsic properties with unprecedented precision and test key predictions of the standard model of particle physics.
