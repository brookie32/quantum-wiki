Xanadu and HyperLight Corporation have achieved a significant breakthrough in photonic quantum computing, demonstrating an optical switch insertion loss of 7 millidecibels (mdB) using thin-film lithium niobate (TFLN) technology. This represents a nearly threefold reduction in loss compared to their previous milestone and is crucial for scaling fault-tolerant photonic quantum processors. This advancement offers advantages over alternative materials like barium titanate (BTO) and could also improve power efficiency in AI data centers.
