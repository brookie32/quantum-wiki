IQM Quantum Computers has successfully demonstrated full-chip entanglement across all 54 qubits of its IQM Emerald processor, a significant advancement in quantum computing. This achievement validates full-lattice coherence on commercial hardware, with measured state fidelities exceeding the genuine multipartite entanglement threshold. The company utilized an automated scheduling compiler and dynamical decoupling to achieve this milestone, highlighting both progress and the ongoing challenges in maintaining coherence in quantum systems.
