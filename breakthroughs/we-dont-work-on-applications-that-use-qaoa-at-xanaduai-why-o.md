We don't work on applications that use QAOA at @XanaduAI. Why? Optimization is hard. Most industrial problems are difficult because of scale (e.g., need to optimize over a million variables) and not because of computational complexity, which is where quantum is most helpful. Even when there are complexity barriers, like for NP-Hard problems, it's not clear that quantum can help beyond only quadratic speedups. QAOA is an elegant framework, but it struggles to scale and to achieve speedups. After 10+ years, the evidence for quantum utility remains thin. As stated in this review paper https://www.nature.com/articles/s42254-024-00770-9, “significant challenges remain in demonstrating a practical quantum advantage”. Groundbreaking and innovative quantum algorithms, built by a team of experts, will be necessary to unlock useful applications of quantum computing to optimization.
