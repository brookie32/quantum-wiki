arXiv:2611.25124v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Learning a Hamiltonian evolution time in Quantum Phase Estimation that produces phase distributions with low Shannon entropy circumvents the fundamental limit of digitization error of phase, independent of the number of phase qubits of accuracy. By introducing an optimization loop to minimize the Shannon entropy of measured eigenvalues produced by a QPE quantum circuit, minimum eigenvalue computation accuracy is greatly improved, which we show numerically on quantum magnet models. This technique works because standard QPE has a natural correspondence between entropy of the phase distribution and the analog accuracy of the minimum eigenvalue phase. This introduces for the first time a type of parameter learning to QPE, which makes near term practicality of QPE for fundamental sciences more feasible by using shorter depth QPE circuits.
