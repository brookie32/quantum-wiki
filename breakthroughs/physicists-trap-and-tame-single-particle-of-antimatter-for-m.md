Scientists have successfully confined a single antiparticle—most likely an antiproton or positron—for several months using an electromagnetic Penning trap. By maintaining the particle at millikelvin temperatures, researchers achieved unprecedented stability and precision in measuring its properties. This achievement represents a significant breakthrough for antimatter research, opening new possibilities for studying fundamental symmetries and potential future applications of long‑lived antimatter.
