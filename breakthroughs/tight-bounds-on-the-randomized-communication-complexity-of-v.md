arXiv:2610.30052v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We prove that the randomized communication complexity of Vector-in-Subspace in mathbb R^n with error at most 1/3 is Theta(sqrt n). Alice holds a unit vector u and Bob holds an n/2-dimensional subspace H; they must distinguish uin H from uin H^perp. An exact one-way quantum protocol uses O(log n) qubits, making the problem a geometric example of an exponential quantum advantage over interactive classical communication. Our lower bound matches Raz's O(sqrt n) upper bound, improves the Omega(n^{1/3}) lower bound of Klartag and Regev, and removes the total-rank restriction in Grupel's Omega(sqrt n) result. The proof establishes a Gaussian moment bound over complete rectangle partitions. A complex Gaussian representation yields a vanishing even moment whose cancellation is preserved when averaging over the partition. We control the signed conditional moments around a real center by decomposing conditional Gaussian measures into fixed coordinates and sub-Gaussian components. Combining this estimate with spherical harmonic analysis gives the communication lower bound.
