arXiv:2609.28726v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We generalize the canonical Hopfield model of associative memory, which stores classical states of individual spins, to store entangled quantum states. The construction is based on truncated projector-sum Hamiltonian, and we explicitly consider a special case that stores dimer singlet coverings of a spin-1/2 system. We analyze the stability of the stored memories and find that the capacity -- the number of stable patterns -- scales faster with the system size than one would expect from a naive analogy with the standard Hopfield model, suggesting a possible quantum advantage.
