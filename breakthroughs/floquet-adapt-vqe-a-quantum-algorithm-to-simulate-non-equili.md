arXiv:2503.11613v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Periodically driven quantum systems exhibit many fascinating phenomena absent in equilibrium systems, but their simulation is more challenging than that of static systems. Consequently, quantum simulation of these systems offers greater opportunity for achieving quantum advantage. To build the foundation for simulating time-periodic Hamiltonians, we utilize the Floquet-Hilbert space formalism to transform the Hamiltonian into a time-independent form and provide its Pauli decomposition. Within this formalism, we develop a hybrid quantum-classical algorithm, Floquet-ADAPT-VQE, that produces three key quantities that govern periodically driven quantum dynamics: the quasienergy, the associated Floquet eigenstates, and expectation values of observables with respect to these eigenstates. The latter is achieved by applying a fixed-depth quantum circuit to an initial Floquet eigenstate---an efficient construction made possible by the Floquet-Hilbert space formalism. We also identify a suitable auxiliary initial state whose squared Floquet energy is independent of the number of auxiliary qubits as well as the driving frequency, which leads to better convergence with fewer ADAPT iterations. We demonstrate our algorithm by performing numerical simulations on a periodically driven XYZ model with a magnetic field. We also explore potential applications of our algorithm for studying various non-equilibrium phenomena in periodically driven systems.
