To transition quantum technology from the research environment to commercial-scale data centers, we need industrial scale. We’re paving the way alongside GlobalFoundries (@GlobalFoundries) by moving quantum manufacturing forward on 300 mm semiconductor lines—accelerating the production of key photonic components like ultra-low loss silicon nitride (SiN) photonics and Superconducting Nanowire Single-Photon Detectors (SNSPDs). Thank you to Nasdaq (@Nasdaq) for recognizing this major milestone!
