arXiv:2609.25816v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: A boolean function f has Fourier dimension k if its nonzero Fourier coefficients span a subspace of dimension k. We consider the property testing task of determining whether a function has Fourier dimension at most k, or is epsilon-far from being so. We show that there is a Theta(k) quantum property tester for this problem. With Gopalan etal's classical lower bound of Omega(2^{k/2}) for this task, this demonstrates an exponential quantum advantage for this task. We complement this result with a ilde{O}(2^{k/2}/epsilon) classical tester, improving the best known upper bound and thus showing the prior lower bound is essentially tight.
