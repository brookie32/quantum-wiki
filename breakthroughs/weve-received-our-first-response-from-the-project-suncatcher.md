On October 1, 2026 the Project Suncatcher prototype satellite launched and completed its commissioning checklist, powering up host and TPU trays and running early AI workloads in orbit. During a ground‑station pass near Svalbard, a Gemma 4 open‑model TPU produced its first “Hello World”—a haiku about space—which confirmed successful operation of data‑center class TPUs in a low‑Earth orbit environment. The team will now test those TPUs under thermal stress and radiation exposure to validate reliability for future twin‑satellite deployment planned for 2027.
