arXiv:2610.12949v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We study quantum-assisted balanced rendezvous on the d-dimensional hypercube in a one-step two-agent quantum-walk framework, where the two agents move the same Hamming distance and meet at a common midpoint. The key structure is a projective t-sphere resolution, which governs exact finite-dimensional quantum attainment. Its scalar analogue recovers the corresponding classical characterization. We characterize finite-dimensional quantum attainment of the no-signalling benchmark, construct quantum strategies from projective sphere resolutions, and establish a rigidity theorem for optimal finite-dimensional strategies. A marked difference appears between nearest-neighbor and higher-cardinality updates: quantum advantage can occur in the former, whereas in the latter the geometry of the relevant Hamming spheres rules out exact finite-dimensional attainment of the no-signalling value. Together, these results connect quantum-walk dynamics, Hamming-sphere geometry, and quantum rendezvous within the quantum Bernoulli noises (QBN) framework.
