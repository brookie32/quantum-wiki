arXiv:2611.14173v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We establish an operational bridge between microscale thermodynamic work extraction and the foundational characterization of nonclassical correlations in bipartite quantum systems. Within the resource theory of thermal operations, we analyze a generalized framework of assisted work distillation where the trusted receiver's system is divided into separate, independent particle storage pools. We demonstrate that concurrent information-theoretic correlations measured across sets of mutually unbiased bases act as an explicit, quantifiable energy resource for local work extraction. Based on this paradigm, we derive a novel class of work-based Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen steering inequalities whose violations certify a one-sided device-independent quantum thermodynamic enhancement. Furthermore, by restricting the dimension of the untrusted party under a one-sided semi-device-independent trust framework, we introduce dynamically scaling work inequalities capable of witnessing superunsteerability. This allows us to activate and certify the hidden thermodynamic utility of weakly correlated entangled states that are otherwise completely invisible in standard, unconstrained scenarios.
