arXiv:2610.30250v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We provide a fully quantum-mechanical description of the continuous measurement of wavelike dark matter. Our focus is cavity haloscopes, although our results inform a broad range of axion searches. We extend the input-output formalism by treating the axion as a quantum field, finding how its quantum state is imprinted onto higher-order moments of observables of the outgoing transmission-line modes. We further develop a novel measurement-record formalism, which computes the joint probability distribution of all measurement outcomes and is well suited to studying the axion in the time domain. With it, we compute the exact axion-induced number distribution for the first time, demonstrating that it is not Poisson. We argue that virialized axion dark matter is expected to be in a Gaussian quantum state, and show that number or quadrature measurements can distinguish this from the predictions of a coherent state or even a Bose-Einstein condensate. Finally, we show that intrinsically quantum effects, such as those from Fock or squeezed axion states, are far too weak to detect.
