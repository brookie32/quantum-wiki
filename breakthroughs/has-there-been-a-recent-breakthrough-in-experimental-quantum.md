In 2026, quantum computing has shifted from experimental to commercially relevant applications , with  error rates dropping below 1% for two-qubit gates, making quantum error correction viable for the first time .  Commercial viability for most enterprises is projected for the early 2030s, but 2026 marks the beginning of quantum industrialization with multiple providers offering access to machines that can perform useful, error-corrected tasks .
