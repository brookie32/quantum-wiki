Based on techniques discovered in the better.codes autoresearch project, we show that Reed-Solomon codes of rate rho and block length n over a field of sufficiently large characteristic, have Cdot n list size bound when requiring fractional agreement alpha with a received word; where C,alpha are constants depending only on rho and alpha0 with an unspecified constant exponent c.
