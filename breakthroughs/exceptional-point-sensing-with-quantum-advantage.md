arXiv:2611.22125v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Exceptional-point sensors exploit singular non-Hermitian dynamics to enhance their response to weak perturbations. Recently, however, a no-go result has been established for a gain-loss-balanced dimer coupled to independent, phase-insensitive reservoirs: the exceptional-point susceptibility amplifies the signal and reservoir noise equally, precluding any quantum advantage. Here, we circumvent this limitation by engineering non-Hermitian dynamics in particle-hole Nambu space. A single common reservoir acts on modes forming a quantum-mechanics-free subsystem and generates an enhanced response protected from additional noise. We demonstrate quantum-metrological advantage for weak-signal sensing and static-parameter estimation. A continuous interpolation of the operator basis at fixed exceptional-point drift, with a singular midpoint, reveals how reservoir noise vanishes toward the protected limit. Our results provide a route to exceptional-point sensing with genuine quantum-metrological advantage through reservoir engineering.
