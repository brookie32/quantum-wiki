arXiv:2603.27627v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Existing experimental realizations of the Dicke model in atomic or ionic systems are based on global observables assuming uniform spin-boson coupling, while inevitable experimental nonuniformity on the one hand requires site-resolved measurement of spin states, and on the other hand provides potential quantum advantage in simulating multi-spin distributions. Here we report the quantum simulation of a nonuniform Dicke-like model, due to either the inevitable experimental imperfection or the engineered spin-phonon coupling pattern, in a two-dimensional (2D) crystal of up to 200 ions. We explicitly demonstrate the sensitivity of few-spin observables and multi-spin distributions to the spatial inhomogeneity of the model, and examine the relaxation dynamics of the nonuniform model by measuring the subsystem entropies of selected ion groups. Our work enables the study of Dicke-like models beyond the symmetric subspace, paving the way toward understanding the role of nonuniformity in its thermalization and quantum chaos behavior.
