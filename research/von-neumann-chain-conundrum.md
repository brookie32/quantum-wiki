The von Neumann chain describes how quantum measurement involves a system, measuring instrument, and observer, where each interaction creates a combined system requiring further measurement, potentially extending infinitely .  Von Neumann argued that breaking this infinite chain requires something distinct from quantum mechanics, such as a "cut" point . This concept relates to fundamental questions about measurement and wave function collapse in quantum mechanics.
