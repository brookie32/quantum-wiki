I'll search for information about this black hole surface resistivity claim to provide an accurate summary. Black holes have a surface electric resistivity equal to 4π ≈ 377 ohms , a property that emerges from analyzing electromagnetic fields interacting with black hole event horizons.  The event horizon behaves like a conductor in some respects but lacks perfect conductivity, allowing electromagnetic flux to penetrate the horizon .  These electromagnetic currents at the black hole surface generate Joule heating, and can exist both when inserted into an external electric circuit and independently .
