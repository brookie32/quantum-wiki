Recent observations from the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) have sparked new interest in the possibility that the universe contains more than three spatial dimensions plus time. Theoretical frameworks such as string theory and braneworld models suggest that extra dimensions could simultaneously explain dark energy, the effects attributed to dark matter, and why gravity is much weaker than other fundamental forces. Ongoing research seeks to determine whether these higher‑dimensional scenarios reflect physical reality or remain mathematical possibilities.
