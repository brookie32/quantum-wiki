Physicists have experimentally verified the two foundational postulates of Richard Feynman’s 1948 path‑integral formulation of quantum mechanics—specifically, how quantum systems evolve between initial and final states—using a real‑world single‑photon experiment for the first time. This confirmation, occurring nearly eight decades after Feynman published his propagator theory, substantiates the central role of the path integral in linking quantum dynamics with classical least‑action principles and underpins many modern fields such as quantum field theory and condensed‑matter physics.
