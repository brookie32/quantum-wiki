The Rust package registry crates.io now supports quantum computing as a category, enabling developers to discover and share quantum computing libraries and tools.  This addition reflects the growing ecosystem of Rust-based quantum computing frameworks, including simulators, circuit builders, and hardware integration libraries. The categorization helps organize quantum-related crates and makes them more discoverable for developers working on quantum computing projects.
