The Payload for Ultrahigh Energy Observations (PUEO) is a NASA Astrophysics Pioneers Program mission that flew above Antarctica on a long-duration balloon and used the Antarctic ice sheet to detect radio signals from extremely energetic astrophysical neutrinos.   The mission aims to reveal the origin and composition of the highest-energy cosmic rays and test fundamental physics at energies far beyond those achievable in human-made particle accelerators.
