A Chinese research team encoded the Riemann hypothesis formula into a physical system and explored it using a quantum computer, with their work published in Nature Communications.   The Riemann hypothesis claims that prime numbers along the number line follow an orderly but obscure formula, and despite a million-dollar bounty, mathematicians have been unable to prove it for 167 years.   The researchers believe quantum computing could serve as a powerful avenue for investigating major mathematical conjectures.
