In 2025 Ben Jones (Manchester) and Joseph Formaggio (MIT) proposed generating a “neutrino laser” by confining thousands of ultracold radioactive atoms in a Bose‑Einstein condensate so that their nuclear decays would emit coherent neutrinos. Two subsequent theoretical analyses confirmed that the required coherence is unachievable, rendering the design physically impossible. The result demonstrates that despite neutrinos’ ubiquity and scientific interest, controlling them to form a laser beam remains beyond current physics capabilities.
