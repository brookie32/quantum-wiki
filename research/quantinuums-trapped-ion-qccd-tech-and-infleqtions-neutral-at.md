Quantinuum's QCCD (quantum charge-coupled device) architecture uses trapped ions in a quantum processor featuring all-to-all connectivity and parallelized operations .  QCCDs employ mazes of electrodes with designated areas for storing and manipulating qubits, allowing ions to be transported through channels rather than containing all ions in a single trap . This post likely compares Quantinuum's trapped-ion approach with Infleqtion's neutral atom quantum computing platform as competing technologies in the quantum computing landscape.
