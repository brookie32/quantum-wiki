A research team has reported a significant piezoelectric effect in ultrathin, ultra-flexible polycrystalline diamond membranes, challenging the century-long scientific assumption that diamonds are non-piezoelectric.   The piezoelectric effect is attributed to asymmetry of grain boundaries within the membrane, where charge polarization accumulates during deformation to create a voltage difference.   This discovery has potential applications in energy harvesting, intelligent sensing, and wearable electronics.
