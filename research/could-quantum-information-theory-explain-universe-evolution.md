The 2022 James Webb Space Telescope deep‑field image reveals thousands of galaxies within a grain‑sized patch of sky, illustrating extreme cosmic structure formation. Gravity from Entropy (GfE) theory explains how local entropy density can decrease—allowing organized systems such as galaxies to emerge and persist—even while the total entropy of the universe rises in accordance with the second law of thermodynamics. This framework reconciles observable low‑entropy structures with a globally increasing‑entropy cosmos.
