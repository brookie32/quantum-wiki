Radio observations of the exoplanet K2-18b using the VLA and MeerKAT telescopes detected no artificial narrow-band signals indicative of extraterrestrial technology.   Located 124 light-years away, K2-18b sits in its red dwarf host star's habitable zone and is a prime candidate for a "Hycean" world with a thick hydrogen-rich atmosphere covering a global liquid water ocean.   The result narrows one specific avenue in the search for extraterrestrial intelligence, even as K2-18b remains a key target in studies of potentially habitable worlds.
