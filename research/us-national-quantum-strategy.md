The U.S. National Quantum Strategy represents an update to federal quantum policy that pursues a whole-of-government effort focused on deployment, commercialization, national-security applications, and cyber resilience rather than treating quantum technology solely as a long-term research initiative.   The strategy directs efforts to build a national quantum computing capability, strengthen domestic supply chains, expand the quantum workforce, and protect American research from foreign acquisition.   The strategy supports the maturing quantum information science and technology ecosystem, including promoting commercialization and deployment, and encouraging partnerships with United States industry.
