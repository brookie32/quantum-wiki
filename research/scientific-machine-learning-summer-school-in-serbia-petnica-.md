The Petnica Summer Institute (PSI) offers intensive summer schools in theoretical physics, phenomenology, quantum technologies, and machine learning at the Petnica Science Center in Serbia.   The 2026 SCIML event runs from August 1-11, 2026 in Valjevo, Serbia, with a registration fee of 550 Euros including accommodation and some waivers available.   The school focuses on peer learning and research with tracks including ML for scientific research alongside theoretical and applied physics.
