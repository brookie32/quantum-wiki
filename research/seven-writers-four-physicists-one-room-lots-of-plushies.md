Robert P Crease attended a workshop designed to ease tensions between physicists and science journalists, hosted by Royal Holloway, London. The event—called “Communicating QBism”—was organized by quantum‑physicist Christopher Fuchs of UMass Boston and featured participants such as Rüdiger Schack (Royal Holloway), John DeBrota (LMU Munich) and Matthew Liefer (Chapman University). During the meeting, Fuchs presented QBism (quantum Bayesianism), offered journalists a chance to critique and understand it, and used quirky plushies to keep the dialogue light yet substantive.
