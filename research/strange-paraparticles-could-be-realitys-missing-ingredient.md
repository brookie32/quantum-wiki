A recent mathematical study suggests the existence of a third class of elementary objects—paraparticles—that are neither conventional matter (fermions) nor forces (bosons) but obey an alternate set of quantum statistics first proposed decades ago. Although no experimental evidence yet exists, paraparticles could potentially account for phenomena missing from the Standard Model, such as dark matter, dark energy, and gravity, and would represent a fundamental shift in our understanding of the universe’s building blocks. Researchers like Vlatko Vedral consider their discovery to be a major paradigm change that could render the Standard Model incomplete.
