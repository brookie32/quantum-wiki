Stanford and ISTA researchers developed a chip that automatically separates partially coherent light into its constituent beams, effectively 'recycling' light.   The technology could enable new microscopy techniques and has applications ranging from computation to satellite communication.   In satellite communications, the chip could help compensate for atmospheric turbulence that degrades laser signals.
