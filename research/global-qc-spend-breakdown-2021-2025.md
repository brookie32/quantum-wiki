Global quantum computing funding grew from approximately $900 million annually in 2021-2022 to $1.8 billion in 2023 and $1.7 billion in 2024, reaching a record $4.2 billion in 2025.   Hardware and enabling technologies have captured 72% of cumulative investment at $7.9 billion, reflecting investor focus on solving foundational scalability and fault-tolerance challenges.   Public funding commitments for quantum research reached an estimated $56.7 billion total, with governments adding $12.7 billion in new commitments over the past year.
