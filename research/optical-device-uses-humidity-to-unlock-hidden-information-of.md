Engineers at UC San Diego developed an optical device that reveals hidden images and changes colors in response to humidity levels, with potential applications in anti-counterfeiting labels, secure data storage, interactive displays, and environmental sensors.   The bilayer device combines a phase-change material with a humidity-responsive hydrogel to enable reversible display of hidden images, with transitions occurring within 300 milliseconds.   The device is scalable and low-cost to produce.
