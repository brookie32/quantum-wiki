I'll search for information about this Tesla Turbine System discussion to provide you with an accurate summary. The Tesla Turbine is a bladeless turbine design that relies on viscous drag rather than traditional blades to generate rotational motion.   It consists of smooth disks with nozzles applying moving fluid to the edge of the disk, where the fluid drags on the disk through viscosity and adhesion, then spirals into the center exhaust as it slows.   Though Tesla projected efficiencies of up to 97%, reported efficiencies tend to be around 40%.
