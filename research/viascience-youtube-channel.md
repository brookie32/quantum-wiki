ViaScience is a YouTube channel devoted to physics education, aiming to present content between graphics-rich, math-free videos and formal university courses.   The channel contains approximately 85 videos totaling about 19 hours of material, primarily focused on quantum mechanics and relativity.   The channel emphasizes physical concepts while including sufficient mathematics to avoid purely descriptive explanations.
