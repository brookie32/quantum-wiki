IBM and Lockheed Martin are establishing a quantum innovation hub at ETH Zurich with Switzerland's first IBM Quantum System Two to be installed at the Swiss National Supercomputing Centre (CSCS) in Lugano by end of 2026.   The system will run IBM's Nighthawk r2 processor with 120 programmable qubits and can execute more than 100,000 circuits per second.   Swiss universities, startups, and companies can access the system for quantum research in chemistry, materials science, optimization, and financial services.
