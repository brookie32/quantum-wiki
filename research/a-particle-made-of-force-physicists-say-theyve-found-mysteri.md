Researchers at the Beijing Spectrometer III (BESIII) Collaboration presented evidence that the particle X(2370), discovered in 2011, is made mostly of glueballs—clusters of gluons that carry the strong nuclear force.   In 2024, after analyzing nearly 10 billion particle-decay events, scientists determined X(2370) has a spin parity of 0−+, consistent with predictions of the lightest glueball.   This result marks a major milestone because glueballs have been predicted since the early 1970s but had never been conclusively identified until now.
