Intel's CEO stated the company is focused on solving quantum error correction, with quantum computing expected to make substantial industrial impact within three to five years, forming a heterogeneous computing network with CPUs and GPUs to jointly tackle high-dimensional computational challenges.   Intel is supporting startups developing dataflow architecture solutions that can deliver similar performance to GPUs at significantly lower power consumption in specific inference scenarios.
