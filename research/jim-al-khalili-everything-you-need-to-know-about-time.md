In the video “Jim Al‑Khalili: Everything you need to know about time,” physicist Jim Al‑Khalili discusses concepts such as space–time from Einstein’s relativity, the thermodynamic arrow of time, and questions about whether all moments coexist or if time travel is possible. He explains the ongoing debate among physicists regarding the true nature of time and its implications for the origins of the universe. The discussion centers on foundational mysteries in physics related to general relativity and entropy.
