Researchers have developed a technique that uses a rotating light pattern to determine a laser's frequency from a single captured image, potentially simplifying laser characterization. This method likely leverages optical interference or diffraction effects to encode frequency information into spatial patterns that can be analyzed without requiring multiple measurements or specialized frequency detection equipment.
