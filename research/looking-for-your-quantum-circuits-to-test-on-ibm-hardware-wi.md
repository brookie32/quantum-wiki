I'll search for information about this Q-LASP initiative on IBM quantum hardware. Q-LASP is a technique that enables multiple IBM quantum experiments to share a single QPU submission, allowing researchers to send batches of quantum circuits that are prepared to run together with results returned separately under each circuit name. This approach significantly reduces QPU time and cost by batching many circuits into a single IBM execution.   The method has been tested in campaigns involving quantum algorithms like TFIM, VQE, and QAOA on IBM's 156-qubit quantum processors.
