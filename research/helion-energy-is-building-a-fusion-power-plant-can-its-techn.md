Helion Energy is building the Orion fusion power plant designed to deliver 50 megawatts of electricity to Microsoft data centers by 2029 .  The company uses a linear fusion system with pulsed magnetic compression that differs from traditional tokamak designs .  Some physicists question whether its ambitious promises can be met, noting fusion projects have a history of missed deadlines .
