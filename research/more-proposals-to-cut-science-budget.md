The Trump administration has proposed cutting spending by 37% at the National Institutes of Health and more than 50% at the National Science Foundation, the country's two major science funders.   Science advocacy groups are calling on Congress to reject the proposal, arguing the cuts would severely damage U.S. scientific competitiveness and benefit competitors like China.   Research funding advocates are hoping Congress balks again, urging lawmakers to reject these cuts as they did with the 2026 budget.
