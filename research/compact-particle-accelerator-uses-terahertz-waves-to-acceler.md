I'll search for information about this recent development in compact particle accelerators using terahertz waves. Researchers developed a compact particle accelerator using laser-generated terahertz radiation to accelerate electron bunches to 99.99% of light speed through a millimeter-scale structure.   They solved the challenge of synchronizing the particles with the accelerating field by slowing the terahertz wave's phase velocity to match the electrons.   This breakthrough has potential applications in ultrafast imaging, materials research, and eventually medical applications.
