Researchers used a trapped‑ion quantum simulator to emulate the Aharonov–Bohm effect within a U(1) lattice gauge theory. By engineering two distinct ion pathways that enclose a controllable magnetic flux, they observed interference patterns whose phase shifts matched theoretical predictions for electrons encircling a flux tube in discrete spacetime. The experiment demonstrates how quantum simulators can reproduce topological gauge‑field phenomena and offers a platform for studying more complex lattice gauge theories.
