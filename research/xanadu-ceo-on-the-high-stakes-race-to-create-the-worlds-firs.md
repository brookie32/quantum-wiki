Xanadu Quantum Technologies is a Canadian quantum computing hardware and software company headquartered in Toronto, Ontario, founded by Christian Weedbrook.   The company's ultimate goal is building a quantum computing data centre in Canada by 2029.   Xanadu achieved a world-first by successfully building a universal photonic quantum computer called Aurora, consisting of four modular and independent server racks that are photonically interconnected and networked together.
