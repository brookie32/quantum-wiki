Constant-overhead fault-tolerant quantum computation can be achieved even under a broad and realistic class of noise, including coherent errors.   Protocols using quantum low-density parity-check codes combined with concatenated Steane codes can achieve constant space overhead and polylogarithmic time overhead.  This research addresses a central challenge in scaling quantum computers by demonstrating that fault tolerance doesn't require exponential resource overhead.
