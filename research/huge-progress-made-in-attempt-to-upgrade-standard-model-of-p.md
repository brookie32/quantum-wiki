Undergraduate researchers have set new limits on 132 theoretical extensions of the Standard Model that involve violations of Lorentz symmetry. Their work refines the Standard Model Extension (SME) and identifies viable pathways for experimental tests at the frontiers of particle physics. The study advances efforts to address phenomena such as dark matter and dark energy that are not explained by the current model.
