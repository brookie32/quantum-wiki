Researchers reported the observation of multiple chiral gravitons using circularly polarized resonant inelastic light scattering, revealing both low- and high-energy gravitons at specific filling factors in quantum Hall systems.   The graviton energies correlate with distinct fractional charges, providing spectroscopic evidence for emergent partons and supporting geometric and parton theories of fractionalized quantum Hall matter.   These findings offer compelling evidence for the existence of partons as individual quasiparticles in fractional quantum Hall systems, unveiling the high-energy sector of quantum Hall physics.
