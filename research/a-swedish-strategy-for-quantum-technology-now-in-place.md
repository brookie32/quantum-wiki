Sweden unveiled its first national quantum technology strategy on August 18, 2026 ,  setting five broad goals through 2036 to guide research, commercialization, workforce development, security and international cooperation .  The strategy aims to provide unified direction enabling Sweden to advance toward a coordinated and long-term national effort in quantum technology , while  highlighting Sweden's quantum computing strengths and calling for better coordination, more skilled workers, improved financing for startups, and early adoption of quantum-safe cybersecurity measures including post-quantum cryptography .
