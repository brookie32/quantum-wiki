The CREDO app uses a smartphone's camera to detect high-energy particles by looking for bright pixels caused by particle impacts on the CCD detector, with detections sent to servers for analysis.   CREDO is a citizen science project searching for Cosmic-Ray Ensembles (extended cosmic-ray phenomena) that are invisible to individual detectors, going beyond traditional single air shower detection.  This Reddit post likely documents a user's experience reporting a potential particle detection event from the app.
