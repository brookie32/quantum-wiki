Researchers developed a method for simultaneous soft X-ray absorption spectroscopy (XAS) measurements of solid-liquid interfaces and bulk liquids using electron-yield and transmission methods.   By controlling liquid layer thickness from 20 nm to 40 μm, they obtained XAS spectra of bulk water and water-metal interfaces.   This technique can investigate catalytic, electrochemical, and biological mechanisms at solid-liquid interfaces.
