The Nikiforov-Uvarov method is used to solve the Schrödinger equation with the molecular Kratzer-Feus potential to obtain analytical bound state energy eigenvalues and eigenfunctions .  This application is essential for investigating the spectroscopic properties, electronic structure, and energetic properties of diatomic molecules .  The method yields vibrational energy levels, rotational constants, and centrifugal distortion constants of the Kratzer–Fues oscillator in closed form .
