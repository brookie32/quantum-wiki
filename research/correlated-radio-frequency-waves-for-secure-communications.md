MIT researchers developed a scalable platform generating pairs of highly correlated radio-frequency waves at room temperature without cryogenic cooling equipment.   The hybrid magnon-photon device outputs synchronized signals with distinct microwave frequencies that remain strongly correlated, preventing attackers from recovering encoded information without the matching signal as a cryptographic key.   These linked radio waves enable noise-resilient communication, high-precision radar, and sensing applications.
