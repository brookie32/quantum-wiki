Quantum chip fabs are manufacturing facilities that produce quantum wafers and components for quantum computers. The U.S. government is investing in quantum foundries through the CHIPS and Science Act, with $2.013 billion in federal incentives going to nine quantum companies, including two domestic foundries (IBM and GlobalFoundries), to establish and accelerate domestic quantum manufacturing capacity.   Quantum computing has significant implications for national defense, advanced materials and biopharmaceutical discovery, financial modeling, and energy systems.
