Researchers at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory’s National Ignition Facility achieved nuclear‑fusion ignition that produced X‑ray emission brighter than any previously recorded on Earth, rivaling the luminosity of certain stars. The experiment used 192 high‑energy lasers focused into a gold cylinder containing a carbon capsule, creating temperatures exceeding those in the sun’s core and driving an unprecedented blast wave. These extreme X‑ray conditions enable new experiments on stellar radiation absorption and help test materials designed for future fusion reactors under blast stresses.
