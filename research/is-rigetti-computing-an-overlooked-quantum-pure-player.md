Rigetti Computing is the only public, pure-play company building superconducting qubit quantum computers , offering investors direct exposure to quantum computing technology.  The company generates revenue primarily from government and enterprise research contracts while also advancing quantum hardware performance and its hybrid cloud platform .  Rigetti uses a modular chiplet architecture for its superconducting processors, with its Cepheus-1-108Q system comprising twelve interconnected 9-qubit chiplets achieving 99.1% median two-qubit gate fidelity .
