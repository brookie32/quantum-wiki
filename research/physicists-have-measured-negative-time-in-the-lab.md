Physicists at the University of Toronto demonstrated that photons passing through a cloud of rubidium atoms can exhibit negative dwell time, appearing to exit before entering.   Weak measurements of atomic excitation confirmed this negative dwell time has a measurable physical effect on the atomic system, consistent with quantum theory.   The results are explained by standard physics and show that negative dwell time is not an artifact, despite appearing paradoxical.
