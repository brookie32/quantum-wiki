Quantum physicist Sean Carroll argues that the cosmos may follow an eternally repeating cycle in which each round of expansion, contraction, and all physical processes—including stars, galaxies, life, and even individual thoughts—restores exactly to its previous state.  By treating quantum possibilities as primary, he finds a model in which every observer experiences the same sequence of events each cycle, thereby avoiding paradoxes such as long‑lived “Boltzmann brains” that otherwise dominate very old, infinite universes.
