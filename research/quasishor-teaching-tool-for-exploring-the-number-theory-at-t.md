QuasiShor is a teaching tool designed to help students understand the number theory underlying Shor's algorithm, particularly the periodic modulo sequences that form the mathematical foundation of this quantum factorization algorithm.  The tool likely enables interactive exploration of concepts like period-finding and modular exponentiation that are central to how Shor's algorithm achieves its quantum speedup.  It serves an educational purpose similar to how universities teach Shor's algorithm to demonstrate quantum computing concepts including quantum parallelism, interference, and measurement.
