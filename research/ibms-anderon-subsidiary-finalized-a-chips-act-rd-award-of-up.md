Anderon, an IBM subsidiary, finalized a $1 billion CHIPS and Science Act award with the U.S. Department of Commerce to support R&D for quantum wafer manufacturing.   The company operates a 300mm pure-play quantum wafer fabrication facility at Albany NanoTech, supplemented by another $1 billion investment from IBM.   Anderon manufactures specialized 300mm wafers supporting superconducting qubit arrays, quantum input/output signaling, and readout signal chain components for the global quantum hardware ecosystem.
