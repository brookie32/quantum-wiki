Microsoft unveiled an upgraded quantum chip called Majorana 2, an updated version of the controversial Majorana 1 chip unveiled the previous year, claiming it keeps the company on track to develop practical quantum computers faster than competitors.   The new chip features 12 qubits (up from 8) with significantly improved qubit longevity exceeding 20 seconds, and uses lead instead of aluminum as superconductors.   However, some researchers remain skeptical of the company's claims.
