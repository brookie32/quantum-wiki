CSIRO has developed portable quantum-enabled timing devices designed to maintain secure timing for critical systems when GNSS signals are disrupted.   The devices generate entangled photon pairs that provide alternative timing capabilities in environments where GNSS signals are disrupted by jamming or spoofing.   Because quantum entanglement is highly sensitive to interference, any attempt to intercept or manipulate the signal immediately alters its quantum state, making the system spoof-proof while enabling secure ground-to-satellite time transfer.
