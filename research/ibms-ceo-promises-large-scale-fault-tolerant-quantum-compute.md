IBM announced plans to invest more than $10 billion in quantum computing over the next five years, targeting the delivery of the first large-scale, fault-tolerant quantum computer by 2029.   The system, called IBM Quantum Starling, will be capable of running quantum circuits comprising 100 million quantum gates on 200 logical qubits.   The 2029 system will be capable of executing 20,000 times more operations than current systems.
