I'll search for information about this Reddit discussion on quantum probabilities. Based on the search results about quantum probabilities, here's a summary:

 Quantum probabilities arise from the Born rule, which defines the probabilities of measuring eigenvalues of an observable.   Quantum probabilities express ignorance about potential measurement outcomes that are actualized through measurement.   Unlike classical probabilities following Kolmogorov's axioms, quantum mechanical probability structures do not satisfy these traditional axioms.
