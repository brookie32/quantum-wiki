Researchers demonstrated "quantum enhancement" in a production-scale, pretrained large language model (LLM) , achieving this by  inserting a small quantum adapter into a projection layer of Meta's Llama 3.1 8B model and executing it on IBM Quantum System Two hardware during inference .  The hybrid model answered several questions correctly that the base Llama model could not , with  the quantum-classical hybrid model lowering perplexity by 1.4% while adding only 6,000 parameters .
