Quantum error correction faces inherent challenges because quantum states must be corrected without measuring them (as measurement collapses superpositions) and entanglement between qubits can cause destructive interference.   Optimal error decoding is a computationally hard problem , suggesting that the practical implementation of error correction may require more sophisticated approaches than initially anticipated.
