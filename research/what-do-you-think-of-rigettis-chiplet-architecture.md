Rigetti's chiplet architecture represents a paradigm shift in quantum hardware design by transitioning from monolithic chips to modular chiplets, unlocking greater control over uniformity and improved fabrication yields.   The Cepheus-1-108Q system, Rigetti's highest qubit-count system, comprises twelve interconnected 9-qubit chiplets operating at 99.1% median two-qubit gate fidelity.   This proprietary chiplet-based architecture is designed to enable higher fidelity, higher qubit systems that will ultimately support fault-tolerant quantum computing.
