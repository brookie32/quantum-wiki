I'll search for information about QuStream and its quantum-proof claims. QuStream is an information-theoretic security system that claims to provide provably unbreakable protection by guaranteeing intercepted traffic cannot be decrypted even with future quantum computers, rather than relying on mathematical hardness assumptions.   The company published research on its methodology in 2024 and completed a Proof-of-Concept in 2025 with NATO and the EU, demonstrating a solution to the "last-mile problem" for Quantum Key Distribution.   The system uses dynamic, single-use encryption keys delivered through decentralized nodes, making key interception and brute-force attacks virtually impossible even with quantum capabilities.
