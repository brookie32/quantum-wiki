This work addresses the challenge of reducing qubit overhead in quantum error correction by developing systematic design principles for ultra-high-rate quantum codes that navigate tradeoffs among encoding rate, distance, check weight, and blocklength.   The research introduces code templates based on pair-partition constructions and halving transformations, identifying column weight as a key parameter that enables larger distances at compact blocklengths.
