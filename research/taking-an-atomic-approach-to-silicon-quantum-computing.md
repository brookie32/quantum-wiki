This podcast episode features physicist and entrepreneur Michelle Simmons, founder and CEO of Australia‑based Silicon Quantum Computing (founded in 2017), who explains how individual phosphorus atoms embedded in silicon serve as high‑fidelity qubits with fast gate operations. She describes the company’s commercial products— the Watermelon quantum feature generator for machine learning and the Quantum Twins analogue processors for quantum chemistry— and previews an upcoming 32‑qubit gate‑based “Chilli” processor series, while also offering career advice for those entering the quantum computing sector.
