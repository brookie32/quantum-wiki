TASI (Theoretical Advanced Study Institute) is an annual four-week summer school in theoretical particle physics held at the University of Colorado Boulder in June, bringing together advanced graduate students to attend cutting-edge lectures from premier researchers.   The 2026 program, titled "Exploring Fundamental Physics from Colliders to the Cosmos," ran from June 1-26, 2026.   Video recordings and lecture notes from TASI speakers are made available publicly for the benefit of students and researchers worldwide.
