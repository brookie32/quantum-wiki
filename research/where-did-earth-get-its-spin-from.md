Earth rotates due to conservation of angular momentum acquired from the rotation of the solar nebula that formed the solar system.   During solar system formation about 4.6 billion years ago, a collapsing gas and dust cloud began spinning, and this rotational motion was transferred to the forming Earth.   Earth continues to spin today because of inertia, an object's resistance to changes in its current state of motion.
