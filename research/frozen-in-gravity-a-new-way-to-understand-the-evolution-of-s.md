Researchers reformulated Einstein's field equations using a framework parallel to nonlinear electrodynamics, showing that general relativity admits gravitational field connections whose connectivity is maintained by spacetime dynamics through an Ohm-type condition for the gravitational field.   This framework leads to conserved "gravitational magnetic" flux and gravitational helicity, providing topological constraints on spacetime evolution.   The findings offer a new perspective that could improve theoretical understanding of cosmological phenomena including black holes, gravitational waves, and the universe's evolution.
