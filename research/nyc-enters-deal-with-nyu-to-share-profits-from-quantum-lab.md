NYC Mayor Zohran Mamdani announced a $30 million investment in a new Quantum Technology Lab at NYU in exchange for a 15-percent share in profits from projects developed at the lab.   The Quantum Fabrication Facility (QFab) is expected to open in 2030 at 770 Broadway and will take up 23,000 square feet.   This represents the first public-private partnership of this scale in the city, allowing NYC to profit when intellectual property is created using city-funded tools.
