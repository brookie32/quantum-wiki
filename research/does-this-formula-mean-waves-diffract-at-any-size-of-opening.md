Diffraction depends on the wavelength and size of the aperture, with diffraction happening dramatically only when the opening is small compared to the wavelength.   The degree of spreading depends on how the opening size compares to wavelength; when large openings occur, diffraction is minimal, but comparable-sized openings to wavelength cause strong spreading.   All waves diffract, including gravitational waves, water waves, and electromagnetic waves.
