Studies of microtubules, anesthetics, and brain organoids are increasingly probing the hypothesis that quantum processes underlie human consciousness. Penrose and Hameroff’s controversial Orch‑OR model has sparked debate; while early experiments suggest both supporting and refuting evidence, some researchers argue that quantum computing could help test its validity. Additionally, speculative discussions propose that conscious observation might play a fundamental role in shaping physical reality itself.
