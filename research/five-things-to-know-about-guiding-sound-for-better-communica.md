Bot operators should contact the site owner to obtain whitelisting or identification; proper bots can be granted access without the automated verification process. They must honor HTTP 429 (Too Many Requests) responses and implement appropriate rate‑limiting—failure to do so may trigger blocking. Support or inquiries about these procedures are directed through the provided contact form.
