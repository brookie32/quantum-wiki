Sabrina Pasterski is a physicist renowned for building and flying her own plane before age 16. She is developing *celestial holography*, a framework that proposes an equivalence (duality) between descriptions of spacetime on the bulk and its celestial sphere at null infinity, aiming to bridge general relativity and quantum theory. The press has dubbed her the “next Einstein” citing her rapid rise, intellectual depth, and potential impact on fundamental physics.
