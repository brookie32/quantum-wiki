Scientists from EPFL, the Weizmann Institute, and Princeton University studied the ν = 1/2 fractional quantum Hall state , and  two independent experiments produced essentially the same result , with  both devices measuring quasiparticle charges consistent with e/4 (one-quarter of an electron's charge) .  Under extreme conditions where electrons are cooled near absolute zero in a two-dimensional layer under a strong magnetic field, they organize into a fractional quantum Hall effect state with quasiparticles carrying fractional charge .  These rare even-denominator states could potentially contain non-Abelian anyons useful for fault-tolerant topological quantum computers .
