Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) are universal function approximators that embed physical laws governing data-sets described by partial differential equations (PDEs) into the learning process.   PINN-based electromagnetic simulation integrates physical principles into neural network learning, enabling accurate modeling of complex electromagnetic systems ,  with implementations that embed the residual of Maxwell's equations into the loss function for scattered field computation.
