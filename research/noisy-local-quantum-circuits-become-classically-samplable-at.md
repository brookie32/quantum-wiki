This research shows that geometrically local quantum circuits with depolarizing noise become classically simulable at constant depth, as entropy accumulates locally from the imbalance between noise volume and boundary constraints.   The result applies to arbitrary geometrically local circuits with unital operations, improving upon previous restricted-gate simulability results.
