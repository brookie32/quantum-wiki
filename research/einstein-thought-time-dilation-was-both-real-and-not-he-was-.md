Einstein’s special theory of relativity predicts that time dilation—where clocks at higher gravitational potentials or moving at high speeds tick slower—is physically real and experimentally confirmed. The author notes that while our daily perception of time can feel altered, the observed relativistic effect is a measurable physical phenomenon rather than mere psychological illusion. This duality has sparked philosophical debate over what constitutes “real” in physics, a topic discussed by historians such as Marco Giovanelli at recent conferences.
