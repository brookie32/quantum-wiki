I'll search for information about this Google quantum computing breakthrough. Google's Quantum AI team demonstrated a technique that reduces errors roughly 40-fold on its Willow processor , addressing  a major bottleneck in producing high-quality, error-corrected magic states that could consume up to 95% of a quantum machine's capacity .  The "magic state cultivation" method injects a noisy state into a small region of a quantum chip, runs coordinated measurements to check for errors and remove flawed attempts, growing an exceptionally pure, low-error quantum state .  Further work is needed to scale the technique for practical quantum computing, but the findings offer a potential path to reducing hardware resources required for fault-tolerant quantum systems .
