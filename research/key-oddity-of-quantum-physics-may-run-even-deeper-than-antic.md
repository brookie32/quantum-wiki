Quantum mechanics is fundamentally probabilistic, with the Born rule converting a system’s state vector into probabilities for different measurement outcomes. A recent analysis by Jiaxuan Zhang shows that prevailing attempts to derive this rule from deeper mathematical principles do not remove its probabilistic character, indicating that the oddity may be more deeply ingrained in the theory than previously thought.
