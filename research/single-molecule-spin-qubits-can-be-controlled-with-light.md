Researchers at NVision Quantum Technologies have produced the first single‑molecule spin qubits that can be individually optically addressed and manipulated with microwave pulses, maintaining quantum coherence for several milliseconds. These molecular qubits offer higher fidelity operations than conventional semiconductor defect qubits and are amenable to photonic integration, making them promising candidates for scalable quantum processors.
