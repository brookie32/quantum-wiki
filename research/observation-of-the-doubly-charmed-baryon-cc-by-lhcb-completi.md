The LHCb collaboration reports the first observation of the doubly charmed baryon Ξcc+ through its decay to Λc+K−π+, with statistical significance exceeding seven standard deviations using proton-proton collision data collected in 2024.   This observation completes the doubly charmed baryon doublet and establishes them as a precision laboratory for studying heavy-quark dynamics in quantum chromodynamics.
