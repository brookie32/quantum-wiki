QpiAI is an Indian startup that integrates AI and quantum computing for enterprise use cases and has raised $32 million in government-backed funding to develop utility-scale quantum computers.   Selected as one of eight startups by India's National Quantum Mission, QpiAI provides optimization capabilities across manufacturing, finance, pharma, and materials sectors.   The company has developed the QpiAI-Indus, a 25-qubit superconducting quantum computer deployed in India.
