NIST finalized NIST Internal Report (IR) 8547 on October 27, 2026, outlining its plan for retiring quantum-vulnerable public-key algorithms.   The final report deprecates RSA and ECC at all approved key sizes after 2030 and disallows them after 2035.   The report replaces quantum-vulnerable algorithms with FIPS 203 (ML-KEM), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA), and FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA) post-quantum cryptographic standards.
