Researchers demonstrated how to switch between electric and magnetic skyrmions in free-space toroidal terahertz pulses using a custom-built nonlinear metasurface .  Information is encoded in the topological shape of the light pulse, a property mathematically protected against environmental disruption .  This technique could enable robust encoding of wireless data streams in the terahertz region of the electromagnetic spectrum .
