Linear Optical Quantum Computing (LOQC) is a quantum computing paradigm using photons as information carriers and linear optical elements like mirrors, beam splitters, and waveplates to process quantum information.   Key components typically include mirrors, beam splitters, phase shifters, and Mach–Zehnder interferometers to implement quantum operations.   Despite progress, significant hurdles remain in practical LOQC implementation, with the primary obstacle being a deterministic source of photons.
