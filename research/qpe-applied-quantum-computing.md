Quantum Phase Estimation (QPE) is one of the most important subroutines in quantum computation that serves as a central building block for many quantum algorithms, implementing measurements for Hermitian operators like Hamiltonians.   In condensed matter physics, QPE is primarily targeted at determining the ground and excited state energies of various Hamiltonians, providing fundamental insights into material properties and molecular interactions.   QPE serves as a critical subroutine for major protocols such as Shor's factoring algorithm and the calculation of ground-state molecular energies in quantum chemistry.
