Recent gravitational wave observations have constrained but not ruled out a fifth dimension; string theory predicts seven extra dimensions so small they wouldn't affect gravitational waves measurably.   Brane world theories propose that our 4D universe is a membrane floating in higher-dimensional space, where gravity might leak into the fifth dimension while other quantum fields remain confined to four dimensions.   Five-dimensional theories with a large extra dimension smoothly embed general relativity while maintaining its observational support, though they remain speculative and lack definitive experimental confirmation.
