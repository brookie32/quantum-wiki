This work investigates how dynamical tensor fields can emerge within the functional renormalization group (FRG) framework, demonstrating that finite kinetic terms are dynamically generated in the infrared regime.   The research establishes agreement with diffeomorphism-invariant Einstein-Hilbert structure in the transverse-traceless spin-2 sector, while examining contributions in longitudinal and trace sectors.
