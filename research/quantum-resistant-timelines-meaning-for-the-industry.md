Recent research papers have dramatically reduced estimates of quantum resources needed to break encryption systems, with what once required 20 million qubits now potentially requiring fewer than 100,000 under newer architectures .  This has prompted major technology companies like Google to accelerate migration timelines to post-quantum cryptography, setting targets like 2029 for transitioning to quantum-resistant encryption across systems .  The industry has shifted from planning around a threat two decades away to one overlapping with actively deployed systems, with cryptocurrency particularly vulnerable since private key encryption was never designed to withstand quantum attacks .
