IBM announced it has signed a definitive agreement to acquire HRL Laboratories, a flagship research and development institution jointly owned by Boeing and General Motors.   HRL's expertise in silicon-spin qubit engineering will complement IBM's existing superconducting qubit program and bring knowledge of silicon-based spin qubit platforms that could offer new insights into how to best scale quantum computers into the next decade.   The transaction is expected to close by the end of the third quarter of 2026.
