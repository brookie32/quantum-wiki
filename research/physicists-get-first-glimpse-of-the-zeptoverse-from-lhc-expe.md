In November 2026 the LHCb experiment at CERN announced the first observation of a highly suppressed decay in which a beauty (bottom) quark annihilates with its antiquark to produce a muon–antimuon pair.  This rare process, occurring at the zeptometer scale, provides a new window into physics beyond the Standard Model and could help probe elusive phenomena such as dark matter.
