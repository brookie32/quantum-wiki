This work reports experimental digital quantum simulation of the one-dimensional Fermi-Hubbard model on a superconducting quantum processor at a scale beyond the reach of exact statevector simulation and challenging for state-of-the-art tensor-network methods.   The researchers encode this problem using up to 120 qubits through an efficient mapping that reduces circuit complexity, and improve accuracy through error suppression to simulate dynamical evolution using up to 90 Trotter steps.   For simulated evolution times at the limit of quantum/classical agreement, the wall-clock runtime of the quantum processor is up to 3000× faster than an optimized classical simulation.
