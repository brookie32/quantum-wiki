Ignacio Cirac, a Spanish quantum physicist based at the Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics, is best known for co‑creating the Cirac–Zoller trapped‑ion proposal that established a viable platform for quantum computation and for pioneering quantum simulators to tackle many‑body physics problems.  In 2026 the Institute of Physics awarded him its Isaac Newton Medal and Prize in recognition of his foundational contributions to modern quantum information science, outstanding experimental vision, and global scientific leadership in quantum technology.
