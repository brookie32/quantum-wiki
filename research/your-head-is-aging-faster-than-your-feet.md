Due to gravitational time dilation in General Relativity, your head experiences time at a slightly faster rate than your feet because it is farther from Earth's gravitational center.   The difference is imperceptibly small, amounting to approximately 90 billionths of a second over a 79-year lifetime, though NIST experiments have confirmed this effect at human height scales.   This phenomenon demonstrates that time passes at different rates depending on gravitational field strength and velocity.
