QRAM (Quantum Random Access Memory) is a quantum computational primitive that generalizes classical RAM to enable coherent, superposed queries of large datasets.   Multiple leading technology companies and academic institutions, including IBM, Google, and IonQ, are actively exploring QRAM and its applications.   While QRAM has a controversial history with significant engineering challenges, ongoing research aims to overcome fundamental limitations in developing scalable passive QRAM systems.
