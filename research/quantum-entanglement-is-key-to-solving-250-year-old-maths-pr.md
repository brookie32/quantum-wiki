A century‑old puzzle posed to Leonhard Euler—the 36 officers problem—was proven unsolvable classically but can be resolved when formulated as a quantum Latin square using entangled states. In 2022 Karol Życzkowski and colleagues demonstrated this solution, and subsequent work by Robin Simoens and Simeon Ball identified quantum entanglement as the key ingredient enabling the arrangement. The result offers insights that could improve error resilience in future quantum computers.
