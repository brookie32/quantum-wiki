I'll search for information about this physics question regarding mosquito nets and air temperature. No significant temperature difference exists between the inside and outside of a mosquito net , though  perceived thermal discomfort is primarily caused by the attenuation of airflow .  The discomfort users experience is not due to higher temperatures inside nets but rather to attenuated airflows that reduce evaporative cooling . Thus, mosquito nets do not lower air temperature itself but reduce air circulation, which impairs the body's cooling mechanisms.
