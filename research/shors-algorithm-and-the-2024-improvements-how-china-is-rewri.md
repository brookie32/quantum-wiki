Chinese researchers have modified Shor's algorithm to potentially allow less powerful quantum computers to crack current cryptosystems.   The modified algorithm, optimized through processing steps, demonstrated factoring a 48-bit number using just 10 qubits.   However, technology required to apply Shor's algorithm at scale to break RSA encryption remains years away and would require a universal quantum computer with thousands to millions of stable qubits.
