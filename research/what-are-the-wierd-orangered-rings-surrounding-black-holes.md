The orange/red rings visible in black hole images represent the shadow of the black hole, which is a glowing ring of ultra-hot gas .  The colors in the image don't represent the actual hues of the gas; rather, they are a color map chosen by researchers to depict brightness of emissions, with yellow indicating the most intense emission and red indicating less intense emission .  Some photons skirt the black hole and keep circling it practically forever, creating a ring of orbiting photons described as a "cosmic movie camera" and an "infinite light trap" .
