A critique published in Nature argues that software errors in Microsoft's foundational research from 2025 undermined claims of a breakthrough into Majorana-based topological qubits.   The critique questions Microsoft's Topological Gap Protocol measurement technique, centering on data quality and analytical methods used to interpret results.   Microsoft has formally rejected the criticism, saying it is a minor anomaly rather than a fatal flaw.
