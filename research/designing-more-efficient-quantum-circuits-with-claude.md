Claude Mythos 5 designed quantum circuits that improve on the best published ones, sometimes by an order of magnitude or more, using plain-language instructions.   Claude successfully worked in a closed loop, proposing circuit designs, receiving feedback and a score from an evaluator program, and improving its next attempt with no human input between rounds.   The research optimized eight circuits spanning chemistry, optimization, physics simulation, number theory and numerical computing, with one case involving elliptic-curve point addition achieving 17 times fewer gates than prior approaches after 53 hours of development.
