Q-Hack India 2026 is a national-level quantum hackathon hosted by QuantumRIT at Ramaiah Institute of Technology, Bengaluru, as part of Quantum Week 2026 and the IBM Qiskit Fall Fest program.   The hackathon brings together students, developers, researchers and quantum enthusiasts to explore real-world quantum computing applications, with participants moving from idea to prototype through a competitive build experience.   The event features multiple tracks including Quantum Biotech & Chemistry, Quantum Security & Cryptography, Quantum AI & Software, and Open Quantum Innovation, with the Grand Finale occurring on October 30–31, 2026.
