Scottish artist Gregor Harvie unveiled a quantum‑inspired installation titled *Superposition* at the University of Strathclyde (20–29 Oct 2024) to mark 100 years since Schrödinger’s equation was published. The piece uses 16 aluminium panels that spin at 400 rpm, revealing switching patterns that analogise wave‑function collapse for superposition and correlated outcomes for entanglement. Each panel has a white side and a coloured side; when approaching the installation the panels stop randomly but show identical sides half the time in most pairs (superposition) or three‑quarters of the time in one pair (entanglement).
