I'll search for information about this LHC development. The 2026 run marks the beginning of the final LHC data-taking run before the High-Luminosity LHC (HiLumi LHC) upgrade.   The LHC has successfully reached its nominal Run 3 performance, with the intensity ramp-up phase completed at the end of March and the machine routinely operating at nominal proton bunch intensities.   With Long Shutdown 3 scheduled to begin in early July, the 2026 run will be short but densely packed, and when the LHC returns in 2030 as the HiLumi LHC, it will operate with substantially higher collision rates.
