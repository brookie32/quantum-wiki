The standard model of particle physics is one of our most successful theories for how the universe works. But, as Dr Harry Cliff explains, it explains only 5 per cent of the the known universe and there are gaping holes in the model. It is in these cracks that Dr Cliff hopes to find new physics that might finally give us a complete understanding of the universe
