An international collaboration of approximately 60 scientists at CERN led by researchers from the University of Calgary conducted a new measurement comparing the spectrum of hydrogen to antihydrogen.   The team found that the antihydrogen atom emitted the exact same light spectrum as regular hydrogen atoms put through the same test.   The results support the theoretical symmetry between matter and antimatter that is core to modern physics models.
