Cigarette smoke penetrates closed windows through small gaps and air leakage pathways, as smoke consists of gases and fine particulates that easily move with air currents.   Air leaks around window frames, doors, vents, and cracks provide routes for outdoor air infiltration, with even modern homes containing sufficient leakage for fine particles to pass through.
