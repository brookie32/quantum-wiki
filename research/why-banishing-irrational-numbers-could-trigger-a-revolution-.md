Quantum mechanics depends on irrational numbers (e.g., √2, π) in its formalism, but some researchers argue removing these non‑rational entities could simplify the theory’s counterintuitive predictions. Drawing on the historical anecdote of Hippasus of Metapontum—who allegedly drowned for uncovering irrationals—the proposal is that an alternative framework, such as one suggested by Tim Palmer at Oxford, might enable a revised quantum theory that dispenses with irrational numbers entirely.
