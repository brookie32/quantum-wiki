PsiQuantum is an American quantum computing company based in Palo Alto, California, developing a general-purpose silicon photonic quantum computer.   Their goal is to build a large-scale, fault-tolerant quantum computer using photons, requiring approximately 1 million physical qubits by the late 2020s.   In March 2026, they announced a $1 billion build-out for utility-scale photonic quantum computers in Chicago and Brisbane, Australia.
