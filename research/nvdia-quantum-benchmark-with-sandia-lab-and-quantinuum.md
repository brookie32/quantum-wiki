QUOPS is an independent cross-platform benchmark developed by Sandia National Laboratories that measures progress toward utility-scale, fault-tolerant quantum computing, with initial results reported for quantum processors from Google, IBM, and Quantinuum.   The QUOPS reference implementation is available in NVIDIA's CUDA-Q platform , enabling researchers to standardize evaluation of quantum hardware readiness for practical applications.
