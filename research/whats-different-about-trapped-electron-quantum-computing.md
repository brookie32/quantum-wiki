I'll search for information about trapped electron quantum computing to provide an accurate summary. Trapped electron quantum computing differs from trapped ion systems in that no laser light is required and electrons are much lighter , which  allows operation with less technical overhead and faster speeds than trapped ion systems .  Electrons serve as natural two-level spin systems that can be manipulated with microwave technology, eliminating the need for qubit control optics .
