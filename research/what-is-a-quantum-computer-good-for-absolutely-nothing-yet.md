Current quantum computers have been characterized as "absolutely nothing" in terms of practical utility, with research showing they have yet to prove more useful or efficient than conventional computers, though long-term applications remain promising.   While current algorithms lack sufficient advancement for practical quantum advantage, chemistry and materials science are considered the most promising near-term applications.
