Dissipative quantum computation (DQC) uses dissipative dynamics that converge to fixed points, enabling quantum computation through environmental coupling rather than purely coherent operations.   Quantum states can be constructed as attractive fixed points of such dissipative dynamical systems, making state preparation a natural application.   Reachability analysis for quantum systems is challenging because the state space is continuous, making analysis more difficult than in classical systems.
