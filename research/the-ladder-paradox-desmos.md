I'll search for information about the Ladder Paradox to provide you with an accurate summary. The ladder paradox is a thought experiment in special relativity involving a ladder traveling horizontally at relativistic speed and undergoing Lorentz length contraction.   The paradox arises because a stationary observer sees the moving ladder fit inside a shorter building due to contraction, while an observer moving with the ladder sees the building contracted instead and the ladder unable to fit.   The resolution lies in the relativity of simultaneity: length is defined by simultaneous events at each end, and since simultaneity is relative, so is length.
