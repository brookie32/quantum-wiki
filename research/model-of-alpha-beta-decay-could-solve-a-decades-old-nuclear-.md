A pair of researchers has created a new model for radioactive decay, which allows alpha and beta decay to happen simultaneously in a single transition. Testing their model's predictions against a full chart of known isotopes, Wenqiang Zhang at the Chinese Academy of Sciences, together with Chong Qi at the Alba Nova University Centre, Stockholm, showed that these simultaneous emissions could better explain the observed emission spectra of several radioactive isotopes.
