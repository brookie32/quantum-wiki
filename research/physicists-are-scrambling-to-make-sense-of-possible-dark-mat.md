An anomalous event—a single burst of light in the LUX‑ZEPLIN (LZ) liquid‑xenon detector—was reported on 1 September 2026, with a roughly 0.5 % chance that it was simply a statistical fluke, yet its energy exceeds predictions for a standard weakly interacting massive particle (WIMP). The signal has sparked rapid analyses worldwide to confirm the observation and to propose alternative dark‑matter models consistent with the higher‑energy signature. Physicists are now racing both to validate the claim and to determine which type of particle could produce such an event.
