PsiQuantum broke ground on construction at Moreton Bay Central in Queensland to build the world's first utility-scale, fault-tolerant quantum computer.   The project is backed by nearly $1 billion in joint federal and Queensland government funding, with an operational launch targeted for the end of 2027.   The facility will eventually hold tens of thousands of photonic quantum chips cooled by one of the largest cryogenic systems ever constructed for quantum computing.
