IQM Radiance is a superconducting quantum computing platform   available with 20 high-fidelity qubits, upgradable to 54, and up to 150 qubits .  The system features 99.9% single-qubit fidelity and tunable couplers natively supporting surface-code layouts .  It is deployed on-site at research institutions like Oak Ridge National Laboratory to integrate quantum computing with classical high-performance computing .
