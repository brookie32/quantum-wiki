IBM announced a $10 billion investment in quantum computing over the next five years to be allocated for research and development, capital expenditures, scaling manufacturing, ecosystem partnerships, and acquisitions.   The funding aims to accelerate development of large-scale fault-tolerant quantum computers, with a target of launching the world's first by 2029.   IBM has deployed more than 90 quantum systems globally and built a quantum ecosystem involving more than 325 Fortune 500 companies, startups, universities and government agencies.
