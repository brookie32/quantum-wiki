Two independent research teams have shown that, under specific quantum conditions involving superposition states, gravity could act repulsively rather than attractively. The studies—conducted by groups in Brazil and the U.K./Israel—argue that quantum effects may allow gravitational forces to push objects apart, a behavior not predicted by Einstein’s general relativity. This theoretical prediction is poised for experimental testing in laboratory settings soon.
