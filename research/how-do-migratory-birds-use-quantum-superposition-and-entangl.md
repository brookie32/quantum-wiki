Migratory birds may use quantum effects in molecular fragments called radical pairs, formed in their eyes, to navigate using Earth's magnetic field.   These birds possess proteins named cryptochromes in their retinas that form quantum entangled electron pairings when exposed to sunlight, with the electrons being influenced by Earth's magnetic field to give the bird directional sensitivity.   Despite over 50 years of research, scientists are still working to fully understand exactly how birds use this quantum-based magnetic sense to stay on course.
