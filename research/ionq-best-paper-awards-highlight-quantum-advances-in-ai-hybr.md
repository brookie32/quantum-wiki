IonQ received four Best Paper Awards from 857 global research submissions ahead of IEEE Quantum Week 2026, with recognized work covering quantum AI, hybrid computing, large-scale linear algebra, and protein-folding applications.   Research partners included NVIDIA, QuantumBasel, Synopsys, Oak Ridge National Laboratory, and Kipu Quantum.   Two papers took first place in their respective tracks, while two received third-place awards.
