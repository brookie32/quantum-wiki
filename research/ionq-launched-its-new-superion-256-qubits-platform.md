IonQ launched Superion 256, a 256-qubit trapped-ion quantum computing platform designed as the foundation for future products, using Electronic Qubit Control technology and semiconductor manufacturing through SkyWater subsidiary, with customer deliveries targeted for 2027.   The platform is designed to scale from 256 qubits toward millions of qubits to support fault-tolerant quantum computing.   Built on standard semiconductor fabrication lines, the design allows IonQ to manufacture systems by the hundreds rather than one at a time.
