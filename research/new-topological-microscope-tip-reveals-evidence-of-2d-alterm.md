A recent study reports the development of a novel topological microscopy tip that has enabled direct observation of two‑dimensional altermagnetism. By scanning thin‑film samples with this advanced probe, researchers detected characteristic spin‑dependent electronic signatures confirming the existence of altermagnetic order in 2D materials. The work demonstrates a new experimental approach for probing exotic magnetic states at the nanoscale.
