QROM is an algorithmic subroutine for loading classical data onto a quantum computer, and constitutes a major bottleneck for quantum applications.   Xanadu researchers developed a new QROM implementation that reduces expensive Toffoli gate counts by approximately twofold by replacing qubit "swapping" with "copying" and consolidating data-unloading steps.   The improvement is already integrated into PennyLane, Xanadu's open-source quantum computing platform, and available to its users today.
