I'll search for information about Hertz's experimental validation of Maxwell's electromagnetic wave theory. Between 1886 and 1889, Hertz conducted experiments proving Maxwell's predicted electromagnetic waves by measuring their velocity and demonstrating it equaled the speed of light.   By generating sparks with a spark gap and detecting them at a distance using metal spheres and a Leyden jar, Hertz confirmed electromagnetic waves travel through space without a medium.   He further demonstrated the waves could be refracted, reflected, and polarized like light, establishing the connection between electromagnetism and optics.
