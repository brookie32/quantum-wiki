Researchers reported the first direct observation of isolated hopfions in a magnetic material created using laser pulses, with their findings published in Nature Physics.   Isolated magnetic hopfions—three-dimensional topological solitons with closed-loop spin textures—were directly observed after nucleation by femtosecond laser pulses in a magnetic material, with their structure and stability confirmed through quantitative imaging and micromagnetic simulations.   This achievement represents an important milestone since hopfions were previously observed only in composite configurations, and may open new possibilities for the development of magnetic technologies.
