Industries from logistics and energy to AI and cybersecurity are beginning to explore how quantum computing capabilities could solve complex problems that classical computers struggle with.   Quantum computers are expected to be useful in various applications ranging from scientific research to cybersecurity, financial optimization, and drug discovery.   While significant publicity hype exists around quantum advantage, current state-of-the-art has primarily demonstrated quantum advantage on computational problems of no practical value, with experiments performed by the research community.
