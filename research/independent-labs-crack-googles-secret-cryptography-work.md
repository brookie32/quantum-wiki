In March 2026, Seattle-based startup Eigen Labs used crowdsourcing and AI agent swarms to match and surpass Google's hidden quantum cryptography research results in just three days.   Google had kept its research on elliptic curve cryptography (ECC) secret due to security risks, using a zero-knowledge proof approach, but the startup independently replicated the findings on quantum attacks against encryption protecting cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin and Ethereum.  This incident highlighted the challenge of responsible disclosure in quantum cryptography research and accelerated awareness of the need for post-quantum cryptography migration.
