Quantum computing requires mathematical frameworks of linear algebra, probability, and complex numbers, which differ fundamentally from the Boolean algebra used in classical computing.   While the fundamental mathematics of gate-based quantum computing can be represented classically, quantum systems offer computational advantages through superposition and entanglement that classical approaches cannot access.   Some researchers argue that quantum computing is a generalization of classical computing rather than an entirely separate field.
