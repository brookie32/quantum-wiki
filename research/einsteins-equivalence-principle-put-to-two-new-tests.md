A cold‑atom interferometer on the China Space Station performed the most precise quantum test of Einstein’s weak equivalence principle (WEP), achieving an uncertainty of 2.8 × 10⁻⁸ and a normalized result of –2.7 × 10⁻⁷. A separate laboratory experiment involving atoms in freefall, carried out by teams in Israel, Germany and the UK, measured the quantum phase shift and found it consistent with WEP, confirming universality of freefall within the tested regime. The WEP—asserting equality of inertial and gravitational mass—is a cornerstone of general relativity; precise tests are essential for detecting potential violations that could signal new physics or a reconciliation with quantum mechanics.
