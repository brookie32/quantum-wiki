Alice & Bob researchers estimate that running Shor's algorithm against RSA-2048 would require 22,593 physical cat qubits and 3.14 days on fault-tolerant superconducting quantum hardware.   This represents 40 times fewer physical qubits than Google Quantum AI's Craig Gidney 2025 estimate.   The efficiency gains come from Alice & Bob's cat qubits, which suppress bit-flip errors at the hardware level so error correction only needs to handle phase-flips, sharply cutting the number of physical qubits per logical qubit.
