Researchers at the University of Utah developed a quantum mechanics-based AI/ML method that improves cancer outcome prediction and identifies treatment targets using comprehensive molecular data from individual patients.   The approach uses algorithms called multitensor comparative spectral decompositions, built on quantum mechanical concepts of entanglement and superposition, to separate patient molecular data into outcome-predicting patterns similar to a prism splitting light.   When demonstrated on neuroblastoma cases, the algorithms discovered new predictors of patient survival that outperformed standard biomarkers.
