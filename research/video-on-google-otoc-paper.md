Google Quantum AI announced a breakthrough demonstrating the first-ever verifiable quantum advantage using the out-of-time-order correlator (OTOC) algorithm, termed Quantum Echoes.   The experiment, published in Nature, ran on the Willow quantum processor and achieved a 13,000× speed advantage over classical supercomputers for this task.   The Quantum Echoes algorithm uses a time-reversal protocol to send a signal into a quantum system, perturb one qubit, and then reverse the signal's evolution to listen for an echo amplified by constructive interference, enabling access to complex quantum correlations.
