 The Qubit Factory is an engineering-style puzzle game based on a gamified quantum circuit simulator designed to provide an introduction to qubits and quantum computing .  The game presents increasingly difficult tasks requiring users to construct quantum circuits, covering fundamentals like qubits and gates as well as advanced algorithms including quantum teleportation and error correction .  Gamification of quantum theory provides new inroads into the subject by allowing users to experience simulated worlds that manifest obvious quantum behaviors .
