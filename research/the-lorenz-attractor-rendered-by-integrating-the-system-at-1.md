The Lorenz attractor is a chaotic dynamical system visualized by numerically integrating differential equations with specific parameter values (σ=10, ρ=28, β=8/3), which produces the characteristic butterfly-shaped trajectory. This visualization demonstrates how sensitive dependence on initial conditions creates a complex, deterministic pattern that never repeats, exemplifying chaos theory. The Lorenz system, derived from atmospheric convection models, is fundamental to understanding nonlinear dynamics and represents one of the most iconic examples of chaos in physics and mathematics.
