IBM's Heron R2 quantum processor features tunable couplers enabling qubits to interact with adjustable strength, resulting in higher-fidelity entanglement and fewer errors during logic gate operations.   The R2 revision increased qubit count from 133 to 156 qubits while refining the tunable coupler design for improved two-qubit gate operations.   The processor achieves typical two-qubit gate error rates below 10^-3, demonstrating significant improvements in entangling operation reliability.
