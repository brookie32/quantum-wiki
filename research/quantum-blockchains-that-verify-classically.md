I'll search for information about this Reddit discussion on quantum blockchains with classical verification. Quantum blockchains with classical verification store ledgers in classical memory while using quantum computers or quantum state information for verification, with each block containing quantum state identifiers and rotations that can be verified by systems capable of analyzing quantum states, including classical computers that emulate quantum computers.   This approach addresses threats from quantum computing algorithms like Shor and Grover that compromise classical cryptography by leveraging quantum technologies for blockchain implementation rather than relying solely on quantum-resistant classical cryptography.
