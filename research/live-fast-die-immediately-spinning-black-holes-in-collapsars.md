Collapsars are massive stars whose cores promptly collapse into black holes and can power long-duration gamma-ray bursts via relativistic jets.   Recent simulations show that collapsar jets powered by neutrino-cooled disks rapidly spin down their black holes to an equilibrium spin of approximately 0.13, consistent with gravitational wave observations and producing jets 4-16 times more powerful than previous models.   For typical long-duration gamma-ray burst timescales exceeding 30 seconds, black holes reach this low spin state, though shorter or lower accretion rate events can leave behind more rapidly spinning black holes.
