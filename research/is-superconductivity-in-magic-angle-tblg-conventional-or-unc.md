I'll search for information about superconductivity in magic-angle twisted bilayer graphene to provide you with an accurate summary. Magic-angle twisted bilayer graphene (MATBG) exhibits superconductivity with a pairing mechanism potentially distinct from conventional Bardeen–Cooper–Schrieffer (BCS) theory , though the debate remains unresolved.  Scanning tunneling microscopy measurements show tunneling spectra inconsistent with conventional s-wave superconductivity, resembling instead a nodal superconductor with an anisotropic pairing mechanism . However,  recent evidence indicates the response is inconsistent with conventional phonon-mediated pairing and supports an unconventional mechanism driven by electron–electron interactions .
