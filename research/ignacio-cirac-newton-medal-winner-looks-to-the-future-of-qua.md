Ignacio Cirac, a theoretical physicist at the Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics, received the 2026 Isaac Newton Medal for pioneering contributions to modern quantum information science and scientific leadership in quantum physics and technology worldwide.   He is best known for co-developing the "Cirac-Zoller" proposal for building quantum computers using trapped ions, which provided an experimentally plausible route from theory to practical quantum computing platforms.   Cirac also demonstrated how controlled quantum many-body systems can act as simulators to solve difficult problems in physics, chemistry, and materials science.
