Physicists have synthesized the first quantum material that functions at room temperature, marking a milestone in solid‑state physics. This breakthrough demonstrates that quantum coherence and related phenomena can be maintained without the need for cryogenic cooling, opening possibilities for practical quantum technologies such as low‑loss electronics, sensors, and components for quantum computing. The discovery also provides a new platform for exploring quantum phase transitions and exotic states of matter under everyday conditions.
