Magnets are usually associated with a familiar property: they produce a magnetic field because the magnetic moments of their electrons align. But nature allows much more unusual forms of magnetic order. An international research team has now identified compelling experimental evidence for p-wave magnetism in europium silicide (EuSi), a chemically simple metallic compound.
