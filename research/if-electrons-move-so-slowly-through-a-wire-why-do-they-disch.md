Although individual electrons drift through a wire very slowly, electromagnetic effects between electrons propagate through the wire much more rapidly, at speeds close to the speed of light.   Electrons interact through electromagnetic fields rather than direct contact, allowing an electron's field to push other electrons down the wire long before the electron physically reaches them.
