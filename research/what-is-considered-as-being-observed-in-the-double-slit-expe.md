I'll search for information about this discussion to provide you with an accurate summary. "Observation" or "measurement" in the double-slit experiment refers to any physical interaction that determines which slit a particle passes through, which unavoidably alters the quantum system's state.   If one measures which slit each particle passes through, the interference pattern never appears.   When photon detectors are placed at the slits, photons are recorded going through either one or the other slit, and the interference pattern vanishes.
