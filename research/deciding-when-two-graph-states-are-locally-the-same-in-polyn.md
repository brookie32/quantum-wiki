Local-unitary (LU) equivalence determines whether two quantum states differ only by independent basis changes on their qubits, a problem that remained computationally open for graph states for over a decade.   A deterministic algorithm now solves this problem in polynomial time—approximately Õ(n^6.38) bit operations for n qubits—and constructs the exact single-qubit unitaries when states are equivalent.
