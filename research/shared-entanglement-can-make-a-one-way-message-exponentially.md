A family of total Boolean functions can be computed with O(log n) bits of one-way classical communication given prior entanglement, but require Ω(n^(1/3)) qubits of one-way quantum communication without entanglement .  With shared entanglement established before inputs are known, a short classical message enables Bob to answer correctly with high probability, whereas without prior entanglement Alice must send an exponentially longer message to achieve the same accuracy . This demonstrates an exponential separation between entanglement-assisted and unassisted quantum communication protocols for total functions.
