I'll search for information about this quantum computing paper. DF-SQD is a hybrid quantum-classical algorithm that derives deterministic auxiliary-field circuits from selected double-factorization leaves of the two-electron tensor.   The circuits generate structured determinant proposals derived from the interaction tensor, with the original Hamiltonian determining which proposals are useful.   This approach enables shallow, number-preserving circuits while preserving a conventional variational interpretation for the final selected-CI energy.
