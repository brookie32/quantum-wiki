I'll search for information about this Reddit post to provide an accurate summary. The three-body problem describes a system containing three bodies that exert gravitational forces on one another.   When a third body is added to a two-body system, things become complicated as the third body attracts the two orbiting each other, pulling them out of their predictable paths.   There is no known general closed-form solution, so numerical methods must be used to solve the equations of motion.
