Quantum Odyssey is a decade-long project developed by scientists behind "Quantum Literacy" research that uses visual programming through an interactive puzzle game to teach quantum computing concepts.   The game allows users to design and optimize real quantum algorithms by solving 500+ optimization challenges without requiring coding knowledge.   It teaches counterintuitive quantum thinking through an immersive, interactive sandbox environment.
