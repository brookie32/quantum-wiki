John Preskill writes a letter to prospective PhD students in 2026, reflecting on how the rise of large language models and other AI tools reshapes graduate training. He argues that while applied AI skills have become more valuable, rigorous research training—especially in theoretical fields like quantum information—is still essential for pushing scientific boundaries and cultivating innovative thinkers. Preskill encourages students to balance deep technical study with interdisciplinary exposure, ensuring they remain adaptable in an era dominated by machine‑learning breakthroughs.
