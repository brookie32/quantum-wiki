Gluon string‑breaking—a process where increasing color‑field energy creates a quark–antiquark pair—has been simulated on a trapped‑ion quantum computer developed at Duke University. The ion‑trap results agree with previous simulations performed using neutral‑atom and superconducting qubit platforms, but indicate that standard models of string breaking may require minor adjustments. This experiment demonstrates the feasibility of simulating nonperturbative QCD dynamics on near‑term quantum hardware.
