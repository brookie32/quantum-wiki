The largest-ever survey of physicists from around the world reveals a distinct lack of consensus across many of physics's most important questions, from black holes and dark matter to quantum gravity.   Even the standard model of cosmology (ΛCDM) did not attain majority support among physicists surveyed.   This surprising outcome may be due to Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) results suggesting dark energy may change over time, contrary to the standard model's assumption that dark energy remains constant.
