A qudit is the generalized unit of quantum information described by a superposition of states, where the number of states d is an integer equal to or greater than two.   Security can be increased by codifying in higher dimensional Hilbert spaces using qudits rather than qubits.   Higher dimensional qudit systems offer larger data capacity and better noise resilience than lower dimensional systems.
