Physicists have discovered a universal "speed limit" on the time quantum information takes to spread through larger systems.   Research published in Physical Review Letters proves this minimum time is linked to a system's entropy and temperature, with implications for understanding quantum information dynamics across physical settings.   This information-sharing process, called "scrambling," spreads information from individual particles across the entire system.
