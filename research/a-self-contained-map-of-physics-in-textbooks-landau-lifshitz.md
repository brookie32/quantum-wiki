The Landau-Lifshitz Course of Theoretical Physics is a ten-volume series initiated by Lev Landau and written in collaboration with Evgeny Lifshitz starting in the late 1930s , covering mechanics, classical and quantum field theory, statistical physics, fluid mechanics, elasticity, electrodynamics, and physical kinetics.  The series was written as a concise, systematic compendium for ambitious students and researchers expected to master wide swathes of theory independently .
