Arqit Quantum agreed to pay $7 million to settle a lawsuit alleging misrepresentations about its quantum encryption technology and revenue sources, resolving roughly four years of legal dispute.   Eligible investors who held Centricus Acquisition Corp. shares as of July 2021 or purchased Arqit securities between September 2021 and December 2022 may file claims for their share of the settlement.   The claim deadline for the settlement was June 22, 2026.
