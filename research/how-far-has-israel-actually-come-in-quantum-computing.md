I'll search for information about Israel's quantum computing progress. Israeli quantum companies raised nearly $500 million in 2025, ranking Israel fourth worldwide in private quantum investment , with  quantum computing companies growing from 1 to more than 10 within six years .  The Israeli Quantum Computing Center (IQCC) provides access to multiple quantum-computing modalities, supporting algorithm testing and workforce development .  Israel is planning a national quantum computing R&D center to help domestic companies integrate, test and benchmark quantum hardware and software components , with  useful quantum computing applications expected to emerge before the end of the decade .
