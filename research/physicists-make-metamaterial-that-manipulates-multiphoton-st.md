Researchers demonstrated that plasmonic metacrystals made from gold nanoantennas can manipulate the statistical properties of multiphoton light while preserving allowed quantum states as they propagate through the material.   The approach could provide a platform for controlling many-body quantum systems, with potential applications in photonic quantum computing, quantum communications, and energy-harvesting technologies.
