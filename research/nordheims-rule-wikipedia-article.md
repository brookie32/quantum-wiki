I'll search for information about Nordheim's rule to provide you with an accurate summary. W.L. Nordheim in 1950 gave a rule to calculate the most probable value of spin in odd-odd nuclei .  The rule states that the two odd nucleons tend to be in a triplet state, aligning their spins as far as possible in jj coupling .  The rule has strong and weak variants, referred to as Nordheim's strong rule and Nordheim's weak rule .
