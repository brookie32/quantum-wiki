Quantum computing could exponentially accelerate data processing and disrupt traditional data center architectures within a decade.   Current data centers will need to redesign their infrastructure significantly to accommodate quantum computers, which require highly specialized and controlled environments with near absolute zero temperatures and minimal electromagnetic interference.   Quantum integration could potentially lower the energy needs of power-hungry facilities while also reducing workloads needed for training AI.
