Researchers have used three‑dimensional structured light beams to manipulate the trajectories of electrons in a vacuum, enabling access to electronic quantum states that were previously unattainable experimentally. By shaping the spatial phase and amplitude of the light field, they can impart controlled angular momentum and energy to individual electrons, allowing precise control over their motion at sub‑wavelength scales. This technique opens new avenues for studying fundamental quantum dynamics and developing advanced electron‑based technologies.
