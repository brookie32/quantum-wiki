Intel acquired Israeli quantum computing software developer Classiq in a deal valued at $2.3 billion in cash and stock, marking the largest acquisition in the history of the global quantum computing industry.   Classiq develops software designed to make quantum computers easier to program and operate, focusing on software connecting complex quantum systems with businesses and researchers seeking to use them.   The deal, expected to close in the first half of 2027, combines Classiq's automated circuit synthesis engine with Intel's silicon spin-qubit manufacturing.
