The Gravity Research Foundation announced the 2026 winners of its essay competition on gravitation, with the $4,000 first prize awarded to "The Gravitational Spectral Radio Forest: A Signature of Primordial Black Holes" by Christopher, Hari, and Shankaranarayanan from IIT Bombay, and other prizes awarded to essays on topics including entanglement and gravitons.   The five award-winning essays will be published in the October 2026 special issue of the International Journal of Modern Physics D.
