Sabrina Pasterski is a theoretical physicist researching celestial holography, which attempts to map the 4‑dimensional universe onto a flat surface so that general relativity and quantum mechanics can be unified into a single framework for gravity. Her work seeks connections between this theory and astronomical observations while addressing how artificial intelligence might accelerate discoveries in physics. Pasterski has been hailed as a prodigious young scientist—often likened to Einstein—but she remains mindful of the limits of such comparisons.
