I'll search for information about this Reddit discussion on Lenz's Law. Lenz's law states that the direction of induced current in a conductor is such that its magnetic field opposes changes in the initial magnetic field.   The law is contained in Faraday's law of induction, expressed by the negative sign showing that induced current opposes the changing flux that causes it.   Lenz's law is based on the law of conservation of energy.
