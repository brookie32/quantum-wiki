Energy can never disappear nor appear —it only transforms from one form to another, a principle known as the law of conservation of energy. When energy appears to vanish, such as when a ball stops bouncing or a light turns off,  the energy converts from potential to kinetic to frictional energy, or heat . In most everyday situations,  most forms of energy are eventually dissipated as heat , though special cases like an expanding universe involve more complex energy dynamics.
