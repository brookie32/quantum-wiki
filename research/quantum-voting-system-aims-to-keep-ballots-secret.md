Quantum-enabled voting protocols that use entangled qubits have been experimentally demonstrated by teams at the Barcelona Institute of Technology and the University of Geneva, providing an intrinsic guarantee of ballot secrecy independent of classical security assumptions.  These systems implement a protocol first proposed by Federico Centrone and colleagues in 2022, where quantum state manipulation ensures each voter’s choice is anonymously recorded while preventing tampering during counting.  Earlier e‑voting proposals, such as the parity‑based scheme by Broadbent and Tapp (2007), also sought anonymity but relied on classical round‑by‑round techniques rather than quantum mechanics.
