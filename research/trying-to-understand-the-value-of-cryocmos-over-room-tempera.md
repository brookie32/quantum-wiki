CryoCMOS operates classical CMOS circuits at deep-cryogenic temperatures to ensure physical proximity to quantum bits, reducing thermal gradients and increasing compactness .  Co-locating CMOS control circuitry at cryogenic temperatures near the qubits helps ensure synchronized, low-latency control while minimizing heat transfer and preserving quantum coherence .  CryoCMOS offers increased operational speed through improved carrier transport and reduced interconnect resistance compared to room temperature alternatives .
