This perspective reviews recent progress on three old yet unsolved problems in supercooled liquids and glasses, emphasizing different approaches between physics and chemistry.   The paper examines growing length scales during supercooling, the possibility of a thermodynamic transition to an ideal glass state, and universal relaxation functions describing molecular motion in deeply supercooled liquids.
