Physicists usually regard time as having only one dimension, unlike space where theories such as string theory allow multiple dimensions. Recent work by Itzhak Bars and others proposes that a second temporal dimension could exist, potentially resolving anomalies in black‑hole physics and quantum mechanics. The idea remains speculative but has gained interest because it offers a novel approach to longstanding theoretical problems.
