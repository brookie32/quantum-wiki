The LHC's 2026 lead-ion collision run marks the first heavy-ion operation in 5 years, featuring lead nuclei colliding at record energies of 6.8 TeV , with  three weeks of operations scheduled following nine weeks of proton collisions .  The primary physics goal is to study quark-gluon plasma, a state of matter believed to have filled the early Universe and recreated in heavy-ion collisions .  The ALICE experiment now features upgraded data processing capable of recording 100 times more collisions per second than previous runs .
