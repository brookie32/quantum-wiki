This entry describes an asymmetric pendulum experiment featuring magnets that demonstrates the physics of acoustic and vibrational forces in aircraft. The pendulum swings rapidly in one direction and slowly in the other, with the directional force difference arising from low pressure area collapse during the fast swing cycle. The experiment illustrates principles of how asymmetric motion can generate net forces, relevant to understanding vibration-based propulsion or aerodynamic phenomena.
