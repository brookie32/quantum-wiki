**Summary**

Researchers have achieved quantum logic operations that are up to 1,000 times faster than previous benchmarks, substantially reducing the impact of noise and decoherence on qubit performance. This speed improvement enhances circuit reliability, bringing fault‑tolerant, large‑scale quantum computers a measurable step closer to practical deployment. The advancement is expected to lower error rates and enable more complex quantum algorithms within coherence limits.
