Combinatorial optimization problems are considered promising applications for quantum computers, as these problems are recurrent in industrial settings and generally difficult for classical computing hardware.   Common quantum approaches include the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) and the Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE).   However, the heuristic nature of these algorithms poses challenges to predicting practical performance, and current systems face implementation constraints that limit their applicability to certain optimization problems.
