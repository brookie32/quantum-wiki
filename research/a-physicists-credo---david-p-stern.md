"A Physicist's Credo" by David P. Stern is a guide emphasizing the importance of keeping detailed notes of ideas, lectures, and work, as "memory fades but what is written down stays yours" and organized notes become essential for continued productivity in middle age.   The piece advises transcribing rough notes promptly, editing and illustrating material clearly, and maintaining an organized numbered and dated notebook.   Stern, who studied theoretical aspects of Earth's magnetosphere at NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center until his 2001 retirement, offers practical wisdom drawn from his scientific career.
