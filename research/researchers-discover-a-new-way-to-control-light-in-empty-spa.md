Researchers have discovered a hidden topological property of light that enables it to naturally develop chiral behavior—spinning and twisting—as it travels freely through empty space.   This breakthrough, involving researchers from the UK and South Africa, shows that light can be "programed" by exploiting its natural geometry.   The discovery could ultimately lead to applications where light carries information, probes biology, manipulates matter and protects quantum signals.
