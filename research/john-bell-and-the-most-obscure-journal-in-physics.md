John Bell published his landmark 1964 paper proving that no local hidden‑variable theory can reproduce quantum mechanics in the short‑lived journal *Physics Physique Физика*, which operated from 1964 to 1968 and was founded by physicists Bernd Matthias and Philip Anderson.  Bell’s theorem, established while he was at CERN, demonstrated that quantum mechanical predictions necessarily involve non‑local correlations, fundamentally challenging local realism.  The paper’s impact far exceeded the modest reach of the journal that carried it.
