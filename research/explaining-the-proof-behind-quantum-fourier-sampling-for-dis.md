Quantum Fourier sampling is a fundamental technique that provides exponential speedups in quantum algorithms, successfully applied to extract discrete logarithms and solve other number-theoretic problems in abelian groups.   Quantum computers can break RSA, El Gamal, and elliptic curve cryptosystems by efficiently factoring integers and extracting discrete logarithms , making understanding the proofs behind these quantum algorithms critical for post-quantum cryptography development.  The quantum Fourier transform enables efficient extraction of periodic function properties, which is essential for computing discrete logarithms .
