The article outlines a site’s bot‑access policy, emphasizing that automated crawlers must contact the administrators to request whitelisting and that properly identified bots may be granted access without the typical verification process. It stresses compliance with HTTP 429 (Too Many Requests) response headers and the implementation of rate‑limiting to avoid blocking. Users experiencing issues are directed to check their connection and use the provided contact form for support inquiries.
