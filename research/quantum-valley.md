Waterloo, Canada, nicknamed Quantum Valley, has built one of the world's most advanced quantum ecosystems, home to institutions like the Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics and the Institute for Quantum Computing.  The term "Quantum Valley" also refers to other regional quantum technology hubs globally, such as  Amaravati Quantum Valley, a planned quantum technology park in Andhra Pradesh, India dedicated to innovations in quantum computing and technologies.
