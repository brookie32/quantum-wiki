Researchers from Cleveland Clinic and IBM used quantum computing to demonstrate a hybrid quantum-classical workflow to approximate the electronic structure of a protein for the first time.   The team modeled the 303-atom miniprotein Trp-cage using a quantum-centric supercomputing workflow and an IBM Quantum Heron r2.   The approach could support pharmaceutical research and molecular simulations using quantum-centric supercomputing.
