This discussion explores the conceptual paradox in Big Bang cosmology: if time began with the Big Bang, how can we meaningfully say the universe "began" without already presupposing a temporal framework? The post examines the tension between modern physics descriptions of time's emergence and the linguistic/philosophical assumptions embedded in terms like "beginning." Physicists typically address this by distinguishing between physical time as a measurable dimension within the universe versus metaphysical questions about causation or existence prior to the Big Bang.
