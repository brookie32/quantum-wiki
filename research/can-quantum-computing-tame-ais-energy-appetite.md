Quantum computing could be a key solution to reducing AI's rising energy consumption, as quantum computers can explore complex problem landscapes more efficiently than traditional systems.   While quantum computing shows significant potential for energy efficiency, its benefits must be weighed against the substantial power demands of maintaining quantum systems, including the cooling infrastructure required for most current architectures.   Quantum-inspired and quantum-based optimization is being explored to help future AI systems achieve equal or better performance with fewer operations and less energy, though whether quantum will significantly accelerate training of frontier AI models remains an open question.
