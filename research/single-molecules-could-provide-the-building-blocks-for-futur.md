Single molecules are emerging as promising components for future quantum networks due to their stable, tunable photon emission and compatibility with photonic integration schemes. Their deterministic behavior at cryogenic temperatures enables efficient entanglement generation across remote nodes, a key requirement for scalable quantum communication. Ongoing research focuses on optimizing molecular photophysics and improving coupling efficiencies to practical photonic platforms.
