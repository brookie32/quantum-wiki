QuantWare announced VIO-40K, a quantum processor architecture enabling 10,000-qubit QPUs that are 100x larger than current industry offerings while fitting in a smaller physical package.   The technology uses a 3D architecture with chiplet modules connected via ultra-high-fidelity links to eliminate signal routing constraints of traditional 2D designs, delivering exponentially more compute per dollar and watt.   Designed as an open scaling standard for all superconducting qubit designs, the company is building Kilofab, an industrial-scale fabrication facility in Delft scheduled to open in 2026, with first VIO-40K devices expected in 2028.
