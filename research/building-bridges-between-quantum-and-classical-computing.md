Physics World Weekly interviewed Yonatan Cohen, CTO and co‑founder of Quantum Machines—a 2018 start‑up from Israel’s Weizmann Institute that develops hybrid quantum–classical control systems to enable scalable qubit architectures. The company supports a broad spectrum of qubits—including superconducting circuits, trapped ions, neutral atoms, spins, and NV centres—and collaborates with firms such as Rigetti Computing and Diraq while offering career guidance for physicists entering the quantum industry.
