I'll search for information about the exchange particle of magnetism to provide an accurate summary. Virtual photons are the exchange particles for the electromagnetic interaction.   The magnetic field is a classical approximation to the photon-exchange picture, and electric and magnetic fields are made of the same "stuff" (photons).   Static electric and magnetic fields are made of "virtual" photons whose energy and momentum don't satisfy the relationship for "real" photons, and these virtual photons are constantly emitted and reabsorbed.
