The problem of determining whether a matrix can be implemented using at most K CNOT gates on qubits with all-to-all connectivity is NP-complete.   The research constructs specific upper-unitriangular matrices whose CNOT complexity relates to the minimum vertex-cover size of a graph.   The proof also demonstrates NP-hardness of approximation and APX-hardness for CNOT-circuit optimization via reduction from Minimum Vertex Cover.
