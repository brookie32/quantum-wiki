Microwave-to-optical quantum transduction is critical for superconducting quantum computing, as interconnecting dilution refrigerators is necessary for large-scale quantum computers with fault-tolerance.   Multiple transduction methods have been experimentally demonstrated, including optomechanical, electro-optic, magneto-optic, and atomic ensemble approaches.   Recent progress includes achieving below one photon of input-referred added noise and upconversion rates two orders of magnitude beyond prior state-of-the-art.
