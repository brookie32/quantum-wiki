This work presents a vectorized adaptation of the TK95 algorithm for generating realistic laser phase noise in quantum system simulations, which stochastically samples power spectra typical for real lasers.   The approach moves beyond simplified power-law approximations by using measured frequency and phase power spectra from actual diode lasers to generate discrete noise signals for improved simulation accuracy.  The technique is particularly relevant for quantum computing platforms like neutral atom arrays and trapped-ion systems where laser phase noise significantly impacts system performance.
