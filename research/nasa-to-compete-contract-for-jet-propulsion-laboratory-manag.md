NASA announced plans to compete the next contract for managing and operating its Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) in Southern California, to ensure continued accountability and strong value for U.S. taxpayers.   Caltech has managed the laboratory since its inception in the 1930s, with previous NASA contracts awarded solely to the university since the facility transferred to NASA in 1958.   The competition aims to evaluate alternative management approaches, as the rapid growth of the U.S. space economy indicates a viable competitive market may now exist for JPL's operations.
