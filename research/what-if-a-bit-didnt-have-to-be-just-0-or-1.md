Quantum computing uses qubits that can represent 0, 1, or both simultaneously through superposition, enabling exponential growth in processing power.   This superposition allows qubits to encode exponentially more information than classical bits.   However, external disturbances like stray fields or temperature fluctuations can collapse qubits into definite 0 or 1 states, limiting quantum advantage.
