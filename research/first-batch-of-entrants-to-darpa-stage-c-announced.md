DARPA announced that Atom Computing, Diraq, IBM and IonQ have advanced to the final phase of its Quantum Benchmarking Initiative, moving their proposed systems into hands-on government verification and validation.   The four organizations represent different modalities of quantum computing: neutral atoms at Atom Computing, silicon CMOS spin qubits at Diraq, superconducting processors at IBM and trapped ions at IonQ.   They join Microsoft and PsiQuantum, which previously reached the equivalent final phase through QBI's predecessor, Underexplored Systems for Utility-Scale Quantum Computing (US2QC).
