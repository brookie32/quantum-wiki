A web-based Einstein-Maxwell system simulator is an interactive tool for visualizing and computing the coupled dynamics of gravitational and electromagnetic fields in general relativity. The Einstein-Maxwell system describes how spacetime geometry is curved by electromagnetic energy, while the electromagnetic field propagates according to Maxwell's equations in curved spacetime. Such a simulator would allow users to explore various physical scenarios involving charged particles or electromagnetic fields in relativistic spacetimes without requiring specialized computational software.
