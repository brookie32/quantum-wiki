IBM claims it has "the most viable path to realize fault-tolerant quantum computing" and is confident it will deliver a useful, large-scale quantum computer by 2029.   The company has publicly committed to a deadline – a 200-logical-qubit fault-tolerant quantum computer by 2029 – and backed it up with a detailed roadmap of intermediate milestones and research results.   IBM's Jay Gambetta stated that "the science has been de-risked… now it comes down to engineering and building it," emphasizing that remaining challenges are about scale-up, integration, and reliability.
