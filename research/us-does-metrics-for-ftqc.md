The U.S. Department of Energy issued a formal Request for Information for a fault-tolerant quantum computing system by 2028, with key requirements including a minimum of 150 to 250 logical qubits and a computationally universal instruction set.   The DOE is seeking companies' technical approaches including system architecture, quantum error correction codes, logical gate sets, and broader software and physical site requirements.
