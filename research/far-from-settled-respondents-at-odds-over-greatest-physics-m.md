A large-scale physics survey found respondents divided on most fundamental mysteries, with even established theoretical answers receiving only minority support.   The interpretation of quantum mechanics remains unsettled a century after development, with Copenhagen interpretation leading at over 35%.   The survey revealed a shift away from traditional cosmology views, with dark matter and dark energy explanations and the standard model of cosmology receiving significantly less support than expected.
