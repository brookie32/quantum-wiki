The U.S. Department of Energy aims to demonstrate fault-tolerant quantum systems by end of 2028 , while  major hardware roadmaps converge on 2029–2030 for first-generation fault-tolerant systems .  Whether the 2028 target holds depends on engineering progress at the edge of what the field currently believes is possible , reflecting ongoing debate about the timeline for practical fault-tolerant quantum computing deployment.
