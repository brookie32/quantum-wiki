I'll search for information about the ICTP-MHPC 2026 program to provide an accurate summary. The ICTP-MHPC is a 12-month Master's program jointly organized by SISSA and ICTP, currently accepting applications for its 12th edition, designed to equip students with skills in advanced computing, AI/machine learning, and modern HPC architectures.   The 2025-2026 program starts September 8, 2025.   ICTP provides scholarships for applicants from developing countries with an earlier deadline due to visa requirements.
