I'll search for information about this discussion regarding the gravitational constant. The gravitational constant "Big G" remains the least precisely known fundamental constant, with an uncertainty of about 1 part in 5,000, and conflicting measurements cannot be fully explained by experimental errors.   Over the past 50 years, a dozen precision experiments have produced a spread of different values, and after a 10-year effort, physicists obtained a new measurement that does not resolve the ongoing debate.   The difficulty stems from gravity's extreme weakness, which allows other interfering signals like electromagnetic forces and ground vibration to overwhelm the gravitational signal.
