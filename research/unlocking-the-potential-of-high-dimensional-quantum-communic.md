This study demonstrates a time-frequency entanglement experiment achieving record 5.7 ebits and advancing scalable high-dimensional quantum key distribution .  The research presents a scan-free method to characterize high-dimensional entanglement in the time-frequency domain .  The results pave the way for scalable high-dimensional quantum information processing and robust high-rate quantum communication networks toward the fully deployable quantum internet .
