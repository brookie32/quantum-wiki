Researchers have discovered a method to use superconducting vortices—magnetic flux defects that typically disrupt superconductivity—as controllable quantum bits (qubits) for quantum computing. By harnessing rather than suppressing these vortices, scientists have found a way to convert what was previously considered a problematic phenomenon into a useful quantum resource. This approach could potentially simplify quantum device design by eliminating the need for separate qubit creation mechanisms.
