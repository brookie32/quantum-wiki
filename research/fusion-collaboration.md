Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory (PPPL) leads collaborative efforts with private companies and other public institutions to make fusion power practical, bringing together decades of expertise in plasma research.   The collaboration between PPPL and Tokamak Energy's ST40 facility from 2019 to 2024 yielded findings confirming that energy confinement in spherical tokamaks scales more favorably than conventional designs, a finding that could shape future commercial fusion plants.   Partnerships between facilities with similar tokamaks remain essential to answer critical physics and engineering questions, with NSTX-U expected to resume operations in 2026 as a user facility helping scientists answer key questions about spherical tokamak scalability for fusion pilot plants.
