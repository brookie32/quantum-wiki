Chanda Prescod‑Weinstein is a theoretical physicist and columnist for *New Scientist* who explores particle physics in her recent book, **The Edge of Space‑Time**. The text delves into the counterintuitive aspects of the quantum world while interweaving poetry to illuminate complex ideas. In discussions with the New Scientist Book Club she argues that physics should be valued for its intrinsic beauty rather than solely for economic contributions.
