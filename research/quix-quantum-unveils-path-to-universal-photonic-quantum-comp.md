QuiX Quantum released Dedalo, a photonic quantum computing architecture designed to support universal, fault-tolerant systems based on logical qubits that can operate alongside classical supercomputing infrastructure.   Dedalo focuses on photon-loss-tolerant logical qubits, modular photonic hardware, semiconductor-compatible fabrication and telecom-style interconnects as a path toward scalable photonic quantum computing.   The system is designed to be manufacturable, energy efficient, modular and room-temperature to co-exist alongside supercomputers where real workloads will be developed, tested and executed.
