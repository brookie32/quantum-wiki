ASML's High NA EUV lithography technology can be used to fabricate qubits with greater precision, marking a milestone toward industrial scaling of more reliable quantum computing units.   This advancement is expected to support innovations in quantum computing alongside AI and advanced connectivity technologies.   While High NA EUV is widely expected to play a central role in advancing next-generation logic and memory technologies below 2nm, it may also prove critical for scalable quantum computing hardware production.
