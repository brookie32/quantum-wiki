I'll search for information about this discussion to provide an accurate summary. The electric field inside a resistor is uniform along its length and has the same magnitude everywhere .  The electric field strength is given by E = ΔV/L, where ΔV is the potential difference and L is the length of the resistor .  In circuits with multiple resistors in series, the voltage drops across each resistor proportionally to its resistance, with resistors of the same material acting like sections of wire with potential reduction following their relative lengths .
