A quantum computing chip fabricated at UC Berkeley was selected by Governor Gavin Newsom as one of California's contributions to a time capsule being buried to honor the nation's 250th birthday.   The 10 millimeter-square chip is the first widely distributed quantum processing unit consisting of eight qubits, produced in collaboration with Berkeley Lab under the direction of Berkeley physics Professor Iran Siddiqi.   The time capsule will be sealed in June and buried on July 4 at Independence National Historical Park in Philadelphia, to be unearthed in 2276 on the nation's 500th birthday.
