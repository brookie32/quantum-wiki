NV diamond T1 relaxometry is a quantum sensing technique that uses nitrogen-vacancy centers in diamond to detect magnetic noise at the nanoscale through longitudinal relaxation time measurements.   The all-optical method monitors NV centers' photoluminescence under laser pulses to identify magnetic noise origins and enables nanoscale resolution with applications in biological and chemical measurements.
