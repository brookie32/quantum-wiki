This discussion explores whether routine cine MRI can detect early warning signs of cardiac chaos—such as mechanical alternans and nonlinear strain dynamics—that precede clinical arrhythmias.  Subclinical alterations in myocardial structure and function occur early during disease progression, while clinically overt symptoms occur later with worse outcomes; identifying such subclinical changes is critical for timely diagnosis and management.   Deep learning approaches using cine cardiac MRI show promise for arrhythmia risk prediction , suggesting potential for detecting prechaotic mechanical signatures before overt arrhythmias manifest.
