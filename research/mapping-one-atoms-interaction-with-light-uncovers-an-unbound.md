Researchers mapped how a single atom absorbs and emits light, revealing an extensive, previously unknown network of quantum states that the atom can occupy. By tracking individual photon interactions, they showed that each light pulse can drive the atom into many coupled quantum configurations, creating an unbounded “state machine.” This finding expands understanding of atomic dynamics and could inform future quantum technologies such as precision sensors or quantum computing components.
