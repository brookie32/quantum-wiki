A "Bell telephone" is a proposed method for faster-than-light communication using quantum entanglement and EPR-Bell correlated particles.   However, attempts to use EPR correlations for faster-than-light communication fail fundamentally because quantum measurements yield only probabilistic outcomes that cannot be used to transmit information faster than light.  The post explains the physics preventing such "instantaneous quantum communication" schemes from working despite instantaneous quantum correlations.
