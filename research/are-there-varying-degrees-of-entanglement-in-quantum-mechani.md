I'll search for information about this Reddit discussion on quantum entanglement degrees. Yes, there are definitely degrees of quantum entanglement for pairs of entangled particles , reflecting varying levels of correlation between quantum systems.  The degree of entanglement between two particles constrains the degree to which either can be entangled with a third particle , a principle known as monogamy of entanglement.  Quantifying correlations and establishing a unique characterization of entanglement degree remains extremely challenging , though various mathematical measures have been developed to assess these varying degrees.
