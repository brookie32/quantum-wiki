The "wiring problem" in quantum computing refers to the need to send control signals to each qubit to perform necessary operations , and  as qubit numbers scale up, the required control signals become impractical and physically impossible to wire into a single chip .  The widely accepted solution involves integrating control components directly into the quantum chips , with various architectures like  WISE (Wiring using Integrated Switching Electronics)  and  unique wiring and control architectures that dramatically reduce the number of physical control lines  being developed to address this critical scalability challenge.
