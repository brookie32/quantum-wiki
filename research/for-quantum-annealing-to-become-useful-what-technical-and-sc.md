Quantum annealing advancement requires increased qubit counts and hardware improvements while overcoming challenges with embedding problems and maintaining qubit coherence.   Key technical progress needed includes enhanced error correction, noise reduction techniques, improved qubit fidelity, and sophisticated algorithms that effectively utilize available qubits in the current NISQ era.   At current hardware sizes, useful applications are limited to niche problems well-matched to existing configurations, requiring more advanced hardware for broader utility.
