Professor Catherine Heymans (Astronomer Royal for Scotland) examines the simulation hypothesis—whether our universe could be a perfect virtual reality—by exploring whether all physical phenomena can ultimately be described mathematically, as computers can only process math. She emphasizes that the question is testable through physics: the standard “shut‑up and calculate” approach of quantum mechanics shows how fundamental particles (quarks) behave in ways that may suggest an underlying computational substrate. Her analysis probes whether reality is composed of real-world quarks or virtual-world qubits.
