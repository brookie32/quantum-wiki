Researchers from RIKEN and collaborators achieved high-fidelity control of multiple silicon spin qubits operating in parallel using a five-qubit silicon array controlled through a shared microwave line, identifying and mitigating key error sources.   This maintains high-fidelity control when several qubits operate simultaneously, representing an important step toward scaling up silicon-based quantum processors.   Silicon spin qubits are a promising platform for building scalable quantum computers as they are compatible with semiconductor manufacturing technologies used for computer chips.
