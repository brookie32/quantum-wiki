Non-EU BSc Physics graduates can apply to MSc Aerospace Engineering programs in Germany, though eligibility varies by university and program. Most German universities require applicants to have completed a relevant bachelor's degree, meet language proficiency requirements (typically B2 level German or English-taught programs), and fulfill specific prerequisite coursework in mathematics, physics, and engineering subjects.
