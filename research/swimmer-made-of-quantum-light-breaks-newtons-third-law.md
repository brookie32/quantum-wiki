Scientists at Nankai University engineered a “river” of interacting photons by directing laser beams into an electrically‑polarized crystal, creating a fluid‑like quantum light medium. A second shaped beam—acting as a swimmer—was introduced and was observed to move upstream without producing the equal and opposite reaction normally required by Newton’s third law. The experiment demonstrates that in engineered quantum systems the action‑reaction principle can be broken, opening possibilities for novel quantum light control and devices.
