This topic explores quantum image preparation methods that combine DCT (Discrete Cosine Transform) with amplitude encoding to reduce quantum preparation cost for RGB images .  The approach partitions RGB images into blocks and encodes histogram-based intensity information as amplitudes in quantum states .  The deterministic framework balances reconstruction fidelity against quantum resource requirements and has been tested on real quantum hardware using standard metrics like MSE and PSNR .
