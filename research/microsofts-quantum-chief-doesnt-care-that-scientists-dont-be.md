Microsoft published a Nature paper in February 2025 on its Majorana 1 chip marketed as a topological qubit breakthrough, but physicists including Winfried Hensinger and Henry Legg flagged that the paper lacks definitive proof of the exotic particles the technology depends on.   Microsoft's quantum chief Zulfi Alam believes his team doesn't need to "prove" they engineered a new state of matter , while  physicists have engaged in open argument over whether the company's results hold up, with some warning that overblown claims can undermine public confidence in the field.
