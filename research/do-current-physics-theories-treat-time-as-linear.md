I'll search for information about how current physics theories treat time to provide an accurate summary. Modern physics theories, particularly special relativity, rejected the notion of absolute universal time, instead treating time as local to each observer.   Time moves relative to the observer—an object in motion experiences time dilation, meaning it experiences time more slowly than when at rest.   General relativity further shows that time and space are interconnected as two sides of the same "spacetime" entity rather than separate linear dimensions.
