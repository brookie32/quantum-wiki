A recent proposal argues that many puzzling quantum phenomena—such as the apparent superposition in Schrödinger’s cat experiment—may arise because conventional physics neglects to incorporate past information into its predictive models. By treating the present state as dependent on the full historical record rather than a standalone snapshot, the approach claims it can resolve long‑standing paradoxes and shed light on the true nature of time. Quantum‑information researcher Jacob Biamonte has endorsed this perspective as potentially foundational for physics.
