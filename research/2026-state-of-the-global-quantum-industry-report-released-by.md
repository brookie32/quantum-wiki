The report offers regional overviews of quantum ecosystem development and examines major regional and global trends shaping the quantum industry.   In 2025, the global quantum market reached $1.9 billion, the pure-play quantum workforce grew 14%, and private venture capital investment more than doubled.   The quantum computing market is projected to grow at 30% annually to reach $3 billion by 2028, quantum sensing at 32% annually to $1.1 billion, with the total global quantum technology market expected to exceed $4 billion by 2028.
