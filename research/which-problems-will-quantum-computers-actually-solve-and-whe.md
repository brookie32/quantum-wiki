Quantum computers will solve specific problems including constrained optimization, efficient simulation of chemical reactions and cryptanalysis , though  they will not replace classical computers as quantum effects only offer computational speed-up on certain tasks .  The commercial timeline for quantum advantage is broadly estimated at 5-10 years, contingent on proving algorithms can consistently outperform classical methods on real problems .
