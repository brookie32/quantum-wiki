Theoretical physicist Carlo Rovelli argues we are asking the wrong questions about reality, insisting that a complete understanding of the universe is unattainable and urging a shift away from entrenched assumptions, such as the existence of absolute space. Inspired by transformative art, he emphasizes that all aspects of reality—from atoms to consciousness—are deeply interconnected and require a new language for discussion. Rovelli’s research focuses on loop quantum gravity, which seeks to unify quantum mechanics with Einstein’s general relativity.
