It's possible to learn a lot about quantum mechanics with simple experiments at home , including  the famous double-slit experiment using a laser pointer, card with slits, and a screen , and  demonstrations of superposition using polarizing filters .  Other accessible experiments include observing quantum tunneling by tuning an AM radio to a weak station and walking behind buildings .
