I'll search for information about the LHC shutdown mentioned in this Reddit post. The Large Hadron Collider will begin Long Shutdown 3 (LS3) in July 2026, seven and a half months later than originally planned, with the overall shutdown duration increasing by approximately four months.   During this shutdown, the ATLAS and CMS experiments will replace many of their detectors and electronics, with the delay providing additional time to develop these sophisticated systems.   These measures will shift the start of the High-Luminosity LHC until June 2030.
