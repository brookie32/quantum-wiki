A reproducible JAX reference implementation of Neural Quantum States (NQS) and Variational Monte Carlo (VMC) for 1D quantum systems, with exact solver validation.   The implementation includes multiple quantum systems (harmonic oscillator, double well, Gross-Pitaevskii, two interacting bosons), neural ansätze (MLP, parity-constrained, permutation-symmetric DeepSets), samplers (jitted Metropolis-Hastings), and optimizers (Adam and Stochastic Reconfiguration).   It provides independent exact solvers in pure NumPy/SciPy for validation purposes.
