Researchers have experimentally demonstrated that a quantum switch can induce anomalous heat flow, transferring thermal energy from a colder to a hotter system while remaining consistent with the second law of thermodynamics. This effect is achieved by a finite‑memory “demon” that must erase information, which increases entropy and prevents any violation of thermodynamic principles. The team also used the same quantum switch to build a quantum refrigerator capable of extracting work while cooling—a practical illustration of engineered anomalous heat transfer.
