Recent studies show that advanced language models can solve well‑defined physics problems with up to 94 % accuracy, a performance that rivals or surpasses human experts once benchmark ambiguities are clarified. Because many areas of contemporary physics involve ill‑defined or experimental questions, AI is expected to excel in analytical tasks but may still struggle with the cutting‑edge, fuzzy aspects of the discipline. Mathematicians warn that an influx of AI‑generated proofs could overwhelm repositories such as arXiv and potentially hinder scholarly progress.
