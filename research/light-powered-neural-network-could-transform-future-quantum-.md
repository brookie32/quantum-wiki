Researchers at Wits University have built an optical neural network that reads hidden topology in structured light without first imaging it, a step towards new communications.   Each light pattern can represent a different symbol with no limit to the number of patterns, and because the information exists in a distortion-resistant feature, the approach could enable laser links through turbulent open air.   The technology has potential applications in quantum communications, sensing, and imaging.
