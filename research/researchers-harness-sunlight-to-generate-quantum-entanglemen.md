Researchers at the University of Ottawa and the Max Planck Institute for the Science of Light have shown that a solar concentrator can generate pairs of entangled photons from concentrated sunlight, providing an energy‑efficient alternative to laser‑pumped sources used in optical quantum computing. Published in *Optica*, the study demonstrates that incoherent natural light can be tailored via concentration to perform nonlinear processes normally thought to require high coherence and power density. These findings challenge long‑standing assumptions about the necessary conditions for entangled‑photon generation, opening a new avenue for scalable, low‑energy quantum technologies.
