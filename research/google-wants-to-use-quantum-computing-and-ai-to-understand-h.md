Google launched REPLIQA, a research program applying quantum science and AI with top universities to understand how molecules work inside human bodies.   The initiative by Google Quantum AI and Google.org includes a $10 million commitment to advance research at five leading academic institutions.   Biological processes like protein folding and drug reactions involve complex atomic interactions that classical computers struggle to simulate, while quantum technologies operate using the same quantum mechanics governing molecules.
