The Quantum Economic Development Consortium released its 2026 State of the Global Quantum Industry report in April 2026, showing the global quantum technology industry made significant strides toward maturity in 2025 with revenues on track to double by 2028.   In 2025, the global quantum market reached $1.9 billion, the global pure-play quantum workforce grew 14%, and private venture capital investment more than doubled.   The report offers regional overviews of quantum ecosystem development and examines major regional and global trends, with particular focus on pure-play companies, workforce, investment, enabling policies, partnerships, and infrastructure.
