QC Ware and IonQ collaborated to evaluate a quantum computing workflow designed for drug‑discovery tasks. The partnership tested the feasibility of using IonQ’s trapped‑ion quantum processors, run via QC Ware’s software stack, to model complex molecular interactions more efficiently than classical methods. This pilot demonstrates a concrete step toward leveraging quantum technology in pharmaceutical research.
