Researchers from IBM Quantum, RIKEN, and NVIDIA have achieved a 79-fold improvement in energy accuracy for quantum-classical simulations of iron-sulfur clusters. This was done by initializing Sample-Based Quantum Diagonalization (SQD) with broken-symmetry unrestricted Hartree-Fock (BS-UHF) reference states, bringing ground-state energy estimates for a 72-qubit [Fe4S4(SCH3)4]2− active space within 4.3 millihartree of high-precision classical benchmarks. The study highlights the importance of reference-state preparation in SQD accuracy.
