arXiv:2611.22108v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Stabilizer-based subspace methods can substantially reduce the quantum resources required for electronic structure calculations, but the resulting projection neglects Hamiltonian couplings to configurations outside the retained subspace. In this work, we introduce Stabilizer-Subspace Perturbation Theory (SSPT) to recover the associated correlation contributions, using the Contextual Subspace Variational Quantum Eigensolver (CS-VQE) as a representative application. The central idea is to exploit the Pauli terms eliminated by stabilizer projection as perturbative operators that generate an external manifold from the optimized reference state. Their energetic contributions are evaluated using an Epstein-Nesbet-type second-order correction or a resolvent-based formulation that retains couplings within the external space. By exploiting the factorized structure of the stabilizer-constrained reference state, the required matrix elements can be evaluated through Pauli expectation values on the reduced contextual register without explicitly reconstructing the full-space wave function. Molecular benchmarks demonstrate substantial reductions in projection-induced energy errors, allowing chemical precision to be achieved with fewer contextual qubits in several cases. These results establish stabilizer-guided perturbation theory as a promising strategy for improving the accuracy of resource-efficient quantum chemistry calculations.
