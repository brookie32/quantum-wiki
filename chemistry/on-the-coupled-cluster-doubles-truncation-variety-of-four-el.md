arXiv:2602.16580v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We extend recent algebro-geometric results for coupled cluster theory of quantum many-body systems to the truncation varieties arising from the doubles approximation (CCD), focusing on the first genuinely nonlinear doubles regime of four electrons. Since this doubles truncation variety does not coincide with previously studied varieties, we initiate a systematic investigation of its basic algebro-geometric invariants. We show that for 4 electrons in n geq 13 orbitals, the CCD truncation variety is not a complete intersection, and we present numerical and symbolic evidence that for the remaining cases it is a complete intersection defined by inom{n-4}{4} quadrics. Using representation-theoretic arguments, we uncover a Pfaffian structure governing the quadratic relations that define the truncation variety for any n, and show that an exact tensor product factorization holds in the distinguished limit of disconnected doubles. We connect these structural results to the computation of the beryllium insertion into molecular hydrogen ({BedotsH_2 o H--Be--H}), a small but computationally challenging bond formation process where multiconfigurational effects become pronounced.
