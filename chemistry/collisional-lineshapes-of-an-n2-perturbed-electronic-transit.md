arXiv:2611.22649v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We demonstrate the feasibility of predicting the lineshape of an electronic molecular transition collisionally perturbed by another molecule from first principles of quantum mechanics. We consider an N_2-perturbed O_2 A-band transition, which is relevant to spectroscopic observations in Earth's atmosphere. This approach simultaneously incorporates the electronic structure, quantum scattering and lineshape effects required to accurately predict pressure- and temperature-dependence of observed lineshapes. The extit{ab initio} lineshapes reproduce measurements to better than 0.6%. Theory also provides new physical constraints on beyond-Voigt lineshape parameters in fits of high-precision data, yielding fit residuals at the 0.08% level. This work motivates advances in molecular electronic structure calculations and demonstrates the potential to generate comprehensive spectroscopic reference data from first principles.
