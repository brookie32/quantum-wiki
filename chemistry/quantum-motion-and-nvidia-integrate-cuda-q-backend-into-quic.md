Quantum Motion and NVIDIA have integrated CUDA-Q as a prototype backend into QUICHE, an open-source interface for quantum chemistry. This integration creates an end-to-end workflow, automating the process from classical molecular integral generation to GPU-accelerated quantum phase estimation. The pipeline was validated by modeling the spin-state energetics of the iron-sulfur complex Fe(SH)4−.
