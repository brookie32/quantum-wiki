arXiv:2607.16866v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Quantum estimates of molecular energy require repeated state preparation and measurement. We bound the minimum repetition count for given measurements and processing rules from above with a practical schedule and from below with independently verified inequalities. For an H_4 chain at H-H distances of 0.90 and 1.80 AA with 2 per cent uniform noise in the ground state, entangling Clifford measurements, which map Pauli products onto one another, cut leading-order repetitions by at least 65.9 and 61.0 per cent, respectively, relative to any schedule using only single-qubit Pauli measurements and Hamiltonian terms. With 69 unaltered measurement settings, each a basis change followed by qubit readout, processing further observables saves at least 12.04 and 6.70 per cent. For four lanthanide and actinide Hamiltonians with depolarized Hartree-Fock covariances, reducing the sum of absolute Hamiltonian coefficients does not proportionally reduce sampling. The bounds cover linear unbiased estimates from independent batches at small error.
