arXiv:2611.22462v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: In the electronic structure of rare earth ions, details can be crucial for understanding the origin of correlated states. Currently, state-of-the-art wavefunction-based methods (e.g. CASSCF) can capture single-ion properties of molecular magnets and some band insulating systems, but are expensive to perform, and cannot be applied to metallic systems. Here, we carry out full multiplet calculations for SmN with the cubic rock-salt structure using the crystal field software Quanty. The calculated experimental observables from our model agree precisely with magnetic susceptibility and inelastic neutron scattering experiments, and the spatially resolved spin density also agrees with DFT predictions. By tuning the crystal field parameters, our full multiplet-derived wavefunctions reveal the presence of orbital differentiation of the ground J=frac{5}{2} manifold into two groups, which are distinct subspaces with different orbital stiffness. This violation of Hund's rule, observed with a fast and inexpensive calculation tool, represents a step forward for microscopic models of rare earth materials, and may provide support for the claim of unconventional triplet superconductivity in SmN.
