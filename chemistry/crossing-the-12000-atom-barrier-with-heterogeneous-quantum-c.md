Researchers developed a workflow combining quantum embedding with heterogeneous quantum-classical methods to simulate protein-ligand complexes, using up to 94 qubits across two IBM quantum processors and supercomputers to run 9,200 circuits over 100 hours.   The team simulated two protein-ligand complexes spanning 11,608 and 12,635 atoms, demonstrating a >40× increase in system size and up to 210× improvement in accuracy over previous state-of-the-art results, with heterogeneous quantum-classical calculations matching coupled-cluster accuracy.
