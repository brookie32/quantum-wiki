arXiv:2611.08348v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: In the present work a method for calculating atomic static electric rank-n tensor quadrupole polarizabilities alpha_Q^{(n)}(J) for arbitrary atomic electronic structure is presented. It is based on a linear-symmetry representation of atomic states using large-scale relativistic configuration interaction wavefunctions, and the quadrupole polarizabilities are obtained by a sum-over-states approach. The major result from applying this method to atomic thulium is alpha_Q^{(0)}(frac{7}{2}) = 2496^{+0}_{-231} au for the electronic ground term ^2F_{7/2} static electric scalar quadrupole polarizability which is roughly a factor of four smaller than same property in the electronic ground state of the cesium atom. This finding adds to the favorable properties of thulium atoms in atomic-clock research.
