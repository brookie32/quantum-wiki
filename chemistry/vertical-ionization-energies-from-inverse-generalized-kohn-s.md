arXiv:2610.22024v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The exact Kohn--Sham (KS) system must have its highest occupied orbital energy equal to the negative of the lowest vertical ionization energy (VIE). No comparable theorem connects the deeper occupied levels arepsilon_k to the higher VIEs I_k. Chong, Gritsenko and Baerends (CGB) established empirically that I_k + arepsilon_kproptosqrt{arepsilon_{HOMO}-arepsilon_k}. We ask whether a similar relation holds for the exact generalized Kohn-Sham (GKS) system, which incorporates a fraction alpha of Hartree-Fock exchange. We invert near-exact CCSD/aug-cc-pVQZ ground-state densities of LiH, LiF, H_2O, HF, CO, N_2 and F_2 at alpha = 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0 and obtain the exact GKS systems at each admixture. As the density is held fixed and only the operator is varied, the resulting occupied spectra separate the effect of exact-exchange admixture on orbital energies from its effect on the density, two contributions inseparable in a self-consistent hybrid calculation. The CGB relation holds broadly across the family, with a proportionality coefficient linear in alpha, c(alpha)=c(0)-salpha, where c(0)=1.185pm0.017 eV^{1/2} and s=1.987pm0.041 eV^{1/2}, with R^{2}ge0.9996 for H_2O, CO, HF and N_2. Using the response-potential matrix elements tabulated by Gritsenko, Braida and Baerends, we decompose the CGB coefficient into response and spectroscopic parts, and find s to agree with the homogeneous-electron-gas step-height coefficient K_g = 1.9932 eV^{1/2}, which we conjecture is its origin. The coefficient vanishes at alpha^{*}=0.597pm0.008. At alpha^{*} the HOMO-referenced GKS occupied levels satisfy arepsilon_kapprox-I_k across the whole occupied range. The zero crossing is fixed by the core orbitals; in the outer valence the CGB coefficient is already indistinguishable from zero at alpha=0.
