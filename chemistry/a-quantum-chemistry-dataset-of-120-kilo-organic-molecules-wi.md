arXiv:2410.19316v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Quantum-chemical datasets of molecular geometries, electronic energies and atomic forces support the development of atomistic machine-learning models. Here we present QO2Mol, comprising 116,127 organic molecular fragments and more than 20 million three-dimensional conformations. The fragments were obtained by bond cleavage and capping of source compounds and contain H, C, N, O, F, P, S, Cl, Br and I. The geometries include optimized structures and configurations generated through torsional sampling and local bond-length and bond-angle perturbations. Electronic energies and atomic forces were calculated at the B3LYP/def2-SVP level of theory. Each conformational record also contains dipole moments, isotropic polarizabilities, electronic spatial extents and frequency-derived correction values. The dataset and its documented generation and validation procedures support molecular energy and force modelling and studies of geometry-dependent molecular properties.
