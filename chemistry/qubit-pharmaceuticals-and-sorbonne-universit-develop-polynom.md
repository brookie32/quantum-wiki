Researchers from Qubit Pharmaceuticals and Sorbonne Université have developed a new method for quantum chemistry, introducing a polynomial-scaling algebraic framework to construct and verify Minimal Complete Pools (MCPs) for variational quantum eigensolvers (VQE). This approach significantly reduces computational overhead, as demonstrated by GPU-accelerated benchmarks on the Jean Zay supercomputer. The team has open-sourced their C++ and CUDA routines for MCP verification on GitHub.
