arXiv:2601.06295v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We describe an explicit basis for the operatorname{SU}(2)-invariant space of the exterior power wedge_{2k} C^{2m} via the combinatorics of plane partitions. In quantum chemistry, this is the space of spin adapted quantum states of an electronic system with m spin orbitals and k electron pairs. We construct our basis by identifying the invariant space with an Artinian commutative ring called the excitation ring. We compute a Grobner basis and enumerate its standard monomials via an explicit bijection to Dyck paths counted by the Narayana numbers.
