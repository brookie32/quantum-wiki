Quandela has announced two new international partnerships: with South Korea's KRIBB to advance drug discovery through hybrid AI/classical/photonic quantum computing, and with Vietnam's FPT Corporation to develop quantum algorithms, provide cloud access to Quandela's photonic systems, and integrate quantum education into FPT's university networks. These collaborations aim to deploy Quandela's hybrid photonic QPUs as specialized accelerators in high-performance computing centers and enterprise cloud networks.
