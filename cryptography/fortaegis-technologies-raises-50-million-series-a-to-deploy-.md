Fortaegis Technologies has successfully closed a 50 million Series A funding round led by Serendipity Capital, with participation from major strategic investors. This capital will accelerate the deployment of their hardware-rooted quantum-safe secure compute architecture, which provides advanced security against AI and post-quantum decryption threats by generating dynamic, quantum-resistant encryption keys from physical silicon properties. The company plans to scale FPGA deployment and develop custom ASICs for various markets, including AI data centers, defense, and telecommunications.
