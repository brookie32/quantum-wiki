Let (E) be chosen uniformly from the geometric isomorphism classes of supersingular elliptic curves over (overline{F}_p) and (elta_E) be the minimum degree of isogenies from (E) to its Frobenius conjugate (E^{(p)}). We establish bounds for the smoothness probability of elta_E and hence affirm Wesolowski's (p^{1/3+o(1)}) attack on the supersingular endomorphism ring problem is unconditional. We also establish a uniform asymptotic formula, with a main term expressed in terms of Dirichlet (L)-values, for the probability that a connection isogeny of degree n exists between E and E^{(p)}.
