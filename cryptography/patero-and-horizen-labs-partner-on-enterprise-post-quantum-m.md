Patero and Horizen Labs have partnered to help enterprises migrate their digital communications to quantum-resistant encryption. The collaboration combines Horizen Labs' advisory services with Patero's discovery and hybrid encryption platforms to identify cryptographic assets, sequence migrations, and validate quantum-resilient controls on live infrastructure. This partnership aims to address the engineering challenges of transitioning enterprise IT architectures to quantum-safe standards.
