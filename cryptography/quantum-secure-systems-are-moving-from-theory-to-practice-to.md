Quantum-secure systems are moving from theory to practice. Together with @IDEMIA_IST Secure Transactions (IST), we’ve validated interoperability between IonQ’s Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) systems and IDEMIA Sphere, IST’s cybersecurity platform. The demonstration shows how quantum-generated keys can be governed, managed, and used in enterprise environments without disrupting existing security controls, compliance requirements, or operational processes. It combines three complementary layers of defense: 🔸 Hardware Security Modules (HSMs) to protect keys and sensitive operations 🔸 Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) to resist future quantum attacks 🔸 QKD as an added layer for organizations with specific threat models The result is a practical blueprint for interoperable, quantum-safe architectures, especially for financial institutions, telecom operators, and government agencies preparing for the quantum era. As Jordan Shapiro, President of Quantum Platform at IonQ, explained: “This validation shows how our QKD systems can plug directly into enterprise-grade environments, giving organizations a practical path to strengthen critical communications with quantum-generated security.” Thank you to our partners at IST for a great collaboration. 👉 Read the full announcement: https://bit.ly/4ldXjuO #QuantumSecurity #QKD #PQC #Cybersecurity
