This paper deals with the hardness of finding short vectors in module lattices. Let K be a number field of degree d and O_K its ring of integers. We show that if a module lattice M of rank n in O_K^n has some Galois-symmetries, namely if it is fixed coordinate-wise (as a set) by a group G of automorphisms of K, then M can actually be seen as a module of rank~n over a subfield~K' of K (K' is the fixed-field of G), whose degree is |G| times smaller than the degree of K. When one wants to find short vectors in M, this translates into the observation that the module lattice M, which is a priori a lattice of rank n d can in fact be seen as a lattice of rank only n d / |G|. Hence, finding short vectors in M is easier than what one could have expected by forgetting about the algebraic structure of M. This result is a generalization of a similar result by Boudgoust, Gachon and Pellet-Mary (Crypto'22), which was restricted to ideal lattices (i.e., modules of rank 1).
