On August 7 2026 a report announced that U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) is purchasing credit‑card records from commercial data brokers. The agency intends to use personal information supplied during the credit‑card application process for investigations or enforcement actions. This move highlights growing government access to consumer financial data through third‑party data aggregators.
