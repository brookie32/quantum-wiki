Royal Bank of Canada (RBC) has launched a multi-year enterprise quantum strategy focusing on internal R&D, post-quantum cybersecurity, academic partnerships, and hardware investments. This initiative includes collaborations with Xanadu, the University of Waterloo, and the University of Toronto to develop quantum applications for financial modeling, optimize portfolios, analyze risk, and enhance cryptographic security. RBC is also migrating to Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) standards and Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) and has invested in Photonic Inc.
