Pseudorandom unitaries (PRUs) are efficiently computable keyed families of unitaries that are computationally indistinguishable from Haar-random unitaries; scalable PRUs allow the security parameter to be chosen independently of the number of qubits. Assuming quantum-secure pseudorandom functions and Vaikuntanathan and Zamir's conjecture on sequential Kac walks, we propose a construction of scalable PRUs. At a technical level, we show how to implement ndot operatorname{polylog}(n) steps of a sequential Kac's (pseudo)random walk by a quantum circuit of size operatorname{polylog}( n).
