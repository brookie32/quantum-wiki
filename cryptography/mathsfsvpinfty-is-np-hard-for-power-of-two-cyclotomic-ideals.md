We prove that exact decision-mathsf{SVP} in the coefficient ell_infty-norm is mathsf{NP}-complete for principal ideals and free modules of rank two over the cyclotomic rings R_n=Z[X]/(X^n+1), where n ranges over powers of two. For each case, we give a deterministic polynomial-time many-one reduction from the Subset Sum problem. For an instance with s positive input integers, the output lattice has dimension O(s). A solution to the original Subset Sum instance can be recovered in polynomial time from any nonzero output lattice vector of norm at most the specified bound. We also transfer these reductions from R_n to the power-of-two cyclic rings S_{2n}=Z[X]/(X^{2n}-1). This gives mathsf{NP}-completeness of decision-mathsf{SVP} in the coefficient ell_infty-norm for principal ideals and free modules of any fixed positive rank over these rings.
