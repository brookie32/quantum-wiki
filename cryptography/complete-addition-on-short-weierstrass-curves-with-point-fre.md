Let E_{a,b}:Y^2Z=X^3+aXZ^2+bZ^3 be a nonsingular short-Weierstrass curve over a field K of characteristic different from 2 and 3. A nonvertical line Y=mX+nZ has no K-rational point on the curve if and only if x^3-m^2x^2+(a-2mn)x+b-n^2 is rootless in K. We give coordinate changes and formulas for the corresponding K-complete Bosma--Lenstra addition law. An incidence count proves that such a line exists over every finite field in the stated characteristic. The classical five-multiplication cross-product identity reduces the output computation by one multiplication. For normalized horizontal, sloped, and depressed families, the full/full costs are 13M+5D+30A, 13M+5D+33A, and 13M+(2ext{ to }5)D+60A, respectively. Horizontal normalization requires a nonzero cube parameter; the depressed family always contains a point-free line over F_p for pge47. The zero-line formula costs 11M+3D_a+2D_{3b}+28A and is complete over finite K exactly when #E(K) is odd. We also give complete doubling formulas, account for changes of coordinates, and apply the five-product identity to published Hessian addition formulas.
