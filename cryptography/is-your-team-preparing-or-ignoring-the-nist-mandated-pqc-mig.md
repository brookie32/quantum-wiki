NIST-PQC migration prepares organizations to replace vulnerable public-key cryptography with quantum-resistant algorithms standardized by NIST.   The urgency comes from the threat that future quantum computers could break current encryption algorithms, and adversaries are already collecting encrypted data today to decrypt later once quantum computers become available.   Organizations require enterprise-wide planning across multiple departments, and the migration process will take years for many enterprises to complete.
