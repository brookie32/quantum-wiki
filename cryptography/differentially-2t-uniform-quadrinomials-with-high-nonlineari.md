In this paper, we discuss some cryptographic properties of a family of quadrinomials constructed as a generalization of a known family of APN quadrinomials. In particular, we show that this family produces differentially 2^t-uniform quadrinomials with the same Walsh spectrum as the Gold power function x^{2^t+1} over F_{2^n}. We also discuss whether this family provides permutations.
