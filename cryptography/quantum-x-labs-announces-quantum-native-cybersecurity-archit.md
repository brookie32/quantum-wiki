Quantum X Labs, under its QuantumQ Security division, has introduced a quantum-native cybersecurity architecture with three layers: Detect (LayerQake), Encrypt (Qatacomb), and Verify (Quantum Verification Suite). This initiative, guided by former Mossad Chief Yossi Cohen, aims to integrate quantum mechanics directly into network defense, moving beyond traditional post-quantum cryptography. While the programs are still in research and IP filing stages, they represent a significant pivot for the company, formerly Viewbix Inc.
