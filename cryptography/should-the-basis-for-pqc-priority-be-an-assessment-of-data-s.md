This discussion examines whether post-quantum cryptography (PQC) prioritization should focus on assessing data shelf life—identifying secrets requiring confidentiality for ten years or more—rather than attempting to predict the uncertain timing of Q-Day (when quantum computers become cryptographically relevant) .  Data with long shelf life like intellectual property, government secrets, and health records faces particular risk from "harvest now, decrypt later" attacks , making a data-centric prioritization approach potentially more practical than waiting for Q-Day estimates.
