arXiv:2608.24092v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We experimentally demonstrate an entanglement-based Quantum-Secure Time Transfer (QSTT) system in an emulated low Earth orbit satellite-to-ground channel using a type-0 Sagnac-based entangled-photon source. Our new QSTT system delivers a finite Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) key rate of approximately 3~bits~ext{s}^{-1} with a 10^{-10} security parameter, providing a 30-fold increase in the QKD key rate compared to the state-of-the-art entanglement-based QKD system delivered by the Micius satellite. Beyond this high key rate outcome, novel to our QSTT system is a GPS-free clock synchronization, optimized use of QKD, embedded post-quantum security, and obfuscation of the system configuration via use of a pre-shared key. Collectively, these enhancements deliver the most efficient and secure deployment of QSTT to date, and point the way forward to high-accuracy ultra-secure time transfer in space.
