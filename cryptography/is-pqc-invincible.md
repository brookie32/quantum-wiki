Post-quantum cryptography (PQC) is the best available defense against quantum attacks, but has real limitations including larger key and signature sizes than RSA or ECC, higher performance and bandwidth costs, and complex migration across existing systems.   During PQC standardization, several promising algorithms were cracked by researchers, and organizations must accept that an algorithm thought quantum-safe today could be found flawed tomorrow.   PQC is necessary but not sufficient as a standalone solution for quantum-safe security.
