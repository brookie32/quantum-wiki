We study extensions of Bernstein's combinatorial primality certificates obtained by varying the linear factors and the coefficient degree in a Kummer-type quotient ring. We then separate the coefficient period from the extension degree and replace an auxiliary factor by direct period tests. The results strengthen certificate criteria under stated hypotheses; they do not establish a smaller general complexity exponent than Bernstein's essentially quartic algorithm.
