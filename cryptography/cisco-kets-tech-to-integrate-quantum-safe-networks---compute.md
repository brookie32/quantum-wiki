Cisco and Kets Tech have partnered to embed quantum‑safe networking solutions into Cisco’s product line. The collaboration aims to develop cryptographic protocols that resist future quantum‑computing attacks, thereby protecting enterprise communication networks against emerging security threats. The announcement was reported by *Computer Weekly*.
