Researchers employed computed‑tomography (CT) scanning to recover the embedded code of a 400‑year‑old cryptex padlock, successfully unlocking it for the first time in modern history. The study demonstrates how non‑invasive imaging can preserve and decipher ancient mechanical cryptograms while highlighting contemporary commercial interest—USB‑driven cryptexts are now available for purchase. This case is cited as a notable intersection of historical cryptography and advanced imaging technology, documented on November 20, 2026.
