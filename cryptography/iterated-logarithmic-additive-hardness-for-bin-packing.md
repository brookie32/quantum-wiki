The question of obtaining a constant-additive approximation for bin packing was highlighted in the third of ten open problems in Williamson and Shmoys' The Design of Approximation Algorithms [SW11, Chapter~17, Problem~3]. We prove that there is an absolute constant c>0 such that, unless mathrm P=NP, no deterministic polynomial-time packing using at most OPT+clog^*OPT bins, where OPT is the minimum number of bins. In particular, no such algorithm has additive error o(log^*OPT). The best known upper bound, due to Hoberg and Rothvoss [HR17], is OPT+O(logOPT), achieved by a randomized algorithm with expected polynomial running time. OpenAI [Ope26] previously showed that, unless mathrm P=NP, no deterministic polynomial-time algorithm guarantees a packing using at most OPT+O(1) bins. We conjecture that the optimal additive approximation error for bin packing is Theta(log OPT).
