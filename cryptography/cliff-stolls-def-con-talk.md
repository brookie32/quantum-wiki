In August 2026, security researcher Cliff Stoll gave a talk at DEF CON in which he recounted the pursuit of a notorious hacker from forty years earlier. He reflected on the techniques used during that chase and how it influenced early intrusion‑detection practices. The session was noted for its engaging historical perspective on cybersecurity and is available as a video recording.
