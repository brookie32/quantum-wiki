Pasqal has signed a research collaboration agreement with King Abdulaziz City for Science and Technology (@KACST), represented by its National Center for Quantum Technologies (NCQT), to advance quantum-safe cryptography in Saudi Arabia. Announced in Riyadh during World Quantum Day, the agreement pairs Pasqal's neutral-atom quantum systems and cloud services with KACST's national research infrastructure. Quantum cryptography is the first focus area of a broader multi-year program. "This agreement reflects KACST's commitment to building strategic research partnerships with global technology leaders, supporting the development of national capabilities, and translating scientific research in quantum technologies into impactful future applications," said Dr. Talal Alsedairy, Senior Vice President for R&D at KACST. The agreement adds to a year of momentum for Pasqal in the Kingdom, following the inauguration of Saudi Arabia's first quantum computer with Aramco in May and announced plans for a joint venture with Eleven Ventures across the MENA region.
