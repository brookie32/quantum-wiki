pQCee has partnered with Carahsoft Technology Corp. to provide its post-quantum cybersecurity software to the U.S. public sector. This agreement allows Carahsoft to distribute pQCee's encryption suites to various government entities through its existing networks. This collaboration addresses the need for secure, post-quantum cryptography solutions in response to Executive Order 14412, enabling government agencies to upgrade their cybersecurity without overhauling existing IT infrastructure.
