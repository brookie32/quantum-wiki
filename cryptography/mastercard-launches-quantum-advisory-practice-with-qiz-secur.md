Mastercard has launched a Quantum Advisory Practice with QIZ Security to help financial institutions transition to post-quantum cryptography, addressing immediate "harvest now, decrypt later" risks. This initiative, aligned with G7 and FS-ISAC guidance, offers services like cryptographic asset discovery and remediation roadmaps. Mastercard is also conducting quantum research with Oxford Quantum Circuits, focusing on financial fraud protection.
