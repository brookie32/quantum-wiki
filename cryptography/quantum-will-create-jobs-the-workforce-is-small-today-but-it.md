Quantum will create jobs. The workforce is small today, but it will be roughly ten times larger by 2030 and potentially a hundred times larger by 2035. Last week in St. John’s, Antigua and Barbuda, IonQ CEO @NiccolodeMasi joined global heads of government, delegates, and business leaders for the 2026 Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting (CHOGM) to discuss what quantum means for the next generation of global economies. To help Commonwealth nations build sovereign capability, IonQ is introducing three fully funded partnerships: 🔸 Quantum-safe migration support for small-state central banks and payment operators 🔸 Dedicated synthetic aperture radar (SAR) imagery and analyst time for coastal and disaster agencies 🔸 300 hours of cloud compute time and engineering instruction for universities in developing member states These partnerships are designed to turn the promise of quantum into national advantage, driving their security, resilience, and economic growth.
