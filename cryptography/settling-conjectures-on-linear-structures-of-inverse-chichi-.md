Belkheyar et al. introduced ChiChi as the even-dimensional, low-latency nonlinear core of ChiLow (Eurocrypt 2025), and Andreoli et al. generalized it into the SWAP and 4-cycle families (ToSC 2025/3), conjecturing five bounds on the linear height and component linear nullity of their inverses, supported by experiments in small dimensions. We settle all five conjectures: Conjectures 1–4 hold for every admissible branch length, and Conjecture 5 is false. In addition, we derive consequences for ChiLow's design and correct one value in the data of Andreoli et al.
