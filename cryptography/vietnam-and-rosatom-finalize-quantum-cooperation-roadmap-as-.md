Vietnam and Russia's Rosatom have established a quantum cooperation roadmap, accelerating Vietnam's quantum information science capabilities. This agreement allows Vietnam to access Russian quantum processors remotely, bypassing significant hardware costs, and includes plans for a joint quantum technology laboratory in Vietnam. Concurrently, Vietnam is exploring integration with European quantum standards and prioritizing post-quantum cryptography for national security.
