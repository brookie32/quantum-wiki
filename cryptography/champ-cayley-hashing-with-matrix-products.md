Cayley hash functions hash binary strings by representing the bits of the message as elements of a semigroup and multiplying the corresponding elements. This gives Cayley hash functions useful homomorphic properties. Here, we propose CHAMP, a Cayley hash function based on products of 2imes 2 matrices over a finite field, and discuss its security, implementation, and performance.
