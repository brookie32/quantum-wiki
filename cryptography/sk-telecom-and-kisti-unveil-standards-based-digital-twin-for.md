SK Telecom and KISTI have developed a digital twin for Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) networks, allowing simulation and verification of network performance before physical deployment. This platform, unveiled at ECOC 2026, uses ETSI standards to connect a Key Management System with a virtual QKD network simulator, enabling cost-effective optimization and multi-vendor interoperability.
