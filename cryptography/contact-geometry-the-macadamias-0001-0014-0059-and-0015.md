The root paper of this series reduces the value gamma_{11}=4/sqrt[11]{972} of the Hermite constant to a statement about chains of lattices Lsupset Msupset N of ranks 11, 10 and 9 whose rank-9 member lies, in the sense of Voronoi, over one of 4 487 813 perfect forms; the gamma_{11} paper excludes all of them but ten, the macadamias. This paper settles four macadamias, the forms 0001:010488327, 0014:001749549, 0059:016902802 and 0015:030291998, by one method: the geometry of the contacts of the form. A section frame of short dual vectors forces the first gluing of M over N to lie near one of finitely many coherent centres; at such a centre the rank-10 metric is compared with an explicit reference lattice, whose Delaunay cells and contact identities give a uniform bound on its covering radius; and a primitive-extension inequality turns that bound into et Lge 972. The proof is computer-assisted: every finite check it uses is stated as a Computational result with the records of the certificate archive that certify it, and all comparisons are between explicit rational numbers.
