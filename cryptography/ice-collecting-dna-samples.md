In 2025, U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) collected almost a million DNA samples. The data are added to federal databases intended for use by the FBI and National Homeland Security agencies to support identification and broader national‑security investigations. This activity reflects ongoing policy efforts to integrate biometrics into law‑enforcement decision‑making.
