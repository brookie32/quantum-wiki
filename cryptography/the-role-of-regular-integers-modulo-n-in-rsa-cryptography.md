We investigate a multi-prime multi-power generalization of the RSA cryptosystem for arbitrary moduli n>1, which under reasonable cryptographic assumptions works correctly for almost all messages m<n. Based on a new sharpening of Carmichael's theorem, tailored to regular integers modulo n, we prove that this generalization is correct precisely for messages represented by regular integers modulo n, thereby generalizing the original RSA correctness theorem. As in the original RSA scheme, decryption can be accelerated by Chinese remaindering, yielding a corresponding generalization of CRT-RSA.
