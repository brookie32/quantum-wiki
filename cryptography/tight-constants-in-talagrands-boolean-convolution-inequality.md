We study the optimal dimension-free constant C_*(a) in Talagrand's Boolean convolution inequality Pr_mu[T_afge u]le C_*(a)/(usqrt{log u}), where T_a is the noise operator, mu is the uniform measure on the Boolean cube, fge0 satisfies E_mu[f]=1, and u>1. We establish explicit upper bounds for every 0<a<1 and prove limsup_{aparrow1}C_*(a)/sqrt{kappa_a}<0.3577, where kappa_a:=(1+a)/(1-a). We establish the lower bound liminf_{aparrow1}C_*(a)/sqrt{kappa_a}ge0.3570.
