The paper demonstrates that quantum discord—not just entanglement—determines the secrecy of quantum key distribution (QKD).  It provides a rigorous proof linking discord to secure communication and applies this result to a practical three‑state QKD protocol, showing robustness against source/measurement imperfections and basis misalignment.  The protocol achieves higher key rates than standard BB84 under realistic implementation conditions.
