There are four critical cybersecurity clocks—each telling time in a different way: the adversary, the regulator, the vendor, and your ability to execute. You control one. If the others get there first, you could face higher costs and a range of risks. IonQ just reported on how a 20,000-physical-qubit quantum computer is estimated to be able to break 256-bit elliptic encryption. This blog by Field Quantum Security Officer Simon Sinisha Patkovic details what’s behind each of the four clocks and what organizations need to know now to improve security exposure.
