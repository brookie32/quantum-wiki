Creotech Quantum's eCAUSIS project, funded by Horizon Europe, has received European Commission approval for its DV-QKD platform, transitioning it from R&D to commercial production. The project, with a total budget of nearly €7 million, involved Creotech Quantum as coordinator, along with AIT Austrian Institute of Technology and Fraunhofer Society. This initiative establishes a European supply chain for QKD modules, with an initial capacity of 1,000 units per year, potentially expanding to 10,000 annually.
