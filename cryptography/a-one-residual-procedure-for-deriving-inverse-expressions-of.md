We present a one-residual procedure for deriving factorized inverse expressions of a class of shift-invariant (SI) transformations. The procedure uses iterative substitution to isolate a single residual input variable and propagates the resulting recurrence along cyclic orbits. For SI transformations with a single nonlinear product term (SNPT-SI) satisfying explicit cancellation conditions, we prove a necessary and sufficient arithmetic criterion for bijectivity. The criterion determines the bijective state sizes and directly yields the corresponding inverse expressions. We apply these results to construct degree-t SNPT-SI permutations attaining the known inverse-degree bound D_t(n)=n-lceil(n-1)/trceil for every tge2 and n>t, except when t=2 and n is even. Fixed-parameter families also attain this bound at infinitely many state sizes. The criterion covers the four main and all 14 additional transformations studied by Liu et al. (Journal of Cryptology, 2026). For hi_{n,t}, we recover known inverse expressions and characterize multiple-preimage fibers. A bounded search certifies bijective families for 69 of 378 anchored parameter sets.
