Elisity has entered into an agreement to be acquired by IonQ (@IonQ_Inc) to accelerate Zero Trust for the quantum era. The proposed combination brings together our enterprise Zero Trust platform with IonQ’s post-quantum cryptography (PQC) and quantum key distribution (QKD) capabilities. Our customers face two overlapping priorities: containing attacks that operate at machine speed and preparing to protect sensitive information against future quantum threats. Together with IonQ, we intend to help them address both while building on the security investments they already trust. Elisity IdentityGraph and our unified policy fabric provide identity context and policy enforcement for users, devices, workloads and AI agents. The shared roadmap would connect that foundation with cryptographic inventory and risk information to help customers prioritize post-quantum upgrades while keeping essential operations running. “Our customers need to prioritize post-quantum cryptography initiatives while securing existing assets to keep essential operations running,” said James Winebrenner, CEO of Elisity. Following closing, Elisity will continue serving customers under its current leadership. Our commitment to innovation, customer success and strong partner relationships remains unchanged.
