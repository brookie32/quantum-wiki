**Fake CAPTCHA Scams** – A modern rendition of phishing attacks employs fabricated CAPTCHA interfaces to convince users to download and execute malicious software. The scam lures victims by presenting a legitimate-looking verification prompt that hides the malware installation step. This tactic leverages existing trust in CAPTCHAs to bypass security awareness, making it important for users and developers to detect anomalous CAPTCHA behavior.
