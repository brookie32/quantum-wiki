Thales has launched Luna 8, a next-generation hardware security module (HSM) designed to protect enterprise cryptographic infrastructure against quantum decryption threats and AI workloads.   The device provides key management, digital signing, PKI protection, and cryptographic agility to support post-quantum cryptography algorithm migrations.   The deployment directly addresses "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" (HNDL) attacks, where adversaries intercept and store encrypted data today to decrypt once quantum computers become available—cited as the primary quantum concern by 61% of surveyed respondents.
