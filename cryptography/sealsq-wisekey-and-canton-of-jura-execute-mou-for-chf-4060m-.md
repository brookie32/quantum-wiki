SEALSQ, WISeKey, and the Canton of Jura have partnered to create a CHF 40-60 million (48.7M–73M USD) Post-Quantum Semiconductor Personalization Hub in Switzerland. This center will focus on developing and implementing post-quantum cryptography using SEALSQ’s QS7001 Quantum Shield technology, serving defense, aerospace, and critical infrastructure sectors. The project aims to create over 250 jobs and will adapt operational methodologies from a similar facility in Spain.
