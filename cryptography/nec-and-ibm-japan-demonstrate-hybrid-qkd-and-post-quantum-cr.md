NEC Corporation and IBM Japan successfully demonstrated a hybrid quantum key distribution (QKD) and post-quantum cryptography (PQC) TLS proxy architecture. This trial, conducted between July and September 2026, integrated NEC's QKD hardware with IBM Quantum Safe Proxy, allowing for multi-vendor cryptographic interoperability without modifying existing enterprise applications. The setup combines QKD and PQC for enhanced session security, with future plans for commercial deployment in financial institutions and data centers.
