Let omega denote the matrix multiplication exponent. Very recently, OpenAI showed that omegale9/4. We demonstrate that, for any fixed eta>0, if omegale9/4-eta, then there is a constant elta=elta(eta)>0 such that heuristic lattice sieving can be improved to (3/2-elta)^{n/2+o(n)} time under the spherical heuristic used in the BDGL analysis. With known matrix multiplication algorithms, our method already yields subquadratic search within BDGL buckets.
