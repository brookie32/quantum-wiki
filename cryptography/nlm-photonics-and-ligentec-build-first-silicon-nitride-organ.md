NLM Photonics, LIGENTEC, and Spark Photonics have successfully created the first Silicon Nitride Organic Hybrid (NOH) modulators on a commercial photonics platform. This breakthrough allows for the integration of NLM's organic electro-optic materials onto silicon nitride substrates, enabling active, low-loss modulation across visible and near-infrared wavelengths. This advancement has applications in photonic qubit routing, quantum key distribution, and laser control in trapped-atom systems, and is supported by NLM Photonics' expanded investor base, securing its materials supply chain and manufacturing pipeline.
