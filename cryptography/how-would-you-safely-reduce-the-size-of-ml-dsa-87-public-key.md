ML-DSA-87 public keys are 2,592 bytes and signatures are 4,627 bytes , creating significant storage and bandwidth challenges for blockchain implementations.  Substituting ML-DSA-44 in place of existing elliptic-curve schemes would increase the byte size per signature by a factor of ~35-40× .  Safe size reduction must respect ML-DSA's security foundation in precise parameter sets and careful constant-time implementation rather than ad-hoc cryptographic tweaks .
