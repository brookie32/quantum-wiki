Post-quantum readiness questions are now appearing in vendor security questionnaires sent to technology suppliers across banking, government, defense, healthcare, telecoms, cloud, and SaaS companies, asking about their systems and encryption once quantum computers become powerful enough to break current cryptography.   Vendor due diligence for post-quantum cryptography has become critical because vendor readiness is a major factor influencing organizations' ability to transition to PQC within recommended timeframes.   Effective PQC vendor questionnaires go beyond simple support questions to probe cryptographic inventory maintenance, crypto-agility capabilities, fourth-party exposure, and progress toward FIPS 140-3 validation.
