The paper presents a pilot‑reference‑free continuous‑variable quantum key distribution (CVQKD) protocol that encodes the secret key on the amplitude of coherent states, thereby eliminating the high‑energy pilot signal normally used to correct phase drift and removing its induced crosstalk. Security analysis against collective attacks shows the scheme retains a positive key rate, which is confirmed by numerical simulations. Proof‑of‑principle experiments over an optical fiber link and a free‑space channel with randomly applied phase demonstrate that the protocol remains functional without a pilot, simplifying both physical implementation and digital signal processing in complex transmission environments.
