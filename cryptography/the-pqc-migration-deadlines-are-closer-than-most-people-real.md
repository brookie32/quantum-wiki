Executive Order 14412, signed June 22, 2026, set federal post-quantum cryptography (PQC) migration deadlines ranging from 30 days to 2031 , with  key establishment required by end of 2030 and digital signatures by end of 2031 .  Multiple regulators have published PQC migration deadlines much earlier than the 2035 full migration target, requiring organizations to plan against the earliest applicable date .  2026 represents the last point at which timely migration is still achievable for most organizations .
