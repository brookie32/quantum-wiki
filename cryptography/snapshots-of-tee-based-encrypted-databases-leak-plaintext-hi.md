In 2026, Zachary Espiritu (MongoDB Research/NYU) showed that snapshots taken from encrypted databases running inside Trusted Execution Environments can leak plaintext frequency histograms via a dendrochronology‑based analysis. The attack derives temporal correlations in snapshot artefacts to recover data distribution patterns without needing decryption, adding a new class of snapshot‑based threats to TEE‑protected systems. This work is relevant for researchers studying database cryptography, trusted computing, and side‑channel attacks.
