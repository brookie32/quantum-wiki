The ICCS Next-Generation Commercial Cryptography (NGCC) round-1 call received 84 public key candidates: 34 signatures, 41 KEMs and 9 key-exchange schemes. The call invites public evaluation. This report collects sixteen candidates that we broke against their unmodified reference implementations. All packages are collected at https://github.com/acprk/ngcc-round1-cryptanalysis (one subdirectory per scheme).
