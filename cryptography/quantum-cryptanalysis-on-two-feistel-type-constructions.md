arXiv:2610.27506v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: With the rapid advancement of quantum computing, the security of traditional cryptographic systems is facing significant challenges. As a fundamental component of symmetric cryptography, block ciphers have attracted increasing attention regarding their quantum security in recent years. In this paper, we propose new quantum attacks on two Feistel-type constructions, i.e., New Structure IV and the lightweight block cipher LBlock, under the quantum chosen-plaintext attack (qCPA) model. First, we construct an 8-round quantum distinguisher for New Structure IV and perform an r(r>12)-round quantum key-recovery attack. Compared with Li et al.'s qCPA-based result presented at INSCRYPT 2024, our attack reduces the time complexity by a factor of 2^{n/2}, where n denotes the subkey length. Second, we design a 9-round quantum distinguisher for LBlock and extend it to an r(r>15)-round quantum key-recovery attack. The proposed quantum distinguisher covers one more round than Liu et al.'s automated-search distinguisher at ASIACRYPT 2025, which results in a 2^{16} reduction in the time complexity of our r-round quantum key-recovery attack on LBlock. Furthermore, by analyzing the structure of the round function and the key scheduling of LBlock, we reduce the subkey guess space for the 15-round attack from 76 bits to 52 bits, lowering the time complexity from (O(2^{38})) to (O(2^{26})) by a factor of (2^{12}).
