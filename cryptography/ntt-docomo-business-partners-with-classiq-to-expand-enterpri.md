NTT DOCOMO BUSINESS has partnered with Classiq to distribute Classiq's quantum software development platform in Japan, aiming to expand quantum computing adoption across various enterprise sectors. This collaboration leverages NTT DOCOMO BUSINESS's existing quantum-safe security infrastructure, including a 600-kilometer quantum cryptography communication network, to offer a comprehensive solution for quantum application development and secure communication. The partnership seeks to identify, co-develop, and validate industrial gate-based quantum computing applications for Japanese enterprises.
