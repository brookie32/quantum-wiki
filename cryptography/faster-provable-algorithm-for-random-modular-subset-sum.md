We study random modular subset sum with n coefficients sampled independently and uniformly modulo Q. The target is t=mathbf adotmathbf xpmod Q, where the hidden binary vector mathbf x is fixed independently of the coefficients. We focus on the balanced density-one setting, a standard hard case with Q=2^n and mathbf x of Hamming weight n/2. In this balanced density-one setting, Bonnetain, Bricout, Schrottenloher, and Shen (ASIACRYPT 2020) reported the best previously known classical time bound of widetilde O(2^{0.283n}) bit operations, using widetilde O(2^{0.283n}) bits of space, under heuristic assumptions. We present a classical randomized algorithm for the same setting that finds a solution with overwhelming probability using at most 2^{0.2614n} bit operations and 2^{0.2373n} bits of space for sufficiently large n, without heuristic assumptions.
