This work explains MIKE for beginners, i.e., those unfamiliar with isogenies. MIKE is both a fast and compact post-quantum NIKE and a beautiful scheme. However, this beauty is only visible once you are familiar with many more advanced concepts in isogeny-based cryptography. The beauty of MIKE then reveals itself in several different layers, each taking a step up in complexity. This work assumes no knowledge of isogenies, and explains three levels of understanding for MIKE, each level building on the previous one, preparing the reader for the full MIKE paper. Each level assumes a different level of knowledge; Level 1 assumes only familiarity with basic cryptography, Level 2 assumes a bit of familiarity with elliptic curves, Level 3 assumes a bit of familiarity with isogenies, which is provided in Level 2, and Level 3½ bridges the gap from Level 3 to the full MIKE paper.
