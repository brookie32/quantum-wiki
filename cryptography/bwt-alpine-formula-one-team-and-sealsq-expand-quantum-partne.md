BWT Alpine Formula One Team and SEALSQ are expanding their partnership to integrate hybrid quantum-classical simulation capabilities into Formula One engineering, leveraging ColibriTD's quantum solvers for complex physics equations in car development. This collaboration aims to enhance simulation accuracy and secure intellectual property using SEALSQ’s post-quantum technology, aligning with SEALSQ's strategy of linking investments from its $200 million Quantum Fund with commercial opportunities.
