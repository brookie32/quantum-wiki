We construct complete addition laws in extended coordinates for twisted Edwards curves (E_{a,d}:ax^2+y^2=1+dx^2y^2), where (a,din K^imes), (ane d), and (operatorname{char}Kne2). If (tin K) makes ((t^2-a)(t^2-d)) a nonzero nonsquare, the construction yields a (K)-complete formula valid for every pair of (K)-rational points. The proof describes the exceptional fibres through the difference morphism. Over finite fields of odd cardinality (q>16), such a coefficient exists for every nonsingular parameter pair, including the same-square-class cases with full rational two-torsion. We give full and mixed evaluation schedules. Full addition costs (8M+D_c) in split elementary models and (10M) or (12M), plus fixed-constant costs, in the same-square-class cases. Doubling costs (4M+4S+min(D_a,D_d)), independently of coefficient selection. Here (M) denotes a general field multiplication, (S) a squaring, and (D_c) multiplication by a fixed (cin K).
