Constructing iO from simple, safe assumptions in the post-quantum regime is an important problem with wide-ranging applications. In this work, we make progress towards this goal. We formulate a new, arguably simple assumption called LWE with Structured Preimage that suffices to build iO. Our assumption is instance-independent, falsifiable and non-interactive. Our assumption posits that a set of LWE (Learning With Errors) samples is indistinguishable from random given a short Gaussian matrix which is carefully chosen to satisfy the required functionality while avoiding problematic leakage. We provide a thorough cryptanalysis of our assumption, using the extensive cryptanalytic effort against the related evasive LWE assumption (Wee, Eurocrypt 2022 and Tsabary, Crypto 2022) to gain confidence. We emphasize that our assumption is superficially similar but fundamentally different from evasive LWE. Thus, we obtain iO from a simple lattice assumption whose hardness is somewhat better understood as compared to brand new assumptions.
