In this short note, we present a tweak to the KLPT2 algorithm that reduces the size of the output from O(p^{15.5}) to ilde O(p^{14.5}).Then, we show that the recent improvement by Nakagawa, Yoshizumi and Onuki to the KLPT algorithm is compatible with the Hermitian module splitting approach, further lowering the reduced degree bound to ilde O(p^{14}).
