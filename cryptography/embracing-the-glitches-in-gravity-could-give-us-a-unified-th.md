Physicist Jonathan Oppenheim proposes a “post‑quantum” model in which gravity and spacetime are not inherently quantum, directly challenging mainstream theory that treats all fundamental forces as quantum. His calculations suggest this simpler framework could also explain the gravitational influence attributed to dark matter, offering an alternative to complex multiverse or string‑theory explanations. Although still speculative, Oppenheim’s bet and conservative approach have prompted renewed attention to non‑quantum descriptions of gravity.
