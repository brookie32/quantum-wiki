This work strengthens the known hardness reduction for the decision variant of Decomposed Learning With Errors (LWE) problem and additionally presents a reduction of its search variant. The Decomposed LWE is t-parametrized variant of LWE, where t denotes the number of decomposed blocks. In both the cases, we reduce t-succinct LWE problem to the Decomposed LWE. The exiting hardness reduction for decision variant relies on Gaussian noise flooding paradigm, as a result the modulus-to-noise ratio is super-polynomial of security parameter. In this work, both the reductions hold in polynomial regime for polynomial number of decompositions.
