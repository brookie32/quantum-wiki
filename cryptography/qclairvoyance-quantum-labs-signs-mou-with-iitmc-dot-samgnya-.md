QClairvoyance Quantum Labs has partnered with IITM–C-DOT Samgnya Foundation, India's National Hub for Quantum Communication, to advance the Indian quantum ecosystem. This collaboration, under the National Quantum Mission, will focus on quantum computing, algorithms, AI, post-quantum cryptography, and next-generation compute architectures, aiming to translate theoretical research into deployable commercial products. Samgnya will provide QClairvoyance with access to hardware testbeds and support India's quantum technology commercialization efforts.
