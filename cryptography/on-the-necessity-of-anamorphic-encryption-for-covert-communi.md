Trey Li’s paper (Cryptology ePrint Archive 2026/2130, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22003016) shows that covert communication can be implemented without relying on anamorphic encryption. He presents a public‑key construction based on the Learning With Errors (LWE) assumption that achieves hidden channels while avoiding the need for anamorphic transformations. The work challenges earlier claims that such encryption is necessary and provides an alternative cryptographic framework for covert messaging.
