arXiv:2609.28351v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In this paper, we investigate the family of GHZ diagonal states, which are mixed multipartite quantum states that are diagonal in a GHZ type basis. We show that the class of GHZ diagonal states is highly symmetric and exhibits rich entanglement properties, making it relevant for applications in multipartite quantum key distribution.
