Today, IonQ completed its acquisition of @ElisityInc. Security has always been about protecting what matters most, and quantum makes that more urgent. Attackers are already collecting encrypted data today so they can decrypt it later, and the networks we run now will need to be secure long before large-scale quantum machines arrive. Elisity brings Zero Trust capabilities that control who and what can access critical systems, along with the ability to see and manage cryptographic risk. Combined with our work in quantum computing, networking, and sensing, this gives customers a clearer path to securing their environments today and preparing for the quantum era.
