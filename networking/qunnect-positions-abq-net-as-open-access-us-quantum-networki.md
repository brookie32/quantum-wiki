Qunnect has announced that ABQ‑Net will serve as an open‑access testbed for quantum networking in the United States. The initiative, highlighted by TipRanks, positions ABQ‑Net as a platform for researchers and developers to experiment with and deploy quantum networking technologies. The effort is part of a broader push to make quantum network infrastructure more widely available and interoperable across the U.S.
