Toshiba secured a contract with the UK to scale quantum‑networking capabilities in partnership with British Telecom (BT) and Colt. The project focuses on expanding the country’s infrastructure for secure quantum communication, enabling entanglement distribution over existing fiber links. It aims to support high‑security data transfer for government, finance, and critical‑infrastructure sectors across the United Kingdom.
