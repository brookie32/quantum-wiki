IonQ, a U.S.-based quantum‑computing company, has received a $7.5 million investment from the U.S. Air Force to support research and development of quantum networking technologies. The grant is intended to advance secure quantum communication solutions for defense and national security applications. This funding reflects growing government interest in leveraging quantum technology for strategic advantage.
