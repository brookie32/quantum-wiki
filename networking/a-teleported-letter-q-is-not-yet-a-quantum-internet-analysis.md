The Eurasia Review analyzes a recent demonstration in which a single photonic qubit encoding the letter “Q” was teleported between two stations—an early milestone in quantum communication. The article argues that, despite this proof‑of‑concept, such isolated teleportation events do not yet constitute a functional quantum internet capable of widespread, reliable end‑to‑end quantum networking. It highlights the technical and infrastructural gaps that remain before true distributed quantum information exchange can be realized.
