Stony Brook University and Brookhaven Laboratory have announced a milestone demonstration of wireless quantum networking, showing that entangled photons can be transmitted over free‑space links without the need for fiber optics. The experiment involved an on‑board light source and a receiver capable of collecting and measuring the quantum states remotely, proving the feasibility of distributed quantum networks in a truly wireless setting. This result paves the way for scalable, low‑latency quantum communication infrastructure.
