Spin–\(S\) U(1) quantum link models (QLMs), which encode gauge and electric fields with spin operators, are extended from the usual \(S=1/2\) case to arbitrary \(S\) via a bosonic mapping that preserves gauge invariance for large representations. The authors present an experimentally feasible protocol using spinless bosons in an optical superlattice to realize a large-scale \(S=1\) QLM, and benchmark the scheme with perturbation theory and infinite matrix product state calculations, demonstrating stable dynamics and enabling studies of confinement/deconfinement phenomena relevant to lattice QED.
