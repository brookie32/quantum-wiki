Chinese researchers developed a quantum teleportation technique that scales up to 100 channels simultaneously, potentially enabling high-capacity quantum communication networks.   The method uses a spatial light modulator to encode a programmable hologram in a 10-by-10 grid of distinct spatial modes that function as independent data channels.   The team successfully teleported the entire 100-mode optical array and an image with fidelities exceeding classical limits, offering a path toward scalable parallel quantum information processing.
