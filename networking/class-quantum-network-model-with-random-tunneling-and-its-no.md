The paper introduces a class of quantum network models that incorporate random tunneling between nodes and derives an effective low‑energy description in the form of a nonlinear sigma model. By imposing symmetry constraints on the lattice hopping terms, the authors identify the appropriate target manifold, topological terms, and coupling constants for each universality class related to Anderson localization. This framework clarifies how disorder and symmetry affect transport and critical behavior in disordered quantum systems.
