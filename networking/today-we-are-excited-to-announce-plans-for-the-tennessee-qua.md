Today we are excited to announce plans for the Tennessee Quantum Communications Research Center with @EPB_Chattanooga—the first dedicated next generation quantum communication research and development innovation lab directly connected to a real-world network. Based in Chattanooga and staffed by IonQ scientists, the lab will house the world’s first commercial Quantum Memory embedded within an operational communications network. We are fully invested in this initiative with a five-year commitment of 15 million, supporting EPB quantum assets that are projected to generate up to 1.1 billion in community benefit in Chattanooga over the next 10 years.
