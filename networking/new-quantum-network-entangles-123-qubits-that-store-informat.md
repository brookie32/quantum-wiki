Researchers are constantly getting closer to making practical quantum computers. Now, a team of researchers has overcome another hurdle in the process. Their study, published in Physical Review Letters, describes a new platform for manipulating entangled atomic networks in measurement-based quantum computation—a process usually hindered by difficulties manipulating only one atom or one group of atoms at a time.
