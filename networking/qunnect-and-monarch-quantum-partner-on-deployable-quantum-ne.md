Qunnect has partnered with Monarch Quantum to develop deployable quantum‑networking hardware for high‑performance computing environments, as reported by HPCwire. The collaboration combines Qunnect’s edge‑computing expertise with Monarch Quantum’s advanced quantum network technologies to create scalable, secure quantum communication links. Together they aim to accelerate the adoption of quantum networking solutions in data‑center and enterprise settings.
