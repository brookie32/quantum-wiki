Qunnect has launched the United States’ first open‑access quantum communication network in New Mexico. The platform connects multiple research and industrial partners via a dedicated fiber backbone, enabling free testing and development of quantum cryptography protocols. It represents a key milestone for nationwide quantum internet infrastructure, offering researchers and organisations the ability to experiment with advanced quantum networking technologies without deployment barriers.
