IonQ has been awarded $7.5 million from the U.S. Air Force’s AFRL Follow‑On Quantum Network program. The funding supports development of advanced quantum networking capabilities and further integration of IonQ’s trapped‐ion platform into military‐grade networks. This award marks a significant milestone in commercial quantum network research.
