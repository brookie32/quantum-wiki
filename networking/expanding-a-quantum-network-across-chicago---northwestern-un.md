**Northwestern University expands a quantum network across Chicago**

Northwestern University announced plans to extend its quantum‑communication infrastructure throughout Chicago, linking university labs with nearby research institutions, government agencies, and industry partners. The project aims to enhance secure data transmission and support national quantum‑technology initiatives; detailed timelines, technical specifications, and partner agreements are still forthcoming.
