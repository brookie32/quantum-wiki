arXiv:2611.17906v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We analyze the notion of indefinite causal order (ICO) from the standpoint of the deterministic de Broglie--Bohm theory, based on the notion of trajectories in configuration space. More precisely, we study the physics of the quantum switch (QS) interferometer within a Bohmian framework and show how the notion of causality is preserved in this approach. Furthermore, we investigate device-independent certification and causal inequalities applied to an entangled quantum system, and show that in this regime too, the notions of trajectories and of a strict causal order are perfectly preserved.
