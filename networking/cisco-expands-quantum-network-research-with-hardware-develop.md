Cisco has teamed up with hardware developer Infleqtion to broaden its quantum‑network research efforts. The partnership focuses on jointly developing and testing quantum communication technologies and related infrastructure components. With Infleqtion’s expertise, Cisco aims to accelerate the practical deployment of quantum networking solutions in commercial settings.
