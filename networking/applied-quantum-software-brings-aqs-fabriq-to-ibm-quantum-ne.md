Applied Quantum Software (AQS) has announced the rollout of its AQS FabriQ quantum software platform on the IBM Quantum Network. The integration allows developers to deploy industrial‑grade quantum applications directly onto IBM’s cloud‑based quantum hardware. This partnership aims to accelerate the practical adoption of quantum computing across enterprise workloads.
