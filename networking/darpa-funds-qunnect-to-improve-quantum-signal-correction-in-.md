DARPA has awarded funding to the startup Qunnect for research aimed at enhancing quantum signal correction in optical fiber networks.   The project focuses on developing error‑correction techniques that mitigate noise and losses in fiber channels, thereby improving the reliability and reach of quantum key distribution and other quantum communication protocols.   This investment underscores DARPA’s strategy to accelerate practical, long‑distance quantum network development.
