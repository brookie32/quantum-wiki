Four firms have confirmed plans to connect to Albuquerque’s inaugural quantum networking service, which is being developed by the University of New Mexico in partnership with private-sector partners. The network will provide secure, low‑latency quantum communication for enterprise applications such as high‑speed cloud services and data‑center interconnects. This deployment marks the first operational quantum internet in the United States, highlighting Albuquerque’s emerging role in quantum technology infrastructure.
