arXiv:2611.33421v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Can noisy dynamics generate entanglement that stays useful at long times? Under collective dephasing, a twelve-spin kicked Ising ring evolves from a uniform product state toward an entangled stationary state that is maximally mixed in the sector fixed by the ring's rotations and reflections. Gates and measurements within each half herald a spin pair that teleports an unknown qubit with a mean fidelity of 90.5%. Simulations show that stronger noise correlations extend the time over which the ring yields pairs that beat the classical teleportation limit.
