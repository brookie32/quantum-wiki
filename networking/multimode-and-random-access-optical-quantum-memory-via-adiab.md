The study presents a photon‑echo quantum memory that supports multi‑mode storage with true random access by employing linearly swept adiabatic rephasing pulses that imprint phase shifts to suppress unwanted echoes. Demonstrated on a \(^{171}\)Yb\(^{3+}\):YSO crystal, the protocol stores and retrieves several complex spectro‑temporal photonic modes, achieving reliable readout across eight distinct spectral channels while dramatically reducing the required rephasing pulse intensity and improving mode‑mapping flexibility.
