arXiv:2611.33864v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Depolarizing noise scales trace distances by a common factor. An encoded qubit is autonomous if its reduced input state determines its output. For D=2^nge4, autonomy forces this isotropy at a sharp threshold of (D^2+5)/3 generic encodings, two more than state tomography requires. For Pauli noise, one generic encoding suffices. With one-bit readouts and no quantum memory or reference, switching encodings cuts the worst-case cost for the Pauli test by a factor of D, up to logarithms. For any family, a channel with leakage O(D^{-3/2}) can transmit one bit perfectly and erase another.
