The Quantum Internet Group at the University of Innsbruck announced that it has successfully linked three trapped‑ion quantum computers into a single network, creating a prototype quantum internet link between separate nodes. This demonstrates the feasibility of interconnecting ion‑trap systems over a shared quantum channel and represents an early milestone in building scalable, distributed quantum computing infrastructures.
