A significant step forward for quantum in the UK. IonQ and the @Cambridge_Uni have opened the IonQ Quantum Innovation Centre at the Cavendish Laboratory’s new Ray Dolby Centre, creating a physical home for the University’s largest corporate research partnership. The Centre brings together Cambridge researchers and IonQ specialists across nine inaugural projects, from scaling quantum computers to exploring applications in finance, engineering and cancer research. It will also house a 256-qubit Superion quantum computer and quantum network, providing infrastructure to advance research and technology development. More than a research partnership, this is a long-term investment in the UK’s quantum future. By bringing together technology, infrastructure and scientific expertise, IonQ is helping build the capabilities, skills and opportunities needed to translate quantum science into real-world impact for researchers, start-ups and industry.
