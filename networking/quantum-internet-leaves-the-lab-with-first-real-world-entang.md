Researchers have successfully generated and verified quantum entanglement over a public telecommunications fiber network, marking the first time entanglement has been used in a real‑world telecom environment. The experiment demonstrated that entangled photons can be transmitted across busy fiber links, advancing the feasibility of a quantum internet. These results lay groundwork for future quantum communication protocols integrated with existing telecom infrastructure.
