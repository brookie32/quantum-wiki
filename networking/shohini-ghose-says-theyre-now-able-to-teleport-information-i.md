Shohini Ghose is a quantum physicist and professor at Wilfrid Laurier University who has published on quantum teleportation .  Quantum information teleportation has already been demonstrated in research labs  and  involves transferring information from one location to another without physically transmitting it .  Her team is working on these possibilities by simulating a quantum network on a quantum computer .
