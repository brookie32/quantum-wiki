The study reports the first experimental realization of logical‑state teleportation between two surface‑code patches on an 81‑qubit superconducting processor. By performing a sequence of joint stabilizer measurements and a single‑qubit measurement‑based teleported gate, the authors transferred a logical qubit with an average fidelity exceeding 0.95, well above current fault‑tolerance thresholds. The work demonstrates that surface‑code architectures can support high‑fidelity, long‑range entanglement operations essential for scalable quantum computing.
