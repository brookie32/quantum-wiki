Stony Brook University and Brookhaven National Laboratory announced that they have expanded New York’s quantum communication network, adding new links to enhance secure data transmission across the state. The expansion connects additional research sites through entangled‑photon channels and quantum key distribution technology. According to coverage on Quantum Zeitgeist—a Google News–aggregated feed—this initiative is part of a broader effort to establish New York as a national hub for quantum‐technology infrastructure.
