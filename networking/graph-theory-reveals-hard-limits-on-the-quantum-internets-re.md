**Article Summary**

Graph theory analysis indicates fundamental limits on the maximum geographical reach of a quantum internet, highlighting constraints such as entanglement fidelity and decoherence across long distances. The study applies rigorous network‑theoretic models to predict how far quantum links can extend before performance degrades beyond practical thresholds. These findings suggest that scaling a global quantum network will require overcoming significant physical and informational barriers identified by the mathematical framework.
