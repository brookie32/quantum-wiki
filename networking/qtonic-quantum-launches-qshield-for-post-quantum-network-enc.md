Qtonic Quantum announced the release of **QShield**, a post‑quantum encryption solution aimed at securing network communications against future quantum‑computing attacks. The software integrates state‑of‑the‑art post‑quantum cryptographic algorithms into existing network infrastructures, enabling enterprises to update their security posture without disrupting current operations. QShield targets compliance with upcoming regulatory mandates for quantum‑resistant cybersecurity and includes real‑time threat detection capabilities.
