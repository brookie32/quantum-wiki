The European Union awarded a €47.5 million grant to support the QIA quantum‑network prototype, part of the Quantum Internet Alliance’s efforts to build a secure, high‑capacity testbed for Europe’s first quantum internet. The funding will fund infrastructure development, research and cross‑border collaboration needed to advance quantum communication technology across the continent.
