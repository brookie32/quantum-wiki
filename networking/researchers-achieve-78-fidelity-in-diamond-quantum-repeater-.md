Researchers have achieved a 78 % fidelity in a diamond-based quantum‑repeater node, demonstrating the ability to entangle photons with high precision. The experiment uses color center defects within a diamond lattice to generate telecom‑wavelength photons, marking an important step toward scalable long‑distance quantum communication networks. This result represents one of the highest fidelities reported for solid‑state quantum repeaters to date.
