Qunnect, a provider of quantum‑security solutions, has secured funding and collaboration from U.S. government partners to advance its quantum key distribution (QKD) research. The partnership aims to strengthen national cybersecurity infrastructure by deploying QKD technologies in critical communication networks. TipRanks reports that this backing reflects growing governmental interest in protecting data against emerging quantum threats.
