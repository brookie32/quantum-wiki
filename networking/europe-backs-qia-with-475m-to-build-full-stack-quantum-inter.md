Europe has pledged €47.5 million to the Quantum Internet Alliance (QIA) in support of developing a full‑stack quantum internet infrastructure. This investment underscores Europe’s strategic commitment to advancing quantum networking technologies and fostering a competitive ecosystem for quantum communications. The funding will enable QIA to accelerate research, standardisation and deployment efforts across the continent.
