IonQ has received a $7.5 million award from the U.S. Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL) to advance its quantum networking capabilities.  The grant will fund research into more resilient, secure quantum communication protocols, supporting the development of infrastructure that could enhance national‑security communications.
