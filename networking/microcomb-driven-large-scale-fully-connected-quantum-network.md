The reported study demonstrates a scalable, fully‑connected quantum communication network that relies on two‑photon Hong–Ou–Mandel interference to guarantee user‑to‑user security even when the network provider is untrusted. The authors realize this architecture with integrated soliton microcomb (SMC) devices for dense, parallel frequency generation and photonic‑encoding chips that provide precise optical locking across the network. This platform enables a large number of nodes to share entanglement and secret keys simultaneously, addressing a key scalability hurdle in practical quantum networking deployments.
