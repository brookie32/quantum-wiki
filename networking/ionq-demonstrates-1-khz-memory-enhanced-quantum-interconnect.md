IonQ has achieved a breakthrough in quantum computing with a memory-enhanced quantum interconnect, linking a trapped ion qubit with a diamond-based quantum memory. This system operates at a record-breaking entanglement rate of over 1 kHz with high fidelity, significantly improving upon previous benchmarks. This advancement, supported by collaborations with Element Six and AWS, addresses photon-loss bottlenecks and is a crucial step towards scalable distributed quantum computing networks.
