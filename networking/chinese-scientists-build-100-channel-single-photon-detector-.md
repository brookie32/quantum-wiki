Chinese researchers have developed a 100‑channel single‑photon detection array that can record photons in parallel, significantly boosting data acquisition rates for quantum communication systems. The detector’s architecture uses highly efficient photodiodes and low‑noise readout electronics to provide high sensitivity across all channels, making it well suited for future quantum internet infrastructure. This advancement improves scalability and reliability of quantum networks by enabling simultaneous monitoring of many qubits.
