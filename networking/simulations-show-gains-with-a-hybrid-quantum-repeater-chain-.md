Simulations indicate that a hybrid quantum repeater chain—combining different physical systems for memory and transmission—enhances entanglement distribution compared to conventional repeaters. According to the reported studies, these hybrid configurations deliver higher key rates and extend usable distances in long‑haul quantum networks. The article highlights the potential of such designs as a step toward scalable, high‑bandwidth quantum communication.
