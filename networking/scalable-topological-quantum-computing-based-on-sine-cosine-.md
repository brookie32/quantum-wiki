arXiv:2603.25952v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We propose a scalable framework for quantum information processing based on Matryoshka-type sine-cosine chains, a hierarchy of one-dimensional chiral-symmetric lattices obtained by repeated (P times) square-root construction starting from the Su-Schrieffer-Heeger (SSH) chain. A chain of order P can host 2^{P+2}-2 protected edge states, so a single physical device can encode a qudit of equivalent dimension (or, equivalently, several independent qubits) instead of one qubit per device. We show that (i) the adiabatic defect-transfer step underlying SSH-based topological gates carries over unchanged to the Matryoshka chain, transferring the full set of defect states simultaneously; (ii) a Y-junction braiding protocol then composes single-qudit gate operations in all energy sectors at once, increasing the operation density per physical device; and (iii) the same edge states provide a quantum memory whose capacity scales exponentially with the chain order at fixed physical length. Numerical fidelity analysis under on-site, hopping, and correlated angle disorder shows partial, chiral-symmetry-protected robustness.
