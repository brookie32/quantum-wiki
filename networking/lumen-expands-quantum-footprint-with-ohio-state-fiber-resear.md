They are partnering with The Ohio State University to research quantum network transport over commercial optical fiber, and integrating Fortinet's post-quantum cryptography into their SASE solution to enhance enterprise security. These efforts aim to overcome challenges in quantum signal transmission and protect against future decryption threats.
