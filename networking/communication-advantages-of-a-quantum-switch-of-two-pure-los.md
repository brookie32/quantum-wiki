arXiv:2611.27548v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The quantum switch can enable communication through otherwise unusable channels, a phenomenon known as causal activation. We show that a switch of two identical pure-loss channels can achieve strictly greater energy-constrained classical and quantum capacities than their sequential use, even when two-way classical communication is available. Remarkably, the switch also activates quantum communication over a range of transmissivities for which the sequential channel has zero quantum capacity.
