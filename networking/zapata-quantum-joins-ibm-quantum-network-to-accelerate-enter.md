Zapata Quantum has joined the IBM Quantum Network to accelerate enterprise application development by integrating IBM's superconducting QPUs with Zapata's Quantum Pilot platform. This partnership aims to bridge the quantum application gap and develop mission-critical quantum software workflows for various industries. Their collaboration builds on previous research, including a generative model study for cancer mutation inhibitors and foundational work on variational quantum factoring.
