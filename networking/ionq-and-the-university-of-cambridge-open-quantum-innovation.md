IonQ and the University of Cambridge have opened the IonQ Quantum Innovation Centre at the Cavendish Laboratory, deploying a 256-qubit Superion quantum computer and a quantum network. This initiative, supported by Innovate UK and aligning with the UK National Quantum Strategy, will host nine inaugural research projects and aims to expand the regional quantum workforce.
