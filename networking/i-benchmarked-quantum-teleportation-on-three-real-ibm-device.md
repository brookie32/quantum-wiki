A researcher benchmarked six-state quantum teleportation on three real IBM quantum processors (Fez, Kingston, and Phoenix) with mean fidelities of 0.901, 0.961, and 0.958 respectively.   The ±i quantum states showed perfect performance in bare control measurements but performed lowest in teleportation, consistent with dephasing during the measurement-and-correction window.
