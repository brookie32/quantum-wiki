arXiv:2609.16364v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The coherent conversion between microwave and optical photons, known as quantum transduction, is critical for connecting superconducting processors to optical networks. Existing methods are limited by complications associated with optical pumping. We propose a pump-free microwave-optical Bell-pair source for teleportation-based transduction. Using a spin or atomic system resonantly coupled to optical and microwave cavities, the scheme generates loss-robust heralded Bell pairs. Across color centers, atomic ensembles, and phonon-mediated systems, this assembly achieves kilohertz-range heralding rates with high fidelity.
