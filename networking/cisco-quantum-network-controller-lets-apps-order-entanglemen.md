Cisco has introduced a quantum‑network controller that allows software applications to request and order quantum entanglement resources on demand. The controller exposes APIs enabling developers to integrate entangled links into their services without managing physical infrastructure, facilitating faster deployment of secure communications such as QKD. This move positions Cisco as a key enabler for adopting quantum networking in commercial environments.
