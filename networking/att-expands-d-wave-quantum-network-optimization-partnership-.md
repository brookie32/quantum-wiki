AT&T has expanded its strategic partnership with D‑Wave Systems to deploy the company’s quantum‑annealing technology for large‑scale network optimization. Building on initial pilots that used D‑Wave processors to improve routing, traffic flow, and resource allocation in telecommunications networks, AT&T is scaling these solutions into production environments to enhance performance and reduce operational costs. The collaboration signals a broader move toward integrating quantum computing with real‑world networking infrastructure.
