The Quantum Internet Alliance has received €47.5 million in European Union funding to support the second phase of its research program aimed at developing a scalable quantum internet infrastructure. This investment follows an initial funding round and will enable further development of key technologies, such as quantum repeaters and secure transmission protocols. The information is sourced from the Telecompaper report summarising the EU’s allocation for this project.
