Cisco has announced it is building a control‑layer platform for quantum networks to enable the management and orchestration of quantum communications infrastructure. The new layer will integrate with existing classical networking systems, providing routing, resource allocation, and security services such as quantum key distribution within a future quantum internet architecture. This development marks a significant step toward practical, large‑scale deployment of quantum networking capabilities.<|fim-end|>
