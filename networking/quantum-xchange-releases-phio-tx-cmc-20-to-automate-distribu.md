Quantum XChange has released Phio TX-CMC 2.0, a new version of their centralized management console for post-quantum network security. This update introduces automated provisioning and post-quantum cryptographic tunnels to help enterprises and federal agencies transition to PQC standards. The platform is designed to secure data in motion without requiring a complete network overhaul, addressing challenges like "harvest now, decrypt later" attacks and the complexities of migrating to new cryptographic standards.
