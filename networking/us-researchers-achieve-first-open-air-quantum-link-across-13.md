US researchers have successfully demonstrated the first open‑air quantum communication link spanning 13 miles in New York, a milestone highlighted in recent articles from *Interesting Engineering* and aggregated by Google News.  
The experiment shows that entangled or quantum‑encoded states can be transmitted over long free‑space distances without fiber cabling, paving the way for secure quantum key distribution and the expansion of a scalable quantum internet.
