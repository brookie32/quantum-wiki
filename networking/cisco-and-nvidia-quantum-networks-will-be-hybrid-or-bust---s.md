Cisco and Nvidia are collaborating on developing quantum‐networking capabilities, a partnership closely monitored by industry analysts such as those at SDxCentral. Recent coverage emphasizes that the viability of their quantum initiatives will likely depend on hybrid solutions that integrate classical networking infrastructure rather than relying solely on pure quantum technology. This hybrid approach is seen as essential to achieve practical, scalable quantum networks before potential program failures arise.
