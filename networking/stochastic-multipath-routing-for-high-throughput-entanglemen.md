The paper introduces a stochastic multipath routing protocol for quantum repeater networks that distributes entanglement over several parallel paths rather than a single linear chain. By employing probabilistic path selection and dynamic resource allocation, the scheme increases end‑to‑end key rates and reduces latency compared with conventional linear‑repeater architectures. Numerical simulations on realistic network topologies confirm significant throughput improvements while preserving fault tolerance against link failures.
