QuTech is coordinating the next 3.5-year phase of the Quantum Internet Alliance (QIA), backed by €47.5 million (52 million USD) from the European Commission. The program will integrate quantum repeaters, integrated photonics, control electronics, and network software into a programmable full-stack quantum internet prototype.
