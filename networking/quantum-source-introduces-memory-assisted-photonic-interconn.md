Quantum Source has introduced QS-LINK, a memory-assisted photonic interconnect that unifies multi-modal quantum computing. This technology overcomes scaling limitations in multi-processor quantum networks by using a single cavity-trapped rubidium-87 atom as an asynchronous quantum memory buffer to establish remote entanglement between separate quantum processing units (QPUs). QS-LINK significantly reduces the attempts needed to achieve entanglement compared to traditional methods, operating at room temperature and supporting various hardware modalities.
