arXiv:2610.30761v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Port-based teleportation (PBT) is a variant of quantum teleportation that does not require corrections on the teleported state: starting with N shared EPR states, Alice can teleport a qudit to Bob by performing a measurement on her system and informing Bob which of his N `ports' to retrieve the state, up to infidelity decaying with N. Existing quantum circuits of PBT rely on representation theory tools and the mixed Schur transform. In this note, we report an elementary efficient circuit for PBT, which has depth operatorname{polylog}(N,d). As a corollary, this gives a simple proof that all n-qubit unitaries can be parallelized to operatorname{poly}(n) depth, recovering a recent result due to Nehoran--Slote--Yuen and OpenAI.
