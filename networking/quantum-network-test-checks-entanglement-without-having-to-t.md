The study introduces a protocol for testing entanglement in quantum communication networks without relying on trusted photon‑detector devices. By employing device‑independent measurements, the method ensures that entangled states can be verified even when detectors may be faulty or maliciously biased. This advances secure quantum networking by enabling reliable entanglement verification under realistic practical conditions.
