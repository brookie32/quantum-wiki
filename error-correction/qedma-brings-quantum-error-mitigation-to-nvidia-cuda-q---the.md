QEDMA has integrated its quantum error‑mitigation technology into NVIDIA’s CUDA‑Q platform, enabling developers to reduce noise in quantum circuits executed on hybrid classical–quantum systems. The partnership expands CUDA‑Q’s capabilities by providing automated post‑processing techniques that correct gate errors without significant overhead. This update is part of ongoing efforts to improve the reliability and performance of quantum applications through industry collaboration.
