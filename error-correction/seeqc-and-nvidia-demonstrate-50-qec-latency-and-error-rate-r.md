SEEQC and NVIDIA have demonstrated a 50% reduction in quantum error correction (QEC) latency and logical error rates. This was achieved by integrating SEEQC's chip-scale pre-decoder technology with NVIDIA's Ising AI model family. The new architecture filters and compresses data closer to the quantum processing unit, significantly improving communication and overall performance for quantum AI workloads.
