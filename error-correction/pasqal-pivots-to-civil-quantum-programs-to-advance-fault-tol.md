Pasqal has shifted its focus toward civil quantum‑program initiatives to further its effort on developing a practical fault‑tolerant quantum computing roadmap. According to the recent HPCwire report, this pivot aims to accelerate progress in scalable quantum hardware and software that can meet industrial and governmental requirements for reliable quantum computation. The company’s strategy is intended to integrate research findings from academia with real‑world application demands as part of its long‑term technology development plan.
