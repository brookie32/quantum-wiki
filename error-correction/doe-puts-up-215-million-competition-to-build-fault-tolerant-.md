In March 2024 the U.S. Department of Energy announced a $215 million prize competition aimed at accelerating the development of fault‑tolerant quantum computers. The program invites researchers and industry teams to submit proposals that demonstrate scalable quantum processors capable of long qubit lifetimes, low error rates, and practical quantum error‑correction techniques. Winning projects will receive substantial prize money for hitting predefined milestones in qubit coherence, gate fidelity, and circuit depth.
