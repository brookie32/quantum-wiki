The U.S. Department of Energy has announced the Quantum Genesis Q Competition, which provides $215 million in funding to advance fault‑tolerant quantum computing technology. The program invites research teams and companies to develop designs and prototypes that deliver reliable, error‑corrected qubit operations for scalable quantum processors.
