AMD has announced a new investment partnership with PsiQuantum, aimed at jointly developing pair‑co‑design solutions for fault‑tolerant quantum computing systems. The collaboration seeks to leverage AMD’s hardware expertise and PsiQuantum’s quantum processing technology to accelerate scalable, error-resistant quantum architectures. This move positions both companies to contribute critical components toward the next generation of practical quantum computers.
