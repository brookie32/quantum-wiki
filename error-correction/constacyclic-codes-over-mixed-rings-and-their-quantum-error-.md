The article surveys constacyclic codes defined over mixed (non‑field) ring structures, detailing their algebraic properties and weight distributions. It establishes key connections between these classical codes and the construction of stabilizer or CSS–type quantum error‑correcting codes, outlining how ring‐based parameters translate into quantum code performance. The work offers a framework for leveraging mixed‐ring constacyclic codes in practical quantum communication systems.
