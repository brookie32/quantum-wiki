AMD has formed a partnership with Israeli startup PsiQuantum (PsyQ) to develop fault‑tolerant, utility‑scale quantum infrastructure. The collaboration leverages AMD’s silicon photonics expertise and PsiQuantum’s photonic qubit technology to build scalable, commercially viable quantum systems that meet error‑correction requirements for practical applications.
