QuEra and Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE) have announced a partnership focused on developing fault‑tolerant quantum computing solutions for high‑performance computing (HPC). The collaboration aims to integrate reliable quantum processors with HPE’s HPC infrastructure to enable scalable, dependable quantum applications. Current public details are limited, but the alliance is reported as an effort to bring practical, error‑corrected quantum technology into large‑scale computational environments.
