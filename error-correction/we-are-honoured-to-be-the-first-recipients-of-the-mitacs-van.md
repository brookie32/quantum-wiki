We are honoured to be the first recipients of the Mitacs Vanguard Award, an award that recognizes a Canadian company that has worked with @MitacsCanada to “translate homegrown research and talent into world-changing innovation.” Mitacs has been a close collaborator since 2019, together, we’ve supported over 40 intern projects with graduate students from across Canada. Those interns have contributed to our architecture, error correction, algorithms, applications and software, including the work that laid out our blueprint for a fault-tolerant photonic quantum computer.
