Quantinuum has announced that its quantum error‑correction scheme now achieves a fidelity of 99.9995 %, translating to only about five erroneous operations per one million logic gates.  This milestone brings the hardware closer to practical fault‑tolerant quantum computing and may accelerate commercial deployment of noisy intermediate‑scale quantum (NISQ) devices.  The result was highlighted in multiple global news outlets, aggregated by Google News for comprehensive coverage.
