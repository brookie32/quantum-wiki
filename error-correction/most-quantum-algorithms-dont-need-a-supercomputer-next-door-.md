A recent study concludes that most quantum algorithms can be executed without the need for a dedicated supercomputer. The findings suggest that typical quantum computations can run on more modest, distributed hardware rather than requiring high‑performance machines. Thus, the barrier to applying quantum algorithms is lower than previously assumed.
