arXiv:2610.29457v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We construct short triorthogonal codes for qudits of odd prime dimension by generalizing doubling, which combines a triorthogonal code with a code admitting a transversal phase gate, and raises the distance by up to two. This gives codes with parameters [[28,1,4]]_3, [[37,1,5]]_3, [[72,1,6]]_3, [[118,1,8]]_3, [[33,1,5]]_5 and [[20,1,4]]_7, each shorter than the cyclic, concatenated and Reed-Muller codes of the same distance, and a doubling construction of the [[15,1,3]]_3 code. When the columns of a generator matrix are regarded as charged points, triorthogonality becomes the vanishing of multipole moments up to octupole order, and doubling becomes a double layer of opposite charges; this picture explains why inputs with nontrivial coefficients can give shorter codes. We also show by exhaustive search that the shortest codes of distance three have length 11 for p=5 and length 7 for p=7. Combined with high-rate distance-two codes, the new codes reduce the cost of qutrit magic state distillation by up to an order of magnitude in regimes with high input noise.
