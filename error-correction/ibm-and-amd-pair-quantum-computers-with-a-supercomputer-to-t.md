IBM and AMD have collaborated to integrate IBM’s quantum processors with an AMD‑based supercomputer, creating a hybrid platform that runs simultaneous quantum–classical simulations. The joint setup is used to explore and test error‑mitigation techniques for near‑term quantum devices, allowing researchers to benchmark performance and validate theoretical corrections in real time. This partnership exemplifies efforts to accelerate practical quantum computing by combining state‑of‑the‑art hardware across both paradigms.
