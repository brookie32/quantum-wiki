The United States announced a $215 million competition aimed at accelerating the development of fault‑tolerant quantum computers.  The contest invites teams—whether academic, industry, or start‑up—to create scalable quantum hardware capable of robust error correction and reliable computation.  It is part of a broader national initiative to establish U.S. leadership in quantum technology.
