An AI decoder that was trained solely on synthetic data has achieved superior results when applied to Google's experimental quantum computing data, indicating that high‑quality simulated datasets can effectively train models for real quantum hardware tasks. The study shows improved decoding accuracy and efficiency compared with decoders trained only on real measurements, highlighting synthetic training as a valuable strategy for advancing quantum error correction.
