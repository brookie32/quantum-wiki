arXiv:2605.00026v4 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We characterize where, in the noise parameter space of the uniform dephasing--depolarizing channel mathcal N_gamma^elta=mathcal E_elta!irc!mathcal D_gamma, a deterministic, nonlinear, target-informed denoising heuristic yields its largest fidelity gain. The procedure pulls the off-diagonal magnitudes of the noisy state toward those of a known target, with efficiency set by a SWAP-test-purified catalyst; it is not a quantum channel, and target access is an explicit classical resource, so the results describe a benchmark procedure, not blind error correction. Our main tool is the covariant purification map mathcal P_cov(rho)=(rho+rho^2)/(1+Tr,rho^2), an exact closed form for one SWAP-test purification round (a rederivation of symmetrization purification: Barenco et al., Cirac--Ekert--Macchiavello) that reduces the catalyst to a scalar eigenvalue iteration. With it we derive the doinfty fidelity-gain peak location on Haar-random pure states (Theorem~3): gamma_{rm peak}(d)ogamma^star(r,elta), with gamma^star(2,0.1)=0.4725 and limiting magnitude 0.2262. Bootstrap-quantified sweeps to d=256 are consistent with both limits. The peak is resource- and protocol-dependent: a catalyst-only reference moves it from approx0.50 to approx0.34, and one purification round instead of two to approx0.39. Bell and uniform d=4 states admit unique-peak theorems for the r=0, elta=0 protocol member. All results are reproducible from the open-source exttt{organic-qc-bench} package with seed~42.
