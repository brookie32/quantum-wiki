Xanadu has set a roadmap for scalable quantum hardware, aiming to achieve fault‑tolerant operation by 2029 and construct a system of roughly 1,000 logical qubits by 2031. The company’s strategy centers on error‑corrected photonic processors to enable practical, large‑scale quantum computation. This plan was reported in a Yahoo Finance coverage piece summarizing Xanadu’s long‑term milestones.
