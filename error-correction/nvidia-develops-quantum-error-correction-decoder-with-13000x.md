NVIDIA has introduced a novel decoder for quantum error‑correction that delivers roughly a **13,000‑fold speedup** over existing methods. The system leverages GPU parallelism to rapidly process error‑syndrome data—particularly for surface‑code protocols—thereby shortening decoding time and reducing latency in quantum processors. This advancement marks a significant step toward practical, fault‑tolerant quantum computing.
