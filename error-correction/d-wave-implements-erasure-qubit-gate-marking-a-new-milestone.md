D‑Wave has announced the first experimental implementation of an “erasure‑qubit” gate on its quantum processor, a technique that identifies and discards faulty qubits rather than attempting to correct their state.  This development is viewed as a significant milestone in practical quantum error correction, potentially increasing both the reliability and scalability of D‑Wave’s quantum annealing architecture.  The move underscores the growing focus on robustness in near‑term quantum devices and may accelerate progress toward fault‑tolerant quantum computing.
