Researchers at Quantum Zeitgeist have demonstrated that relative entropy, a statistical measure of the distance between probability distributions, can be used to establish tighter bounds on error rates in quantum systems. By applying this technique to noise models in quantum operations, they provide more accurate predictions for fault‑tolerant computing thresholds and error mitigation protocols. This advancement offers a useful tool for improving the reliability of near‑term quantum devices.
