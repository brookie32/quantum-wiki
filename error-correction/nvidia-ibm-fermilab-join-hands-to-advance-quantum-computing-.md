In March 2024 NVIDIA, IBM and Fermilab announced a collaborative initiative to accelerate quantum‑computing research. By combining NVIDIA’s GPU‑accelerated software stack, IBM’s commercial quantum platforms, and Fermilab’s expertise in large‑scale simulation and particle‑physics data analysis, the partners aim to develop next‑generation quantum processors and algorithms that can be deployed across scientific and industrial workloads. The partnership also includes joint educational programs to build a workforce skilled in both quantum theory and practical hardware development.
