NVIDIA has launched the CUDA‑Q platform to enable developers to test fault‑tolerant quantum applications using simulation on existing hardware. The platform integrates with NVIDIA’s AI and HPC stack, allowing users to design, simulate, and evaluate quantum algorithms within familiar development environments. Its release aims to accelerate progress toward practical, error-resilient quantum software.
