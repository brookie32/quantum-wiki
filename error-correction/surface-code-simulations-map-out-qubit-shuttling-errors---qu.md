Researchers used large‑scale surface‑code simulations to quantify errors that occur when qubits are physically relocated—“shuttled”—within quantum processors such as trapped‑ion or superconducting architectures. The study identified specific error pathways, including motional heating and decoherence during transport, that degrade logical gate fidelity. These quantitative results provide benchmarks for designing fault‑tolerant shuttling protocols and help guide scaling strategies in future quantum hardware.
