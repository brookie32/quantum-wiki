arXiv:2610.12782v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We introduce coset lifted product (CLP) codes, a family of quantum LDPC codes that combine the protograph structure of lifted product codes with the coset action of Aydin, Tamo, and Barg ite{AydinTamoBarg2026}. Given a finite group G, a subgroup H le G that need not be normal, a protograph A over mathbb F_2[G], and a protograph B over mathbb F_2[N_G(H)], the two protographs act on the cosets G/H from the left and from the right to produce quantum CSS codes. Further, for groups of odd order, we give an explicit formula for the dimension as a sum over the irreducible representations rho of G of hypergraph product dimensions. A search over 14,843 pairs (G,H) with H restricted to be non-normal and 120 le n le 400 yields certified codes that exceed the best published value of Q=kd^2/n at check weights 6,7 and 9, i.e., we obtain [[360,20,20]] at w=6, a [[360,24,20]] code at w=7, and codes [[252,16,18]] and [[288,16,20]] at w=9. Our search also produce high-distance codes with parameters [[168,10,14]], [[240,8,20]] at w=6 and [[216,8,18]] at w=7.
