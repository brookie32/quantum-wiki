The U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) has announced a $215 million program aimed at establishing America’s first fault‑tolerant quantum computer. The initiative will create a national competition among researchers and firms, providing substantial research funding and infrastructure support for the development of scalable, error‑corrected quantum computing systems. This effort marks a significant governmental push to accelerate the U.S. quantum technology roadmap.
