Researchers at the Max Planck Institute for the Science of Light developed a new method for designing quantum error-correcting codes using large language models to discover families of quantum low-density parity-check codes.   The structured concept evolution framework moves beyond traditional design problems by evolving algebraic specifications, achieving a reduction in cost per logical qubit by roughly an order of magnitude compared to the surface code.   This demonstrates the potential of artificial intelligence in advancing quantum information science.
