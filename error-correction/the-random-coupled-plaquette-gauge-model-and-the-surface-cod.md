The authors introduce the **random coupled‑plaquette gauge model (RCPGM)**, an enhanced statistical‑mechanical mapping that explicitly accounts for Y‑error correlations in the surface code with noisy syndrome measurements.  Parallel‑Tempering Monte‑Carlo simulations show that this approach raises the logical‑error threshold to about **6 %** under phenomenological depolarizing plus bit‑flip syndrome noise (compared to ~4.3 % for the earlier “uncoupled” random plaquette gauge model) and to roughly **1.4 %** in a realistic circuit‑level noise setting (vs. ~0.7 % before).  The results expand the available mappings for quantum error correction and set new benchmarks for practical decoder designs.
