The Seoul Economic Daily editorial argues that South Korea should prioritize research and investment in quantum error‑correction techniques rather than simply increasing the number of qubits in existing devices. The piece contends that robust error mitigation is essential for scalable, fault‑tolerant quantum computing and will yield longer‑term technological gains. Accordingly, it calls on policymakers to shift funding and development focus toward algorithms and hardware designed to correct quantum errors.
