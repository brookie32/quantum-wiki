Researchers demonstrate that simplifying error models by limiting the number of logical connections (edges) improves quantum error‑correction performance on existing hardware. A reduced-edge model decreases correlated noise between qubits, allowing more accurate calibration and shorter correction cycles. The study shows that even modest reductions in edge density can yield noticeable gains in fidelity for near‑term quantum processors.
