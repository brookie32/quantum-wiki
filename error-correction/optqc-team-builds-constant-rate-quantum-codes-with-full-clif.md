In a recent release, the OptQC team announced the construction of constant‑rate quantum error‑correction codes that support the full Clifford group directly. These codes preserve a fixed ratio between logical and physical qubits while enabling all Clifford operations without additional overhead, potentially improving the efficiency of fault‑tolerant quantum algorithms. The innovation was highlighted in a feature by Quantum Zeitgeist, which aggregated coverage from global news sources.
