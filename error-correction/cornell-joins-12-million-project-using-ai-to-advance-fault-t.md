Cornell University has joined a $12 million collaborative effort aimed at speeding the development of fault‑tolerant quantum computing through artificial intelligence. The program brings together academic and industry partners to design AI-driven error‑correction algorithms and simulate larger qubit systems, with the goal of making practical quantum computers more scalable. Funding supports research that identifies optimal control strategies and improves the reliability of emerging quantum hardware.
