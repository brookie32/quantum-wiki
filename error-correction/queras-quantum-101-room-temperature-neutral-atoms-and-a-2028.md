QuEra Technologies is advancing a platform of room‑temperature neutral‑atom qubits using optical tweezers, with the goal of scaling to millions of entangled atoms.  
The company’s roadmap targets the first fault‑tolerant quantum machine by 2028, requiring integrated error‑correction and large‑scale atom trapping.  
Dealroom tracks QuEra’s funding rounds and key milestones as it moves toward a practical, fault‑tolerant quantum computer.
