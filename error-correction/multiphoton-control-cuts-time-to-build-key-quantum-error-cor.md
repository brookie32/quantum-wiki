Multiphoton control techniques have been shown to accelerate the construction of essential quantum error‑correction codes, significantly reducing the time required compared to conventional methods. Researchers reported that by leveraging simultaneous multi‑photon interactions, they achieved faster state preparation and initialization for stabilizer code implementations, a critical step toward scalable fault‑tolerant quantum computing. The findings suggest multiphoton control could streamline experimental workflows in emerging quantum hardware platforms.
