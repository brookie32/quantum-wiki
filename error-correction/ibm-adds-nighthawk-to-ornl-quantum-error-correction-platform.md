IBM announced that it has integrated its Nighthawk technology into Oak Ridge National Laboratory’s (ORNL) quantum error correction platform, enhancing the lab’s ability to develop fault‑tolerant quantum computing architectures. The collaboration aims to accelerate research in quantum error mitigation and hardware–software co‑design at ORNL, bringing a new generation of optimization tools to the national laboratory’s quantum infrastructure.
