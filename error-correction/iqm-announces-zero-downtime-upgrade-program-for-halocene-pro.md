IQM Quantum Computers has launched a zero-downtime upgrade program for its Halocene quantum error-correction product line, enabling a seamless transition from 150-qubit processors to systems exceeding 1,000 qubits. This program, designed for HPC and enterprise data centers, ensures continuous operation during hardware upgrades by installing new units alongside existing ones. It also integrates with current software toolchains and allows for hybrid AI and simulation workflows with on-site data residency.
