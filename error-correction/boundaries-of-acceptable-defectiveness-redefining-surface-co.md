This Nature article investigates how quantum surface‑code error correction behaves when the underlying qubit errors are non‑uniform (heterogeneous). The authors derive new limits on the density of defective elements that a topological code can tolerate while still preserving logical fidelity, showing that tolerances shift from the conventional homogeneous‑noise thresholds. These results provide design guidelines for building more robust quantum processors under realistic noise conditions.
