Diraq and Dell Technologies have partnered to integrate silicon spin-qubit quantum processors with classical HPC and AI server infrastructure, with Dell deploying an HPC server cluster directly inside Diraq's Sydney lab for ultra-low-latency quantum-classical interconnect and real-time control. The collaboration focuses on hybrid orchestration software for automated qubit calibration and error correction, aiming to scale CMOS-compatible silicon QPUs into standard data centers for applications in optimization, logistics, finance, and AI-driven drug discovery.
