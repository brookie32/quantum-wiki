Pasqal, a quantum‑technology startup, has been selected to head an EU Defence Fund–backed initiative focused on fault‑tolerant quantum computing. The project aims to accelerate the development of scalable, reliable quantum processors that can support advanced computational tasks relevant to defence scenarios. Funding from the European Union’s defence budget will support research and infrastructure needed to bring these next‑generation quantum systems closer to operational readiness.
