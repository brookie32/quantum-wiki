A recent report highlights that quantum error‑correction schemes can be substantially improved by actively monitoring and correcting errors in “helper” or auxiliary qubits. By tracking these ancillary state errors, the overall logical error rate of a quantum processor can be reduced, leading to higher fault tolerance with fewer physical resources. The article summarizes current experimental progress toward implementing this enhanced correction strategy in real quantum hardware.
