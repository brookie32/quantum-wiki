arXiv:2611.24044v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Reliable assessment of quantum error correction requires understanding how coherent errors affect logical performance. Although tensor networks naturally capture coherent interference, entanglement growth and large qubit counts make repeated sampling costly. Here we develop a compiled Clifford-Augmented Matrix Product State (CAMPS) framework that reuses Clifford transformations across shots, recycles qubits released by measurements, evolves a compressed residual state, and accelerates sampling through rotation fusion and compilation tailored to the MPS layout. Surface-code benchmarks under fixed-sign coherent noise yield accurate logical error estimates at small bond dimensions, while random-sign simulations up to distance d=9 and on the [[72,12,6]] bivariate bicycle code match the Pauli-twirling approximation results. Compared with uncompiled CAMPS, compiled CAMPS converges at smaller bond dimensions and achieves order-of-magnitude sampling speedups at equal bond dimension, opening a scalable route to studying coherent error propagation in larger fault-tolerant circuits.
