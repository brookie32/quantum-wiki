In a new joint paper, PsiQuantum and @AMD researchers have introduced PRISM, a machine-learning decoder for real-time error correction in photonic quantum computers. As part of the two companies' recently announced strategic partnership, these findings underscore how PsiQuantum and AMD are bringing together industry-leading teams, technologies, and methodologies to prepare critical industries for the arrival of fault-tolerant, utility-scale quantum computing.
