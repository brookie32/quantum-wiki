PsiQuantum and Brookhaven National Laboratory have announced a formal collaboration to advance quantum computing technology. The partnership combines Brookhaven’s advanced laboratory facilities with PsiQuantum’s expertise in silicon‑photonic qubits, targeting scalable, error‑corrected quantum processors. Both organizations aim to accelerate progress toward practical, fault‑tolerant quantum devices through joint research and development efforts.
