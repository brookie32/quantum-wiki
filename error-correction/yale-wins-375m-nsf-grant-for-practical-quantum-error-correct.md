Yale University has secured a U.S. National Science Foundation award of **$37.5 million** to establish the first Practical Quantum Error Correction Center (PQECC). The grant will fund research, development, and deployment of robust quantum error‑correction techniques essential for scalable quantum computing. As announced by HPCwire, the center aims to accelerate progress toward fault‑tolerant quantum systems.
