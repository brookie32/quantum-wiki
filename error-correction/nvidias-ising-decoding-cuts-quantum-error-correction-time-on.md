NVIDIA has implemented its Ising‑decoding algorithm on Amazon Web Services, dramatically reducing the runtime of quantum error‑correction routines used in near‑term quantum applications. By reformulating the error‑correction problem as an Ising model that can be solved efficiently on NVIDIA’s GPU‑based AI hardware, the approach shortens processing times and improves overall throughput for cloud‑quantum workloads. The result is a more practical, cost‑effective use of quantum resources in commercial settings.
