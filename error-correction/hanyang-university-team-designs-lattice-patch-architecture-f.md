Hanyang University researchers have introduced a novel lattice‑patch architecture aimed at improving the mapping of surface‑code quantum error‑correction circuits. This design restructures the qubit layout into modular patches that can be efficiently tiled, potentially reducing resource overhead and simplifying logical gate implementation. The development is reported as a step toward more scalable and fault‑tolerant quantum processors.
