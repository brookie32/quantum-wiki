arXiv:2610.22315v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Classical simulation of magic state factories, including distillation, cultivation, and code switching, is challenging because of their substantial non-Clifford content. We introduce exttt{magna} (magic-state-factory assessment via global network algebra), a classical simulator that simulates such protocols as cheaply as Clifford circuits. exttt{magna} expresses the figures of merit of a protocol, namely the acceptance rate and the fidelity of the accepted states, as tensor networks, which it reduces globally by exploiting a structural correspondence with transversal gates on quantum codes. exttt{magna} further supports feedforward conditioned on arbitrary functions of mid-circuit measurement outcomes, including decoder outputs. We simulate Bravyi--Haah distillation with up to 100 output magic states at a cost that does not grow exponentially in the number of outputs, and achieve state-of-the-art sampling times for distance-5 magic state cultivation on both color codes and RP^2 surface codes. Finally, we validate nonlinear feedforward on a logical-level distillation protocol with 15 rounds of decoder-dependent feedforward. exttt{magna} aims to contribute to the key infrastructure for the large-scale evaluation and optimization of magic state factories.
