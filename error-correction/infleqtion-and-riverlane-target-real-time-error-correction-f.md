Infleqtion and Riverlane are collaborating to develop real‑time error‑correction techniques for neutral‑atom quantum computers, aiming to improve coherence times and gate fidelity. Their approach focuses on integrating fast feedback protocols directly into the atom trapping and manipulation hardware. According to recent coverage aggregated by Google News, this work represents a significant step toward scalable, fault‑tolerant photonic‑neutral atom quantum processors.
