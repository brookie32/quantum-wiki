Pasqal, a French quantum‑technology startup, has been selected as the leading partner in an EU‑funded consortium focused on scalable quantum error correction for neutral‑atom qubits. The project, supported by a Horizon Europe grant, brings together European academic institutions and industry partners to develop fault‑tolerant hardware and software architectures that can be integrated into commercial quantum processors. By improving qubit coherence and algorithm reliability, the effort aims to accelerate the transition from experimental prototypes to robust, large‑scale quantum computing systems.
