IBM and AMD have announced a collaboration with Oak Ridge National Laboratory to develop advanced quantum error‑correction techniques aimed at enhancing qubit reliability for large-scale quantum processors. The partnership will leverage Oak Ridge’s high‑performance computing resources, IBM’s expertise in quantum hardware and software, and AMD’s accelerator technology to test and refine error‑resilience protocols across different qubit platforms. This initiative is intended to accelerate progress toward fault‑tolerant quantum computation by jointly validating new error‑correction codes and improving system stability for future commercial applications.
