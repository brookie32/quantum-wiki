U.S. federal agencies are funding a $300 million initiative to develop advanced “quantum‑clock” hardware aimed at enhancing blockchain security for Bitcoin and Ethereum. The program seeks to create ultra‑stable time‑keeping devices that can detect timing attacks on mining equipment, thereby reducing vulnerabilities in Proof‑of‑Work consensus mechanisms. CoinDesk reports the push as part of a broader effort to strengthen cryptographic infrastructure against emerging quantum threats.
