Oratomic has secured a $775 million investment to accelerate its development of fault‑tolerant quantum computing systems. The capital will support research into scalable error‑correcting architectures and hardware advancements aimed at achieving practical, large‑scale quantum processors. This funding follows growing interest in building robust, reliable quantum machines capable of outperforming classical computers on complex tasks.
