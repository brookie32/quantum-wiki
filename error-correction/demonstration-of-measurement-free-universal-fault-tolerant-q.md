The preprint “Demonstration of Measurement‑Free Universal Fault‑Tolerant Quantum Computation” reports a proof‑of‑concept experiment that implements logical quantum gates within a fault‑tolerant code without relying on mid‑circuit syndrome measurements. By replacing measurement–based error correction with purely unitary operations, the authors reduce overhead and preserve qubit coherence while demonstrating universal gate capability on an encoded logical qubit. The work suggests a viable path toward scalable fault‑tolerant quantum processors that avoid the latency and hardware complexity of frequent measurements.
