New paper out: Check what Edwin and Mike did with our fantastic team at @IonQ_Inc: - QEC experiments with 9 different codes from 3 families. - Quantum LDPC codes with breakeven performance. - Logical error rate up to 9x better than a previous experiment on superconducting qubits. - All that without any ion transport or coolant ion thanks to a first-of-its kind implementation of the OMG architecture. Thank you Edwin Tham, Michael Goldman, Shantanu Debnath, Ashay P., Jyothi Saraladevi, Jason Nguyen, Erik Nielsen, Neal Pisenti, Ken Wright, @johnkgamble
