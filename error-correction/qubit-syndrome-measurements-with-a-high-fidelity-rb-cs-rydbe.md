arXiv:2603.13492v4 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We demonstrate an inter-species entangling Rydberg gate between rubidium (Rb) and cesium (Cs) atoms with fidelity mathcal F = 0.975pm 0.002. The two-species atom array enables in-place quantum non-demolition (QND) qubit measurements which are a key capability for quantum error correction. We demonstrate this functionality with multi-atom error syndrome measurements achieving QND measurement fidelities of {mathcal F}_{rm QND} = 0.933(12) and 0.865(17) for two- and three-qubit plaquettes, respectively.
