Nvidia has announced the launch of an orchestration layer specifically for fault‑tolerant quantum computing. The new platform is designed to simplify programming, scheduling and error correction across diverse quantum hardware, enabling developers to build more reliable quantum applications. It represents the company's effort to bridge classical and quantum systems, helping accelerate practical, scalable quantum computing solutions.
