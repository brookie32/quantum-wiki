Riverlane and Nvidia have leveraged artificial‑intelligence techniques to accelerate quantum error correction (QEC), a key hurdle for building reliable quantum processors. By training deep‑learning models on GPU hardware, the partners can more quickly identify and correct qubit errors, reducing latency in logical‑qubit operations. This development is expected to speed progress toward fault‑tolerant quantum computers by improving QEC efficiency at scale.
