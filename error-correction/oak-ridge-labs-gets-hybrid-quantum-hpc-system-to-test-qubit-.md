Oak Ridge National Laboratory has installed a new hybrid quantum–high‑performance computing (HPC) platform to facilitate advanced qubit error‑correction research. The system couples quantum processors with classical HPC resources, enabling rapid simulation and real‑time feedback for error mitigation experiments. This development positions Oak Ridge at the forefront of experimental work on scalable quantum computing architectures.
