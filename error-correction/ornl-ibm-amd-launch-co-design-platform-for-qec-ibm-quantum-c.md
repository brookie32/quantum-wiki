In 2026 Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL), IBM, and AMD announced a co‑design platform aimed at speeding up research in quantum error correction (QEC). The platform integrates IBM’s quantum computing software stack with AMD’s hardware acceleration capabilities, allowing researchers to jointly design, simulate, and test QEC codes on realistic hardware models. By enabling close collaboration between hardware developers and algorithm designers, the initiative seeks to accelerate progress toward fault‑tolerant quantum systems.
