Infleqtion has announced the integration of its qLDPC (quantum Low‑Density Parity‑Check) code implementation with NVIDIA’s CUDA‑Q logical framework, a development highlighted by *The Quantum Insider*. The announcement was reported through multiple global news outlets and aggregated across Google News. This partnership is aimed at enhancing quantum error correction performance on NVIDIA GPU-accelerated platforms.
