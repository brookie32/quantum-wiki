arXiv:2609.09994v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Magic-state cultivation suppresses errors by measuring logical Clifford symmetries and discarding inconsistent outcomes. For an entangled resource several branches are accepted, so the retained branch carries a multibit classical record whose corruption produces a logical-frame error. We show that this record layer is exactly a binary linear code. For any third-level Clifford-hierarchy unitary U in C_3, the branches UZ(a)|+rangle^{otimes k} are resolved by commuting Hermitian Clifford observables C(v) = UX(v)U^{agger}, so a measurement schedule is a generator matrix and postselection on valid records gives P_{wv} = O(q^{d}) at record distance d. A Plotkin bound then limits every binary record, linear or not. The physically realizable Clifford family already saturates it: at distance four, six logical measurements suffice for |CSrangle and seven for |CCZrangle, against eight and twelve for independent repetition. Restricting to fault-tolerantly measurable parities therefore costs nothing in record length. The saving survives compilation: in a native-CZZ-assisted Steane realization the [6,2,4] CS schedule is the unique minimum-cost distance-four solution, reducing the cultivation core by 26.6% in active locations, and exact state-vector simulation without a final ideal code-space projection confirms higher acceptance and roughly half the residual Steane-boundary weight. Coding thus reduces logical redundancy and compiled overhead together.
