Quint Computing has secured €9.1 million (≈US$10.5 million) in equity financing to fuel its next‑generation fault‑tolerant quantum computing research. The capital is intended to accelerate experimental development of error‑corrected qubits and support the design of scalable, practical quantum processors. The funding round was reported by HPCwire.
