Researchers from Harvard, QuEra, and Caltech have developed a new fault-tolerant quantum magic state factory architecture for qLDPC codes. This new design reduces space-time resource costs by 10x to 40x compared to existing methods. The factory utilizes 3D and 4D balanced-product codes and can achieve logical error rates as low as 10^-9.
