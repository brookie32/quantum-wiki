The paper introduces a fault‑tolerant protocol for preparing logical eigenstates of the Pauli \(X\) and \(Z\) operators in surface codes via purely unitary operations, bypassing conventional state‑distillation techniques. It achieves this through a sequence of lattice‑surgery/teleportation steps that deform the code space while preserving error rates below the fault‑tolerance threshold. The reported implementation demonstrates reduced qubit overhead and circuit depth compared to previous methods for generating these fundamental encoded states.
