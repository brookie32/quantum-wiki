IBM, Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL), and AMD have jointly launched a quantum‑error‑correction research platform that couples IBM’s 120‑qubit processor with traditional CPU and GPU resources. The initiative is designed to enable large‑scale testing of error‑correcting codes by integrating the quantum hardware into a high‑performance computing environment. This partnership aims to accelerate progress in scalable, fault‑tolerant quantum computation.
