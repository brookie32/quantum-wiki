Nvidia has released **CUDA‑Q Logical**, an extension to its CUDA‑Q platform designed to enable fault‑tolerant quantum research. The new tool provides a virtual environment for modeling logical qubits, error‑correction codes, and scalable hardware architectures, allowing researchers to develop and test quantum algorithms without requiring physical quantum devices. It is presented as part of Nvidia’s broader effort to accelerate access to realistic quantum simulation and development tools.
