QuEra Computing and NVIDIA have introduced PPVM, an open-source digital-twin simulation framework for quantum processors. PPVM unifies generalized stabilizer tableau simulation with Heisenberg-picture Pauli propagation, allowing for emulation of quantum processors, hardware noise, and classical control logic. It offers various simulation backends and is tailored for early fault-tolerant neutral-atom architectures, providing a unified execution target for testing adaptive error-correction protocols and hardware noise models.
