Pasqal has been selected to head a European Defence Fund (EDF) project focused on fault‑tolerant neutral‑atom quantum computing. The initiative seeks to develop robust quantum hardware capable of supporting defense-related computation, positioning France as a leader in next‑generation quantum technologies. The EDF will provide funding and coordinate collaboration across European partners to accelerate progress toward practical, error‑corrected neutral‑atom systems.
