arXiv:2603.24588v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We construct asymptotically good nested Calderbank-Shor-Steane (CSS) code pairs from Hsu-Anastasopoulos codes and MacKay-Neal codes. For fixed balanced triples with even component degrees and j_Zge4, we prove that the coding rate stays bounded away from zero and that the relative distances on both sides stay bounded away from zero with probability tending to one as the blocklength grows. Moreover, within an explicit low-degree search window, we prove that all 56 balanced triples in that window with even common row degree, column degree at least four, and positive design quantum rate attain the classical Gilbert-Varshamov (GV) bound on both constituent sides, and consequently the CSS GV bound at fixed finite degree.
