The paper presents an adaptive fuzzy fault‑tolerant PID controller for regulating the liquid level in a single‑tank process under sensor faults and external disturbances.  
Using fuzzy logic it continuously adapts controller parameters to compensate for detected faults, while the PID core maintains stability and tracking accuracy.  
Simulation results demonstrate superior robustness and disturbance rejection compared with conventional fixed‑parameter controllers, validating the approach for fault‑intolerant industrial level control.
