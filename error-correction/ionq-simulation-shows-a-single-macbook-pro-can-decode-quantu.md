IonQ’s recent simulation shows that a standard MacBook Pro is capable of decoding quantum‑error‑correction codes in real time for systems up to **408 logical qubits**. The study demonstrates that advanced quantum error‑processing can run on consumer‑grade laptops, potentially lowering the barrier to access and experiment with larger quantum devices. This marks an important step toward practical quantum computing by enabling robust error correction outside of specialized data centers.
