DARPA has announced a partnership with IBM and IonQ to advance the development of a fault‑tolerant quantum computer. The collaboration marks the next phase in DARPA’s quest to build scalable, error‑corrected quantum hardware for advanced computation and national security applications. This initiative follows prior research milestones aimed at meeting rigorous performance and reliability benchmarks.
