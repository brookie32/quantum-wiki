NVIDIA has released a new extension to its CUDA‑Q quantum computing framework that adds support for fault‑tolerant quantum systems. The update introduces tools for simulating error‑correcting codes, advanced noise modeling, and more robust compilation workflows, enabling developers to design and test resilient quantum algorithms. It is part of NVIDIA’s broader strategy to integrate quantum computing capabilities into its HPC and AI platforms.
