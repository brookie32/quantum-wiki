The National Science Foundation has awarded a new research center at the University of California, San Diego dedicated to building reliable, self‑correcting quantum computers. The center will assemble interdisciplinary teams to develop fault‑tolerant hardware and software that can autonomously detect and correct errors in quantum systems. This initiative follows recent breakthroughs in quantum error correction and aims to accelerate the transition from experimental prototypes to scalable, practical quantum machines.
