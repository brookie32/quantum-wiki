SEEQC announced that its quantum‑error‑correction decoding algorithm has achieved a new speed record using NVIDIA’s Ising™ platform, according to a report by HPCwire. The improved decoder leverages NVIDIA’s high‑performance hardware to accelerate the mapping of physical qubit errors to logical corrections, significantly reducing the time required for error mitigation in quantum processors. This development highlights the growing role of GPU acceleration in advancing practical quantum computing.
