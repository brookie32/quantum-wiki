Two recent papers in *Nature Physics* demonstrate that autonomous quantum error correction (AQEC) has now achieved the energy‑expenditure break‑even point, significantly extending qubit coherence times. The first study reports an 18 % increase in ion‑trap qubits’ lifetime using a single‑pulse AQEC sequence, while the second achieves a 4 % improvement for superconducting transmon qubits with continuous error suppression. These results mark important milestones toward practical fault‑tolerant quantum computing.
