Google’s Quantum AI team has shown that “magic state” cultivation—an essential step for universal quantum operations—works effectively on the Willow processor, cutting error rates by about forty‑fold compared to previous methods. Published in *Science* (Oct 15 2026), this demonstration removes a longstanding bottleneck and moves quantum hardware closer to practical scalability. The breakthrough confirms that high‑quality magic states can be produced with fewer resources than earlier estimates suggested.
