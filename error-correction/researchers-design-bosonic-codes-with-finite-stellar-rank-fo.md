Researchers have engineered bosonic quantum error‑correcting codes that possess finite “stellar” rank, enabling practical implementation on constrained hardware. By tailoring code parameters to limited physical resources, the new designs reduce overhead while maintaining high fidelity against common bosonic noise channels. This approach advances the feasibility of robust quantum computation in realistic experimental settings.
