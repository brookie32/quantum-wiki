Quint Computing has secured €9.1 million in funding to advance its quantum‑error‑correction (QEC) technology, a critical component for scalable quantum computers. The investment will support research and development aimed at improving qubit coherence and fault tolerance, positioning Quint as a key player in the emerging QEC market.
