Rice University has joined the Department of Energy’s Quantum Science Center to focus on advancing quantum error‑correction (QEC) research, adding its expertise to the national effort on scalable quantum computing. The partnership aims to develop new QEC protocols, hardware‑compatible algorithms, and foundational techniques that can be deployed across diverse quantum platforms. This collaboration positions Rice as a key contributor to the DOE’s broader mission of accelerating quantum technologies into practical, fault‑tolerant systems.
