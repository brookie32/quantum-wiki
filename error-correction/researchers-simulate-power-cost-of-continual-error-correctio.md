Researchers have performed numerical simulations to quantify the energy required for continuous quantum error correction—a key operation in maintaining qubit fidelity during computation. The study estimates that error‑correction overhead can constitute a substantial fraction of the total power budget of a fault‑tolerant quantum processor, highlighting a critical cost barrier for large‑scale devices.
