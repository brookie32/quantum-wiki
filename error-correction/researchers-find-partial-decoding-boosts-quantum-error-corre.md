Researchers have demonstrated that incorporating a *partial decoding* strategy into quantum error‑correction schemes can markedly enhance their effectiveness compared to traditional full‑decoding approaches. By selectively correcting only subsets of errors, the method reduces syndrome measurement complexity and lowers resource overhead while maintaining robust protection of logical qubits. This finding offers a practical pathway toward more scalable and fault‑tolerant quantum processors.
