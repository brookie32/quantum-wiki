IBM and AMD have open-sourced Pelagos, a GPU-accelerated quantum error-correction (QEC) decoding framework for evaluating qLDPC codes at supercomputing scale. This release, announced at SC26, aims to advance quantum computing through vendor-neutral, efficient error correction, building on a joint QEC co-design platform. Pelagos offers significant speedups and efficient multi-GPU orchestration, enabling large-scale simulations crucial for fault-tolerant quantum computing.
