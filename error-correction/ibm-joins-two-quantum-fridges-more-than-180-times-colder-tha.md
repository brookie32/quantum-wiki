IBM has partnered on the development of two quantum refrigerators capable of operating at temperatures more than 180 times colder than deep space, a level necessary for advanced quantum‑computer experiments. The new fridges enable researchers to maintain qubits in ultra‑stable, low‑entropy states essential for error‑resilient processing. This initiative reflects IBM’s continued investment in pushing the limits of quantum hardware performance.
