FaultScope, a software tool for quantum computing, has been reported to identify errors within quantum error‑correction circuits. According to coverage on Quantum Zeitgeist, the program scans circuit designs to pinpoint faults that could undermine fault‑tolerant operations in near‑term qubit devices. The article highlights FaultScope’s potential to improve reliability of quantum error‑correction protocols.
