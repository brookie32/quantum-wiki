IBM has provided datasets collected from three of its quantum computers to evaluate artificial‑intelligence methods for correcting errors in quantum computing, according to a Stock Titan article. Researchers are using this data to benchmark and train AI models that aim to mitigate qubit noise and improve the reliability of near‑term quantum processors. The collaboration underscores efforts to enhance error correction through machine‑learning techniques on real hardware.
