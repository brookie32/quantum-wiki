BBVA, FirstQFM, Florida Atlantic University, and the Jülich Research Centre have been granted early access to D‑Wave’s Gate Simulator, a tool that allows developers to model and test quantum algorithms on classical hardware. This initiative, highlighted by *Quantum Zeitgeist*, aims to accelerate research and application of quantum computing across banking, finance, academia, and scientific research institutions. The announcement underscores the expanding collaboration between industry leaders and academic partners in advancing practical quantum technologies.
