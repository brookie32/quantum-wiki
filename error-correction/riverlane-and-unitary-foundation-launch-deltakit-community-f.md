Riverlane and the Unitary Foundation have jointly launched the "Deltakit Community Fund," an initiative designed to support and accelerate the development of open-source quantum error correction technologies. This fund aims to foster collaboration and innovation within the quantum computing community by providing resources and support to projects working on error correction, a critical challenge in making quantum computers practical and reliable. The program represents a significant industry effort to advance quantum error correction through community-driven, open-source development.
