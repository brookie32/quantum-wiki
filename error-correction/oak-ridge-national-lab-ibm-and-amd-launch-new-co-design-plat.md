Oak Ridge National Laboratory, IBM, and AMD have announced a new co‑design platform focused on quantum error correction. The collaboration aims to develop tools that model and mitigate errors in quantum processors, enhancing the reliability of future quantum computers. This initiative represents a joint effort between government research, industry leaders, and semiconductor manufacturers to push the boundaries of quantum technology.
