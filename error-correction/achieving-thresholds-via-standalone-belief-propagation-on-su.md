arXiv:2603.05381v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: The standard approach to Belief Propagation (BP) decoding passes messages on the Tanner graph of the quantum error-correcting (QEC) code, and is known to lack a threshold for the surface code. We propose novel BP decoders that exchange messages on the decoding graph and obtain thresholds (both for code capacity and circuit-level noise) via standalone BP for the surface code under depolarizing noise. Our approach, similarly to the minimum weight perfect matching (MWPM) decoder, is applicable to any graphlike QEC code. The thresholds observed with our decoders are close to those obtained by MWPM. This result opens the path towards scalable hardware-accelerated implementations of MWPM-compatible decoders.
