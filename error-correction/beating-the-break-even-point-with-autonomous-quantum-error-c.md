Researchers achieved autonomous quantum error correction (QEC) on a logical qubit encoded in the multi‑level spin states of a single trapped ion and surpassed the break‑even point for qubit lifetime without using measurement or real‑time feedback. By engineering spin–motion couplings, they transfer error‐induced entropy into motional modes that are dissipated through sympathetic cooling with an ancilla ion, thereby eliminating the need for syndrome readout. Repeated application of this protocol extended qubit coherence under both natural laboratory conditions and injected low‑frequency magnetic noise, demonstrating hardware‑efficient QEC and robust quantum memory using the ion’s multilevel structure.
