IBM, AMD, and Oak Ridge National Laboratory have collaborated to pair IBM’s newly announced 120‑qubit Nighthawk quantum processor with an AMD supercomputer for quantum error‑correction experiments. The initiative aims to explore hybrid classical–quantum systems capable of fault‑tolerant operations by leveraging the massive compute power of AMD’s hardware. According to aggregated coverage from Google News, this partnership represents a significant step toward scaling qubit counts while integrating advanced supercomputing resources to manage quantum errors.
