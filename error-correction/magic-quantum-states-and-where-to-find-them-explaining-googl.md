I'll search for information about this Reddit post on magic states and Google's quantum computing research. Google's magic state cultivation is a technique that reduces errors by approximately 40x on its Willow quantum processor, addressing a critical bottleneck in quantum computing .  The approach works by injecting a noisy state into a small chip region and using coordinated measurements to screen out errors while "growing" a much purer state .  This addresses a significant resource constraint, as producing magic states could occupy up to 95% of a quantum computer's capacity .
