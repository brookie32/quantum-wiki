QuEra has joined a $12 million Department of Energy (DOE) initiative aimed at developing quantum error‑correction schemes powered by artificial intelligence and machine learning. The project seeks to leverage AI techniques to design more robust fault‑tolerant protocols for quantum computing. By participating, QuEra contributes its expertise in quantum hardware and software to the DOE’s broader effort to accelerate practical quantum technologies.
