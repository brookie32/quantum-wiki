The National Science Foundation awarded a $37.5 million grant to a Yale‑led Center for Quantum Error Correction Research, advancing efforts to develop reliable quantum computing systems. The announcement was reported by *The Quantum Insider* and is part of a broader portfolio of grants supporting error‑correction technologies in quantum information science. This funding reflects NSF’s commitment to ensuring the development of scalable, fault‑tolerant quantum hardware for future technological applications.
