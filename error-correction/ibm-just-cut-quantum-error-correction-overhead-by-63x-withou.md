IBM Research's Spacetime PEC protocol combines error detection with probabilistic error cancellation to reduce quantum sampling overhead by up to 63x, validated on a 27-qubit processor.   The technique merges error detection with probabilistic error cancellation to cut quantum error correction overhead without requiring additional qubits.   The result is from a preprint that has not yet been peer-reviewed and has not been independently replicated on other quantum systems.
