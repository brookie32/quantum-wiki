The article reports that researchers have devised a simple classical simulation framework capable of benchmarking quantum error‑correcting codes constructed from Calderbank–Shor–Steane (CSS) circuits. By efficiently estimating logical error rates and other key metrics on small device sizes, the method helps validate CSS‑based error correction schemes prior to large‑scale experimental deployment. This approach offers a practical tool for early testing and optimization of quantum error‑correction protocols in near‑term hardware.
