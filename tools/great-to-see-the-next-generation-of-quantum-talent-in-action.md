Great to see the next generation of quantum talent in action! Earlier today, Daniel Felipe Nino hosted a hands-on PennyLane (@PennyLaneAI) workshop as part of IQC’s USEQIP undergraduate summer school in Waterloo. He walked students through core quantum algorithmic primitives and explored how to bridge the gap between textbook algorithms and the quantum resources required for their execution at scale. Thanks to everyone who joined us, and thanks to IQC (@QuantumIQC) for hosting.
