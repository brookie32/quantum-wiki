On September 14th at IEEE Quantum Week, Xanadu (PennyLane) will host a workshop titled “Preparing for fault-tolerant applications by programming with PennyLane.” The session, focused on fault‑tolerant quantum computing, is presented by Ben Lau, Daniel F. Nino, and Catalina Albornoz (@quantumcatalina). It runs from 1:00 – 2:30 PM and aims to teach participants how to program quantum systems ready for the fault‑tolerant era.
