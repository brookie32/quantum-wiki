In a 2026 study, scientists used a programmable trapped‑ion quantum simulator to emulate a (1+1)-dimensional \({\mathbb Z}_2\) lattice gauge theory and investigate the dynamics of string breaking. By applying site‑dependent magnetic fields through focused laser beams they recreated static external charges and their confining potential, enabling real‑time observation of how an initially stretched “flux tube” evolves. The experiment found that when the string tension is weak, charges propagate freely, whereas increasing tension produces localized coherent oscillations that precede the eventual breaking of the string.
