A cold‑atom quantum simulator of the repulsive Hubbard model was used to investigate the metallic state between an underdoped and overdoped regime at temperatures lowered by a factor of several. Thermodynamic measurements revealed a compressibility maximum that shifts with interaction strength, indicating a line of inflection points in the equation of state; lattice‑modulation spectroscopy showed suppressed low‑energy response in antinodal Brillouin‑zone regions, a hallmark of a pseudogap. The combined data provided an experimentally‑determined pseudogap phase diagram as a function of doping and interaction strength, establishing the existence and character of a pseudogapped metal within the Hubbard model.
