The authors use a programmable optical‑lattice simulator of interacting erbium atoms to realize the one‑dimensional Hubbard model with crystalline symmetry protection, and they identify a topological phase transition between distinct insulating phases that are locally indistinguishable but globally distinct. The critical point is detected via non‑local string‑order parameters, matching predictions for a Mott–Haldane insulator transition; stacking two identical systems removes the transition in accordance with the invertibility of symmetry‑protected topological phases, while symmetry‑breaking disorder suppresses it and disorder averaging restores it. These results show that topology, information, and observer‑dependent mixed‑state order jointly determine quantum phase transitions in programmable quantum matter.
