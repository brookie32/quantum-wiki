Scientists have experimentally observed “paraparticles” – a theoretically plausible third class of quanta beyond the familiar bosons and fermions – in a quantum simulation with 16 qubits performed by Max Hays’ team at MIT. The result follows recent mathematical work by Hazzard and Wang (2025) showing that paraparticle statistics can be realized and distinguished from ordinary particle behavior through their exchange properties and occupation limits. This observation expands the fundamental taxonomy of quantum particles, suggesting richer possibilities in the quantum world than previously accepted.
