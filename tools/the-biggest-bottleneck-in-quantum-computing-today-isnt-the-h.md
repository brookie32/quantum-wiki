The biggest bottleneck in quantum computing today isn't the hardware, it's talent. Xanadu and Booz Allen (@BoozAllen) are tackling this head-on. Through a new collaboration, Booz Allen engineers are getting hands-on training with PennyLane (@PennyLaneAI)—Xanadu's open-source quantum platform—to build job-ready skills. The goal? Bridge the gap between classical and quantum engineering, delivering mission-ready talent to government and commercial clients from day one.
