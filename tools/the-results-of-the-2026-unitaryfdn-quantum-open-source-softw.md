The results of the 2026 @unitaryfdn Quantum Open Source Software (QOSS) Survey have placed @PennyLaneAI, Xanadu’s quantum open-source software platform, among the top open-source tools in the quantum ecosystem. The QOSS Survey is the largest global survey of the quantum open-source community and helps shape the future of the ecosystem. In 2026, PennyLane: -Saw a 36% increase in usership, -Was ranked the #1 tool for quantum resource estimation (QRE), -Was ranked the #2 overall quantum SDK, alongside PennyLane Lightning as the #2 choice for simulation and Catalyst as the #2 choice for compilation. Thank you to everyone who filled out the survey; we look forward to continuing to build the future of quantum open-source software together. Media
