The study reports the first direct observation of universal conformal‑field‑theory (CFT) spectra in a quantum simulator by measuring the finite‑size excitation energies of a programmable Rydberg atom chain tuned to quantum critical points described by Ising and tricritical Ising CFTs. Using a pulse‑modulation spectroscopy technique, the authors resolved energy ratios that match theoretical CFT predictions, distinguished parity sectors via local control, and drove transitions between distinct boundary‑condition‐dependent spectra in the tricritical case. They also measured the dynamical structure factor of the critical system, linking it to Ising correlators, thereby establishing a practical method for probing unknown universality classes in future experiments.
