In September 2026 Google unveiled the Gemini 4 Argon model, a frontier‑AI system with enhanced reasoning capabilities for complex tasks, alongside new natural‑voice models and Connected Apps that integrate everyday services directly into Gemini. The company also released WeatherNext 3—an advanced climate‑prediction tool—and introduced productivity extensions such as Googlebook and a Windows‑compatible Gemini application. Parallel AI initiatives enabled the mapping of human DNA, real‑time monitoring of global methane emissions from orbit, and preliminary studies of fruit‑fly neurobiology.
