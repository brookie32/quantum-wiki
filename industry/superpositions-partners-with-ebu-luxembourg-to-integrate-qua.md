European quantum software company Superpositions has entered into a strategic educational partnership with the European Business Institute of Luxembourg (EBU) to power its Q-Ready academic initiative. Under the agreement, EBU is incorporating quantum computing and hybrid quantum-classical algorithms into its business school curriculum, providing students with direct hands-on access to the Superpositions Studio platform.
