It uses specialized AI agents and a test-driven development approach to prevent errors and ensure scientific accuracy in complex calculations. The platform has successfully simulated a proton-transfer problem and a Fermi-Hubbard model, demonstrating its ability to deliver accurate results and optimize quantum circuits.
