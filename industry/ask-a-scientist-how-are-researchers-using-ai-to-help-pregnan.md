Google researchers are using artificial intelligence to improve prenatal ultrasound access by training local healthcare workers to perform simple, portable “blind sweep” scans that do not require specialized sonographers. A machine‑learning model interprets the resulting images offline, accurately determining gestational age and fetal position so women in under‑resourced areas can receive timely diagnostic information. The team's approach was discussed in a virtual interview with an OB‑GYN physician and an AI researcher.
