Canada has long played a leading role in the global quantum ecosystem — a standout in quantum R&D and commercialization. Our 2023 acquisition of Toronto's Entangled Networks was one recognition of that strength. Now, we're pleased to announce a leader in Canada’s quantum and emerging technologies, Lisa Lambert, is joining us as Vice President, Global Strategy & Managing Director, Canada. Lisa joins IonQ from Quantum Industry Canada (QIC), where she served as inaugural CEO, helping establish QIC as the national voice of Canada's quantum sector and working with business, government, and international partners to advance the commercialization and industrialization of quantum technologies. Lisa will lead IonQ Canada while contributing to the company's global growth strategy. She will help drive growth, strengthen strategic relationships, and expand opportunities for customers and partners across key sectors as we continue scaling IonQ's business and expanding the reach of our full-stack quantum platform globally. Join us in welcoming Lisa to the IonQ team!
