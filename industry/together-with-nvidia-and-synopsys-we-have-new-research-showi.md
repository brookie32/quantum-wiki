Together with @nvidia and @Synopsys, we have new research showing that quantum algorithms integrated into mainstream engineering software can accelerate complex industrial design by up to 14.6%. Our results illustrate that hybrid quantum computing can address major computational bottlenecks for classical supercomputers, improving runtime for complex dynamic simulations such as processing time for complex car and jet engine simulations. This paper earned a 1st Place Best Paper Award at #IEEEQuantumWeek in Toronto.
