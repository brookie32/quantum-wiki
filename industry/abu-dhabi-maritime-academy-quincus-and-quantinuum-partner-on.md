The Abu Dhabi Maritime Academy (ADMA), Quincus, and Quantinuum have partnered in a multi-year research collaboration. They will evaluate hybrid quantum computing, high-performance computing, and AI algorithms using live operational data to address bottlenecks in maritime logistics. The goal is to improve solution quality, asset utilization, and carbon efficiency in areas like berth allocation, vessel stowage, and voyage routing.
