Plurilock Security Inc. will hold a shareholder meeting on November 18, 2026, to vote on its acquisition by Quantum eMotion Corp. for C33.8 million (~24 million USD). Plurilock shareholders will receive C0.084 in cash and 0.0763 of a Quantum eMotion common share for each Plurilock share. This acquisition aims to integrate Quantum eMotion's quantum random number generators into Plurilock's cybersecurity solutions.
