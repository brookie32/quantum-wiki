Scientists at Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL) developed LuGo, an algorithm that significantly reduces the number of quantum gate operations needed for computational fluid dynamics (CFD) by over 95%. This was achieved by using classical supercomputers like Frontier for heavy initialization routines before quantum processing, cutting down 2,000,000 logic gates to 91,000. LuGo was validated on various quantum hardware platforms and is a step towards practical high-dimensional fluid simulations on quantum processors.
