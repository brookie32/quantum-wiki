Google Beam is extending its reach to six countries by partnering with 18 channel partners, including Industrious and HP. Internal tests show the technology improves team connection and cuts follow‑up meetings, leading organizations such as Netflix and Bain to adopt Beam for remote interviews. The expansion allows bookings and trials at select Industrious locations starting in October.
