Our ongoing work with integrated photonic sources for Gottesman-Kitaev-Preskill (GKP) qubits forms a foundational layer for fault-tolerant quantum computing. GKP qubits are specifically designed to be robust against errors, and measuring their Wigner function reveals the exact quantum state grid structure required for computation. We are continuing to build heavily on this foundation every day in our mission to build quantum computers that are useful and available to people everywhere.
