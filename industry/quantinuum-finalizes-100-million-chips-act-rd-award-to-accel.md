Quantinuum has finalized a 100 million CHIPS Act R&D award to accelerate the manufacturing of trapped-ion quantum hardware. This funding will strengthen domestic semiconductor manufacturing, advance integrated optics, and scale fault-tolerant quantum computers by transitioning trapped-ion processing units to volume-manufacturable semiconductor platforms. Collaborating with GlobalFoundries and Monarch Quantum, Quantinuum aims to reduce complexity, improve gate reproducibility, and establish a resilient domestic supply chain for quantum hardware.
