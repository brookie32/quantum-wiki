On September 8, 2026, Brookhaven National Laboratory announced a partnership with PsiQuantum aimed at giving scientists new tools for researching fault‑tolerant quantum computers. The collaboration intends to accelerate the development of quantum applications and support broader objectives of NASA’s Genesis Mission. It is part of an initiative designed to push forward practical quantum‑computing research.
