PsiQuantum Australia announced that it spearheaded the development of photonic quantum computing in 2025 and has now begun constructing quantum processors using its technology. The company’s design is based on an integrated silicon‑photonic architecture aimed at producing scalable, fault‑tolerant systems. This marks a significant step toward commercializing large‑scale quantum computation in Australia.
