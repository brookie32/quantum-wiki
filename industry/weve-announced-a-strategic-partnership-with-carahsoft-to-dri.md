We've announced a strategic partnership with @Carahsoft to drive quantum computing, networking, sensing, and security adoption across Federal agencies and their industry partners. Carahsoft will serve as our Public Sector distributor, enabling government agencies to purchase IonQ technology through its reseller partners. Steve Harris, IonQ SVP and GM, Public Sector, says the partnership will make quantum solutions more readily accessible at a time when “quantum progress is an immediate and critical requirement.” Carahsoft will support procurement and pricing, access to applicable contract vehicles, channel sales, and product resale and distribution.
