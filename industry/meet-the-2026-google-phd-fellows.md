Google.org is investing $13 million to fund the 2026 Google PhD Fellows program, a global initiative that provides early‑career researchers with financial support, computational resources, and a collaborative community. The goal is to enable these scientists to tackle bold foundational questions and accelerate breakthroughs across diverse scientific fields.
