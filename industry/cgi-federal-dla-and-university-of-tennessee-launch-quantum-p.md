CGI Federal, the DLA, and the University of Tennessee have partnered to launch the "Quantum Pathfinder" initiative. This program will explore how AI and quantum computing can optimize supply chain resilience, particularly in warehouse management, reverse logistics, and inventory positioning within military contexts. Hosted at CGI Federal's Knoxville center, the initiative aims to solve logistical bottlenecks and strengthen supply chains during disruptions.
