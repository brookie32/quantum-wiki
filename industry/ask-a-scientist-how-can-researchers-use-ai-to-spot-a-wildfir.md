Google Research employs AI-powered analysis of satellite imagery to detect early-stage wildfires worldwide, distinguishing true blazes from harmless controlled burns such as backyard grills. By processing frequent high‑resolution images and running machine‑learning models, the system can spot tiny fires—often the size of a car—before they spread, giving firefighters earlier warning and better situational awareness. The overarching goal is to monitor the entire Earth every 20 minutes by 2030, providing continuous wildfire surveillance from space.
