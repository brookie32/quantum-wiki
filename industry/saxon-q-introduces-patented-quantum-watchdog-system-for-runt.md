SAXON Q has introduced Quantum Watchdog, a patented system designed to detect hardware failures in quantum processors during live operation. This system monitors physical telemetry and runs verified test calculations to ensure the integrity of QPUs. Quantum Watchdog will be offered as a configurable option on SAXON Q's SXQ128 and SXQ512 processors, with initial implementations in early 2027.
