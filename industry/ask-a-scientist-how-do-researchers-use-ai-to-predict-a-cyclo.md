Google DeepMind researchers employ AI models—such as WeatherNext—that are trained on decades of atmospheric data to predict cyclone formation, intensity, and trajectory with higher accuracy than traditional numerical methods. The models run efficiently on a single processor rather than large supercomputers, enabling faster and more widespread forecasts. This improved, open‑source technology gives communities additional warning time before extreme weather events.
