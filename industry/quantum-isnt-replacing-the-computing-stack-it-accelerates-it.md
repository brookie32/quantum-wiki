Quantum isn’t replacing the computing stack. It accelerates it. IonQ designs and builds quantum from the chip up: a foundry with @SkyWaterFoundry, computers that are data center-ready, and the networking, sensing, and security that connect them to the real world. That’s how our systems run alongside existing HPC and AI infrastructure without a new data center build, so each part of a complex problem goes to the resource best suited to solve it.
