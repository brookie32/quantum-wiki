QuiX Quantum has partnered with Tohoku Electronic Industrial Co., Ltd. (TEI-C) for exclusive distribution of its integrated photonic processing hardware in Japan. This includes the Alquor 2.0 quantum photonic processor and the PACU control unit. The partnership aims to accelerate access to commercial quantum hardware for Japanese customers, aligning with Japan's national quantum initiatives.
