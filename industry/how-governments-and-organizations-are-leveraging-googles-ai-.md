Google has advanced AI-powered tools for forecasting and detecting natural disasters including floods, wildfires, earthquakes, and extreme weather through its decade-long crisis resilience efforts.   The company collaborates with governments, UN agencies, organizations, scientists, and first responders to help communities prepare for and respond to disasters.   Google.org has launched a $30 million fund to support organizations leveraging generative AI to improve public services, providing grants ranging from $500K to $3M alongside technical expertise and cloud credits.
