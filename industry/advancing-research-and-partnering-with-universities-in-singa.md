Google has opened a new research hub in Singapore and committed more than $6 million in grants to the National University of Singapore (NUS) and Nanyang Technological University (NTU). The funding supports breakthroughs in AI for scientific research, computational efficiency, enhanced safety frameworks, and the development of future scientific leaders. The initiative is overseen by Yossi Matias, Vice President of Google and General Manager of Google Research.
