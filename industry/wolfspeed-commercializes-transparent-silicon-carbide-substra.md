Wolfspeed has launched transparent silicon carbide (TSiC™) wafers for quantum computing and integrated photonics, available in 150mm and 200mm sizes, with 300mm in sampling. This technology offers a scalable platform for solid-state spin qubits and quantum photonic integration, addressing manufacturing bottlenecks faced by other materials like diamond. TSiC™'s optical and thermal properties enable monolithic integration of quantum devices, overcoming previous limitations in wafer-scale fabrication.
