Xanadu is proud to support qConnect 2026 as an official sponsor. On October 21–22, Quantum City and the University of Calgary (@UCalgary) are bringing together industry leaders, innovators, and investors in Calgary to bridge the gap between quantum pilots and real-world commercial adoption. From quantum computing use cases to co-developing solutions across energy, defense, and critical infrastructure, qConnect is helping build the supply chains and partnerships required for utility-scale quantum.
