Security is paramount for the future of quantum computing. Xanadu’s Rafal Janik (@rafjanik) will be at the University of Maryland (@UofMaryland) tomorrow at the Quantum Zero Trust Architecture Seminar, hosted by ARLIS (Applied Research Laboratory for Intelligence and Security). Rafal will be speaking to an audience of researchers and security experts on "Securing the Quantum Data Center: Zero Trust for Utility-Scale Photonic Quantum Computing," diving into what it takes to protect quantum infrastructure.
