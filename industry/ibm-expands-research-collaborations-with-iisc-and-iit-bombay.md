IBM is expanding its research collaborations with IISc and IIT Bombay in India. The partnerships focus on advancing quantum-centric supercomputing and sovereign AI, including hybrid quantum-classical computing architectures, AI for scientific and energy applications, and optimizing large language models for Indic languages. These collaborations aim to align advanced quantum and AI software frameworks with India's national technology initiatives.
