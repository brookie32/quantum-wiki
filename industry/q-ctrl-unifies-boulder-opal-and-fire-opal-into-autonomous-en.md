Q-CTRL has integrated its Boulder Opal and Fire Opal software into a single platform for end-to-end quantum infrastructure. This unified system automates the entire quantum computing lifecycle, from processor bring-up to workload execution, significantly reducing manual effort and improving performance. The technology is currently live on Elevate Quantum’s Q-PAC system and will be accessible via cloud in December 2026.
