D-Wave Quantum Inc. and SkyWater Technology have extended their quantum manufacturing partnership through a multi-year agreement. This collaboration ensures SkyWater's continued support for D-Wave's quantum hardware development, including the production of its Advantage2 processors and future quantum annealing and gate-model quantum computers. Additionally, D-Wave's quantum annealing algorithms have been applied to optimize SkyWater's operational workflows, reducing fab maintenance lot delays by up to 30%.
