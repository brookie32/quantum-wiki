A multi‑year effort by HHMI Janelia Labs and Google Research has produced the first complete brain map (connectome) of a male fruit fly (Drosophila melanogaster), a foundational model organism in neuroscience. The project, led by research scientists Michał Januszewski and Viren Jain, used advanced imaging and computational techniques to catalog every neural connection, providing an unprecedented resource for studying the brain’s circuitry. These visualizations highlight how detailed mapping of a tiny brain can accelerate understanding of neuronal networks and inform broader neurological research.
