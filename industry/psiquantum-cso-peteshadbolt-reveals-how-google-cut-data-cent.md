"To switch that traffic, one way is to convert from light to electricity, switch the electricity, and convert back to light. People can do it, but it’s really power hungry, and all these pluggable transceivers lead to unreliability." "The holy grail would be to stay in the optical domain and move the light around." "Google did that starting back in 2013. They have literally physical mirrors bouncing the light around." "It's what a kid would come up with.
