PsiQuantum CEO Victor Peng joined leaders from across the government and the quantum computing industry at today's White House Summit on American Quantum Innovation. The sense of urgency in Washington around strengthening and sustaining American leadership in quantum computing has never been greater. At PsiQuantum, we're proud to partner closely with agencies across the U.S. government like @DARPA's Quantum Benchmarking Initiative, the Department of @CommerceGov, the @USAirForce Research Laboratory, and National Labs across the Department of @ENERGY ecosystem in our efforts to build and deploy this critical technology. Today's gathering marked an important opportunity for the private sector to hear directly from the Administration about how we can continue working together to ensure this technology, once fully realized, delivers greater prosperity and security for the American people.
