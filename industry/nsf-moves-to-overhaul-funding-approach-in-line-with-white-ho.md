On 11 September 2026 the National Science Foundation announced it would revise its grant‑funding model to align more closely with White House priorities, as outlined in a letter signed by Acting Director Brian Stone. The reform is expected to cut back core science programs while redirecting resources toward initiatives prioritized by the administration; specific funding mechanisms for these new projects remain unannounced. NSF staff have expressed concern that streamlining could further reduce cash available for basic research.
