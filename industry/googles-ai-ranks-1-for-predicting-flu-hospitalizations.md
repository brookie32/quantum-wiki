Google’s AI‑based flu forecasting model outperformed all 39 competitors in the CDC’s FluSight 2025–26 season, matching the observed U.S. hospital admissions for influenza better than any other submitted model. Developed with Google’s Empirical Research Assistance (ERA) platform—an optimization tool published in *Nature*—the model demonstrates that AI combined with human expertise can enhance disease‑outbreak predictions. ERA’s underlying technology is now offered to trusted testers as an experimental scientific tool.
