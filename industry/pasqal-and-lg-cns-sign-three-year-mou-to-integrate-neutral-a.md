Pasqal and LG CNS have signed a three-year agreement to integrate Pasqal's neutral-atom quantum processing units (QPUs) into LG CNS's AI Data Centers in South Korea and the Asia-Pacific region. This partnership aims to deploy hybrid quantum-classical computing platforms for high-performance AI workloads, expanding Pasqal's presence in the APAC market.
