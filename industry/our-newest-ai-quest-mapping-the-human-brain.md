**Polar Peaks** is an interactive AI Quest launched by Google Research and the Stanford Accelerator for Learning that teaches students aged 11‑14 how to map neurons in the human brain using artificial intelligence. The program guides learners through data cleaning, model testing, and emphasizes human oversight to ensure responsible AI use while illustrating how AI accelerates neuroscience research. Teacher training modules are available via the Google AI Educator Series to support classroom implementation.
