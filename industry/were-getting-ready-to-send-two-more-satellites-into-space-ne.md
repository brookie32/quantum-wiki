🚀 We're getting ready to send two more satellites into space! Next Monday, October 19, our newest @capellaspace synthetic aperture radar (SAR) satellites, Acadia-11 (C-21) and Acadia-12 (C-22), are targeting launch aboard @SpaceX's #Bandwagon5 mission from Cape Canaveral, Florida. Our mission, “Catch Me If You Can,” gets its name from the satellites' planned formation flight, with one following the other along the same orbital path. Together, they depict two fish circling and chasing one another, symbolizing the orbital dance happening hundreds of kilometers above Earth. These satellites will help advance bistatic SAR and interferometric SAR (InSAR) capabilities, enabling more precise monitoring of changes on Earth's surface. Our satellites are designed, built, and operated by our incredible team in Colorado. A huge thank you to everyone whose hard work and dedication make missions like this possible! We're ready for liftoff! Watch the launch live: https://www.spacex.com/launches/bandwagon5
