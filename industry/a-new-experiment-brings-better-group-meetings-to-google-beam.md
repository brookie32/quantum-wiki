Google Beam, an experimental video conferencing platform, now supports group meetings with true-to-life sizing and spatial audio to make hybrid meetings feel more inclusive.   Using HP Dimension's immersive display, participants joining from non-Beam devices appear in their actual size positioned around a table, paired with spatial audio that anchors voices to speakers.   Research suggests this approach can increase the sense of social connection by 50% and improve the ability to contribute to conversations by 21%.
