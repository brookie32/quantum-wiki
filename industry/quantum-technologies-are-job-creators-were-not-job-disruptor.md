“Quantum technologies are job creators. We’re not job disruptors.” That was one of the messages our CEO, @NiccoloDeMasi, shared with Andrew Marr on LBC as they discussed what the next era of computing could mean for economies and societies around the world. Quantum is moving beyond the laboratory. From computing and cybersecurity to sensing and navigation, governments and businesses are starting to consider how these technologies could reshape critical industries and infrastructure, and unlock new opportunities across healthcare, materials science, engineering, logistics and financial services. For IonQ, the opportunity is about more than building powerful quantum systems. It is about helping build the skills, infrastructure, manufacturing capacity and workforce needed to put quantum technology to work.
