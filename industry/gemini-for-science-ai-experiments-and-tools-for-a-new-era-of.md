Gemini for Science is a collection of science tools and experiments designed to expand the scale and precision of scientific exploration.   The suite bundles three experimental prototypes targeting distinct phases of the scientific method: Hypothesis Generation, Computational Discovery, and Literature Insights.   Google also launched Science Skills, a specialized bundle integrating insights from over 30 major life science databases that allows researchers to perform complex workflows like structural bioinformatics and genomic analyses in minutes rather than hours.
