On November 18, 2026, Quandela announced two international collaborations: a tripartite partnership in Qatar with Mekdam Holding Group and MEEZA to deploy photonic quantum systems across GCC states, and a memorandum of understanding with Taiwan-based Hermes-Epitek Corporation to co-develop and industrialize integrated photonic quantum hardware. These agreements aim to advance sovereign quantum infrastructure in the Middle East and photonic component industrialization in Asia.
