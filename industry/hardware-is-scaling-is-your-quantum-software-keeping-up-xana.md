Hardware is scaling. Is your quantum software keeping up? Xanadu’s Catalina Albornoz (@quantumcatalina) and Josh Izaac (@3rdquantization), together with co-organizers from ORNL (@ORNL), IonQ (@IonQ_Inc), Google (@Google), Entropica (@EntropicaLabs), and Quantinuum (@QuantinuumQC), bring you the Quantum Software 2.6 workshop at IEEE Quantum Week (@IEEEQuantumWeek): a full-day workshop tackling the software scaling bottleneck. Hear from Lee O’Riordan, Ross Duncan, and Fred Chong, alongside other key minds across the ecosystem who are defining the next-generation quantum stack. Plus, an end-of-day panel you won't want to miss featuring Robert Wille, Blake Johnson, Justin Gage Lietz, and Laura Schulz.
