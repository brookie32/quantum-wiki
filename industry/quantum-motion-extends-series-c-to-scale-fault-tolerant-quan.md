**Summary**

Quantum Motion has secured an additional round of Series C financing aimed at scaling the development of fault‑tolerant quantum hardware. The investment will support the company’s efforts to build larger, more reliable quantum processors and accelerate its technology roadmap toward practical, error‑corrected qubits. This funding milestone positions Quantum Motion as a leading player in the emerging fault‑tolerant quantum computing sector.
