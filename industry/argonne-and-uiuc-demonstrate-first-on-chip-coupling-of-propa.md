Scientists from Argonne National Laboratory and UIUC have developed the first-of-its-kind hybrid circuit, integrating propagating spin waves with a superconducting resonator on a single chip. This innovation allows for strong coupling between microwave photons and spin waves, addressing issues of signal noise in quantum devices. By using a rare-earth-free YSGG substrate, the team achieved significant power isolation, paving the way for compact, on-chip microwave isolators for quantum circuits.
