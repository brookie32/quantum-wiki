Google’s ATLAS project offers an expansive snapshot of how AI is being adopted across workplaces and everyday life, highlighting its growing economic impact. The July 2026 Innovation & AI article features insights from Zanna Iscenko, AI & Economy Lead in Google’s Chief Economist’s Office, and Scott Strand, Head of StratOps, which discuss the broader implications for productivity and market dynamics. The piece emphasizes the role of generative AI and data‑driven analysis in shaping the contemporary AI economy.
