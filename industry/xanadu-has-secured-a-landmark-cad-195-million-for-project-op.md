Xanadu has secured a landmark CAD $195 million for Project OPTIMISM, the largest government investment in quantum manufacturing in Canadian history. As part of Project OPTIMISM, we’re launching Inception, a 158,000 sq. ft. advanced photonics R&D and manufacturing facility. Inception is designed to enable the scalable production of the components that will power future quantum data centres, which are expected to deliver commercially meaningful solutions. We’re scaling the global future of photonic quantum hardware, right here in Toronto.
