arXiv:2603.16845v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We present an efficient protocol for estimating M observables to precision arepsilon from only O(log (M)/arepsilon^2) copies of a Gibbs state rhoproptoexp(-eta H), given access to its Hamiltonian H. The protocol uses single-copy, nonadaptive measurements and a total Hamiltonian simulation time of widetilde{O}(eta M/arepsilon^2); we show that the sample complexity is optimal in a black-box setting. The key idea of our protocol is a new interpretation of quantum Gibbs samplers as detailed-balanced measurement channels: measurements that preserve the Gibbs state when outcomes are marginalized, and thus enable Gibbs samples to be reused for measuring subsequent observables. Consequently, shadow tomography of thermal states admits a general efficient algorithm when controlled Hamiltonian simulation is available, substantially boosting the data acquisition rate in quantum thermal simulation workflows.
