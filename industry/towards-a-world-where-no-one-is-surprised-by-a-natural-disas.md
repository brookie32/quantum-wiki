Google has invested in AI research to generate reliable flood predictions globally, addressing the world's most common natural disaster .  The company has introduced systems like Groundsource that use public reports and Google Maps to predict urban flash floods .  These AI-driven approaches can potentially be applied to other natural disasters like landslides and heat waves to improve global resilience .
