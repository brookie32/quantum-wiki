Google employs generative AI to accelerate scientific discoveries, enabling earlier disease detection, improved natural‑disaster forecasting, and the dissolution of language barriers that support education and workforce adaptation. The company now offers AI tools in more than 300 languages, reaching roughly 86 % of the world’s population (about 7 billion people). An accompanying interactive resource—the AI & Economy ATLAS—provides real‑world insights into how individuals globally are applying these technologies.
