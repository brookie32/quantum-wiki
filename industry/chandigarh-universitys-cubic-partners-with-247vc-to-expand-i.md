Chandigarh University's CUBIC has partnered with 247VC Investment Trust to create an investment and mentorship pipeline for quantum and deep-tech startups. This collaboration, established during the PRAGATI Q-Foundry 2026, aims to transition academic quantum research into industrial and commercial deployment. CUBIC will identify eligible seed and early-stage ventures, facilitating their access to funding and mentorship from 247VC.
