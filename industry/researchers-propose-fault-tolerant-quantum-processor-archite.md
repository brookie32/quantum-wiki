Researchers have proposed a new fault-tolerant quantum processor architecture that uses 20 or fewer external wires, a significant reduction from current designs. This design features a fixed, clocked planar array where quantum data flows through hard-wired operation tiles. This advancement could lead to more scalable and robust quantum computers.
