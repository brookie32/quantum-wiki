On Monday, we had the honor of welcoming a delegation of senior leaders from the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia to our French headquarters and quantum computer production site. Led by His Excellency Engineer Abdullah Alswaha, Minister of Communications and Information Technology, the delegation observed a quantum processing unit and a live demonstration of atom-by-atom rearrangement, and toured the line where we assemble, calibrate and test the systems we ship to customers. The delegation included His Royal Highness Prince Abdulaziz Bin Turki Bin Talal; His Excellency Engineer Haytham AlOhali, Governor of the Communications, Space and Technology Commission (CST); and Saeed AlDobas, Executive Vice President of AI Infrastructure and Cloud at @HUMAIN. The visit reflects the deepening cooperation between France and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia in advanced technologies, following the deployment of Saudi Arabia's first quantum computer earlier this year. Quantum computing sits within the Kingdom's Vision 2030 agenda, which places advanced computing among its national technology priorities.
