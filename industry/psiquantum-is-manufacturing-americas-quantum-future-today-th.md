PsiQuantum is manufacturing America's quantum future. Today, the company announced that we have signed a Letter of Intent with the U.S. Department of @CommerceGov for $100 million in proposed federal incentives under the CHIPS and Science Act to advance American quantum computing and semiconductor leadership. With this planned funding, combined with PsiQuantum's existing U.S.-anchored supply chain, the company will accelerate the domestic manufacturability and performance of our state-of-the-art components, including Barium Titanate (BTO) for higher-performance optical switches, high-temperature single-photon detectors, and advanced packaging approaches.
