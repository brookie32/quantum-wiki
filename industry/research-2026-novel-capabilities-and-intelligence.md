Google Research unveiled several new AI systems at its Research@ 2026 event, emphasizing applications in healthcare, crisis resilience, and environmental monitoring. The highlighted breakthroughs include AMIE for clinical decision support, Earth AI for public‑health analytics, Project Suncatcher for orbital data processing, and an advanced geospatial reasoning agent that pushes planetary‑intelligence capabilities forward. These tools aim to accelerate scientific discovery and deliver practical benefits worldwide.
