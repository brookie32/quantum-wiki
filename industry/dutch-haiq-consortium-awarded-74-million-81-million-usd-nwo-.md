The Dutch HAIQ Consortium, led by Leiden University, secured a €7.4 million NWO grant for a project combining human intuition and AI to optimize quantum control and circuits. The 28-partner initiative will use human-in-the-loop gamification and machine learning to address quantum computing optimization challenges and upgrade QuTech's Quantum Inspire platform.
