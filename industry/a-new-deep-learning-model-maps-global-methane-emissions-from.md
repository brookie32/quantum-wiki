In September 2026 Google Research and NASA’s JPL introduced MAPL‑EMIT, a deep‑learning system that uses data from the EMIT satellite instrument to map global methane plumes. Trained on 3.6 million simulated plumes, it detects roughly 50 % more emissions than human experts and identifies over 23,000 previously unseen sources—including nearly all of the world’s largest landfill emitters—enabling rapid, targeted climate mitigation actions. The resulting plume database is publicly released via Google Earth Engine, with open‑source models on Kaggle and inference tools on GitHub.
