Infleqtion, in partnership with Honeywell Aerospace and UC Santa Barbara, has developed a chip-scale silicon nitride optical cavity. This prototype aims to miniaturize lasers and optical stabilization systems for quantum hardware. This innovation enables volume manufacturing and paves the way for field-deployable quantum systems like atomic timing devices and compact quantum computers.
