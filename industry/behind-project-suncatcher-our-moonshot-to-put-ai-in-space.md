Project Suncatcher is Google’s “moonshot” to demonstrate scalable artificial‑intelligence infrastructure in space. The initiative launches a prototype satellite equipped with AI compute chips, evaluating their performance under extreme radiation, vibration, and vacuum cooling conditions. Engineers are investigating key challenges—such as radiation shielding, thermal management, and power constraints—to enable reliable, high‑throughput AI workloads beyond Earth.
