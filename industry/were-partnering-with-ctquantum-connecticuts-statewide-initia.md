We’re partnering with @CTquantum, Connecticut’s statewide initiative anchored by @UConn and @Yale, to accelerate quantum innovation and expand access to our trapped-ion quantum computers. The agreement includes more than 150 hours of cloud runtime for Connecticut’s universities, research labs, startups, and enterprises, advancing quantum algorithms, optimization, materials science, and workforce development. “Regional quantum ecosystems thrive when researchers and innovators have access to advanced quantum computers,” said Scott Millard, IonQ's Chief Business Officer. Together, we're taking Connecticut’s quantum community from exploration to application.
