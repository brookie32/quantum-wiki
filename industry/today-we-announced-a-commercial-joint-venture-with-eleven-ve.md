Today we announced a commercial joint venture with Eleven Ventures to deploy, commercialize and scale Pasqal's neutral-atom quantum computing systems across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and the wider MENA region. The venture intends to bring Pasqal's systems to market for regional customers and build local quantum talent, so that capability is developed within the Kingdom rather than accessed from abroad. HRH Prince Abdulaziz Bin Turki Bin Talal will serve as Chairman of the Board of Pasqal Arabia. "We believe that quantum today is where AI stood a few years ago, on the edge of changing everything. The difference is that this time, the Kingdom and the region are not watching from a distance, we are moving first, and in Pasqal we found a partner that will not wait either. Together we plan to deploy real systems, raise a generation of quantum talent, and make the Kingdom the home of this technology for the entire region." said HRH Prince Abdulaziz Bin Turki Bin Talal.
