Google Research is partnering with Cathay Pacific to pilot an AI‑powered contrail mitigation system across the Asia-Pacific region. The trial uses machine‑learning models to adjust aircraft altitudes, thereby reducing heat‑trapping contrails and collecting data for global research on contrail formation and avoidance. Insights gained will advance understanding of how to mitigate contrail impacts in this rapidly growing aviation corridor.
