PsiQuantum and AMD have expanded their strategic partnership to co‑design the computing infrastructure required for utility‑scale, fault‑tolerant quantum computers and hybrid quantum-classical systems.  The collaboration focuses on developing the hardware foundation—encompassing processors, memory, interconnects, and control electronics—that will enable large‑scale quantum operations.  Together, they aim to create a scalable platform that supports both quantum workloads and classical integration for future supercomputing solutions.
