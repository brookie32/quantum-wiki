Google is extending its AI beyond simple text translation to create models that comprehend the real‑world usage of more than 300 living languages, including slang, emotion, and tone. By collaborating with local communities—especially in regions lacking reliable internet—the company aims to produce culturally respectful tools that let users be understood on their own terms. This effort builds on Google Translate’s legacy from 2006 and now covers roughly 86 % of the global population.
