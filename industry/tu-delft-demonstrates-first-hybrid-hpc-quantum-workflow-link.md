TU Delft and QuTech have successfully linked the DelftBlue supercomputer with QuTech's Tuna-17 quantum processor, demonstrating the first hybrid HPC-quantum workflow. This integration was tested with an electrical power-flow calculation, utilizing the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) to optimize circuit parameters and process quantum circuits.
