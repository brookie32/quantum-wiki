Google AI researchers use machine‑learning models to forecast river and flash floods days ahead by analysing global weather, hydrological data, and historical news reports (Groundsource methodology). These forecasts provide early warnings for more than 2 billion people across 150 countries and are available through the Flood Hub tool or an open‑source API. The article is part of Google’s “Ask a Scientist” series, which explains how AI has evolved flood prediction capabilities over the past decade.
