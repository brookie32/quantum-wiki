Over the past five years IonQ has expanded our business from leadership in quantum computing to become the world’s leading quantum platform solution and merchant supplier. We have been an innovator at every stage. As we evolved, we grew our revenue, capabilities, and now engineering solutions—opening growth opportunities across every part of the platform. We are uniquely positioned as the only company that delivers a full suite of quantum products: our computing, networking, sensing, and security solutions are each industry-leading. With SkyWater Technology now closed, IonQ is the only vertically integrated, full-stack quantum platform and the largest merchant supplier to the U.S. and allied quantum ecosystem. SkyWater provides a capital-light way to scale, with proven U.S.-based fabricators bringing critical chip capacity onshore at a moment when moving onshore matters more than ever. This is reflected in our updated full-year 2026 guidance of 450 million to 460 million, which highlights both the market traction of our quantum platform and the foundational manufacturing scale provided by SkyWater.
