The NSF and UKRI have jointly committed 10 million to fund eight transatlantic research teams in molecular quantum information science. This initiative, with 5 million from NSF and £3.7 million from UKRI, focuses on foundational chemistry research to manipulate quantum states in complex molecular architectures. This second phase of funding aims to synthesize custom molecular systems, engineer molecular-scale spin qubits, and model quantum information transfer, directly supporting international research partnerships.
