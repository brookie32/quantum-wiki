Announced today, Florida International University will be the first to bring an IonQ Superion 256 computer to the State of Florida. The installation will create a central hub for quantum research and education across the region. @FIU's quantum computer will be housed at a specialized, secure site on its main campus in Miami, following completion of its on-campus data center. Scientific applications and research by students, faculty, visiting scholars, and affiliated research centers are expected to span material science, artificial intelligence, logistics, cybersecurity, and more.
