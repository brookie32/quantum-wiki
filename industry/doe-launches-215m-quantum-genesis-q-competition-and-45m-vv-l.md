The U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) has launched the Quantum Genesis Q Competition, offering up to 215 million to develop Scientifically Relevant Quantum Computers (SRQCs) with a minimum of 100 logical qubits. Concurrently, a 45 million National Laboratory call aims to establish a Quantum HPC Validation and Verification (V&V) Testbed to benchmark these systems. This initiative seeks to accelerate fault-tolerant quantum hardware for complex problem-solving in various scientific fields, with an applicant webinar on September 25, 2026, and submissions due by October 19, 2026.
