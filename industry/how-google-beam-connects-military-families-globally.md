Google has partnered with the United Service Organizations (USO) to deploy the HP Dimension powered by Google Beam—a 3‑D video communication platform—in 20 locations worldwide for deployed service members. The technology captures lifelike body language and eye contact, enabling family members to feel truly present despite long distances. A new docuseries follows four military families using this system to strengthen their connections across miles.
