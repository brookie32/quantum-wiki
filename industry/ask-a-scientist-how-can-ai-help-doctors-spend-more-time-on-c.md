Google researchers have developed AMIE, an AI tool that securely collects and summarizes patient information before appointments. By automating baseline data gathering, the system frees physicians to spend more time on collaborative care and treatment planning. The project is expanding to include audio‑video interactions as it prepares for larger‑scale clinical trials.
