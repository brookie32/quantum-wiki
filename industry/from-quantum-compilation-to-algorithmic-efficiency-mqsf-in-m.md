From quantum compilation to algorithmic efficiency, MQSF in Munich featured two days of fantastic discussions around what it takes to build a utility-scale quantum software stack. A highlight was Josh Izaac's talk, "Python in the Front, Party in the Backline," which shared how Backline enables low-latency compilation and execution for intertwined quantum-classical workloads directly from Python. Huge thanks to the organizers at MQSC, Munich Quantum Valley, and the Chair for Design Automation at @TU_Muenchen for bringing the ecosystem together. Looking forward to the next one!
