Scientists partnered with CSIRO and Google AI to generate two high‑quality reference genomes for Australian giant kelp, then used a trained DeepVariant model to sequence 600 wild specimens from the Tasmanian coast—where populations have dropped to just 5 % of historic levels. The resulting genetic data inform breeding programs aimed at producing heat‑tolerant kelp for replanting efforts. This initiative is part of Google Australia’s Digital Futures Initiative, supporting the restoration of coastal marine ecosystems and giant kelp forests worldwide.
