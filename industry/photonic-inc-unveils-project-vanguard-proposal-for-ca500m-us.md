Photonic Inc. proposes Project VANGUARD, a CA500 million (US359.1 million) semiconductor facility in Canada, to address fabrication and packaging bottlenecks in quantum computing, AI, and other sectors. This multi-tenant facility, anchored in Vancouver, aims to create shared domestic infrastructure, reduce reliance on foreign foundries, and secure sovereign supply chains.
