Last week, Christian Weedbrook (@_cweedbrook), Founder and CEO of Xanadu, took the stage at the Creative Destruction Lab (@creativedlab)'s Super Session to reflect on what it takes to scale a deep-tech company from startup to a public market debut on the TSX and NASDAQ. According to Christian, surviving the deep-tech grind doesn't just come down to physics — it comes down to human resilience. “You can't stop someone who's not going to give up when it's based on the foundation of learning and enjoying what they're doing.” Thank you CDL and Sonia Sennik for the great conversation!
