Multiverse Computing has secured $570 million in a Series C round led by prominent investors, boosting its total valuation to roughly $2.5 billion.  
The capital infusion will support the company's data‑center software platform, aimed at delivering high‑performance compute for AI and machine learning workloads.  
FinSMEs reports the deal as a key milestone following the company's prior Series B round that enabled significant infrastructure expansion.
