Watch Duty has deployed a Gemini‑powered tool that converts noisy fire‑radio audio into real‑time text, allowing new wildfire dispatches to be identified in under 30 seconds. The solution was developed with pro‑bono support from a Google.org Fellowship and is part of Watch Duty’s rapid‑response strategy for emergency communication. Key contributors include Senior Director Eve Andersson (Social Impact) and Software Engineer Stephanie Chew.
