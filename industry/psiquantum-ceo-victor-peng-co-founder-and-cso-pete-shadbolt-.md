PsiQuantum CEO Victor Peng, Co-Founder and CSO Pete Shadbolt, and CFO Susan Kim started this week in South Chicago with a roundtable alongside local community leaders and a visit to Affordable Welding, which supplied roughly 400 tons of structural steel for our building at the Illinois Quantum and Microelectronics Park. Two years since we announced that we would be the anchor tenant at the IQMP, our ties across South Chicago are deeper and stronger than ever.
