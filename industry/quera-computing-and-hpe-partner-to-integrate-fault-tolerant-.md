QuEra Computing and HPE are partnering to integrate QuEra's neutral-atom quantum processing units (QPUs) with HPE Cray supercomputers, enabling on-premises, hybrid quantum-classical supercomputing for national laboratories and enterprises. This collaboration aims to develop integrated hardware, co-design algorithms, and establish low-latency software for secure and efficient high-performance computing.
