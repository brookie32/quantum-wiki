We announced a partnership with Blue Origin to launch the Project Suncatcher satellites on its heavy‑lift New Glenn rocket, targeting Q1 2029 for deployment into sun‑synchronous orbit. Each satellite, equipped with data‑center rack‑level compute capacity, will harness abundant space solar power to scale AI infrastructure—a key milestone in our long‑term roadmap.
