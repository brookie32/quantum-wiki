MBHB, an intellectual property law firm, has launched a new Quantum, Optics, and Semiconductors Practice Group in Chicago. This group will counsel hardware developers, research universities, and government contractors, focusing on patenting challenges in quantum innovation. The practice will address issues like 35 U.S.C. § 101 eligibility and federal regulations related to public funding.
