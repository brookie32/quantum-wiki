Quantum eMotion Corp. has completed its C31.98 million acquisition of Plurilock Security Inc., a Canadian cybersecurity company. This strategic move combines Quantum eMotion's quantum cryptography with Plurilock's cybersecurity operations, enhancing their offerings in critical services, identity management, and cloud security across North America and NATO. Plurilock's shares are set to delist from the TSX Venture Exchange around November 26.
