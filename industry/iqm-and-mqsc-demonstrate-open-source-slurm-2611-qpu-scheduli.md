IQM Quantum Computers and Munich Quantum Software Company (MQSC) have integrated IQM superconducting processors into NVIDIA’s open-source Slurm 26.11, enabling quantum processing units (QPUs) to be scheduled as native supercomputing resources. This was achieved using Slurm 26.11’s Hierarchical Resources (HRES) plugin framework and the Quantum Device Management Interface (QDMI). This integration simplifies the allocation, reservation, and accounting of QPU access within standard classical submission workflows, moving away from bespoke site-specific scheduler plugins previously required for QPU integration in supercomputing facilities.
