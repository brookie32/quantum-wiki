**Operation Blue Skies** is an initiative launched by Google Research in partnership with the UK Government and aviation stakeholders to use artificial‑intelligence models for reducing the climate impact of air traffic. The project focuses on helping airlines avoid forming contrails over the North Atlantic, thereby mitigating aviation’s contribution to atmospheric warming at the scale of flight corridors. Technical program managers at Google are coordinating the development and deployment of these AI tools to optimize flight routes and operating procedures for lower emissions.
