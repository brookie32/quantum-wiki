In October 2026, Google unveiled Gemini 4 Argon (targeted at complex tasks) and the world model Genie 4 for interactive simulations, along with updates that enhance Android’s scam‑blocking capabilities. The company also launched a SynthID Detector to verify AI‑generated content. These releases fit into Google’s ongoing effort—spanning over two decades—to broaden AI benefits in areas such as health care, crisis response and education while improving everyday productivity and security.
