QFactorial and Monash University have received a A1.12 million Australian government grant to develop a hybrid quantum-classical optimization solver for freight and last-mile delivery. The 15-month project aims to advance QFactorial's routing and fleet-utilization platform. This initiative, part of the Critical Technologies Challenge Program, involves a consortium including IBM Quantum, AWS, and Fujitsu, and seeks to overcome limitations of classical solvers in large-scale logistics.
