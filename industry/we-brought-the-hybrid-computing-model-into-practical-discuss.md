We brought the hybrid computing model into practical discussions at our evening reception at the IonQ Quantum House during @Supercomputing. A highlight of the evening was a candid conversation featuring leaders from @nvidia, @AWS, and KISTI | Korea Institute of Science and Technology Information sharing perspectives on integrating quantum processors alongside classical supercomputers, preparing sites for deployment, and moving from research to production workloads. The evening reflected an industry focused not on possibilities alone, but on practical pathways to the adoption of hybrid computing. Thank you to everyone who joined us. We'll continue the conversation throughout SC26 and at the IonQ Booth #5238 at McCormick Place. #SC26 #QuantumComputing #HybridComputing #AI #HPC #Innovation
