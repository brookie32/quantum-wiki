Plurilock Security Inc. shareholders have approved the company's acquisition by Quantum eMotion Corp. The acquisition, valued at C$0.28 per share, involves a cash and stock deal. Plurilock is now seeking final court approval, after which the transaction will close and Plurilock will be delisted from the TSX Venture Exchange.
