Google DeepMind’s AlphaGenome model can analyze a 1‑million‑base‑pair segment of DNA and generate thousands of variant‑effect predictions, speeding the identification of mutations that may cause disease. This tool is part of the company’s broader AlphaGenome Atlas initiative, which supports genomics research across diverse populations. Research scientist Žiga Avsec heads DeepMind’s genomics team, developing models that help scientists better understand how genetic variation influences disease risk.
