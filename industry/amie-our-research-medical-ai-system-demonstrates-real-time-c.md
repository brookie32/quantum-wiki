Google Research’s AMIE—built on Gemini and Project Astra with a multi‑agent architecture—has demonstrated real‑time, expert‑level video consultation capabilities in a first‑of‑its‑kind randomized study using simulated patient actors and primary‑care physicians. Clinical evaluators rated the system favorably across key competencies (history taking, diagnostic accuracy, management appropriateness, and communication), while patients preferred the video experience over text chat. AMIE remains a research prototype, and additional studies are required before clinical deployment.
