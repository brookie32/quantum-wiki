The European Quantum Industry Consortium (QuIC) has published a position paper urging the European Commission to harmonize EU dual-use controls on quantum technologies. They warn that fragmented national implementations of export controls on quantum technologies are causing administrative difficulties, shipment delays, and revenue loss for European quantum companies. QuIC proposes reforms focusing on single market harmonization, expanded allied trade, clearer rules for intangible transfers and cloud access, and differentiated scrutiny for supply chain components to ensure European competitiveness.
