Quandela demonstrated a live hybrid AI-quantum computing system at NVIDIA GTC Berlin, showcasing photonic quantum processing units (QPUs) as integrated accelerators within HPC and AI infrastructure. The demonstration highlighted a low-latency interconnect between NVIDIA accelerated computing nodes and Quandela's photonic hardware using NVIDIA NVQLink, enabling a hybrid workflow for a quantum reservoir-computing algorithm. This development underscores Quandela's role in advancing European quantum computing infrastructure and its collaboration with partners in Germany and France.
