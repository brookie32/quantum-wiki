Google's May 2026 updates center on the new "agentic" era, featuring the Gemini 3.5 model and Gemini Omni for advanced reasoning and creation.   The announcements include proactive tools like the updated Gemini app, Universal Cart for shopping, and new hardware including the Googlebook and Fitbit Air.   The company also broadened personal wellness tools with the new Google Health app and launched REPLIQA, a $10 million initiative applying quantum science and AI to life sciences.
