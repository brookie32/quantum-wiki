Classiq has partnered with FreeYa Mind Campus to open a Quantum Applications Hub in Iași, Romania, which will serve as Classiq's regional headquarters. This hub integrates Classiq's quantum software platform with the campus's IBM Quantum System Two. The collaboration aims to drive quantum software development, education, and enterprise projects across Eastern Europe.
