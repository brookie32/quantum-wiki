Xanadu’s quantum computing effort focuses on creating scalable, fault‑tolerant systems through modular, network‑enabled photonic hardware. By integrating Gottesman–Kitaev–Preskill (GKP) qubits directly onto photonic chips, the team engineers quantum states that exhibit high resistance to errors. This approach positions them to advance toward practical, large‑scale quantum computers.
