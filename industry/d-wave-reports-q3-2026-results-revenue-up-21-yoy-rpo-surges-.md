D-Wave Quantum Inc. reported a 20.7% year-over-year revenue increase in Q3 2026, reaching 4.51 million, driven by growth in professional services and QCaaS. The company also saw a significant surge in Remaining Performance Obligations (RPO) by 1,221% to 38.3 million and finalized a 100 million CHIPS Act funding agreement. These achievements highlight growing commercial adoption and advancements in both their annealing and gate-model quantum computing technologies.
