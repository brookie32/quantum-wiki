What will it take for Canada to build the next generation of global tech giants? Last week at the Canadian Global Growth Forum hosted by the Canadian Venture Capital & Private Equity Association (@cvca), Christian Weedbrook (@_cweedbrook), Founder and CEO of Xanadu, took the stage with Julien Camirand Lemyre (@JulienCamirand) (Nord Quantique), Dr. Paul Terry (Photonic Inc. @TeamPhotonic), and Simon Chong (@Georgian_io) to break down how Canada can maintain its edge in quantum computing. The discussion focused on scaling hardware, commercializing R&D, and building enduring deep-tech giants at home. Exciting times ahead for the Canadian quantum ecosystem!
