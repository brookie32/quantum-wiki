In early October 2026 Project Suncatcher successfully lofted a prototype satellite into Earth orbit aboard SpaceX’s Transporter‑18 rideshare, built in partnership with Planet. The team has confirmed communication and is now collecting data on how its TPU hardware tolerates the radiation, thermal extremes, and physical stresses of space to inform long‑term research on scalable machine‑learning infrastructure in orbit. A peer‑reviewed paper detailing the mission’s design and early results was published in *Joule*.
