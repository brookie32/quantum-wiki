A full day of activations at @Supercomputing made one thing clear: the future of computing will be hybrid. At the IonQ Quantum House, we brought together leaders from across quantum, AI, HPC, industry, and government to explore how quantum systems work alongside classical infrastructure to solve increasingly complex challenges. Across every session, one theme emerged: the hardest challenges across industries will require integrated workflows that match each part of a problem to the resource best suited to solve it. That's what IonQ is deploying: quantum processors working alongside GPUs, CPUs, and AI to unlock new possibilities for scientific discovery and commercial innovation. Thank you to our speakers, customers, partners, and attendees who joined us for a packed first day of thoughtful discussion and collaboration.
