PsiQuantum Co-Founder and CSO @PeteShadbolt visited Bowen High School in South Chicago today, where he announced that the company is providing an additional investment to support the purchase of critical technology equipment for students at the school. As the anchor tenant at the Illinois Quantum and Microelectronics Park, PsiQuantum is helping equip the communities surrounding the Park to access the opportunities created by America’s largest quantum computing project.
