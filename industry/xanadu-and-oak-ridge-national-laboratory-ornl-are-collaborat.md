Xanadu and Oak Ridge National Laboratory are collaborating to integrate PennyLane, Xanadu's quantum software framework, with Frontier, a leading-edge supercomputer. This partnership aims to make quantum computing capabilities accessible on one of the world's most advanced classical computing systems. The collaboration likely enables researchers to leverage hybrid classical-quantum computing for complex scientific and computational problems.
