Google’s DeepMind has released the WeatherNext 2 AI model, which achieves state‑of‑the‑art accuracy in predicting cyclone tracks, intensity and wind profiles—offering a leap equivalent to about a decade of meteorological progress. The breakthrough, detailed in a Nature paper, demonstrates an extra day of reliable warnings for tropical cyclones, a critical improvement for disaster preparedness. WeatherNext 2 is now open-sourced for the global research community.
