Christian Weedbrook (@_cweedbrook), Founder and CEO of Xanadu, recently delivered the keynote for the Schulich Ernest C. Mercier Lecture hosted by the Schulich School of Business (@SchulichSchool) and Lassonde School of Engineering at York University. Christian shared the journey of building Xanadu from scratch, our mission to build energy-efficient photonic quantum data centers, and how we are advancing quantum technology across academia, government, and industry. A huge thank you to York University (@YorkUniversity), Matthew Lombardi, and everyone who joined us!
