IonQ reported record Q3 2026 revenues of 173.83 million, a 336% increase year-over-year, driven by strong performance in both its IonQ Platform and SkyWater Foundry Services segments. The company achieved GAAP net income of 563.96 million and raised its full-year 2026 revenue guidance to over 505 million, with a backlog reaching 1.2 billion. IonQ also announced significant commercial and defense deployments, including advancements with DARPA and NVIDIA, and expanded its foundry and quantum cybersecurity capabilities with strategic partnerships and acquisitions.
