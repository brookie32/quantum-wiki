The University of Amsterdam (UvA) has launched the Q-SIM4MMD initiative, investing €800,000 across four quantum simulation projects. These projects aim to explore quantum simulation and machine learning for molecular and materials discovery, focusing on applications like molecular design, bioinformatics, and developing safer molecules. The initiative connects researchers across various disciplines and seeks to integrate quantum approaches with classical modeling.
