Google’s Crisis Resilience initiative aims to eliminate surprise natural disasters by providing early warning systems worldwide. The Android Earthquake Alerts System, expanded globally, turns smartphones into a real‑time seismic warning network; AI models deliver up to seven days’ notice for riverine floods across 150 countries, 15‑day cyclone forecasts via WeatherNext, and near‑real‑time wildfire boundary maps in 34 nations, with the FireSat satellite constellation capable of detecting fires as small as a classroom within 20 minutes. In 2025 alone, Google facilitated over ten million daily crisis information connections, collaborating with partners to enhance preparedness for earthquakes, floods, cyclones, and wildfires.
