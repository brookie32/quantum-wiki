SkyWater Technology has launched SkyWater Quantum Solutions, a new merchant foundry division focused on process development, chip integration, and manufacturing for quantum computing, networking, and sensing applications. The division offers two standardized process platforms, SC250 for superconducting circuits and SP90 for quantum photonic routing, and has already secured a joint development agreement with Qolab to fabricate its Quantum System-in-Package (QSiP) hardware.
