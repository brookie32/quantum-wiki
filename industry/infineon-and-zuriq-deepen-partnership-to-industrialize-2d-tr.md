Infineon Technologies and ZuriQ AG have expanded their partnership to industrialize 2D trapped-ion Penning micro-trap chips for quantum computing. This collaboration aims to scale trapped-ion quantum processing units by combining ZuriQ's 2D Penning micro-trap designs with Infineon’s semiconductor fabrication and packaging capabilities. Their joint efforts have successfully demonstrated a 3x3 array of nine individually controlled ion qubits, paving the way for scalable quantum hardware.
