The AI data center is going hybrid. Pasqal and LG CNS just signed to build it 🤝 Pasqal signed a three-year MoU with LG CNS, the AI transformation arm of LG Group, to bring neutral-atom quantum computing into AI data centers at scale. AI's compute demand is outgrowing what classical architecture alone can deliver. Quantum is how the next generation of infrastructure keeps up. Here's what we're building together: 👉 Pasqal's quantum hardware, integrated directly into LG CNS's AI platforms, including its AI Haus and AI Data Center environments 👉 Quantum resources deployed and managed inside AI Data Center infrastructure, ready for enterprise workloads 👉 AI and quantum-enabled data center solutions, taken to market globally "By combining our neutral-atom quantum processors with LG CNS's deep infrastructure expertise, we are further embedding Pasqal within Korea's world-class technology ecosystem and laying the groundwork for next-generation data centers capable of tackling the world's most complex challenges," said Wasiq Bokhari, CEO of Pasqal.
