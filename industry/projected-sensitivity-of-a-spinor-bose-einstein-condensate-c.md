arXiv:2610.22346v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Light spin-zero bosons with scalar and pseudoscalar couplings mediate a parity- and time-reversal-violating monopole-dipole interaction between unpolarized matter and polarized spins. For proton spins, the interaction has been probed directly only at ranges lambda gtrsim 5 mm; below this range, laboratory limits are inferred from neutron-dominated nuclei through nuclear-spin models. We analyze a direct search with a single-domain ^{87}Rb spinor Bose-Einstein condensate in close proximity to a source mass. The F=1 and F=2 manifolds act as two co-located magnetometers that precess in opposite directions in a magnetic field but in the same direction under the exotic interaction. They thus form a natural comagnetometer, in which the sum of their precession angles suppresses field noise by a measured 44 dB and a weighted sum cancels the field altogether. Taking as a starting point the demonstrated magnetometry [Palacios et al. Proc. Nat. Acad. Sci. extbf{119}, e2115339119 (2022)] and comagnetometry [Gomez et al. Phys. Ref. Lett. extbf{124}, 170401 (2020)] performance of the ^{87}Rb single-domain spinor condensate at ICFO [Palacios et al. New J. Phys. extbf{20}, 053008 (2018)], we study the projected sensitivity as a function of source-mass geometry and in-progress improvements to atom loading methods, trap beam geometry, and magnetic environment. With an insulating bismuth germanate source mass with a spherical top, whose apex sits 50 mum below the center of the condensate, one week of acquisition would reach |g_s^N g_p^p|approx 2imes10^{-22} with these upgrades and 3imes10^{-24} at the projection-noise limit (lambda=100, mum, 95% C.L.). This is more than three and five orders of magnitude below existing laboratory limits. The improvement covers boson masses of 0.3-50 meV/c^2 after the upgrades and 0.09-70 meV/c^2 at the projection-noise limit.
