# Papers: Quantum Computing & Physics Research Overview

## Current State
The quantum computing and physics research landscape is highly active, spanning error correction, noise modeling, quantum information theory, and topological systems. Recent preprints (primarily via arXiv) reflect a maturing field where both theoretical foundations and near-term hardware challenges are being addressed simultaneously. The volume and diversity of submissions indicate rapid, broad-front progress.

---

## Key Developments
- **Quantum error correction** advancing below surface code threshold thresholds on real superconducting hardware (Google Willow processor)
- **Noise modeling** using few-shot cross-device transfer techniques for NISQ-era hardware
- **Gatemon qubits** exhibiting gate-dependent offset charge shifts in weak tunneling regimes
- **GNN-based decoders** enabling low-latency quantum error correction acceleration
- **Quantum sensing** achieving image classification using compressed sensing with a single photon
- **Topological systems** explored in mixed-state Floquet open quantum systems (SSH model)
- **Entanglement entropy scaling** studied in variable-range extended Ising models
- **Bohmian trajectory methods** applied to solve time-dependent Schrödinger equations via score matching
- **Quantum batteries** modeled in distance-modulated planar architectures

- **Google Quantum AI** – surface code error correction demonstrations
- **arXiv/academic institutions** – primary dissemination channel for theoretical and experimental work

## Outlook
Research is converging toward **fault-tolerant quantum computing**, with error correction and noise characterization as critical near-term priorities. Theoretical work on entanglement, topology, and quantum dynamics continues to build the foundational toolkit. Hardware-software co-design (e.g., GNN decoders, noise transfer learning) will be essential as systems scale beyond NISQ toward practical quantum advantage.
