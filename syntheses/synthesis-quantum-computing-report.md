# Quantum Computing Report: Knowledge Synthesis

## Overview
Quantum computing is advancing rapidly across hardware modalities, software benchmarking, and real-world application demonstrations. Major milestones in 2025-2026 include verified quantum advantage claims, fault-tolerant architecture progress, and significant government investment in national quantum programs. The ecosystem is maturing from proof-of-concept demonstrations toward scalable, commercially relevant systems.

---

## Key Developments
- **IBM** demonstrated "Trusted Quantum Advantage" beyond classical supercomputers with ecosystem partners including University of Chicago and Qedma
- **D-Wave** achieved 3D analog-digital quantum simulation using 728 qubits and demonstrated a two-qubit gate breakthrough for dual-rail erasure qubits
- **IBM** successfully linked modular cryogenic cells, advancing toward its 2029 Starling quantum computer roadmap
- **Pasqal** achieved first on-chip neutral-atom qubit trapping via photonic integrated circuits
- **Germany** committed €122M to the QUDORA-led NFQC-1k trapped-ion fault-tolerant quantum computer project
- **EuroHPC JU** selected 13 European QPU startups for its Quantum Grand Challenge program
- A global consortium launched **QOBLIB**, a standardized quantum optimization benchmarking library
- **IQM Quantum Computers** reported record backlog in its first public earnings as a Nasdaq-listed company
