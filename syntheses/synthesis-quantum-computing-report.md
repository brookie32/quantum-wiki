# Quantum Computing Report: Knowledge Synthesis

## Current State
Quantum computing is experiencing rapid, multi-front advancement across hardware modalities, error correction, and early commercial deployment. Major breakthroughs in qubit counts, entanglement fidelity, and cross-processor connectivity are pushing the field toward fault-tolerant systems. The first credible demonstrations of quantum advantage over classical supercomputers are now being formally claimed and benchmarked.

---

## Key Developments
- **IBM** demonstrated meter-scale superconducting L-couplers linking dual 120-qubit processors at 99% state transfer fidelity, and co-led a "Trusted Quantum Advantage" demonstration with academic and industry partners
- **IQM** verified full-chip entanglement across a 54-qubit Emerald processor and reported its first public earnings with record order backlog
- **D-Wave** showcased 3D analog-digital quantum simulation using 728 qubits and demonstrated a two-qubit gate breakthrough for dual-rail erasure qubits
- **Pasqal** achieved first on-chip neutral-atom qubit trapping via photonic integrated circuits
- **Chalmers University** developed control-error-robust TRIO pulse gates for superconducting processors
- **Cornell** demonstrated standing-wave EIT cooling for trapped ions
- **Germany** committed €122M to a QUDORA-led fault-tolerant trapped-ion quantum computer project
- **EuroHPC JU** selected 13 European QPU startups for its Quantum Grand Challenge initiative
- A global consortium launched **QOBLIB**, a standardized quantum optimization benchmarking library
