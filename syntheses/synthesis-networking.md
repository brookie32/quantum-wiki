# Networking (Quantum) – Knowledge Wiki Overview

## Current State
Quantum networking is transitioning from theoretical research to early-stage physical infrastructure, with entanglement distribution, quantum memory, and quantum key distribution emerging as core technical pillars. Significant public and private investment is accelerating prototype deployment across North America, South Korea, and Europe. The field remains pre-commercial but is crossing meaningful performance thresholds.

---

## Key Developments
- **Entanglement rate milestone**: 1,000+ entanglements per second (1 kHz) achieved between trapped ion qubits and solid-state quantum memory nodes
- **EU funding**: €47.5M grant awarded to the Quantum Internet Alliance (QIA) for quantum network prototyping
- **Indefinite causal order**: Experimental QKD leveraging quantum superposition of operational order, pushing cryptographic boundaries
- **Hybrid repeater chains**: New scalable protocols for long-distance entanglement distribution with error correction
- **Thin-film lithium tantalate photonic circuits**: Enabling scalable quantum network nodes with atomic frequency comb memories
- **Non-Gaussian teleportation**: Real-time heralded resource-state generation advancing quantum communications primitives
- **ABQ-Net expansion**: Four quantum firms joining New Mexico's regional quantum network ecosystem

---

## Key Players & Companies
- **IonQ** – Deploying Superion 256 systems and SiV quantum memory hubs (South Korea, via SDT partnership)
- **Infleqtion / Cisco** – Collaborating on distributed quantum network architecture
- **Arq** – Pre-seed startup focused on quantum internet infrastructure ($1.4M raised)
- **Quantum Internet Alliance (QIA)** – EU-backed consortium driving standardized quantum network prototypes

---

## Outlook
Quantum networking is converging on repeater-based, error-corrected architectures capable of city- and eventually continental-scale entanglement distribution. Near-term priorities include increasing entanglement fidelity and rate, standardizing hardware interfaces, and integrating quantum memory into deployable nodes. A functional quantum internet remains 5–15 years away but foundational infrastructure is actively being laid.
