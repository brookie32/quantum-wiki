# Quantum Networking: Knowledge Wiki Overview

## Current State
Quantum networking is rapidly advancing from theoretical foundations toward early experimental and commercial infrastructure, focused on enabling secure, long-distance quantum communication. Research is converging on entanglement distribution, quantum memory, and teleportation protocols as the core primitives for a functional quantum internet. Both academic institutions and startups are accelerating development, supported by growing regional investment ecosystems.

---

## Key Developments
- **Quantum Key Distribution (QKD)** exploring indefinite causal order to enhance security protocols
- **Real-time heralded teleportation** resource-state generation demonstrated for practical quantum communications
- **Picosecond-resolved entanglement distribution** successfully tested over urban free-space channels
- **Post-quantum cryptography** frameworks developed for signature-free, forward-secure authenticated key exchange
- **Distributed resource theories** formalizing entanglement and magic as quantifiable assets in fault-tolerant quantum networks
- **Multi-cavity spin ensemble coupling** advancing quantum memory candidates for network nodes
- **Telecloning protocols** (N-to-M symmetric) enabling remote quantum state inference across network nodes

---

## Key Players & Companies
- **Arq Quantum Technologies** – raised $1.4M pre-seed to build quantum internet infrastructure (backed by Big Sur Ventures)
- **ABQ-Net (New Mexico)** – regional quantum technology network, recently expanded with four new quantum company members
- Academic and national lab researchers driving arXiv-published advances in entanglement distribution and quantum memory

---

## Outlook
Quantum networking is progressing toward near-term utility networks capable of reducing service latency and supporting multi-user entanglement architectures. Standardization of teleportation primitives and post-quantum cryptographic integration will be critical milestones. Within the next 5–10 years, metropolitan-scale quantum networks with real hardware deployments are an increasingly realistic near-term target.
