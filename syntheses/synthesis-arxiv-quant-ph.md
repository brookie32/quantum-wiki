# arxiv-quant-ph: Knowledge Wiki Overview

## Current State
Quantum computing research is advancing rapidly across theoretical foundations, hardware validation, and near-term applications. The field is simultaneously pursuing quantum advantage demonstrations, error mitigation, and practical algorithmic development. A strong trend toward hybrid classical-quantum and tensor-network approaches characterizes the current literature.

---

## Key Developments

- **Quantum Advantage**: New proofs establishing exponential quantum advantage in Fourier dimensionality testing and IQP circuit sampling, with qubit connectivity shown to significantly impact advantage thresholds
- **Algorithms & Optimization**: Hybrid qudit-qubit algorithms for integer programming; Quantum Koopman algorithms for high-dimensional dynamical systems; VQE-based solutions for relativistic bound-state equations (Bethe-Salpeter)
- **Machine Learning Integration**: Physics-informed variational quantum classifiers for phase detection; quantum feature selection for biomedical data; neural tensor-network foundation models (Hamilton-Zero) for ground-state computation
- **Boson Sampling**: Formal proof of the hiding conjecture for Gaussian boson sampling with arbitrary squeezed input modes, strengthening hardness arguments
- **Hardware & Validation**: Many-body quantum Mpemba effect proposed as a calibration/validation protocol for quantum hardware; advances in automatic qubit uncomputation for cleaner circuit programming
- **Quantum Information Theory**: Optimal quantum strategies analyzed for XOR* and FFL non-local games; parity floor benchmarks established for fixed-map quantum denoising networks
