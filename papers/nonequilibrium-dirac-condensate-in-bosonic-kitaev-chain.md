arXiv:2609.29082v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We explore a nonequilibrium Bose-Einstein condensate (BEC) in a bosonic Kitaev chain. Within a mean-field framework, we show that bosonic superfluid order develops once the pairing strength exceeds a critical threshold. The condensate order parameter adopts an alternating phase between even and odd sites, giving rise to a spontaneous sublattice ordering. By mapping the emergent sublattice degrees of freedom onto a pseudospin, the elementary Bogoliubov excitation is described by a non-Hermitian Dirac Hamiltonian, displaying two prominent features: a re-entrant bosonic Andreev bound state localized around boundaries, and a macroscopic degeneracy of exceptional Majorana bosons at the critical point.
