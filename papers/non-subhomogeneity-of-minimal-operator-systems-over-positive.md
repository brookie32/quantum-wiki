arXiv:2608.24648v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: The minimal operator systems over {rm Mat}_k(mathbb C)_+, kgeqslant2, and the Lorentz cones mathcal L_m, mgeqslant4, are not subhomogeneous. For the 2imes2 cone we construct extreme positive maps of arbitrarily large output dimension. Positive retracts give the remaining cases. Equivalently, for fixed kgeqslant2 and d, there exist s>d and an entangled positive operator on mathbb C^kotimesmathbb C^s such that every compression of the second factor to mathbb C^d is separable.
