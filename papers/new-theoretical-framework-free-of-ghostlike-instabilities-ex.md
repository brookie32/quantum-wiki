Elementary particles and the forces governing their interactions are described by relativistic quantum field theory, in which symmetry serves as a fundamental guiding principle. For more than half a century, the possible symmetries of such theories were believed to be restricted by the Coleman–Mandula theorem and its supersymmetric extension, the Haag–Łopuszański–Sohnius theorem.
