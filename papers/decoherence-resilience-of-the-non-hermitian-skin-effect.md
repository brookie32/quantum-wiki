The study investigates how environmental decoherence influences the non‑Hermitian skin effect (NHSE) using photonic quantum walks with tunable dephasing and amplitude‑damping channels. Dephasing does not destroy NHSE; in fact, it can enhance the drift velocity beyond that of purely coherent dynamics, showing that directional bulk–to–boundary transport remains robust. In contrast, amplitude damping suppresses NHSE if applied before the loss operator but allows the effect to survive and even strengthen at large loss strengths when applied afterward, revealing a strong dependence on the order of decoherence processes.
