arXiv:2611.14139v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quantum synchronization in open systems can arise from both coherent interactions and collective dissipation, yet their interplay remains poorly understood. Here we develop a general framework for two quantum oscillators coupled through a fast mediator. Adiabatic elimination reveals two distinct synchronization channels, coherent exchange and collective dissipation, and yields an analytical description connecting dissipation and coherence-dominated regimes. We show that synchronization is governed by the joint balance of coherent coupling, dissipation, and reservoir-induced fluctuations, rather than by coherent coupling alone. Continuous monitoring of the shared output further converts reservoir information into a resource, substantially enhancing conditional synchronization, with the strongest gain near the coherent-dissipative crossover. These results establish a general mechanism for coherent-dissipative quantum synchronization and its measurement-enabled control in open quantum systems.
