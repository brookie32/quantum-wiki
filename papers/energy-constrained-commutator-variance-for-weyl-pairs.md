arXiv:2609.19163v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Let W be a representation of the Weyl relations on a separable Hilbert space and write mathcal R_W(K)={W(f):fin K}''. If the symplectic pairing of K_1 and K_2 is nonzero, there is a self-adjoint unitary Binmathcal R_W(K_2) such that, for every normal state rho, one can choose a self-adjoint unitary A_rhoinmathcal R_W(K_1) with operatorname{Var}_rho(-i[A_rho,B])=4. Hence the optimized mean-input-energy-constrained coefficient equals 4 at every admissible energy threshold. For the fixed trigonometric Weyl witness h, an exact identity for 4-h^2 reduces the deficit to a phase-fixed problem for commuting squared Weyl translations. For the one-mode quadratic energy G_M=frac12 R^TMR-frac12sqrt{et M}, with M>0 and u^TOmega v=pi, we prove 4-gamma_{G_M,E}(h)=frac{u^TMu+v^TMv+2pisqrt{et M}}{4E}+O(E^{-2}). The lower bound holds over all normal states satisfying the mean-energy constraint, and a localized Zak construction attains the same coefficient. For G=dGamma(H_1) and witness directions u,vinoperatorname{dom}H_1^{1/2}, the fixed-witness deficit is Theta(E^{-1}) whenever at least one direction lies outside ker H_1; if both directions are zero modes, the constrained supremum equals the endpoint at every positive threshold.
