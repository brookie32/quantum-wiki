arXiv:2607.21248v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We theoretically demonstrate that the molecular orientation angle heta provides a structurally intrinsic, continuously tunable control parameter for engineering optical Schrodinger cat states via high-harmonic generation (HHG) in H_2^+. Coupling time-dependent Schrodinger equation simulations to the fully quantized HHG framework, we evaluate the Wigner functions of the post-selected harmonic-mode states under two complementary conditioning strategies. Conditioning on resonance-enhanced low-order harmonics exploits the complementary dipole selection rules of the 1sigma_go1sigma_u and 1sigma_go1pi_u transitions, driving a kitten-cat crossover whose direction is opposite in the two channels as heta is varied. Conditioning on plateau harmonics instead exploits two-center destructive interference, producing a reentrant catokittenocat transition controlled by the order-dependent interference angle heta^*(q). In both cases the crossover is decoupled from the laser intensity, focal geometry, and molecular density, offering a degree of control with no counterpart in atomic targets.
