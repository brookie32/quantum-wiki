arXiv:2611.14406v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The theory of turbo iterative decoding was brought into the quantum coding framework through the development of quantum serial turbo codes and quantum block turbo codes. In this paper, we present a theory for multidimensional quantum block turbo codes, a turbo decoding technique for multi layer product codes. We describe its algorithm and simulate the performance for the 3D case on a depolarizing channel.
