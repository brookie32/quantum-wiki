arXiv:2611.26693v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Chiral (or sublattice) symmetry describes bipartite systems such as the Su-Schrieffer-Heeger (SSH) chain where same-sublattice A-A and B-B hoppings are forbidden, enabling the protection of topological phases. Here we identify new topological phases protected by half-chiral symmetry wherein chiral symmetry is preserved on only one sublattice, as may occur with sublattice imbalance (e.g. graphene-hBN) or localized orbitals (e.g. zincblende). Unlike conventional symmetry operators described by group theory, half-chiral symmetry is an algebraic constraint on the sublattice-projected Hamiltonian such as PHP=0, requiring a new framework for topological protection. We develop such a framework, identifying real-space bulk topological invariants and corresponding topological boundary modes that stick to zero energy. In two dimensions we construct a half-chiral Kekule honeycomb model and show that its unusual higher-order topology protects a single zero-energy mode at a single corner.
