This study introduces a heuristic/graph‑theory algorithm that rapidly identifies optimal pathways through the complex rotational–hyperfine manifolds of bialkali molecules, enabling high‑fidelity state preparation. Using this approach, the authors construct isolated quantum‑state networks—such as a closed four‑state loop with minimal population leakage and an optimized three‑state set robust against magnetic‑field noise—for applications in quantum simulation and computation with ultracold molecules.
