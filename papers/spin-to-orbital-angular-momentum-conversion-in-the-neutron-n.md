arXiv:2611.09677v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: The neutron nucleus weak interaction generates a small parity non conserving rotation of the neutron spin as the neutron moves through matter. This parity-odd neutron rotary power can reach up to 2.2e-3 rad * cm-1 for slow neutrons in 139La, which possesses a p-wave resonance at 0.7 eV where the parity-odd asymmetry is greatly amplified. This spin rotation must conserve total angular momentum. Here we show that this conservation occurs through neutron orbital angular momentum generation. We experimentally demonstrate that a neutron prepared initially in a longitudinal spin-orbit state experiences no change in total angular momentum.
