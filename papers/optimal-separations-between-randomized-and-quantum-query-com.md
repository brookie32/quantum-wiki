arXiv:2611.08919v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: A function f:Dsubseteq A^No{0,1} is permutation-invariant if every permutation of its input positions preserves both its domain and its value. We study how this symmetry limits quantum speedups. For 1/6leqarepsilon<1/2, we prove that every such function over an arbitrary finite alphabet A satisfies R_arepsilon(f)=O(Q_arepsilon(f)^2), where R_arepsilon and Q_arepsilon denote randomized and quantum query complexities with error at most arepsilon. This improves the previous cubic bound (ITCS 2019). We further establish bounds for graph and hypergraph properties, for which vertex relabeling preserves both the promise and the answer. For the same range of arepsilon, we obtain R_arepsilon(f)=O(Q_arepsilon(f)^4) for graph properties in the adjacency-matrix model and R_arepsilon(f)=O_k(Q_arepsilon(f)^{2k}) for k-uniform hypergraph properties with fixed kgeq2, where the implicit constant depends only on k. These improve the previous bounds R_{1/3}(f)=O(Q_{1/3}(f)^6) and R_{1/3}(f)=O_k(Q_{1/3}(f)^{3k}), respectively (SICOMP 2024) These bounds are tight for permutation-invariant functions and nearly tight for graph and hypergraph properties. Unstructured search gives a matching quadratic separation with R_{1/3}(f)=Theta!left(Q_{1/3}(f)^2right). For every fixed kgeq 2, we construct explicit families of invariant promise problems on k-uniform hypergraphs satisfying R_{1/3}(f)geq Q_{1/3}(f)^{2k-o(1)}.
