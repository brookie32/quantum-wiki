The 11th International Conference on Quantum Information and Quantum Control (CQIQC-XI) is fast approaching. If you're attending, be sure to visit Paarth Jain’s poster session on August 18th: "Quantum algorithm for simulating non-adiabatic dynamics at metallic surfaces". He’ll be breaking down our first-of-its-kind quantum algorithm for simulating non-adiabatic dynamics at metallic surfaces, and all the exciting technological applications it opens the door to. Read the paper the session is based on: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16264
