arXiv:2611.17928v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We construct a nonintegrable spin-1 chain that acquires a Yang--Baxter integrable no-jump Hamiltonian through a single dissipative channel per bond. Energy is the only nontrivial finite-range charge of the isolated periodic bulk. Interference cancels the reverse spin conversions, leaving directed moves that obey nil-Coxeter relations and generate commuting charges of increasing range on the open chain. We obtain the full Liouvillian spectrum at any chain length and identify an exponentially large set of blocked spin configurations whose superpositions are all stationary. Resolving the bond outputs leaves the no-jump Hamiltonian unchanged; each bond on which two configurations differ then contributes equally to their dephasing. In a two-component Bose--Hubbard chain, opposite loss phases produce counterpropagating species whose motion and collisions enter a common transfer matrix when interspecies attraction matches intraspecies repulsion. At this coupling, redistributing a pair costs no interaction energy in the atomic limit. Such bond interference offers a route to integrability in other open quantum many-body systems.
