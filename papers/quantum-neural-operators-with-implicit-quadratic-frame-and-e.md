**Quantum Neural Operators with Implicit Quadratic Frame (QuanONet)**  
QuanONet is a quantum neural operator designed for the noisy intermediate‑scale quantum era that extends the classical universal approximation theorem to continuous nonlinear operators in a purely quantum setting. By implicitly generating a quadratic feature frame via its density matrix, QuanONet achieves an \(\mathcal{O}(p^2)\) expressivity bound—surpassing the linear capacity limits of comparable classical models—and introduces a trainable‑frequency strategy (TF‑QuanONet) that adapts base frequency spacing without inflating parameters, yielding superior performance and optimization robustness in high-dimensional regimes (\(p \to 256\)).
