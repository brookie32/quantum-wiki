arXiv:2611.06729v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: In this study, we report on the use of Thomson coherent microwave scattering (CMS) to characterize photoionization rates in oxygen and nitrogen at 800 nm over a range spanning approximately three orders of magnitude, from about mid-10^9 s^{-1} range to the to mid-10^{12} s^{-1} range, corresponding to laser intensities in the vicinity of 10^{13}-10^{14} W/cm^2, a range of significant practical relevance to filamentation physics.
