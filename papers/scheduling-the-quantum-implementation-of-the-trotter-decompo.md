arXiv:2611.26884v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: For the quantum computational task for Hamiltonian simulation, the arrangement in which sub-Hamiltonians appear in the Trotter decomposition may have a large influence on the precision, under a given circuit complexity. In this paper, we design several scheduling strategies by combining two methods; that is, the existing simultaneous diagonalization scheme leveraged in order to guarantee the performance in a relatively short circuit depth, with a method inspired from a Trotter-Suzuki decomposition of the second or the fourth order for a better accuracy. By conducting numerical simulations on various Hamiltonian instances, we confirm a systematic advantage over previous methods. Thus, our proposal may eliminate the need of dealing with several Hamiltonian-dependent scheduling strategies.
