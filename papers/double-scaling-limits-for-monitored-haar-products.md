arXiv:2607.05693v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We study products of independent Haar matrices interspersed with a fixed corank-one projection. As the matrix dimension N and product length L grow with L/Noau, we prove that the empirical distribution of squared singular values converges to the free multiplicative Poisson law with S-transform S(z)=exp(au/(1+z)), for both the unitary and orthogonal ensembles. Lagrange inversion yields its moments explicitly, proving Beenakker's conjectured Erlang formula for every moment order.
