arXiv:2610.23398v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We extend our previous study of squeezed two-component Schrodinger cat states to a phase-referenced single-mode family with an arbitrary number N of coherent components and general relative phases. Within the established circular-state construction, we derive a closed complex overlap kernel for the ordered action of displacement, phase rotation, and squeezing, retaining exact finite-amplitude normalization. We distinguish finite-displacement orthogonality from local phase-estimation sensitivity quantified by quantum Fisher information (QFI). For equal-phase states, we compare N=2,ldots,9 at a common mean photon number and include coherent-state and squeezed-vacuum benchmarks. A bound on the higher photon-number sectors establishes the fixed-energy asymptote F_{Q,N}=4ar n(N-ar n)+o(1). Its leading term agrees with the known QFI of a vacuum--N-photon superposition. The resulting decrease of the ideal local quantum Cramer--Rao bound does not establish unlimited attainable precision: prior phase information, sample size, phase-reference resources, optical loss, state preparation, and measurement choice remain relevant.
