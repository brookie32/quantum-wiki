arXiv:2611.09406v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We study the interplay of latent symmetries and multiparticle quantum states upon the introduction of additional particles. We show how physical symmetries in multimode quantum systems give rise to symmetries in the multiparticle state space of indistinguishable fermions and bosons. Latent symmetries, hidden symmetries that are exclusively visible in the eigenspectral domain, offer new ways to engineer the functionality of multimode quantum systems. In this vein, we present multiboson state generation as an application of latent symmetries to the multiparticle domain. Finally, we demonstrate that even in systems that are entirely devoid of symmetry, latent symmetries can be induced in the multiparticle state space, as a result of quantum indistinguishability.
