arXiv:2611.09097v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Using the Majorana representation of spin-1/2 operators, we study the pm J Edwards--Anderson model in a transverse field at T=0. We show that, in this representation, the quantity describing the coupling between neighboring spins commutes with the Hamiltonian and, unlike in the Kitaev model, is not subject to any Gauss law. The resulting Z_2 gauge freedom allows the disorder in the signs of the exchange integrals to be eliminated by an exact gauge transformation, so that the spectrum of the system coincides with that of the model with uniform couplings. The resulting theory is quadratic in the Majorana fields. Computing the local Green's functions, we find that the Edwards--Anderson order parameter vanishes for Delta>Jsqrt{Z} and equals 1/4 for Delta<Jsqrt{Z}, which signals a second-order transition from a paramagnetic phase to a spin-glass ground state that is degenerate over spin configurations and replica symmetric at T=0.
