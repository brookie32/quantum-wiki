This study explores how hop‐derived molecules may impart a “freshness” sensation by comparing their structures to menthol, a known cooling agent. Using ligand‑based virtual screening, the authors applied high‑performance computing, quantum‑inspired, and quantum‑computing algorithms to assess structural and chemical similarity across a database of hop and botanical compounds. The analysis highlights candidate hop constituents whose menthol‑like features could influence freshness perception in beer.
