arXiv:2611.27256v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: This paper investigates measure synchronization (MS) in a two-component ultracold atomic system confined in a double-well potential, which realizes a quantum many-body bosonic Josephson junction. Through spectral-statistics analysis, we identify the quantum chaotic parameter regime and confirm that the system achieves MS within this regime with weak dependence on the initial energy imbalance. We further show that chaotic dynamics not only significantly shortens the time scale for establishing synchronization but is also accompanied by entanglement entropy approaching the random-state prediction. These results reveal close connections among quantum chaos, MS, and quantum entanglement in conservative quantum many-body systems and may motivate further studies on the underlying mechanisms of chaos-assisted synchronization.
