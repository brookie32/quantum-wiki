arXiv:2610.11097v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We show that performing lattice surgery on sparse stabiliser codes with sparse ancillas requires ilde{Omega}(k^2) ancillary qubits in the worst case. As a result, for codes with large k, constant overhead ancillas can only realise a small fraction of possible measurements.
