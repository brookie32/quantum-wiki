arXiv:2512.03984v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: The accurate and efficient detection of quantum entanglement remains a central challenge in quantum information science. In this work, we study the detection of entanglement of polarized photons for measurement devices that are specified by rotational covariance. We derive explicit positive operator-valued measures (POVMs) showing that any such (idealized) setting is classified by one real measurable parameter r. In particular, we give a POVM formulation of the Klein-Nishina formula for Compton scattering of polarized photons. We provide an SDP-based entanglement certification method with computable bounds that operates on the full measured statistics, also considering semi-device independent scenarios. Furthermore, we show that, while Bell violations are impossible with two rotationally covariant measurements, EPR steering can still be certified under one-sided symmetry constraints. Finally, we demonstrate how our approach can be applied by analyzing a rotationally covariant showcase experiment using scattered polarized light in a selection of soft drinks. Our results motivate further investigation of entanglement detection with lemonade- based detectors.
