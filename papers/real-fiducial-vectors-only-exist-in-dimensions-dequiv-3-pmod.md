arXiv:2610.13168v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We investigate restrictions on the dimensions and coordinate fields of real Weyl-Heisenberg fiducial vectors. Our main result is that such vectors can exist only in dimensions dequiv 3pmod{4}. This result is motivated by evidence showing that real Weyl-Heisenberg fiducial vectors can exist only in dimensions d = 4m^2 + 3, m in Z_{geq 0}. We further restrict the possible dimensions when the coordinate field is a totally real number field, an even or odd degree extension of Q, a real quadratic field, or a maximal real subfield of a cyclotomic field. Lastly, we establish non-existence over odd-degree number fields under either positivity of all non-trivial cyclic-shift overlaps or a conjectural containment of a distinguished quadratic field in the SIC field.
