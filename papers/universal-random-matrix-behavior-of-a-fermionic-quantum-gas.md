The study demonstrates that random matrix theory (RMT)—specifically, Fredholm determinant–based predictions—accurately describes the counting statistics of each spin component in an ultracold two‑state fermionic gas, even under strong attractive interactions. Experimental single‑atom measurements confirm that the spatial organization dictated by Pauli exclusion follows the universal Fermi‑sphere point process predicted by RMT. This provides a direct experimental validation of RMT’s applicability to strongly interacting quantum systems.
