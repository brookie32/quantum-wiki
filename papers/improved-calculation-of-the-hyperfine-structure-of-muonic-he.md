arXiv:2609.17454v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Using perturbation theory for the fine-structure constant alpha and the electron-to-muon mass ratio, we calculated new contributions to the hyperfine structure of the ground state of the muon-electron helium atom. Compared to our previous results, we calculated new corrections in second-order perturbation theory. This calculation reduces the discrepancy between the theoretical value and the new experimental hyperfine structure measurement.
