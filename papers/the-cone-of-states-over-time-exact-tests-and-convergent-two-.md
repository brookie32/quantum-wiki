arXiv:2611.18001v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: A state over time (stote) pairs a state rho with a channel E as the Hermitian, generally non-positive operator (rhootimesmathbf{1})star J_E. We study the convex cone widehat{Q} generated by these operators. Our main result is a convergent outer semidefinite hierarchy with an explicit trace-norm Hausdorff bound on its trace-one slices. Specializing constrained symmetric-extension theory to the input--channel representation, we show that Bose-extension rounding preserves the channel marginal constraint and gives error at most 2d_A^2(d_A-1)/(k+d_A-1) at level k. A complementary inner hierarchy retains all channels at each chosen pure-input direction. For d_Age2, its error is at most 2(d_A-1)sin^2elta, where elta is the Fubini--Study covering radius; nested sets of N directions give error at most 2pi^2(d_A-1)N^{-1/(d_A-1)}, independently of d_B. These bounds provide explicit size--accuracy guarantees and certified support-value bounds, without guaranteeing finite termination for exact boundary membership. For binary classical outputs, we obtain an exact SDP membership test, including indefinite output blocks and mixtures that are not single stotes. At faithful marginal the test reduces to a scalar inequality; every trace-one member of this sector with input marginal rho has a constructive realization using at most operatorname{rank}rho pure-input stotes with binary measurement channels.
