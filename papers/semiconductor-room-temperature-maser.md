First demonstrated is a continuous‑wave semiconductor maser based on silicon‑vacancy (VSi) centers in 4H‑silicon carbide (SiC), enabled by an active feedback loop that improves the resonator’s quality factor to support operation above room temperature.  
At 110 K the device acts as a high‑performance preamplifier with measured gain >10 dB (simulations predict >30 dB) and can also function as an optically pumped microwave photon absorber, lowering the resonator mode temperature by ~40 K relative to the environment.  
Its ultranarrow linewidth yields a ≈9‑order‑of‑magnitude improvement in contrast‑to‑linewidth ratio, enabling room‑temperature magnetometry with an estimated sensitivity of 20 pT/√Hz and establishing SiC as a platform for compact, electrically driven maser diodes.
