arXiv:2610.35622v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The low-energy sector of the lanthanide f^n shell is to first approximation governed by the interplay of three interactions: Coulomb repulsion, spin-same-orbit coupling and the ligand crystal field potential. To rationalize the energy landscape in different chemical environments, it is desirable to extract the phenomenological crystal field parameters from experimental data; however, in practice, high-dimensional fitting procedures complicate their routine determination. Here, we present a computational extraction procedure based on real tensorial sets and the U(7) canonical chain that, unlike existing operator equivalent methods, takes all J multiplets of the lowest-energy LS term into account, ensuring meaningful results even in the presence of considerable J-mixing. Two series of lanthanide compounds, [Ln(III)(C_8H_8)_2]^-} and doped Ln(III):Y_2O_3} (Ln in {Ce, Pr, Nd, Sm, Eu}) serve as examples for high- (D_{8h}) and low-symmetry (C_2) scenarios. Consequences for the generalisation toward actinides are briefly discussed.
