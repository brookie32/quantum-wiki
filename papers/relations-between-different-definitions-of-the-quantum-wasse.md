arXiv:2605.03027v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: The quantum Wasserstein distances defined by Golse, Mouhot, Paul, and Caglioti and by De Palma and Trevisan coincide for qubits when a single operator appears in the cost function. As a consequence, the square of the self-distance equals the Wigner-Yanase skew information in this case. Moreover, for finite-dimensional systems of dimension dge2 and any number of operators, we show that the Golse-Mouhot-Paul-Caglioti self-distance is bounded from below by the De Palma-Trevisan self-distance.
