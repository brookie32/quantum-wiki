arXiv:2611.23796v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Tracking a fixed fraction of a quasiperiodic spectrum tests whether repeated critical--localized alternation extends over a nonzero spectral weight. We find such alternation in a matched-dimer chain by varying only the spacer on-site energies and following a fixed interval of integrated-density-of-states (IDS) labels. An exact Bloch-determinant identity links the physical transfer discriminant to the bands of a periodic reference operator. For the golden cell frequency, we prove that, on a common full-measure set of phases and for every finite value of the spacer control, the physical spectrum in the open reference bands is critical, with purely singular-continuous spectrum and zero Lyapunov exponent, while the spectrum strictly outside the bands is pure point, with exponentially decaying eigenfunctions. Validated IDS bounds certify repeated critical--localized alternation of the same fixed spectral fraction at successive spacer controls. Spacer shifts move the physical levels and reference-band edges at different rates, allowing us to reshape finite-chain return windows and place their endpoints at prescribed controls. An affine recalibration relocates the control family exactly.
