A hybrid magnon–photon platform using a printed‑circuit‑board microstrip resonator coupled to a yttrium iron garnet film produces correlated microwave signals at room temperature.  By simultaneously coupling two cavity photon modes with distinct magnon modes, the system splits an input photon into non‑degenerate magnon polariton pairs that retain strong intermode correlations and display random nonlinear dynamics.  The resulting multichannel correlations enable reliable microwave communication across two noisy channels.
