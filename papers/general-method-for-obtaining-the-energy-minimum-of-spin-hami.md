arXiv:2605.03022v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We introduce a highly general method to determine the energy minimum of spin Hamiltonians over separable states when the single-particle reduced density matrices are fixed. We present efficient numerical methods that reduce the problem to calculations in a two-particle system. For a broad class of very general spin models, we present analytic formulas for the minimum. For ferromagnetic Ising and Ising-like models of spin-j particles, the minimum over separable states on two-colorable lattices, and over symmetric separable states on fully connected graphs, is given by a compact analytic formula involving the quantum Fisher information. For antiferromagnetic Ising-like and XY-like models of spin j-particles living on a complete graph, the energy minimum for symmetric separable states is given with the expectation value of single-particle operators. For the ferromagnetic Heisenberg chain of spin-1/2 particles, the minimum is expressed via the Uhlmann-Jozsa fidelity. These relations enable the extraction of the quantum Fisher information and quantum fidelity of the corresponding single-particle reduced states from correlation measurements on ground-state ensembles of suitably engineered spin models. For a number of other models, we obtain not necessarily tight lower bounds. We also present bounds for various levels of multiparticle entanglement.
