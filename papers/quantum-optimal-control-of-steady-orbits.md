arXiv:2606.15383v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Periodically driven dissipative systems can settle into steady orbits - fixed infinite loops on their dynamical manifolds. In quantum mechanics, steady orbits occur in cooling engines, lasers and masers, precision metrology devices, and magnetic resonance. Steady orbits and stroboscopic steady states are a promising target for quantum optimal control, but the numerical complexity is prohibitive: infinite loops defeat state-of-the-art algorithms that use explicit numerical propagation in the time domain. We report a solution to this problem and introduce a numerically efficient quantum optimal control algorithm for steady orbits. We use this algorithm to design microwave pulse sequences that dynamically polarise nuclear spins and enhance their magnetic resonance signals, while taking into account instrumental distortions. Improved performance relative to leading current methods is demonstrated experimentally.
