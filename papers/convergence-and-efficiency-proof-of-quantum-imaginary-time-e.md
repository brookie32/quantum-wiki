Quantum imaginary time evolution (QITE) has been proven to converge to the global minimum without suffering from local‑minimum traps, critical slowing down, or exponential resource scaling for a broad class of bounded‑order quantum systems. The required evolution time is shown to scale linearly with system size and inversely with the energy gap. A subsequent complexity analysis demonstrates that QITE can be efficiently compiled into parametric quantum circuits, enabling optimal parameter determination for many physically relevant problems in physics, chemistry, combinatorial optimisation, and quantum machine learning.
