arXiv:2611.27200v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We investigate the unconventional photon blockade in a second-order nonlinearity coupled double-cavity system with asymmetric dissipation. By treating the cavity decay rates as independent parameters, we analytically derive two exact families of optimal blockade conditions. While the first family recovers and generalizes the symmetric-dissipation results of previous studies, the second family, arising uniquely from an asymmetric dissipation ratio of kappa_B = 2kappa_A, completely locks the relative phase of the interference pathways. Importantly, we find that this asymmetric dissipation not only renders the optimal blockade condition strictly immune to frequency detuning, but also enhances the blockade depth by an order of magnitude compared to the resonant case. Furthermore, the mean photon number remains almost unaffected, proving that this enhanced robustness is achieved without sacrificing the brightness of the single-photon source. These findings offer a promising route toward robust single-photon generation in passive cavities with second-order nonlinearity.
