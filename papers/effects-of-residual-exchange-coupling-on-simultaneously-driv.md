arXiv:2609.15751v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Exchange coupling and microwave drives are widely used control mechanisms for spin qubits. However, the influence of exchange coupling on microwave-driven spin dynamics is not fully understood. We report simultaneous drive measurements of two spin qubits as a function of exchange coupling and drive power. When the Rabi frequency exceeds exchange, we observe a beating pattern in the Rabi oscillations. In the opposite limit, two exchange-split patterns emerge in the Rabi chevrons. We find that these exchange-induced effects are suppressed when the difference in the Rabi frequencies exceeds the exchange coupling. A theoretical model is developed that reproduces the main features in our data.
