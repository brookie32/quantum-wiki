arXiv:2611.33955v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Preparing an entangled state faithfully requires an amount of resource given by its entanglement cost. With less entanglement, the best achievable fidelity is governed by the Renyi entanglement of formation of orders above one. We prove that, for any finite-energy multimode bosonic Gaussian state, all these quantities are additive and attained by a Gaussian pure-state decomposition. It follows that the closest separable state in fidelity to any Gaussian state is Gaussian, and that the strong converse exponent of entanglement dilution, which sets how fast the optimal fidelity decays below the cost, is exactly computable. For channels, we prove that pure Gaussian inputs minimize the output Renyi entropies of every multimode Gaussian channel at all orders alphage1, and we obtain the optimal fidelity of simulating pure-loss and quantum-limited amplifier channels with finite entanglement, for any number of uses.
