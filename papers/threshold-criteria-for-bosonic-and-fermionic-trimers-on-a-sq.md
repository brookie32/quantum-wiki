arXiv:2610.37142v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We compare two three-particle lattice models on the square lattice at total quasimomentum zero: three identical bosons with attractive contact interactions, and a two-fermion plus one distinguishable particle system with attractive interspecies contact. Using the Birman--Schwinger principle combined with symmetry reduction and strong-coupling asymptotics, we distinguish shared methodology from model-dependent spectral conclusions. For bosons, the ground-state energy behaves as minus three times the coupling plus six minus three over twice the coupling plus higher-order corrections, and exactly two bound states exist below the threshold for large coupling; the second lies at a finite distance 0.03542 below the threshold, a genuine eigenvalue rather than a threshold resonance. For fermions, the critical mass ratio is pi divided by pi minus two, approximately 2.75194; above it, a doubly degenerate level appears at strong coupling. Near onset, the binding energy follows a linear-over-logarithmic law. The results are compared with numerical finite-grid Faddeev computations and with recent three-dimensional classifications of threshold states.
