arXiv:2611.33888v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We study local spectral dephasing as a mechanism for decoherent histories in isolated quantum many-body systems. We prove that vanishing projector-weighted spectral concentration is asymptotically equivalent to vanishing time-averaged local memory. With independently sampled waiting times and suitable conditions on the initial state and history weights, this local property suppresses normalized interference across complete fixed-length history families with high probability. For translation-invariant lattices, spectral estimates provide conditions under which dephasing occurs at long times. Exact diagonalization and sparse time evolution of a spin chain illustrate the results.
