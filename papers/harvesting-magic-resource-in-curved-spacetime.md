In Communications Physics (2026), Yang et al. study the extraction of non‑stabilizerness (“magic”) from the quantum vacuum by coupling a qutrit Unruh–DeWitt detector to a scalar field in anti‑de Sitter spacetime. They introduce an analytic series technique that resolves divergent coherence terms, enabling computation where conventional numerical integration fails; their results show that greater curvature and higher dimensionality strongly suppress the amount of extractable magic from the vacuum. The method is positioned as broadly useful for quantum field theory and relativistic quantum information investigations.
