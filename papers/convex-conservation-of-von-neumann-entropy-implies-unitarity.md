arXiv:2608.29373v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: On the space of density matrices D(H), Kadison's theorem establishes that all invertible convex transformations K:D(H)o D(H) are unitarity or antiunitary. For a finite Hilbert space, we propose a similar theorem which replaces the invertibility requirement with that of entropy conservation. Thus, we argue, the supposition of reversibility in quantum mechanics may be replaced with a principle of entropy conservation.
