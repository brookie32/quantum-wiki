A fully integrated silicon photonic chip was fabricated that unites a micro‑resonator quantum‑frequency comb source with on‑chip pump‑rejection filters, high‑speed EO phase modulators, and a four‑channel line‑by‑line pulse shaper.  The device enables programmable single‑photon operations such as tunable frequency‑beam splitters that achieve >94 % success probability and >99.9 % fidelity, and it can synthesize arbitrary single‑qubit gates in the frequency basis.  Using this platform, high‑dimensional frequency‑bin entangled states are generated and coherently manipulated entirely on chip, demonstrating control over two‑photon quantum walks and other multi‑mode photonic protocols.
