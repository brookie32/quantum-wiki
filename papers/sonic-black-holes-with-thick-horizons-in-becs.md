arXiv:2607.11396v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We consider simple one dimensional models of acoustic black holes formed by Bose-Einstein condensates where the flow is stepwise homogeneous. We shall concentrate on the case in which an extended sonic region is present, i.e. a region where the flow velocity equals exactly the speed of sound. These models can be thought of as acoustic black holes with a thick horizon. Particular attention will be devoted to the number of created particles and to the density-density correlation functions, these latter being at present time the experimental tool to identify Hawking like radiation in acoustic black holes.
