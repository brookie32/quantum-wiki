The authors propose a neutron-based measurement of the gravitational constant (G) using a phase‑grating Moiré interferometer that boosts usable flux by orders of magnitude while preserving interferometric area. They develop a Monte‑Carlo simulation capable of modeling nonlinear gravitational potentials and systematic effects, allowing quantification of uncertainties in G at a level comparable to CODATA 2022 evaluations. This work presents both the instrument design and the computational framework needed for an independent, high‑precision determination of G.
