arXiv:2611.18413v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Dicke superradiance, the collective spontaneous emission of identical quantum emitters into a common reservoir, produces a burst with a characteristic delay t_{SR} and a peak intensity that scales quadratically with the emitter number N. This strong dependence makes the burst sensitive to how many emitters actually participate. In experiments, however, this number is rarely fixed: it fluctuates between preparations and decreases during emission through atom loss. Here we extend the analytical treatment of superradiance to variable emitter numbers using the Schwinger-boson mapping of the collective spin onto two bosonic modes. In this formulation, collective decay annihilates an atom in the excited-state mode and creates one in the ground-state mode. We find that number fluctuations enhance emission and that the ensemble-averaged emission trace encodes the initial number distribution. We show how to reconstruct second-order correlations from photon counting with finite detection efficiency and temporal resolution, even when the full distribution cannot be resolved. For atom loss at rate r, we derive exact results for the population dynamics and show that the peak intensity scaling is governed by the single scaling variable g_{r}=r t_{SR}. The peak remains quadratic in N for g_{r}1, and crosses over to linear scaling under strong loss with g_{r}gg1.
