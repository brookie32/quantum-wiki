The paper demonstrates that entanglement‑enhanced learning (EEL) can reduce the sample complexity of quantum process tomography for Pauli channels by converting non‑commuting measurements into commuting ones, even when noisy entangled resources are used. The authors provide a theoretical framework for error‑mitigated EEL, quantify the noise‑induced overhead (1.33 ± 0.05 per qubit in experiments), and perform hypothesis testing on up to 64 qubits and parallel‑gate noise learning on a 16‑qubit superconducting device. Results show that noisy quantum memory still yields a learning advantage over classical approaches.
