arXiv:2611.09738v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Experimentally certifying polarization entanglement in annihilation photons is challenging because polarizers are unavailable in the high-energy regime and one needs to rely on Compton polarimetry. Previously proposed schemes designed for this task are either experimentally unfeasible or require a specific source state model. We address these issues by reformulating Compton scattering as a continuous-outcome quantum instrument, thereby enabling the prediction of the scattering angles and the post-scattering polarization state. Our framework allows connecting Compton polarimetry with entanglement witnesses by deriving experimentally accessible lower bounds on Bell state fidelities for arbitrary initial two-photon polarization states. We discuss how this witness can be extracted from coarse-grained coincidence measurements with detectors of almost any angular resolution. The presented scheme does not require knowledge of angle-independent empirical factors like detector efficiencies and source activity. Thus, our results provide a feasible route towards experimental entanglement certification in para-positronium decay with current technology.
