Publisher Correction issued on 14 September 2026 for the Nature Machine Intelligence article *Quantum neural operators with implicit quadratic frame and expressivity advantages* (originally published 3 September 2026). The correction notes that a missing Greek letter “Λ” was omitted from equation 3 in the PDF, which has been updated to \(M(u)=B(u)\,\Lambda\,B^\dagger(u)\) to match the HTML version. The authors Ruocheng Wang and Xiaoqiu Zhong are co‑first authors of the work.
