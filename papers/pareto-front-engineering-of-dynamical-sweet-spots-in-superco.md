The authors present a fully parameterized, multi‑objective framework for engineering dynamical sweet spots (DSSs) in superconducting qubits, optimizing both energy relaxation (T₁) and pure dephasing (Tφ). Applied to fluxonium devices, their method boosts Tφ by 3–5× while keeping T₁ in the 100 µs range and proves that a fundamental upper bound on T₁ cannot be bypassed even with first‑order noise suppression. The approach also produces leakage‑suppressed single‑ and two‑qubit control protocols that achieve high‑fidelity gates, offering a general Pareto‑front strategy to enhance qubit coherence and operation.
