arXiv:2611.27711v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We assess the transferability of the machine-learned Skala exchange-correlation (XC) functional beyond ground-state energetics using molecular vertical excitation energies and singlet-triplet gaps calculated with the Delta-self-consistent-field (DeltaSCF) approach. We compare Skala with PBE, r^2SCAN, and PBE0 on subsets covering small-molecule excitations (QUEST1), inverted singlet-triplet gaps (ST-gap), and intramolecular charge-transfer excitations (QUEST6). Both spin-unrestricted Kohn-Sham (UKS) and restricted open-shell Kohn-Sham (ROKS) formalisms are considered. On QUEST1, Skala achieves the lowest mean absolute errors (MAEs) for singlet and triplet excitation energies with both formalisms, although PBE0 and r^2SCAN provide more accurate singlet-triplet gaps through error cancellation. For ST-gap, Skala yields the lowest gap MAEs with both UKS and ROKS. Evaluating the mixed-spin and triplet energy components on the same orbitals optimized for the ROKS open-shell singlet yields negative splittings for seven of eight ST-gap molecules with Skala. Separate triplet optimization increases the gaps, but six remain negative. All three conventional functionals give positive common-orbital splittings and optimized gaps for all eight molecules. For QUEST6, treated with UKS only, Skala yields more accurate singlet excitation energies than PBE and r^2SCAN but remains less accurate than PBE0. Across QUEST1 and ST-gap, Skala yields lower MAEs with UKS than ROKS. Overall, these results support Skala's transferability to molecular excited states while highlighting its dependence on the excitation class and open-shell treatment.
