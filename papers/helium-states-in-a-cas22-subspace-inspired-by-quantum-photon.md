arXiv:2611.06448v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We present a four-mode Fock-state photonics implementation of the variational quantum deflation (VQD) algorithm to calculate the ground-state and excited-state energies for the neutral helium atom in a CAS(2,2) Subspace. Its CAS(2,2) electronic Hilbert space is encoded into a bosonic Fock-space representation and subsequently optimized using a continuous-variable quantum circuit. The circuit architecture under study is constructed of single-mode phase shifts, beam splitters, and cross-Kerr interactions. The variational circuit is initialized in the Fock state ket{1100} and its performance is benchmarked against exact diagonalization of the active-space configuration-interaction (CASCI) Hamiltonian. We also extend the method to optimize from the first to three excited states of the helium atom with the aid of the variational quantum deflation (VQD) method in a CAS(2,2). We then investigate the performance of this circuit architecture as a basis for implementing the VQD method. In the ideal noiseless simulation setting, we see that the survival probability of the ground and the excited states within the target CAS subspace is unity, because the total photon number and the photon number in the two spin sectors alpha and eta are preserved. We demonstrate that the proposed approach enables the determination of the ground and excited-state energies within the CAS(2,2) electronic Hilbert space using a continuous-variable quantum circuit and the variational quantum deflation (VQD) algorithm, while satisfying the orthogonality conditions with negligible errors.
