arXiv:2512.23299v5 Announce Type: replace Abstract: If the phase space-based Glauber-Sudarshan distribution, P_{arrho}, has negative values the quantum state, arrho, it describes is nonclassical. Due to P's singular behavior, this simple criterion is impractical to use. Recent work has presented a general, sensitive, and noise-tolerant certification functional for the detection of nonclassical behavior of quantum states arrho. There, it was shown that when this functional takes on negative values somewhere in phase space, xi_{P}(x,p) < 0, this is sufficient to certify the nonclassicality of a state. Here we give examples where this certification fails. We investigate states which are known to be nonclassical but the certification function is nonnegative, xi_{P}(x,p) geq 0, everywhere in phase space. We generalize xi, giving it an appealing form, al S, which allows for slight improvements in certification, but al S also fails for mixed very weakly nonclassical states. More important than a slight improvement in sensitivity of al S over xi is that we showed how very sensitive xi and al S are, and more important still is our simple derivation of al S allowing us to generalize xi and al S to multiple modes.
