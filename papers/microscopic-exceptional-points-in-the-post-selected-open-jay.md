arXiv:2606.14982v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Phenomenological non-Hermitian Hamiltonians track selected signatures of complex reservoir dynamics, while post-selected no-jump effective Hamiltonians derived from microscopic open-system theory reveal the underlying system--reservoir physics. We derive the conditioned Jaynes--Cummings spectrum and propagator at finite temperature using Moore--Penrose normalized generators on the full Hilbert space. Within the Born--Markov approximation, full Bohr-frequency resolution excludes exceptional points; partial resolution permits coalescence when transition splittings compete with relaxation and the reservoir responses vary negligibly over the coupled transitions. For zero-temperature examples on shifted resonance, where the bare detuning compensates the reservoir-induced differential Lamb shift, unequal independent losses produce an excitation-dependent threshold, while equal shared losses with vanishing cross Lamb shifts and an orthogonal coupling phase produce a threshold independent of excitation number within the partial-resolution regime. Our shared-loss examples show oscillatory conditional transfer below the common threshold, monotonic transfer toward unit population at it, and a lower asymptotic population above it.
