arXiv:2610.28144v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We investigate photon-to-axion-like-particle (ALP) conversion in a finite, linearly polarized laser pulse for a single ALP field with simultaneous scalar-type and pseudoscalar-type couplings to photons. Varying the collision angle between the strong pulse and the probe provides a continuous kinematic scan of the resonant ALP mass. For fixed kinematics in this setting, the scalar-type and pseudoscalar-type ALP conversion amplitudes interfere coherently, making both the photon-to-ALP conversion probability and the polarization rotation depend on the difference between the probe polarization angle alpha and the coupling angle heta_g. Conversion is maximal at |alpha-heta_g|=90^irc, where the polarization rotation vanishes, while the rotation magnitude peaks at alpha-heta_g=45^irc modulo 90^irc, where conversion is half-maximal. These complementary polarization-angle dependences provide polarization-resolved signatures of mixed scalar--pseudoscalar photon couplings.
