Researchers demonstrated tunable superradiant frequency combs by coupling a driven ensemble of rare‑earth‑ion spins to an on‑chip superconducting resonator, observing a dynamical phase transition from continuous‐wave to periodic pulsed superradiant emission. A driven‑dissipative cavity‑quantum‑electrodynamics model explains the emergent pulsed regime as collective spin dynamics stabilized by coherence growth, disorder, and dissipation. This work establishes a dual‑rail (optical and microwave) superradiant comb source, links pulsed superradiance to continuous time‑crystal behavior in driven open systems, and opens new avenues for atom‑based programmable frequency‑comb generators.
