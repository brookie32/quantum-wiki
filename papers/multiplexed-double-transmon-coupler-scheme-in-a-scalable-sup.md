The paper introduces a control‑line multiplexing scheme based on a double–transmon coupler to reduce wiring overhead in scalable superconducting quantum processors. Experimental results show that the architecture effectively suppresses static \(ZZ\) coupling while enabling accurate two‑qubit operations, demonstrated with fast z‑control CZ and parametric iSWAP gates. High‑fidelity entangled states were achieved, producing Bell states with >99 % fidelity and three‑qubit GHZ states at 96 %.
