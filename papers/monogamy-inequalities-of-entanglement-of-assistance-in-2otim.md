arXiv:2607.18643v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The monogamy relations characterize the distribution of quantum correlations among the multipartite quantum systems. We study the monogamy relations of the entanglement of assistance in 2otimes 2otimes d systems. We present explicitly the relations satisfied by the concurrence, the tangle and the concurrence of assistance, which can be used to derive rigorous monogamy relations. Detailed examples are given to illustrate our results.
