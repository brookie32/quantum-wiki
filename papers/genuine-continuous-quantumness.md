The authors introduce **nonlinear squeezing** as a unified theoretical tool that captures and verifies genuine quantumness (non‑Gaussian behavior) in the noise of continuous‑variable quantum states. Using this framework, they experimentally certify non‑Gaussianity for multi‑photon‑added coherent light, demonstrating third‑order and fifth‑order quantum nonlinearities—an improvement over previous state‑of‑the‑art techniques—and provide a scalable method for probing intricate quantum properties in advanced experiments.
