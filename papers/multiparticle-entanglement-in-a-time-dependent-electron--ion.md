arXiv:2611.14142v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Photoionization provides a natural setting for entanglement transfer in the time domain: an outgoing electron can become entangled with the residual ion, while subsequent spontaneous emission may transfer these correlations to an emitted photon. We quantify the entanglement dynamics of the composite electron--ion--photon system using the von Neumann entropy of the one-versus-rest bipartitions, showing that the initially entangled electron--ion system evolves through a genuinely multiparticle-entangled state before approaching an entangled electron--photon state. Logarithmic negativity further resolves the pairwise entanglement, revealing in particular transient ion--photon entanglement. Further, its enhanced sensitivity to weakly populated Schmidt modes additionally exposes high-dimensional entanglement associated with the photon-energy continuum.
