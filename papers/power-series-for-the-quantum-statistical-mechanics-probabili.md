arXiv:2608.21451v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: A power series for the Wigner-Kirkwood pair commutation function for quantum statistical mechanics in classical phase space is given with terms automatically generated by recursion. The calculated second virial coefficient agrees with the measured values of helium for temperatures greater than 65 K. Prospects for a general quantum Monte Carlo algorithm are discussed.
