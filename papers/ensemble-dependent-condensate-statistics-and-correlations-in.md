arXiv:2610.36118v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The choice of statistical ensemble has a measurable impact on the statistical description of Bose-Einstein condensates beyond the well-known grand canonical anomaly. We develop a segmented-contour approach for the ideal Bose gas, which refines the saddle-point approximation by including non-Gaussian corrections in the canonical and microcanonical ensembles. The method applies to a broad class of noninteracting spectra, and provides efficient access to condensate fluctuations, higher moments, and correlation functions. For a three-dimensional harmonic trap with fixed anisotropy, we find that the ratio of the maxima of the microcanonical and canonical condensate-number variances approaches the theoretically predicted asymptotic value 1-3zeta(3)^2/[4zeta(2)zeta(4)]approx 0.391 only extremely slowly with increasing particle number. We also show that ensemble effects are visible in the second-order correlation function g^{(2)}(x) near the condensation crossover. After ballistic release from a three-dimensional harmonic trap, the time evolution of g^{(2)}(x) preserves the differences between statistical ensembles.
