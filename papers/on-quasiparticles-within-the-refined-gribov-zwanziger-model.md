arXiv:2609.04423v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: The problem of the effective excitations of a gauge theory in the nonperturbative regime remains not completely understood. Using the Refined Gribov-Zwanziger (RGZ) theory as effective model for pure gauge Yang-Mills theories, we revisit the quasiparticle (quadratic) excitations of the theory. A novel interpretation of such quasiparticles is proposed, in the case of real mass poles, taking into account the manifest {al PT}-symmetry of the RGZ Lagrangian.
