arXiv:2607.21515v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: A central challenge for cavity-QED-based quantum technologies is to make high-cooperativity optical interfaces compatible with site-resolved arrays of single atoms. Here, we demonstrate optical tweezer arrays of individual ^{87}Rb atoms inside a fiber Fabry-Perot microcavity with single-atom cooperativity C sim 90. We combine background-free site-resolved fluorescence imaging of extended arrays with collective coupling to a common cavity mode for arrays with a mean atom number up to ar{N} simeq 36. These results establish a high-cooperativity platform for many-body cavity-QED with site-resolved detection and control.
