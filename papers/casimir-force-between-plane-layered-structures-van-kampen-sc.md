arXiv:2608.16567v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Based on the van Kampen-Schram method, a formula for the Casimir force between two plane-layered structures is obtained, which includes the sum of the Matsubara frequencies and a contour integral. It is shown that the contour integral is not taken into account in the Lifshitz formula. Taking it into account gives a small contribution at high temperatures when the Lifshitz formula is approximately correct. However, at low temperatures, its contribution is significant, and the Lifshitz formula for the finite temperature does not transform into the Lifshitz formula at zero temperature. An example of interaction of metal plates is considered.1
