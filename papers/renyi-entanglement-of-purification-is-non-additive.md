arXiv:2608.28897v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Entanglement of purification is a fundamental measure of total correlations whose additivity remains unresolved. We study its additivity for classical states on two qubits at different Renyi orders. For every alphain[0,1), we prove nonadditivity within this family, witnessed by two copies of a single state. We first solve the one-copy optimization exactly for the entire family at every Renyi order. We then restrict the two-copy optimization to a natural finite set of purifications and exhibit one whose entropy is strictly below the product value. In contrast, for alphain[2,infty] we prove additivity under tensor products within this family. The interval alphain[1,2), including the von Neumann case alpha=1, remains open, and we conjecture additivity there throughout the same family.
