arXiv:2610.38338v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We construct a time evolution operator for a dynamical quantum models with a multi-component random Gaussian field characterized by a long correlation time. We analyze three paradigmatic cases: non-Abelian SU(2) symmetry class for the three-component Gaussian noise and Abelian symmetry classes U(1) and Z_2 for the two- and one-component noises correspondingly. Starting with a limiting case of the infinite correlation time (Keldysh model) we shown that statistics of the two-component random field with a non-zero mean is described by the Rice distribution. We derived and solved the system of coupled linear differential equations for the exact diagonal and off-diagonal single-particle Green's functions. We showed how the finite time correlation in Keldysh models are accounted by a continuous fractions method.
