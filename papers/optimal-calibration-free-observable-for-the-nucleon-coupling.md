arXiv:2608.07456v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: A dual-alkali single-cell ^{87}Rb-^{39}K-^{3}He comagnetometer can read an axionlike dark matter signal through two optical-rotation channels, encoding the ratio R=xi_n/xi_p of the field's neutron and proton spin couplings in their relative response. The inter-species phase difference Deltaarphi has been proposed as a calibration-free readout that is sensitive to R. Treating the extraction of R as a statistical estimation problem, we show that the optimal observable is the complex inter-channel ratio, which splits into Deltaarphi and an amplitude ratio, of which only Deltaarphi is insensitive to the relative gain and hence calibration-free. For our choice of comagnetometer parameters, above sim!100 Hz the phase difference alone captures most of the coupling-ratio information. At lower frequencies Deltaarphi is not near-sufficient: there the amplitude ratio would improve the precision on R by a factor of gtrsim2 below sim!40 Hz. Recovering that information, however, requires the relative gain to be known sufficiently accurately, so Deltaarphi stays the robust observable even where it is not the optimal one.
