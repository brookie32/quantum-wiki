arXiv:2611.14968v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Superconductor-insulator-superconductor Josephson junctions with crystalline tunnel barriers have remained elusive by epitaxial techniques such as MOCVD or MBE that produce the junction materials and devices powering today's electronic and photonic systems like transistors and lasers. Here, we overcome the key materials hurdle to realize the first functional nitride crystalline barrier Josephson junctions by MBE. Despite an AlN barrier thickness of approximately 4.5 nm, the NbN/AlN/NbN trilayers exhibit coherent Josephson tunneling with critical current densities of approximately 17 A/cm2, an IcRn product of approximately 2.5 mV, and a superconducting gap voltage of approximately 3.7 mV with a BCS-like gap evolution with temperature. Hysteretic current-voltage characteristics and Fraunhofer current modulation with magnetic field establish underdamped SIS Josephson behavior. The realization of crystalline barrier Josephson junctions by MBE opens the doors to a richer materials palette with alloys and superlattice electrodes and tunnel barriers, as well as potential monolithic integration of superconducting quantum circuits with active electronic and photonic devices such as HEMTs and lasers.
