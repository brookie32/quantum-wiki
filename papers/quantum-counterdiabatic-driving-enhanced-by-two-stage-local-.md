This study evaluates local counterdiabatic protocols for the transverse‑field Ising model, deriving a closed‑form expression for ground–state fidelity and demonstrating that approximate adiabatic gauge potentials can suppress diabatic losses without requiring Hamiltonian diagonalization or knowledge of spectral gaps. A new two‑stage protocol—combining the local counterdiabatic drive with an additional simple single‑body control—further improves state preparation accuracy while remaining fully implementable with only local operations. The approach is validated experimentally on a trapped‑ion system, achieving high‑fidelity adiabatic evolution for up to 14 qubits and confirming that quantum shortcuts to adiabaticity can be realized in many‑body platforms without non‑local control resources.
