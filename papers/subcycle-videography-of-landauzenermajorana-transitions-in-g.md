The authors present attosecond‑precision, sub‑cycle videography that captures electron dynamics throughout the entire first Brillouin zone of graphene under intense optical fields. By imaging band‑structure evolution in real time, they directly observe non‑adiabatic Landau–Zener–Majorana tunnelling—a strong‑field interband transition where electrons are accelerated and tunnel between Dirac bands within a single lightwave cycle. The work demonstrates how ultra‑fast, sub‑cycle measurements can resolve the quantum pathways of coherent electron motion in two‑dimensional materials.
