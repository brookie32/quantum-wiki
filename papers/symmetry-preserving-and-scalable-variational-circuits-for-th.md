arXiv:2610.30270v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We study variational preparation of ground states and sector-resolved excited states in the (1+1)-dimensional lattice Gross-Neveu model. We construct fixed circuits preserving either total fermion number or every flavor number, and a problem-specific scalable-circuit adaptive variational quantum eigensolver (SC-ADAPT-VQE). Classical circuit benchmarks cover massless and massive systems through 30 qubits, using exact-diagonalization and tensor-network reference results. Imposing flavor-number conservation improves the achieved energies in the completed two-flavor comparisons. Selected massive fixed-ansatz benchmarks with one to four flavors have relative energy errors of approximately 1 imes 10^{-5} - 2 imes 10^{-4}, smaller than the quoted literature errors where available, although discretizations and computational resources differ. The adaptive pool combines translated bulk and boundary-sensitive generators with an interaction-sensitive cross-flavor family. Selected massive SC-ADAPT benchmarks with one to three flavors have relative energy errors below 2.1 imes 10^{-3}. For one flavor, matrix-product-state evaluations extend the transferred circuits to 60-400 qubits without target-side operator selection or parameter optimization, giving energy deviations of approximately 0.35% - 1.76% from density-matrix renormalization-group reference values at maximum bond dimension 256. At fixed flavor number, range caps, and append count, the adaptive construction has a precompilation local-factor count linear in volume and a volume-independent shared-parameter count; fixed-accuracy performance at arbitrary volumes remains unestablished. Sequential deflation also prepares low-lying states within a fixed flavor-number sector, with projector fidelities providing a basis-independent assessment of degenerate target eigenspaces.
