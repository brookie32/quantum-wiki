arXiv:2609.13032v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We prove mathsf{QMA} = mathsf{QMA_1}, i.e., every quantum Merlin-Arthur proof system can be made perfectly complete. Our construction uses only Hadamard, Toffoli, and X gates, yielding a universal gate set for mathsf{QMA_1}. As a consequence, quantum 3-SAT is mathsf{QMA}-complete. The construction relativizes to classical oracles, so known classical-oracle separations of mathsf{QMA} from mathsf{QCMA} extend to mathsf{QMA_1}.
