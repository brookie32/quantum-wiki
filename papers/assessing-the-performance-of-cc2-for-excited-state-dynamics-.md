arXiv:2604.05734v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: During the ultrafast electronic relaxation of pyrazine, the bright state undergoes rapid decay accompanied by coupled electronic and nuclear motion. We investigate the vibrational modes associated with this process using trajectory surface hopping simulations. To enable accurate ab initio excited-state dynamics, we apply CC2 analytical gradients and nonadiabatic coupling vectors within the Q-Chem package. Using both a reduced vibronic coupling model and full-dimensional simulations, we demonstrate that CC2 predicts that the dark A_ext{1u} state actively participates in the initial relaxation and the subsequent dynamics. The full-dimensional simulations yield a B_ext{2u} decay time of 25--26 fs, in good agreement with the experimental measurement of 22pm3 fs. Furthermore, we identify the Q_ext{8a} and Q_ext{9a} modes as dominant tuning coordinates whose motions are phase-locked to the population oscillations between the A_{1u} and B_{3u} states.
