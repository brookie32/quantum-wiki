The authors theoretically predict quasi‑solitonic excitations in chains of Rydberg atoms under strong nearest‑neighbor blockade, where localized wave packets propagate directionally atop specially reviving initial states linked to quantum many‑body scars. These solitons can carry energy, exhibit long coherence times, and maintain non‑ergodic dynamics, making them viable for quantum information transfer on current Rydberg atom simulators. The study also maps these excitations to a classical nonlinear system and suggests they may underlie the anomalous energy transport observed in numerical simulations of Rydberg arrays.
