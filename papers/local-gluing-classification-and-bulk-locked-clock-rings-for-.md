arXiv:2611.17816v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We classify local reflection gluings of the Z_N plaquette model on lattice Klein bottles and solve the obstructed literal seam. Within the monomial far-corner ansatz, all commuting gluings form a family labelled by a unit u of Z_N. They exist for every N at even seam length and, at odd length, exactly when -1 is a square modulo N. We determine the anyon transport, including exchange seams with P^2=C for charge conjugation C, and the relation phases that distinguish an unfrustrated code from a commuting but frustrated Hamiltonian. For the literal gluing, the 2L_x noncommuting plaquettes form self-dual clock rings whose sectors are locked by the bulk. This gives an exact reduction of the spectrum to clock-ring spectra, closed-form ring ground energies for N=2,4, and a closed-form ground-state degeneracy. We further determine twisted-coefficient homology on the stated surface domains and compare it with logical modules on registered lattice grids; composite dimensions need not give squares of cyclic groups. Dressed-isometry gates and monodromy are reported with their finite-grid scope. Finally, the qudit Gottesman--Knill theorem bounds the computational interpretation: Clifford deformations and Weyl measurements with stabilizer inputs and no external magic states cannot supply a non-stabilizer resource. Charge-conjugation measurements that do supply such states lie outside this operation model. The new results concern explicit seam classification, phase-sensitive consistency and bulk-locked spectra, not a new general no-go theorem or a universal computing architecture.
