A complete treatment of the relativistic‑recoil effect resolves the long‑standing fine‑structure anomaly in heavy muonic atoms, as demonstrated for muonic ⁹⁰Zr.  Ab initio QED calculations fitted to historical precision spectra yield a root‑mean‑square charge radius of r₍ᵣₘₛ₎(⁹⁰Zr)=4.2732(7) fm, a four‑fold improvement in fit quality and six‑fold reduction in uncertainty; this value is consistent with previous muonic data but exceeds the accepted literature radius by >3σ.  The same analysis for μ⁻¹²⁰Sn gives r₍ᵣₘₛ₎(¹²⁰Sn)=4.6518(34) fm, in agreement with established values, confirming that an incomplete QED treatment caused the earlier anomaly and restoring muonic‑atom spectroscopy’s predictive power.
