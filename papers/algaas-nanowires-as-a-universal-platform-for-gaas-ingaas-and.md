arXiv:2608.24495v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Optical quantum dots (QDs) are central to photonic quantum technologies, with fabrication approaches tailored to different spectral ranges. A key challenge, however, is the realization of a unified platformnicode{x2014}a single growth method combined with a host material offering a designable architecturenicode{x2014}enabling wavelength tunability across the full emission range and co-integration of multiple quantum dots. Here, we introduce AlGaAs nanowires as a universal host for GaAs, InGaAs, and InAs QDs. Building on our previous demonstration of high-quality GaAs QDs, we realize InGaAs QDs with tunable emission by varying the growth duration from 2 to 5 s, achieving emission at 780 and 920 nm. We further showcase the platform's versatility for multi-quantum-dot devices by co-integrating two InGaAs QDs, as well as GaAs and InGaAs QDs within a single nanowire. Finally, we initiate a first step toward pure InAs QDs by growing a pristine InAs segment on AlGaAs nanowires, demonstrating material compatibility.
