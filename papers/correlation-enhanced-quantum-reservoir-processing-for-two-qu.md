arXiv:2611.09268v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quantum reservoir processing (QRP) has been applied to entanglement quantification, yet its potential for generalizable steering detection and the discovery of high-dimensional quantum correlations remains largely unexplored. Here, we introduce correlation-enhanced QRP (CEQRP), extending QRP to two-qutrit systems with two-body spin-correlation readouts. Some CEQRP-based classifiers substantially improve cross-family generalization, with logistic regression trained on full-information CEQRP features achieving approximately 96% accuracy on unseen Werner states and over 97% accuracy on unseen isotropic states at both (m=4) and (m=5). Independently, we establish two interconnected steering criteria for mixed two-qutrit states: a necessary-and-sufficient all-versus-nothing criterion for states admitting three linearly independent pure conditional states, and an asymptotic sufficient criterion, with the two criteria linked through invertible local filtering. These criteria provide rigorously certified steerable samples for training-set augmentation. Combining CEQRP-based classification with these theory-certified samples enables the discovery of new Alice-to-Bob steerable partially entangled states, including states at (p=0.45) whose steerability is independently confirmed by semidefinite programming (SDP) using seven projective measurement settings. The trained classifiers further guide finite-setting searches for steering boundaries. Our results establish a pathway from generalizable classification to the discovery of high-dimensional quantum steering.
