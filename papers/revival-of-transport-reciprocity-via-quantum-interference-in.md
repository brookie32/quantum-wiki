arXiv:2607.26898v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Structural asymmetry combined with optical nonlinearity often leads to nonreciprocal light transport. We explore the mechanism by which 1-photon interference effects can revive reciprocity in such nonlinear models. To this end, we study correlated 2-photon scattering where an artificial atom is asymmetrically (a) side-coupled to an infinite waveguide at two spatially separated points, and (b) direct-coupled to two semi-infinite waveguides. The setup (a) gives robust reciprocal transport for the two photons. However, the setup (b) shows a transition from a nonreciprocal to a reciprocal regime by tuning the interference effect via an additional tunneling path for photons between the two waveguides.
