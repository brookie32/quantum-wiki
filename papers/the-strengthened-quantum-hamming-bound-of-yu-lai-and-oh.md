arXiv:2610.30088v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We prove a conjecture of Yu, Lai, and Oh: their rounded family of strengthened quantum Hamming bounds, which interpolates between Hamming-type bounds and the Singleton bound, holds for arbitrary exact quantum subspaces of dimension K>1. The result covers every integer local dimension pge2, every integer tge1, both distances 2t+1 and 2t+2, and every interpolation parameter ein{0,ldots,t}. Yu, Lai, and Oh proved these bounds for pure codes and for general codes of distance three or four; the case e=0 contains the ordinary quantum Hamming bound for general codes, recently proved by Zhang and Chen. More generally, for pge3, we prove the inequality Kf(0)le p^nEf(X), where Xsimoperatorname{Bin}(n,1-p^{-2}), for every real polynomial f of degree at most D, with 2le D<d, that is nonnegative on {0,ldots,n} and positive at zero; here n and d are the code length and distance.
