arXiv:2607.03274v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We formulate false-vacuum decay in a mixed-field Ising chain with r^{-(1+sigma)} interactions as a spatially nonlocal Euclidean phi^4 theory featuring a fractional spatial kinetic term sim |q|^sigma. The nonlocal bounce is anisotropic in space-time and develops algebraic spatial tails. Combining thin-wall arguments with numerical solutions of the full nonlocal saddle, we find two nucleation regimes separated by sigma = 1. For 0<sigma<1, the lifetime exponent scales with the energy bias h of the metastable state as Bsim h^{-1/sigma}; for 1<sigma<2, the leading Coleman scaling Bsim h^{-1} is recovered, while long-range effects are retained in the subleading correction sim h^{sigma-2}. The same exponents are recovered directly within a two-kink description of the Ising chain on the lattice. Our results show that tunable long-range interactions fundamentally reshape bubble nucleation and alter false-vacuum decay in quantum simulators.
