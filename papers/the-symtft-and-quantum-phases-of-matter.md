arXiv:2611.21715v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: The symmetry topological field theory (SymTFT) provides a unified framework for ordinary and generalized symmetries of quantum many-body systems. By encoding symmetry in a topological theory in one higher dimension, it separates symmetry data from the details of a specific quantum system. In this review, we give a pedagogical introduction to this framework and survey its applications to phases of quantum matter. For fusion category symmetries in {(1+1)}d, we explain how the SymTFT characterizes anomalies, classifies gapped phases, and organizes the symmetry structure of gapless phases. We also give a broad overview of further developments of this framework, including extensions to symmetries in higher dimensions, continuous symmetries, fermionic systems, mixed states, and spacetime symmetries.
