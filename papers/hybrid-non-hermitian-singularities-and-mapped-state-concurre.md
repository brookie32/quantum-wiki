The study analyzes a coupled double‑cavity system mediated by a superconducting qutrit and demonstrates that while the effective two‑mode Hamiltonian is Hermitian, its four‑component Bogoliubov operator dynamics are governed by a non‑Hermitian dynamical matrix. This matrix hosts exceptional points, lines and hybrid diabolical‑exceptional (diabolically degenerate) singularities, and the authors provide a quadrature‑based protocol to extract the spectrum from experimentally accessible cavity‑field observables. By mapping the four‑component dynamical vector onto a virtual two‑qubit representation, they use concurrence as a diagnostic of eigenvector nonseparability and its evolution under the dynamical matrix.
