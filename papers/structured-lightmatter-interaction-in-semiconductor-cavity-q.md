The study demonstrates structured light–matter interaction at the single‑photon level in a semiconductor cavity QED system. By positioning an epitaxial InAs quantum dot on the periphery of a micro‑pillar cavity, four spectrally close structured cavity modes are engineered; tuning the dot’s emission into resonance produces cavity‑enhanced photons with spin‑locked chiral orbital angular momentum and controllable spin–orbit entanglement, all within a single wavelength‑scale device.
