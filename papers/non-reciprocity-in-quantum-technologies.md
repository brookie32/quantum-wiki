Non‑reciprocity—allowing transmission in one direction while blocking it oppositely—is a key resource for quantum technologies, enabling directional amplification, information routing, and topologically protected states. Recent work has demonstrated low‑loss, fully integrated non‑reciprocal devices with minimal or no magnetic bias, primarily in microwave superconducting circuits but also advancing in optical and nanophotonic platforms, thus compatible with scalable quantum computers and networks. These technologies improve high‑fidelity qubit readout, robust state transfer, and enhance the sensitivity of quantum sensors for metrology.
