arXiv:2501.07354v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We report on the characteristics of a microwave photon counter device based on a superconducting transmon qubit. Its design is similar to [arXiv:2307.03614], with an additional bandwidth tuning circuit that allows optimizing the device efficiency and noise. Owing to this new feature and to improvements in device fabrication, a power sensitivity of 3 dot 10^{-23} W/sqrt{Hz} is reached. We confirm the high performance of the device by measuring single spin microwave fluorescence.
