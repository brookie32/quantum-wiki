arXiv:2611.26834v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We report the extension of the B-spline R-matrix (BSR) to R-matrix with time dependence (RMT) interface to include support for multiconfiguration target states and jK coupling, including the one-body spin-orbit (SO) interaction. Inner-region input data generated by BSR are transformed through the interface for time propagation in RMT. Two examples demonstrate the working interface: high-order harmonic generation in helium and weak-field, near-resonance response in carbon. The helium comparisons employ previously studied laser parameters, displaying excellent agreement with the established Belfast R-matrix input codes in the time-dependent dipole and harmonic spectrum, both with and without SO. Further structure comparisons in helium show close agreement in the SO-induced changes in energy and oscillator strengths. The two codes coincide on the much larger SO-induced change in the dipole response in carbon, demonstrating agreement for multiconfigurational target states. These extensions allow the flexibility of BSR structure calculations to be used in time-dependent studies of heavier and more complex atoms.
