arXiv:2611.17451v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The interaction of traveling photonic pulses with quantum systems is a fundamental process for many quantum technologies such as quantum communication, control, and measurement protocols. The so-called SLH formalism, named for the triple of operators that parametrize the dynamics, in combination with virtual emission and absorption cavities provides a general toolbox to describe such processes. However, modeling open quantum systems driven by propagating quantum pulses becomes cumbersome once several components, channels, and temporal modes are involved. Here we present QuantumInputOutput.jl, an open-source framework that automates this derivation process: first, symbolic expressions for the Hamiltonian and Lindblad operators are derived using predefined SLH rules and symbolic quantum algebra. Next, the obtained operators are converted to (time-dependent) numerical matrices or are used to obtain higher-order mean-field equations. The numerical expressions are presented in a form immediately suitable for solving the time evolution of the master equation and analyzing the output modes via correlation functions. Besides its usefulness for numerical simulations, the framework also provides a convenient tool to derive symbolic expressions for one-dimensional quantum systems such as multilevel emitters in cavities or waveguides.
