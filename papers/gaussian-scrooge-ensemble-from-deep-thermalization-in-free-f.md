arXiv:2610.10279v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Deep thermalization indicates the emergence of universal ensembles of pure states in a subsystem after measuring its environment. We investigate this phenomenon in a free-fermion chain using local measurements that preserve Gaussianity and break particle-number conservation. Although the measurement outcomes provide partial information about local conserved charges within a finite subsystem, we argue that this information becomes negligible in the thermodynamic limit. This effective non-revelation underlies the emergence of a fermionic Gaussian Scrooge ensemble, determined by the subsystem's generalized Gibbs ensemble. We construct this universal ensemble directly at the subsystem level and provide numerical evidence that it describes the late-time projected ensemble at finite temperature. Our results extend the description of thermalization in non-interacting integrable systems beyond the reduced density matrix to universal statistics of conditional pure states.
