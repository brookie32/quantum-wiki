The authors propose and demonstrate an on‑demand microwave–optical transducer based on a room‑temperature Rydberg atomic ensemble that stores microwave photons in a collective highly excited state via cascaded electromagnetically induced transparency and retrieves them as optical photons.  In a cavity‑free configuration the device achieves ~90 % area‑normalized storage efficiency, 2.3 MHz bandwidth and a noise‑equivalent temperature of 26 K while remaining cryogenically compatible and scalable to high single‑photon conversion efficiencies without an optical cavity. This results in a practical interface for quantum repeaters and other platforms requiring efficient microwave–optical coupling.
