arXiv:2607.14284v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Spacelike singularities supported by matter satisfying the null energy condition (NEC) are expected to fall within the Belinski--Khalatnikov--Lifshitz (BKL) paradigm. We show that controlled violations of the NEC in holography can lead to different black hole interiors. In a holographic model dual to a strongly coupled non-Hermitian PT-symmetric QFT, we uncover a novel non-Kasner regime within its real, PT-restored phase. The deep-interior geometry describes an isotropic FLRW cosmology undergoing super-accelerated expansion and approaching a Little Rip. This regime leaves a characteristic imprint on two-sided heavy-operator correlators, allowing it to be distinguished from a standard Kasner interior. Our construction provides a concrete holographic realization of a Little Rip cosmology and lays the groundwork for a Little Rip/CFT correspondence, in which such cosmological regimes can be explored through observables in a non-Hermitian quantum field theory.
