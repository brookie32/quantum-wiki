arXiv:2511.22992v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Despite various approaches to encode nonclassical light, most existing formulations either suffer from limited practicality or rely on optimization procedures that are computationally demanding. Here, we develop a framework for quantifying optical nonclassicality without invoking any optimization, using generic classicalization channels, p-norms and linear functionals generated by displaced rotationally-invariant operators. However, for a subset of such measures, involving s-ordered distributions and a Gaussian classicalization channel, we analytically establish a no-go theorem demonstrating that no such universal measure can exist. We further substantiate our theoretical result through explicit examples involving both Gaussian and non-Gaussian states, followed by a generic criterion for a no-go theorem, whose the existence remains to be established.
