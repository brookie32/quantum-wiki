arXiv:2610.20910v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Jellium, a system of electrons in a uniform neutralizing background, and the uniform electron gas (UEG), a system of electrons with constant density and no background, are two standard models of the electron gas. We prove that the ground-state energy per unit volume of Jellium, as defined by Lieb and Narnhofer, coincides with the UEG energy defined by Lewin, Lieb and Seiringer, i.e. [ e^hbar_{rm Jel}(rho_0)=e^hbar_{rm UEG}(rho_0). ] This equality was previously established in the classical case by Lewin, Lieb and Seiringer and independently by Cotar and Petrache.
