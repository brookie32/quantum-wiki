arXiv:2611.33987v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We propose a unified quantum algorithm for preparing exact and asymptotic quantum many-body scar (QMBS) states in the spin-1 {it XY}, domain-wall-conserving, and Onsager scar models. Model-dependent isometries map the exact scar states to Dicke states and the target asymptotic QMBS states to gapless magnon excitations of the spin-1/2 ferromagnetic Heisenberg chain. Exact QMBS states are prepared by applying the inverse isometries to Dicke states. For asymptotic QMBS states, block encoding first prepares the magnon excitations from Dicke states to a prescribed fidelity, after which the inverse isometries map the prepared states back to the physical chains. At fixed target fidelity, the block-encoding success probability has a positive lower bound independent of system size and scar index, implying that a constant number of amplitude amplification rounds suffices. Our algorithm enables systematic studies of nonergodic dynamics on programmable quantum simulators.
