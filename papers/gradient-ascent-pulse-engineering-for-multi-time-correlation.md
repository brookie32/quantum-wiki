arXiv:2611.22326v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In quantum optimal control, gradient methods are often used to optimize functions of a state or propagator at a final time, while experimentally addressable observables and performance metrics may depend on multi-time correlations. We develop QRT-GRAPE, which combines the quantum regression theorem (QRT) with Gradient Ascent Pulse Engineering (GRAPE) to obtain analytic gradients for objective functions defined by multi-time correlations. The method incorporates the time evolution of an auxiliary operator in QRT into the forward propagation and backward adjoint propagation used in GRAPE. As an application, we consider the trade-off between the photon-emission probability and the conditional Bell-state fidelity in heralded remote entanglement generation among two nonidentical atom--cavity systems. Starting from a flat-top Rabi pulse pair selected by a search over pulse amplitudes and durations, QRT-GRAPE increases the photon-emission probability product from 0.2802 to 0.3216 and the conditional Bell-state fidelity from 0.9356 to 0.9441.
