arXiv:2608.13109v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Temporal modulation of the measurement rate provides a powerful mechanism for controlling open-system dynamics through measurement backaction. We demonstrate this mechanism in a minimal exactly solvable model of a continuously monitored quantum particle on a switching lattice. We derive exact analytical expressions for the long-time current under both periodic and stochastic switching, revealing a common slow-switching limit and identifying a measurement-induced crossover between Zeno- and anti-Zeno-dominated transport regimes that controls the direction of the current.
