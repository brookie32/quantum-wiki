Discrete time quasicrystals constitute a class of non-equilibrium matter characterized by temporal order without strict periodicity, in contrast to conventional time crystals.   This work experimentally observes a DTQC in a driven-dissipative ensemble of strongly interacting Rydberg atoms, displaying non-equilibrium dynamical response with a different finite Abelian group symmetry.   Investigating these phenomena is essential for expanding fundamental understanding of far-from-equilibrium quantum matter and spontaneous symmetry breaking beyond periodic regimes.
