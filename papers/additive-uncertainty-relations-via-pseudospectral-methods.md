arXiv:2610.32014v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Given Hermitian observable A, the variance of A is given by the difference of expectations langle A^2rangle - langle Arangle^2, where the expectation of an observable implicitly assumes a choice of state for the underlying system. As a sub-case of a general lower bound for the sum of variances for finitely many arbitrary elements in a C^*-algebra, we prove that the sum of the variances for d observables A_1, ots , A_d is greater than some expression in the expectations langle i[A_j,A_k]rangle for jneq k plus the square of the smallest singular value of the associated localizer. In special cases, this expression is just a root mean square average. Our technique also derives a general containment between the Clifford pseudospectra and quadratic pseudospectra for d observables.
