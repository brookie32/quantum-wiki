arXiv:2611.27623v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We study penalty strategies for converting constrained binary optimization problems to quadratic unconstrained binary optimization (QUBO) form on the example of the sparsest k-subgraph problem. We adopt an augmented Lagrangian approach involving a linear penalty in addition to the commonly used quadratic one, and show the benefits of this approach. From the constraint structure, we derive sufficient bounds on the penalty coefficients. We conduct extensive experiments comparing combinations of our different penalty strategies with a wide range of classical and quantum QUBO solvers. Our experiments show that to obtain a good-quality solution, the right penalty strategy is almost as important as the quality of the QUBO solver, and iterative approaches appear to be the most efficient. We discuss the mechanisms that lead to a degradation of the solution quality due to large quadratic penalties.
