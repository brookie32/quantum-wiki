arXiv:2611.27417v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We introduce Reset Quantum Channels (RQCs), a class of quantum channels acting on a Hilbert space structured as a direct sum of an arbitrary number of subspaces. Within each subspace, the state evolves unitarily; across subspaces, it is transferred by measure-and-prepare resets along given states. In this general setting, we show that the determination of their invariant states reduces to a stochastic eigenvalue problem, whose size is set by the number of reset states rather than by the dimension of the Hilbert space, and we discuss the uniqueness of these states. We further address the primitivity and the spectrum of these channels, which we compute explicitly when the reset states commute with the unitary evolutions. Applications include a small system coupled to several thermal reservoirs. We also consider random unitary evolutions and analyze the averages of the invariant states in this setup. Finally, we show that a suitable continuous-time limit yields Lindblad generators of reset type on the algebra of block-diagonal operators.
