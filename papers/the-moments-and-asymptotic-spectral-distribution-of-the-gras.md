arXiv:2611.27914v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In this paper, we study random density matrices constructed by taking partial traces of projections onto random subspaces of a bipartite Hilbert space H_A otimes H_{B} distributed according to the pushforward measure of the action of the unitary group on the Grassmannian ext{Gr}_{n}(H_A otimes H_{B}). This ensemble models a `typical' quantum subspace. All tracial moments of the density operator, as well as mixed tracial moments of reduced density operators, are computed explicitly using Weingarten calculus. There are several consequences of these results. In particular, we prove that the empirical spectral distribution of the density operator converges almost surely to a rescaled Marchenko-Pastur law. We also prove that this ensemble can be written as a sum of asymptotically free random density operators, leading to an alternative proof of the convergence of the spectral distribution. Finally, we compute the asymptotic expansion of the average entanglement entropy.
