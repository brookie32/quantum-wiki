arXiv:2611.24461v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The performance of HgCdTe APDs for single photon detection at T=80 K has been characterized using a hybrized Resistive Trans-Impedance Amplifier (RTIA) and a deported Capacitance Trans-impedance amplifier (CTIA). The single-photon detection capacity was characterized with the HgCdTe APDs operated in linear mode with high APD gain, M>400, and hybridized with the RTIA. The measurements revealed an external photon detection efficiency (PDE) of 42 % to 55 %, and a dark count rate (DCR) of 2.6 MHz to 17 MHz, for an amplitude threshold ranging from 2.5 to 4.5 times the rms noise of the amplifier. Taking into account the unity gain quantum efficiency, QE=63%, this corresponds to an internal PDE ranging from 66 % to 86 %. Using the deported CTIA, it was demonstrated that the high level of DCR is not the intrinsic limit of the APD technology. The CTIA approach enables direct measurement of the APD dark current, Idark, with gain-normalized dark current levels as low as Idark/M=10.9 fA at M = 450. This corresponds to a DCR of 150 Hz which is compatible with the requirements for most of the quantum photonics applications.
