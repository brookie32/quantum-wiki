arXiv:2611.26801v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We present high-resolution measurements of n=2-3 transitions in Ne-like tungsten, rhenium, osmium, iridium, and bismuth using a transition-edge sensor (TES) microcalorimeter. These measurements provide new benchmarks for Ne-like ions in the high-Z regime (Z > 74), extending previous EBIT studies of the sequence. To achieve sub-eV accuracy, we developed a dedicated x-ray fluorescence source employing well-characterized K-shell emission lines from metallic elements. Our results offer stringent tests for relativistic atomic structure theories and demonstrate the advantage of TES spectroscopy for precision measurements in highly charged ions.
