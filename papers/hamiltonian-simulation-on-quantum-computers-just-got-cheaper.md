Hamiltonian simulation on quantum computers just got cheaper. While Quantum Signal Processing (QSP) offers asymptotic optimality, constant factors dictate practical performance. In recent research by Matthew Pocrnic (@mpocrnic97) and Danial Motlagh, we reduce the constant factor overheads for QSP-based simulation by deriving an improved bound on the polynomial degree needed to approximate the time-evolution operator. Read more: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02734
