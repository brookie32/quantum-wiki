arXiv:1211.1917v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Physics has long lived with a split personality that desires determinism for measured systems while demanding that experimenters decide what to measure on a whim. Intriguingly, such a free will assumption for experimenters has thwarted many attempts to provide a satisfactory explanation of how quantum probabilities evolve to clear-cut measurements. An overview of this quantum measurement problem is presented without equations, and the lesson is drawn that denial of experimenters' free will may be the only workable solution. A holistic view is offered of a self-consistent superdetermined spacetime block universe of events. The goal of future research is to develop the underlying principle that generates this Escher-like global pattern.
