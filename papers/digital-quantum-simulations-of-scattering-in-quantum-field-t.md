The authors demonstrate a digital quantum simulation of scattering in a one‑dimensional Ising field theory on a 104‑qubit processor, observing evidence of inelastic particle production when two light‑particle wavepackets collide. Their algorithm prepares the initial wavepackets by efficiently generating multipartite W states and employs mid‑circuit measurements with conditional operations, yielding circuit depth that remains constant regardless of the spatial volume—an improvement over earlier polynomial‑scaling methods. The same preparation protocol has also been applied to construct wavepacket‑preparation circuits for scalar field theories, the Schwinger model, and two‑dimensional Ising field theory, as reported in *Nature Physics* (11 September 2026).
