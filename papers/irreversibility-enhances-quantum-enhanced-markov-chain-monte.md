arXiv:2606.23350v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Detailed balance underlies conventional Markov-chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) algorithms. Yet in classical systems, breaking detailed balance generates irreversible probability currents and can accelerate sampling. Whether irreversibility can similarly enhance quantum MCMC remains an intriguing question. Here we show that irreversibility provides a new route to improving the quantum-enhanced MCMC (QEMC), which combines quantum proposals with classical acceptance. By integrating quantum-generated candidate paths with allocations of probability flows, we develop an irreversible quantum-enhanced Monte Carlo (IQEMC). On spin-glass benchmarks, IQEMC improves the spectral gap over QEMC with a polynomial scaling advantage as the system size increases. In addition to numerically demonstrating faster convergence of magnetization estimates, we verify this improvement experimentally on a quantum processor. The results establish irreversibility as a physically grounded mechanism for enhancing quantum MCMC.
