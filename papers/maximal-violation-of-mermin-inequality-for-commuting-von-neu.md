arXiv:2611.13981v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Summers and Werner proved that Bell inequalities are violated maximally and generically in the vacuum of a relativistic quantum field theory in [S. J. Summers and R. Werner, Commun. Math. Phys. 110 (1987), 247-259]. Their criterion concerns two-party case, that is, there are two algebras of quantum observables: one algebra and its commutant. In this case, the maximal CHSH value is converged to in every normal state exactly when the algebra is strongly stable. In the paper, we study the corresponding question for three and more mutually commuting von Neumann algebras. We focus on the multipartite Mermin inequalities instead of CHSH ones. For the tripartite Mermin functional we show that maximal violation in every normal state is equivalent to the existence of a common sequence of asymptotic GHZ stabilizers. Strong stability of each local factor is an necessary condition for maximal violation, and a joint embedding of an infinite GHZ tensor product is sufficient. Counterexamples show that strong stability and type III_1 of the individual algebras, even together with tripartite duality, do not suffice to arrive at the maximal violation. The results are also extended to n>=3 parties.
