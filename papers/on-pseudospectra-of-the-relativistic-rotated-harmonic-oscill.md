arXiv:2611.15180v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We establish an upper bound to the norm of the resolvent of a relativistic version of the non-self-adjoint operator obtained by a dilation analytic transformation of the quantum harmonic oscillator. The estimate implies a decay for large complex energies along rays with angles larger than that of the dilation. In this way we positively answer a question left open in [Proceedings of the Royal Society A 2025].
