The 2026 Nature Communications paper demonstrates that quantum annealers can accurately access finite‑temperature critical points, overcoming previous limitations from noise and thermal fluctuations. By combining real‑time temperature inference with fine energy–scale tuning and an optimized embedding scheme, the authors generate effective Boltzmann samples for a two‑dimensional toroidal Ising ferromagnet up to 2 640 spins, extracting both the critical temperature and universal critical exponents. This work establishes quantum annealing as a viable tool for studying equilibrium and non‑equilibrium finite‑temperature critical phenomena in large classical spin systems.
