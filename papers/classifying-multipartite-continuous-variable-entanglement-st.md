This work develops a data-augmented neural network to classify entanglement structures in multipartite continuous-variable quantum systems using homodyne measurement data, employing a quantum data augmentation method that combines classical data processing with quantum physical principles to enhance network performance.   Testing on tripartite and quadripartite states demonstrates that the network successfully identifies entanglement structures among various partitions with significantly improved accuracies through data augmentation.
