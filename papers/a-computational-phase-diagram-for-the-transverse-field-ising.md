arXiv:2610.02079v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We study the transverse field Ising model, defined by the Hamiltonian H =frac{1}{2}sum_{i, jin [n]} J_{ij} Z_i Z_j +sum_{i=1}^n h_i^z Z_i + etasum_{i} X_i where J is the symmetric interaction matrix, and eta is the transverse field strength. Let Delta(J)=lambda_{max}(J)-lambda_{min}(J) be the spectral width of J. When the inverse temperature etageq0 satisfies Delta(J)dotfrac{anh(etaeta)}{eta}leq1, we give a randomized classical algorithm that approximates the partition function Z(eta)=operatorname{Tr}(e^{-eta H}) to a given relative error epsilonin(0,1) in time polynomial in n, eta, the model parameters, and epsilon^{-1}. When Delta(J) dot frac{anh(eta eta)}{eta} > 1 , we show that approximating Z(eta) within an exp(o(n))-multiplicative factor is extbf{NP}-hard, and thus unlikely to admit an efficient classical or quantum algorithms under standard complexity theoretic assumptions. Furthermore, in the regime Delta (J)dot frac{anh(eta eta)}{eta}leq 1, we provide an efficient randomized classical algorithm that approximates Pauli string observables of the Gibbs state rho_eta = frac{e^{-eta H}}{operatorname{Tr}(e^{-eta H})} within an arbitrarily small additive error. In the special case when the observable is also diagonal in the X-basis, i.e. P in {I, X}^{otimes n}, the algorithm further achieves arbitrarily small relative error.
