arXiv:2509.03970v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We develop a scattering-theory formalism and use it to predict that a resonantly driven atomic ensemble weakly coupled to an optical mode can generate light with non-Gaussian correlations. Our approach---based on a perturbative diagrammatic expansion of multi-photon interactions---shows that photon-photon interaction mediated by the emitters causes the transmitted light to have a connected third-order correlation function g_c^{(3)} containing non-Gaussian field correlations. The temporal pattern of g_c^{(3)} is explained using the microscopic transport processes in terms of our diagrams. We derive an optical-depth-dependent bound on the perturbative truncation error and benchmark our predictions against cascaded master-equation and quantum trajectory simulations for small ensembles with ODleq 2, identifying a regime of controlled accuracy under weak driving. Our predictions can be experimentally demonstrated in state-of-the-art nanofibre-coupled atomic ensembles.
