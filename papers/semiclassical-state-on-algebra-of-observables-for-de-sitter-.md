arXiv:2610.14175v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: In de Sitter static patch holography, coupling bulk quantum fields to an observer's dynamical clock yields a type II_1 von Neumann algebra M_+ of gauge-invariant observables. However, the unique tracial state on this algebra fails to reproduce semiclassical bulk correlation functions. We resolve this issue by introducing a family of heavy-clock states, representing an observer reference frame with heavy inertia. We prove that in the heavy-clock limit, n-point correlation functions of gauge-invariant dressed observables reproduce the bulk quantum field correlation functions on de Sitter spacetime. Furthermore, we investigate a generalized observer Hamiltonian widehat{H}_{rm obs}=|widehat{pi}| that incorporates both forward-running (pigeq 0) and backward-running (pi <0) clocks. The resulting von Neumann algebra M of gauge-invariant observables remains a type~II_1 factor and is isomorphic to M_+otimes M_2(C), where M_2(C) is the algebra of 2imes2 complex matrices transforming between the two clock orientations. Time-reversal symmetry is implemented in this setting as an anti-unitary operator that exchanges the two clock orientations, yielding a type~II_1 von Neumann algebra F of gauge-invariant, time-reversal-invariant observables. We generalize the heavy-clock state construction to both M and F, showing that n-point correlation functions calculated in heavy-clock states match the corresponding semiclassical bulk correlation functions. Finally, by coupling Einstein gravity to the Brown--Kuchar dust field, we derive the observer Hamiltonian widehat{H}_{rm obs}=|widehat{pi}| from a perturbative reduction along the observer's worldline in the G o 0 limit. This provides a field-theoretic foundation for the observer model developed in this work.
