arXiv:2608.02600v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In this paper, we present a unified framework for proving lower bounds for estimating functionals of quantum states. We therefore resolve several open problems by establishing lower bounds that match known upper bounds: we show that it requires widetilde{Omega}(N^2) samples to estimate the Uhlmann fidelity, trace distance, and von Neumann entropy. Moreover, they immediately imply matching query lower bounds of widetilde{Omega}(N) by quantum sample-to-query lifting. These lower bounds imply the near-optimality of a dozen quantum algorithms since 2016.
