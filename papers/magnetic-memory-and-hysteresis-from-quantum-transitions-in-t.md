Quantum‑annealing experiments on D‑Wave machines have shown robust hysteresis in large transverse‑field Ising systems, a behavior not captured by existing theory.  
We introduce a hybrid model that couples low‑order Landau–Zener transitions with semiclassical domain‑wall dynamics; the two pathways are iteratively fitted to account for decoherence and measurement without an explicit open‑system description. The approach reproduces measured hysteresis loops, non‑monotonic magnetization reversals, and transiently negative susceptibilities in chains of up to 4,906 qubits, while providing analytically derived scaling laws for defect density and hysteresis area that match experimental data.
