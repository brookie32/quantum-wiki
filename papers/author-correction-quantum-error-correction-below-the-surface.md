Google Quantum AI demonstrated two below-threshold surface code memories on the Willow superconducting processor that markedly reduce logical error rates while achieving real-time decoding, indicating potential for large-scale fault-tolerant quantum computing.   The distance-7 code achieved a logical error rate of 0.143% per correction cycle with error suppression by a factor of 2.14, and the logical memory exceeded the lifetime of its best physical qubit by a factor of 2.4.   The system maintains below-threshold performance with real-time decoding, achieving decoder latency of 63 microseconds at distance 5 across a million cycles.
