This 2026 Nature Communications article reports a spin–photon interface operating at telecom C‑band (1.55 µm) using InAs/InAlGaAs quantum dots embedded in a deterministically positioned circular Bragg grating. Polarization‑resolved spectroscopy of a positive trion in an in‑plane magnetic field provides the electron and hole g‑factors, while heralded two‑photon correlation measurements demonstrate long inhomogeneous dephasing times for the ground‑state hole spin qubit. These results enable coherent spin manipulation compatible with fiber‑optic quantum communication at telecom wavelengths.
