arXiv:2611.07000v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: In a previous work we conjectured that the multi-invariants of the ground state of a gapped (d-1)+1-dimensional system, labeled by bipartite (d+1)-edge-colored graphs encoding closed oriented d-manifolds M, compute the partition functions Z(M) of the low energy topological quantum field theory (TQFT), up to non-universal factors determined by the local structure of the triangulation. In this paper we determine which combinations of partition functions can be isolated from the non-universal factors. The answer is governed by oriented bordism. If M is null-bordant, Z(M) is obtained from an explicit, state-independent finite family of multi-invariants, up to a power of Z(S^d) which is fixed to be Z(S^d)^{hi(M)/2} for even d and can be removed for odd d. Conversely, the obstruction is measured by a quotient of the group of "residues" of colored graphs which receives a surjective map from the oriented bordism group, with kernel precisely the torsion subgroup as far as smooth manifolds are concerned. In d=4 the obstruction is the signature: Z(M)/Z(S^4)^{hi(M)/2} can be extracted if and only if sigma(M)=0. This is due to the fact that the first Pontryagin class p_1 has a local expression and thus e^{ihetasigma(M)}=e^{frac{i}{3}hetaint_M p_1} cannot be separated from the non-universal factors. Torsion invariants, in contrast, are not obstructed: for the ground state of the 4+1-dimensional invertible phase with partition function (-1)^{int w_2w_3} we compute the multi-invariants exactly, and two graphs with identical local data, encoding the Dold manifold and CP^2imes S^1, give opposite signs.
