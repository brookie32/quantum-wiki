arXiv:2607.04905v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Based on Geroch's process of dropping a system into a black hole from the vicinity of the horizon, we investigate in this paper the influence of deformation on the Bekenstein entropy bound both for (3+1) and (2+1) dimensions in the context of a generalized uncertainty principle (GUP). While providing a coherent framework that sets an upper limit on the entropy across dimensions we show, within a semiclassical treatment, that while a negative GUP deformation yields a universal relaxation of the bound, a positive deformation tightens it. Our results may be interpreted as a response to Planck-scale modifications of the near-horizon redshift.
