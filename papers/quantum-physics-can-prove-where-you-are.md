Researchers demonstrated that a person’s location can be certified using quantum entanglement, without relying on trusted quantum devices. In the cited experiment, distant parties exchange entangled particles and compare measurement statistics; the impossibility of faster‑than‑light signaling ensures that only someone at the claimed position can reproduce the correct correlations. This technique could secure critical systems such as banking, power grids, and satellite navigation against spoofing attacks.
