arXiv:2603.04744v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: In continuous-variable quantum systems, anharmonicity underlies diverse processes such as proton transfer, false vacuum decay and ferroelectric ordering, which cannot arise in a purely harmonic potential. Here, we experimentally demonstrate trigonometric-gate-implemented Fourier synthesis (TGIFS), a compilation scheme for quantum simulation of anharmonic dynamics in continuous-variable-discrete-variable (CV-DV) quantum simulators. TGIFS uses native CV-DV gates, allowing a target anharmonic potential to be determined entirely by classical control parameters, without modification of the hardware. We programmably implement TGIFS with a single trapped ion, simulating coherent dynamics in a continuously tunable family of double-well potentials and synthesising a quartic phase gate. The same circuit structure extends to arbitrary potentials and to multiple modes, giving a route to universal programmable simulation of nonlinearly coupled anharmonic potentials as achievable circuit depth increases.
