A near‑quantum‑limited maser amplifier was demonstrated using nitrogen impurity (P1) spins in diamond at millikelvin temperatures. By microwave pumping of a four‑spin cross‑relaxation mechanism, the device delivers more than 30 dB gain at 6.595 GHz with an added noise of ~2.55 quanta above the standard quantum limit and a –63 dBm 1 dB compression point. It is the first non‑superconducting cryogenic amplifier achieving such performance, offering a new avenue for low‑noise microwave quantum technologies.
