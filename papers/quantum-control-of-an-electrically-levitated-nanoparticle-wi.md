arXiv:2611.27634v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quantum control of levitated nanoparticles offers a route towards macroscopic quantum states. Here we propose a novel way to achieve it for a nanoparticle levitated electrically in a linear Paul trap together with a single ion, a system recently realized experimentally. By suitably engineering the trap's AC potential, one can parametrically modulate the ion-nanoparticle distance and thus the Coulomb coupling to enable a resonant interaction. For realistic parameters, we calculate that this enables sympathetic cooling of the nanoparticle to its motional ground state by a single, continuously laser-cooled ion. Additional AC drives enable the preparation of squeezed nanoparticle states and of ion-nanoparticle entanglement. Moreover, non-Gaussian states such as Fock and cat states, prepared in the ion with standard trapped-ion techniques, can be transferred to the nanoparticle with high fidelities. We provide complete protocols for each of these states, including all known decoherence sources. Our results establish electrically trapped ion-nanoparticle systems as a platform for quantum control of levitated matter.
