arXiv:2602.06421v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: The oxygen-vacancy PL6 center in 4H-SiC shows a far higher optically detected magnetic resonance contrast than the divacancy, for reasons that have been unclear. Strain-dependent photoluminescence-excitation spectroscopy of single centers yields its excited-state Hamiltonian and state-resolved intersystem-crossing rates, and first-principles calculations trace the enhanced, more spin-selective intersystem crossing to the oxygen-modified excited state. The centers remain photostable under resonant excitation, enabling 99.7% initialization, coherent optical and spin control, and 5.7~ms coherence.
