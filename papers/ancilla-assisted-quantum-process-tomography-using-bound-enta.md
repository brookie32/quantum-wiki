arXiv:2605.19182v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Lu extit{et al.} [Ann. Phys. (Berlin) extbf{534}, 2100550 (2022)] raised the question of whether bound entangled states can be useful for ancilla-assisted quantum process tomography (AAQPT). We answer their question in the affirmative by constructing bound entangled states that are faithful and hence allow complete reconstruction of an unknown quantum channel. We also study the effect of local filtering, which can increase the computable cross norm and realignment of a state while destroying its faithfulness. We further examine the performance of bound entangled probes, taking into account the stability of channel reconstruction rather than faithfulness alone. We find that bound entangled states can provide better-conditioned reconstruction than any separable probe, despite their entanglement being nondistillable.
