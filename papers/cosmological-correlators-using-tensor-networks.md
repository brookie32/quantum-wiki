arXiv:2603.26090v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We develop tensor-network methods for computing cosmological correlators nonperturbatively in de Sitter space and use them to test the proposal that suitably defined in-in correlators can be obtained from an in-out formalism by gluing the expanding and contracting Poincare patches. Focusing on interacting 1+1-dimensional phi^4 theory, we formulate finite-time lattice observables using Matrix Product State (MPS) techniques and analyze the regulator subtleties associated with the singular behavior near the patching surface. We consider two different contour prescriptions: a finite-time real-contour and a complex contour which properly accounts for the regulator. In the regulated light-field theory, exact-diagonalization and MPS checks support recovery of the full complex in--in/in--out equality at the tested finite cutoffs, suggesting a non-perturbative resolution of light field singularities in perturbation theory. We briefly discuss how these methods extend to low-angular-momentum sectors in 3+1 dimensions, and why regimes of rapid entanglement growth may eventually motivate quantum-computing implementations.
