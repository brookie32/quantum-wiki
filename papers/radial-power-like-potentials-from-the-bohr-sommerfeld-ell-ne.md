arXiv:2610.36421v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Two new Bohr-Sommerfeld (B-S) type quantization conditions for d-dimensional radial potentials are proposed for non-singular and singular potentials, respectively. The power-like potentials hbox{V=operatorname{sign}(m),r^m} for m=-3/2,-1,-1/2, 1,3,4,6 and V=log r (that corresponds to m=0) are studied in detail for radial quantum numbers n_r in [0,100] and for angular momenta ell=1,2,3,4,5 in dimensions d=2,3. It is shown that for large n_r the B-S energies E_{BS} always tend to the exact ones. For m=2 (the radial harmonic oscillator) and m=-1 (the d-dimensional analogue of the 3D Coulomb potential) the B-S spectrum coincides with the exact one for the first and second quantization conditions, respectively. In general, up to a small parametric shift of the radial quantum number, the analytic formulas for the energy spectrum can be written for arbitrary power-like potential. A comparison of E_{BS} with the energies E^{(L)}_{BS}, obtained for the radial potentials modified by the Langer correction for d=3, are in good agreement in general: in detail, it was checked for the quartic oscillator V=r^4. It is demonstrated that introducing a dimensionless {it WKB correction} gamma into the r.h.s. of the proposed B-S type quantization condition allows us to reproduce the exact energies with gamma being very small for positive degrees m geq 0 and sufficiently small for non-positive ones m < 0 for all n_r, ell studied.
