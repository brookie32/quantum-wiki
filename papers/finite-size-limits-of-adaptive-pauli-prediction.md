arXiv:2610.13652v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Can quantum data reliably answer questions chosen from earlier results? We prove that M adaptive Pauli predictions on n qubits require Omega(sqrt{min{n,M}}) copies at fixed accuracy. Published upper bounds make this tight for Mle n. Thus n adaptive predictions cost Theta(sqrt n) copies, whereas a fixed list costs only O(log n). The lower bound uses diagonal states and commuting observables, identifying a statistical cost of adaptation. We also quantify how this cost increases with precision.
