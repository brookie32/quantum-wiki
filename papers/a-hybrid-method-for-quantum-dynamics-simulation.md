arXiv:2307.15231v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We propose a hybrid approach to simulate quantum many-body dynamics by combining Trotter-based quantum evolution with classical dynamic mode decomposition. In quantum simulation, the interest often lies in estimating observables rather than explicitly obtaining the wave function. Instead of characterizing a quantum state, our method predicts observables of the quantum state at later times by using data from a set of short-time measurements from a quantum computer. For a fixed set of measurements, the global error bound for our method scales at most as O(t^{3/2}), with constants determined by the DMD approximation error and the conditioning of the learned modes. We apply our method to quench dynamics in the Hubbard model and nearest neighbor spin systems and show that the observable properties can be predicted up to a reasonable error by controlling the number of data points obtained from the quantum measurements.
