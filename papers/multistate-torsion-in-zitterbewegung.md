arXiv:2609.37767v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We derive nonlinear Zitterbewegung velocity of electrons augmented by band torsion in crystals. The equation of motion with multistate quantum geometric tensors and torsion culminates in transient nonlinear photocurrent responses distinct from the recently measured resonant steady-state photovoltaic effects induced by circularly polarized light. Tomography protocols for measuring the multistate quantum geometric torsion tensors are proposed.
