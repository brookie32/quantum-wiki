arXiv:2610.29397v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: This work revisits the debate on fractional uncertainty relations that arose a decade ago and tries to settle it. After a careful study of the position-momentum uncertainty product of the Levy wave packet, a novel lower bound is proposed for uncertainty relations in fractional quantum mechanics. This bound is nearly linear in the Levy index and is satisfied by exact, calculable solutions available in the literature.
