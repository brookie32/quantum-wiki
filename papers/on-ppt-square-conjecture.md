arXiv:2108.01588v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: An analysis of the PPT square conjecture based on the characteristics of positive mappings given in [9] will be carried out. First, a general strategy for analyzing the PPT conjecture will be presented. Then, a detailed analysis of the certain tensor cones and their corresponding classes of mappings will be given. Special emphasis will be put on key mappings used in quantum information.
