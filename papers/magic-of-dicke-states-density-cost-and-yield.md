arXiv:2611.17623v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Dicke states are an elementary family of many-body quantum states, consisting of equal superpositions of all configurations of L qubits with n excitations. We show that their magic, as measured by the stabilizer Renyi entropy (SRE), is extensive at every filling f=n/L except half-filling, and we obtain the magic density analytically, together with the subleading terms. In the context of fault-tolerant quantum computation, we also investigate the yield of Dicke states, and find that they can produce on average an extensive number of CCZ states (or T states with a catalyst) at any 0<f<1. This yield is not self-averaging, but fluctuates from run to run by O(L), as if it were set by a hidden phase shared by all qubits. At half-filling, the SRE grows only logarithmically, while the preparation cost, average yield and relative entropy of magic remain extensive, so that a small SRE does not necessarily signal a weak resource. These results can be understood by viewing the Dicke state as a fixed-magnetization projection of a product of tilted spins.
