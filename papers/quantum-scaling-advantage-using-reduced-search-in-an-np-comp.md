The 2026 study demonstrates an empirical scaling advantage for solving representative NP‑complete problems by integrating a space‑reduction algorithm with quantum solvers. The combination reduces the search space, allowing quantum resources (qubits and gate depth) to grow more slowly than classical computational requirements. Consequently, the research indicates that suitably pre‑processed instances can be handled more efficiently on near‑term quantum hardware compared to conventional algorithms.
