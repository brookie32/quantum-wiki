arXiv:2610.02146v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We give a deterministic classical algorithm that estimates |langle x|U|0^nrangle|^2 to additive error arepsilon in poly(n, 1/arepsilon) time, where U is a constant-depth quantum circuit comprised of gates with bounded fan-in and arbitrary connectivity, and x is an arbitrary n-bit output string. This improves over prior state-of-the-art algorithms that takes n^{O(log(n))} time for the same task, n^{O(log(log(n))} when U is geometrically local, and n^{O(1)} for 2D geometrically-local circuits.
