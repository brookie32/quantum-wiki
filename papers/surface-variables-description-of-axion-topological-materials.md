arXiv:2607.10509v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We study the response of axion topological materials under the presence of external static electric and magnetic fields. We focus on the macroscopic quasi-static magnetoelectric response of topological insulators. We use techniques based on surface variables that have been previously employed in soft condensed matter problems for the description of heterogeneous systems composed of multiple homogeneous materials. A complete description of the whole system is written in terms of surface degrees of freedom, which in this case correspond to effective surface charge and surface current densities. We obtain a set of integral equations satisfied by these variables. We present exact analytic solutions for axial-symmetric cases. We develop a numerical method for the solution of the integral equations by means of a boundary finite element method. We apply the numerical method to topological insulators with different geometries such as spheres, hollow spheres and toroidal surfaces. We show that that a variational principle can be used to recover the surface variables equations.
