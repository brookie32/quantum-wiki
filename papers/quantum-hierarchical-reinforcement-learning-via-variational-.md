arXiv:2605.03434v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: While parameterized quantum computations have shown success in standard reinforcement learning (RL), whether these advantages adapt to hierarchical RL (HRL) remains a critical open question. This work demonstrates that variational quantum circuits (VQCs) can effectively enhance HRL agents based on the option-critic architecture. Evaluated in standard environments, a hybrid HRL agent with a quantum feature extractor outperforms classical baselines while using fewer parameters. We also identify an architectural bottleneck: using VQCs for option-value estimation severely degrades learning. Further ablations reveal how quantum circuit design affects performance. Our work establishes design principles for parameter-efficient hybrid HRL agents.
