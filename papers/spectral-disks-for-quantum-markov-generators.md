arXiv:2611.23556v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Spectral bounds for Markov generators require a choice of time scale. In quantum dynamics, a rate read off from a jump representation, the escape rate from a pure state and the smallest spectral disk need not agree. We prove that the spectrum of every GKLS generator written without a Hamiltonian lies in a disk tangent to the imaginary axis, with radius given by the norm of the operator Phi^agger(I) built from the jump map Phi. This settles the first two scaling conjectures of "Corralling spectra of generators of time-continuous Markov evolution (both classical and quantum)", J. Phys. A: Math. Theor. {59}, 175202 (2026). The third, escape-rate conjecture fails for a qutrit with a single normal jump. Nevertheless, twice the escape rate bounds every decay rate of any positive trace-preserving semigroup For GKLS generators with vanishing canonical Hamiltonian, four times the escape rate always supplies a spectral disk, and the factor four is optimal uniformly in dimension. We compare these bounds with classical Cassini ovals and thermodynamic ellipses, explain the obstacles to their quantum extensions, and formulate optimization over completely positive jump-map representations as a semidefinite program.
