The paper demonstrates a quantum‑enhanced confocal microscopy technique that transforms a coherent state into a tightly concentrated “Fock‑like” probe via an \({\cal O}(1)\) depth quantum circuit, mimicking classical wave‑optics lens focusing in Fock space. Using superconducting cQED hardware they deterministically generate probe states with mean photon number up to \(N=500\), achieving a variance reduction of \(21.5\pm1.1\;\text{dB}\) compared to coherent light. The method provides efficient state preparation and data extraction for high‑photon‑number quantum sensing, addressing key challenges in practical quantum metrology.
