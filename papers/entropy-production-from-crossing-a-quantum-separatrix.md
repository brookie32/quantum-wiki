arXiv:2611.26737v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: If the Hamiltonian of a large quantum system depends on a variable parameter, its parameter-dependent energy spectrum may often form a dense lattice of avoided crossings. If the parameter slowly changes, the system's time evolution through the avoided crossings tends in the semi-classical limit to change from adiabatic to diabatic across a narrow energy range that has recently been defined as a quantum separatrix. Here we show that in the extreme semi-classical limit of a quasi-continuously dense spectrum, all quantum separatrices form a universal one-parameter family, and that evolution through the separatrix becomes efficiently ergodizing. If the unitary evolution is realistically averaged over small variations in the parameter time dependence, quantum separatrix crossing generates diagonal entropy according to a universal formula. Numerical examples confirm that the results for quasi-continuous spectra remain clearly recognizable in finite systems, with only small finite-size effects.
