arXiv:2611.27980v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: In two-dimensional Fermi liquids, odd-parity deformations of the Fermi surface relax anomalously slowly at a rate sim T^4, where T is the temperature. However, the leading-logarithmic prediction for the magnitude of this rate sim m^4 ln m, where m is an angular harmonic index, exceeds exact-diagonalization results and the ln m scaling is not observed. We resolve this discrepancy by evaluating the 2D Fermi-liquid collision integral for general regular isotropic potentials to leading order in T/T_F, including the nonlogarithmic term beyond the ln(T_F/T) term. We find that the local temperature modulation of the odd mode, which we call the thermal dressing, suppresses relaxation by collinear scattering, with residual relaxation set by large-angle scattering. Such large-angle processes give corrections of order ln m/ln(T_F/T) that are not small and must be resummed. We benchmark our results against exact-diagonalization calculations for constant interactions and find agreement over a range m^2 T/T_F lesssim 0.4, and we provide closed-form results for the slowest odd-mode relaxation rate of a spin-polarized dipolar gas.
