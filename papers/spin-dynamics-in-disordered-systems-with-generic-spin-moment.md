arXiv:2611.24346v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We develop a microscopic framework for the disorder-averaged density-matrix dynamics of pseudospin-1/2 systems with anisotropic and nonlinear spin-momentum couplings. Starting from the Diffuson, we introduce a matrix-valued rational approximation for its frequency dependence that does not rely on a low-frequency or weak-coupling expansion. The resulting pole equation is recast as a quadratic eigenvalue problem, from which the real-time evolution of the disorder-averaged density matrix is constructed. We apply the formulation to a two-dimensional electron gas with Rashba and linear and cubic Dresselhaus couplings, a d-wave altermagnet with Rashba coupling, and a three-dimensional double-Weyl model. Comparisons with direct numerical simulations demonstrate quantitative agreement for both the post-quench spin dynamics and spin relaxation times across a broad range of coupling strengths, extending beyond the diffusive regime. The matrix-rational formulation provides a unified description of ultrafast spin dynamics and long-time relaxation.
