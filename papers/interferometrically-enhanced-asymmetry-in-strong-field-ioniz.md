arXiv:2604.12646v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We demonstrate that quantum light statistics can be used to control strong-field ionization at the tunneling step leaving the average driving field unchanged. Using a bichromatic linearly polarized field composed of a strong coherent driver and a weak bright squeezed vacuum (BSV), we show through simulation that the asymmetry of the resulting photoelectron momentum distributions is significantly enhanced, with the skewness increasing by roughly two orders of magnitude compared with an equally weak coherent perturbation. A semiclassical analysis based on the strong-field approximation (SFA) reveals that the effect originates from asymmetric fluctuations in the instantaneous field amplitude, which strongly modify the tunneling ionization probability while leaving the electron's continuum dynamics essentially unchanged. The resulting amplification provides a proof of concept for enhancing phase-sensitive signatures of sub-cycle ionization dynamics.
