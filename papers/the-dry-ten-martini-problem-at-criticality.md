arXiv:2112.06869v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We solve the dry ten martini problem (lean-verified this time) by showing that every labeled almost Mathieu gap is open for lambdaneq 0 and every irrational frequency. In particular, we prove that the maximum logarithmic height of each labeled gap is non-decreasing in the coupling lambda and extend an initial open seed gap for each gap label n from lambdain(0,lambda_{n}) to all lambda> 0. The non-decreasing property follows from restoring time-reversal symmetry to the almost Mathieu operator H by pairing it with its magnetic inverse ar{H}. This allows us to express the logarithmic coupling derivative of the maximal gap height as the expectation value of a non-negative operator, yielding the required monotonicity.
