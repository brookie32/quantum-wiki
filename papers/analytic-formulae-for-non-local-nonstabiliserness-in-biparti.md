arXiv:2603.09155v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We conjecture analytic expressions for the non-local magic of bipartite pure qudit states of prime local dimension. Our construction relies on the Schmidt-aligned state attaining the minimum over local unitaries, a hypothesis that we support with numerical evidence for pairs of qutrits and ququints. For composite local dimensions, we find that the analogous expressions do not in general reproduce the global minimum, but can still provide computationally cheap approximations to the non-local magic. We also find that relations between non-local magic and entanglement diagnostics that hold for two qubits generally do not extend to qutrit and higher-dimensional systems. As a few-body application, we evaluate the qutrit non-local magic generated by elastic pion scattering, treating hadronic isospin as the qutrit. We find that p-wave charged pion scattering realises the maximum non-local magic allowed in the system just below the rho-meson resonance.
