arXiv:2501.01647v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We propose a method to transfer macroscopically superposed states between two optical cavities mediated by a mechanical oscillator, which works in a nonlinear regime of optomechanical interaction. The scheme relies on phonon-induced dynamic resonance, where the motion of the mechanical oscillator dynamically sets on/off the resonance between the two cavities. It assumes the high-amplitude limit of the oscillator, weak coupling between optical cavities, and the adiabatic approximation. We show that, under these conditions, various multi-photon quantum states, especially Schr{odinger cat states, can be faithfully transferred in a deterministic process. Our approach thus suggests the possibility of transferring large-photon-number quantum states, beyond the few-photon regime typically accessible with linearized optomechanical interactions.
