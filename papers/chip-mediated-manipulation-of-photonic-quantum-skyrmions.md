Researchers demonstrated the creation and reconfigurable control of photonic quantum skyrmions on a chip‑based silicon nitride nanophotonic platform.  The skyrmion topology is coherently encoded in single‑photon spin‑texture states and then transferred into a non‑local topological configuration shared by two entangled photons; this transfer remains robust against noise even after the entanglement disappears, revealing a quantum‑topology‑based transfer scheme that outperforms standard entanglement‑assisted protocols.  The work represents a major step toward resilient quantum interconnects and distributed quantum information processing.
