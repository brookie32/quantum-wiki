On 27 October 2026, Shiyu Wang reported experiments with a superconducting quantum processor that revealed an island of regular motion embedded within otherwise chaotic many‑body dynamics. By applying real‑time feedback control, the team was able to steer the system’s evolution into this ordered region. This work demonstrates how controllable regular behavior can be identified and exploited in complex quantum systems.
