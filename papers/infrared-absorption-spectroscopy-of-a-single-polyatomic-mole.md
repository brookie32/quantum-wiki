Researchers have demonstrated a non‑destructive infrared absorption spectroscopy technique that detects single‑photon absorption in an individual polyatomic molecular ion (CaOH⁺) co‑trapped with an atomic ion. By measuring the recoil imparted to the molecule, enhancing it with a non‑classical motional state of the two‑ion crystal and reading out through the atomic ion, they achieved single‑photon sensitivity for the O–H stretching vibration. This method opens a route for quantum‑state detection and preparation of complex molecules using mid‑IR spectroscopy.
