arXiv:2508.03162v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Engineering economical and scalable processes for direct air capture (DAC) from humid air relies on atomistic modeling to search and identify promising sorbent materials from a vast chemical space. Machine learning has emerged as a powerful tool for predicting material properties in an efficient yet accurate manner, but these models require large sets of carefully curated training data. We present the Open DAC 2025 (ODAC25) dataset comprising nearly 60 million DFT single-point calculations for CO2, H2O, N2, and O2 adsorption in ~15,000 MOFs. ODAC25 introduces functionalized MOFs, high-energy adsorbate placements, and synthetically generated frameworks to maximize the versatility of models trained on the dataset. ODAC25 improves upon the accuracy of DFT calculations in previous works and applies engineering intuition from recent work regarding the treatment of adsorbate-induced MOF flexibility. We release state-of-the-art machine-learned interatomic potentials trained on ODAC25 and demonstrate their utility via Henry's coefficient predictions. ODAC25 enables MOF screening for DAC at a resolution previously unattainable.
