arXiv:2611.09752v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We propose a synthetic temporal-mode converter (STC) that combines programmable waveform matching with denser time multiplexing for heterogeneous quantum interconnects. Programmable couplers and optical delays reshape known single-photon temporal waveforms through coherent interference between time bins. Conversion to a common single-bin mode concentrates amplitudes from N bins in lceillog_2Nrceil mixing stages, simultaneously improving temporal indistinguishability and freeing channel time. Simulations yield mode overlaps above 99.9% for exponential and Gaussian inputs. For N=256 and 0.5,dB loss per stage, STC gives a 45.6-fold higher photon-pair coincidence rate than temporal gating at equal mode overlap for two exponential inputs. Relative to long pulses, the shortened temporal matched mode can support an approximately 41-fold higher photon-pair coincidence rate when the available time-multiplexing capacity is fully utilized.
