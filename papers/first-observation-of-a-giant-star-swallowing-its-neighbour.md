A recent study reports the first direct observation of a massive star consuming a close‑by lower‑mass companion within its common envelope, offering new insights into the dynamics and outcomes of stellar mergers. Analysis of the engulfment process was enabled by high‑resolution photometric and spectroscopic monitoring, revealing complex mass transfer streams and rapid orbital decay. Additionally, researchers demonstrate a quantum‑based positional technique that could improve the localization of astrophysical events such as this merger, underscoring potential applications in both astronomy and secure identification technologies.
