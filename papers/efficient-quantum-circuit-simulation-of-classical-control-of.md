arXiv:2611.17454v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We show that quantum circuits with classical control of computational order admit an efficient simulation by fixed-order quantum circuits: any such transformation acting on N independent quantum channels can be deterministically, exactly, and universally simulated using at most N^2 queries. We generalize these constructions to extended, bipartite quantum channels and quantum instruments, and demonstrate that they can be implemented catalytically, without consuming the additional N^2-N queries required for the simulation. In the case of quantum circuits with quantum control of computational order, we prove a similar simulation result, but restricted to unitary input channels (hence non-universal). These results show the limitations of the computational power of these classes of transformations in the studied scenarios.
