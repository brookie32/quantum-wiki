arXiv:2610.33100v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Under applied forcing, static droplet retention is accompanied by contact angle asymmetry, with unequal apparent contact angles on opposite sides. Yet, the relation between the asymmetry and forcing lacks a common constitutive basis. Here, we show that the asymmetry emerges from minimizing the forcing-dependent free energy. The response of the asymmetry to forcing defines a susceptibility whose zero-forcing value yields a universal constitutive relation between the asymmetry and forcing. The constitutive relation is quantitatively validated across liquids, volumes, and forcing levels.
