arXiv:2610.37122v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We establish an equivalence between matrix samplers and quantum-proof randomness extractors. The classical sampler--extractor equivalence is established by a seminal work of Zuckerman (Random Structures & Algorithms, 1997). Using this equivalence, we construct explicit strong matrix samplers with optimal randomness overhead and near-optimal sample complexity via constructing quantum-proof extractors. Specifically for matrix dimension D, accuracy arepsilon and failure probability eltaleqarepsilon, our matrix sampler uses m+O_alpha(log(D/elta)) random bits and O_alpha(arepsilon^{-2-alpha}log^{1+alpha}(D/elta)) samples, for any constant alpha>0. Compared with the matrix sampler of Xun and Zuckerman (CCC 2025), our construction has improvements in two aspects. Under the same optimal random seed length, we can replace the operatorname{poly} D dependence in the number of samples by O(log^{1-gamma} D). Under the same scale of sample size, we can remove their loglog D factor in the length of the random seed. Our underlying strong quantum-proof extractor has output length n-O_alpha(a+log(1/eta)) and seed length at most (1+alpha)log a+(2+alpha)log(1/eta)+O_alpha(1) for n-bit sources with conditional min-entropy n-a and error eta. We also obtain strong quantum-proof extractors for every constant entropy rate, for any error as small as 2^{-Omega(n)}, having optimal seed length O(log(n/eta)) and output length Omega(n), improving previous constructions.
