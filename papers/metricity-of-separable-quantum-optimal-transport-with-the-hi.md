arXiv:2609.03769v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We prove that the square root of the separable quantum optimal transport cost associated with the orthogonal projection onto the antisymmetric subspace defines a genuine distance between density matrices. Equivalently, this establishes the triangle inequality for the order-two Beatty-Franca quantum optimal transport construction induced by the Hilbert-Schmidt distance between pure states. The result also proves metricity of the corresponding distance derived from separable SWAP fidelity. The proof replaces the unavailable gluing argument by convex-roof duality and a dimension-independent interpolation result for Hermitian operators.
