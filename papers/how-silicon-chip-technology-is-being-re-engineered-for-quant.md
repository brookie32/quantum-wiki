Silicon transistors are cheap, fast and reliable, and researchers are applying this technology to quantum computers.   Recent papers tackle the challenge of connecting and controlling 'quantum transistors' in silicon quantum processors.   A quantum computer based on silicon microchips could theoretically perform complex calculations in hours that would take classical computers longer than the lifetime of the Universe.
