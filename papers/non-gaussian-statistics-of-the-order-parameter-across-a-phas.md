Researchers measured the full probability distribution of the order‑parameter amplitude across a continuous phase transition in an interacting lattice Bose gas using single‑atom‑resolved momentum‑space detection. They reconstructed an effective Landau potential that shows a non‑trivial minimum in the superfluid phase, vanishing at the transition, and found non‑Gaussian statistics manifested by non‑zero high‑order cumulants that change sign abruptly near criticality. Numerical comparisons indicate that these cumulant sign changes obey critical scaling in homogeneous systems and are captured by a low‑temperature quantum model, but not by classical models, highlighting the importance of order‑parameter statistics in critical phenomena.
