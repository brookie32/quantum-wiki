arXiv:2611.14188v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We prove a nonasymptotic electric-flux truncation bound for the entire vacuum-relative low-energy window of the original pure compact U(1) lattice Hamiltonian. On a three-dimensional periodic cubic lattice with Ngeq3 sites per direction at heta=0, in the gauge-invariant zero-global-flux sector, the factorial-squared tail-norm estimate holds for every normalized vector in the window, without physical eigenvalue-isolation, window-edge gap, or nondegeneracy assumptions and without a window-dimension factor. It controls finite-time hard-wall evolution from the normalized projection of the same initial state. An analytic finite-volume strong-coupling example in this model certifies a nonvacuum band and an order-one plaquette signal change preserved by a specified finite cutoff. The same estimate yields precision-dependent sufficient cutoffs, an alternative linear isometric encoding by polar decomposition for reference-entangled inputs, and local electric-square readout bounds. At fixed positive bare coupling, physical volume, window width, and duration, cutoffs scaling as etalog N/loglog N with eta>2 make state and bounded-readout truncation errors vanish under lattice refinement.
