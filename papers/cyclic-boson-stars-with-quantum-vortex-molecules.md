arXiv:2611.21734v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We find a new type of stationary boson star with cyclic rotational symmetry in the minimal model of complex scalar field coupled to gravity. Unlike conventional axisymmetric spinning boson stars, these configurations can carry noninteger angular momentum per particle in units of the reduced Planck constant, revealing a new way for boson stars to carry angular momentum. We identify the vortex assemblies within these stars as self-gravitating quantum vortex molecules, in which gravity supports a stationary vortex arrangement despite internal circulation. These structures provide a relativistic realization of quantum vortex molecules studied in condensates and motivate possible laboratory analogues.
