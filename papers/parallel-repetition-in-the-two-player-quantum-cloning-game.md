arXiv:2608.00930v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We study parallel repetition in the two-player quantum cloning game, a monogamy-of-entanglement game motivated by quantum position verification. Colisson Palais, Escola-Farras, and Speelman bounded the value of n copies between (3/4)^n and os^{2n}(pi/8). For two copies, we prove that neither bound is tight. An explicit challenge-dependent strategy achieves value (5+sqrt{17})/16>9/16, so strong parallel repetition fails for the unrestricted game. A block Gram matrix argument gives the upper bound (11+sqrt{65})/32<os^4(pi/8) and strictly improves the previous parallel-repetition upper bound for every n. For every n, challenge-independent strategies have optimal value (3/4)^n.
