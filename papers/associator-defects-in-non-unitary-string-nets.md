arXiv:2601.06821v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We study obstructions to realizing fusion categories in physical Hilbert spaces by varying positive inner products on elementary fusion spaces. We introduce an associator defect to measure the smallest failure of recoupling maps to preserve the induced inner products in order to do so. By using a determinant identity from the pentagon equation, we prove that the minimum is attained and vanishes when the category is unitarizable. As an example, we compute the defect for the Yang-Lee category and its double, studying its behavior under stacking and passage to Drinfeld centers, which results in a Morita-invariant measure of bulk nonunitarity. We also obtain lower bounds for quantum-group categories with fusion multiplicities. Finally, we study how these obstructions appear in physical realizations of fusion. Under the stated assumptions on local splitting operations and coherent measurements, a positive defect imposes quantitative tradeoffs between implementation errors and operator norms. We also obtain bounds on the response to local perturbations. These results show how the failure of categorical unitarity will place quantitative constraints on physical realizations of potential fusion.
