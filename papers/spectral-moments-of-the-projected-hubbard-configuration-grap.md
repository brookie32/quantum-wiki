arXiv:2611.19929v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: At strong repulsion, the lowest band of n lattice fermions is the spectrum of the hopping projected onto states without double occupancy, H_infty=P_0H_tP_0. We study its moments on the square lattice and, where possible, any finite graph. (1) For spin-independent Hermitian hopping (no on-site terms) on Ngeq2 sites, mu_2=frac{n(N-n)}{N(N-1)}sum_{ineq j}|t_{ij}|^2 for any spin composition. (2) On any finite graph, mu_4 is exactly linear in the same-spin pair count P_{rm same} (four hops move at most three particles). For uniform hopping on a simple graph with Ngeq4, mu_4=A+alpha P_{rm same} in closed form: the slope alpha=-8C_4(N-n)(8N-11n-2)/[N(N-1)(N-2)(N-3)] is set by the number C_4 of 4-cycles, the intercept, via hard-core bosons, by edge, edge-pair and 4-cycle counts. With arbitrary Peierls phases, mu_4 is a spin-independent baseline plus a coefficient times sum_CosTheta_C over 4-cycle Wilson phases. (3) At finite U, the second-order Schrieffer-Wolff correction is Hilbert-Schmidt orthogonal to H_infty on every triangle-free graph, so the band variance has no 1/U term; its U^{-2} coefficient is negative at the low fillings computed. (4) Exchange loops carry the ordinary charge-e harmonic, alphaosPhi_square on Limes L tori, Lneq4, while the doublon hopping acquires a doubled phase, t_de^{2iA_{ij}}: the second-order single-doublon Hamiltonian is exactly pi-periodic (h/2e) under a boundary twist, and the exact two-electron U-band follows up to O((2lambda)^L/U^{L-1}). Result (1), the closed form in (2) without phases, the orthogonality in (3) and the doubled phase are machine-checked in Lean 4. Repulsive binding and the pair charge 2e are known; new are the exact moments and the derivation of the doubled phase. We make no claim about many-pair phase stiffness, off-diagonal long-range order or superconductivity.
