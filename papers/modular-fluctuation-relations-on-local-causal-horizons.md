arXiv:2611.33384v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Local causal horizons provide a setting for studying horizon thermodynamics in the neighborhood of any regular spacetime point. Here, we investigate this description beyond mean energy and entropy balances by studying fluctuations of boost-energy transfer induced by unitary perturbations of the horizon reference state. Using the modular structure of the horizon algebra and a thermal reference state, we construct an energy transfer probability measure and derive integral and detailed fluctuation relations with the mean entropy production being expressed in terms of the Araki relative entropy. The formulation accommodates type-III algebras without introducing a reduced density matrix and can be extended to global bifurcate Killing horizons admitting the required thermal reference state. For coherent scalar-field perturbations, we obtain a closed form for the characteristic function, identify the mean transfer with the excess boost-energy flux, and derive a thermodynamic uncertainty relation bounding the variance in terms of the mean transfer. We then explore the geometric implications by postulating a stochastic horizon entropy--area correspondence that associates an area change with each counted energy transfer. Under this additional hypothesis, we obtain fluctuation relations for area changes and an area change uncertainty bound for coherent perturbations.
