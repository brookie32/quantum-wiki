This study presents a quantum‑relativistic protocol for securely verifying an entity’s position even when the prover is untrusted. Using phase‑randomized weak coherent states and two spatially separated verifiers (2 km apart), the authors experimentally demonstrate position verification with sub‑75 m accuracy. The results highlight that position‑based authentication can be achieved in practice, offering potential applications for secure transactions, high‑value asset tracking, and authenticated communications.
