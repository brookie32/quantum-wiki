arXiv:2609.09523v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We find through direct, time-domain measurement of trapped Yb^+ ions that the excited {}^3[11/2]_{9/2}^o state has a collision-free lifetime of 17.8(6) min, long enough for use in atomic clockwork or quantum information processing. We locate and benchmark a broad ``repump'' transition at 701, nm that readily transfers population back into the Doppler cooling cycle with the ground state, which may allow for quantum nondemolition measurement of sub-structure in the metastable manifold. Using a bi-stable Coulomb-crystal manometer, we find that the collisional quenching of this metastable {}^3[11/2]_{9/2}^o manifold by background gas is more efficient than for {}^2F_{7/2}^o, but nonetheless we observe an average lifetime exceeding 10 minutes at the base pressure of our apparatus.
