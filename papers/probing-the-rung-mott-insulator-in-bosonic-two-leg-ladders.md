arXiv:2611.32680v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Mott insulators typically appear at integer densities, but certain lattice geometries or non-local interactions can stabilise Mott insulators at fractional densities. In this work, we study strongly interacting two-leg ladders using ultracold bosonic atoms around half-filling, and identify the rung-Mott insulating (RMI) phase through its characteristic rung occupation in site-resolved measurements. We probe its excitation spectrum via lattice modulation spectroscopy, and interpret the observed spectra using numerical simulations that include the driven dynamics, coupling to multiple excited states, and energy offsets. As with other Mott insulators, the RMI phase is characterized through its incompressibility, which we probe via a site-resolved analysis of densities and chemical potentials. Our results provide direct insight into the excitation dynamics of a strongly correlated ladder system, and pave the way for studying transport and further dynamical properties in low-dimensional quantum systems with programmable lattice geometries.
