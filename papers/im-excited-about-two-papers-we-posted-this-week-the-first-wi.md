I’m excited about two papers we posted this week. The first, with Chris Pattison and Jeongwan Haah, proposes a design for a fault-tolerant quantum processor that receives instructions via just a few external wires, a promising step toward a more scalable solid-state quantum architecture. https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23919 The second, with Yuanxi Liu and @RobertHuangHY, constructs an efficient quantum algorithm that outputs the phase diagram of a many-body quantum system, a potentially powerful tool for physics discovery. https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25064 It occurs to me that all my co-authors, who did almost all the hard work, are my current or former students. I feel lucky.
