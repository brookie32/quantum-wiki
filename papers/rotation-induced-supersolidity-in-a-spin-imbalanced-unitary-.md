arXiv:2611.27538v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We show that a supersolid can be spontaneously generated by a persistent current in a spin-imbalanced unitary Fermi gas in a ring. As polarization increases, stable superflow turns dissipative via vortex emission before superfluid and crystalline order coexist. Pairing and spin densities are modulated with an even number of nodes, where the pairing phase jumps by pi and excess majority spins accumulate. Even at fixed polarization, the modulation period changes with the winding number, unlike in FFLO states. The modulation slides freely, supporting a gapless translational Goldstone mode.
