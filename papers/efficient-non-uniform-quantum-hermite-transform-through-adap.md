arXiv:2609.20739v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: On the span of the first N oscillator modes, Gauss--Hermite quadrature gives an exact change of basis between mode coefficients and N weighted position space samples. We implement this transform with O(Noperatorname{polylog}(N,1/arepsilon)) logical gates and polylogarithmic quantum width. The operator-error bound arepsilon holds on arbitrary superpositions and includes all auxiliary registers. The construction uses signed averages on adaptive windows to convert uniform-grid samples into weighted Hermite-root samples. Their varying widths control the amplification cost, giving the near-linear bound.
