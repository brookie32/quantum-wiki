arXiv:2611.27314v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Using modern multiloop calculation methods, we obtain correction to energy levels of muonic hydrogen-like atoms due to the leading-order (proptoalpha (Zalpha)^3) Wichmann-Kroll electronic potential. Our result is expressed in terms of generalized polylogarithms. As a byproduct, we also obtain the Wichmann-Kroll potential in momentum space in terms of the same functions.
