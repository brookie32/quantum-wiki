The study presents circuit‑level simulations of two distributed fault‑tolerant primitives—transversal non‑local CNOT and logical teleportation—using surface and bivariate‑bicycle QLDPC codes. Results from the Transversal Multiple Code Block Simulator show that transversal non‑local CNOTs achieve up to an order of magnitude lower logical error rates than teleportation at comparable code distances and noise levels, and identify code distances (≈ 11 at p ≈ 10⁻⁴ and ≈ 29 at p ≈ 10⁻³) that suppress logical errors below 10⁻¹², enabling efficient large‑scale quantum algorithms on distributed architectures. These findings guide the selection of fault‑tolerant operations, codes, and inter‑module link requirements for scalable quantum computing.
