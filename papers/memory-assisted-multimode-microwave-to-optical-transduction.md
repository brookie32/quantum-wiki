Memory‑assisted multimode microwave‑to‑optical transduction is demonstrated using a low‑doping \(^{171}\)Yb\(^{3+}\):Y\(_{2}\)SiO\(_{5}\) crystal cooled to 30 mK. By exploiting zero‑first‑order Zeeman transitions at zero magnetic field, the system achieves long optical and spin coherence times, enabling on‑demand retrieval with 0.4–0.3 noise photons over storage durations of 460–620 µs while preserving multimode capacity via spin and optical inhomogeneous broadening. This technique offers a practical noise‑mitigation strategy for coherent links between superconducting qubits, boosting entanglement generation rates by enabling synchronization in quantum repeaters.
