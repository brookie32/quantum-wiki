arXiv:2610.11079v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We show that the fast surgery framework of Baspin-Berent-Cohen can be instantiated using at most ilde{O}(n(k + d)) ancilla qubits, improving on the previously achievable O(ndot k dot d) overhead. This result also establishes that fast surgery can be performed at a cost comparable to the ilde{O}(nk) overhead required for regular surgery. We build the surgery ancilla by first starting with a dense, but expanding system; and then applying expansion-preserving sparsification methods from gauging and code embeddings.
