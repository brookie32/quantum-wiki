arXiv:2609.35547v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We present the first worst-case, sublinear protocol to estimate the fidelity between a known target state and unknown lab-prepared state using Pauli basis measurements. More precisely, for d-dimensional states, we estimate the fidelity to pm arepsilon using o(d^{0.9675})/arepsilon^2 copies, which applies to all target states. Furthermore, our results establish improved guarantees for Pauli shadow tomography and Distributed Inner Product Estimation.
