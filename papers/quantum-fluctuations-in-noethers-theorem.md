arXiv:2611.08906v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: When deriving the expression for the Noether's current one obtains a term that is an integral over the Euler-Lagrange equation. This term vanishes along the classical path and so does not contribute to the current. However, quantum particles do not necessarily follow the classical path; this concept is the basis of Feynman's path integral quantization formalism. Thus, for a quantum system, the Euler-Lagrange term does not vanish and, hence, corresponds to a quantum fluctuation of the Noether's current owing to the system deviating from the classical path. Here, we compute this quantum fluctuation from Noether's theorem and the path integral formalism. We find that, for a time invariant system, the expression for the quantum fluctuations is give by minus the functional expression for the Hamiltonian, Delta E = -H(y), evaluated at the distance from the classical path, y. As an example we evaluate quantum energy fluctuations of a single Harmonic oscillator. In the short time limit we recover the energy-time uncertainty relation and in the long time limit we recover the zero point energy of the quantum harmonic oscillator.
