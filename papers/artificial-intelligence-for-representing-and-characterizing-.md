In its July 2026 review, Du et al. examine how machine learning, deep learning, and language models are used to represent and characterize large‑scale quantum systems generated by analog simulators or quantum computers. The survey identifies two core applications—predicting quantum properties and reconstructing system states—and discusses their roles in certification, benchmarking, algorithmic enhancement, and the discovery of critical phenomena. It concludes with a discussion of outstanding challenges and promising future directions for integrating AI into quantum science.
