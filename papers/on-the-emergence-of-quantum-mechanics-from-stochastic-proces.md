arXiv:2602.22095v4 Announce Type: replace Abstract: The stochastic--quantum correspondence reinterprets quantum dynamics in terms of an underlying stochastic process on a configuration space. We generalize its lifting prescription, overcoming restrictions on the reachable density matrices without enlarging the configuration space or its associated Hilbert space. A compatibility relation and Kraus dictionary connect stochastic kernels to completely positive, trace-preserving (CPTP) maps: every finite-dimensional kernel admits a compatible lift, and these lifts collectively encompass all quantum channels as the kernels vary. We describe sufficiently regular composable CPTP dynamics through time-local Lindblad generators. We establish that every rooted quantum dynamical family admits a stochastic realizer on the same configuration space, and that a revival of configuration-space distinguishability rules out every Markovian realizer on that space.
