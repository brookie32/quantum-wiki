arXiv:2611.33280v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We present the first direct tensor network calculation of flavor-resolved parton distribution functions (PDFs) for a pion-like isospin triplet in the two-flavor Nambu-Jona-Lasinio model in (1+1) dimensions. Four-fermion interactions bind the mesons without confinement. Using staggered fermions and a charge-symmetrized Hamiltonian, we prepare the bound states and compute light-cone correlators by real-time evolution. The resulting quark and antiquark PDFs obey the expected isospin relations under u leftrightarrow d exchange between charged pions and exhibit equal u and d distributions in the neutral pion. A flavor-independent antisymmetrization of the field ordering removes the vacuum-subtracted anticommutator contribution and restores charge-conjugation relations in both the correlators and PDFs across the triplet within numerical accuracy. These results provide benchmarks for quantum simulations of the partonic structure of the meson.
