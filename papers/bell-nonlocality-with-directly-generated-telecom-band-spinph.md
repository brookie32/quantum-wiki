The study reports the first direct generation of high‑fidelity spin–photon entanglement between a single Rubidium atom and telecom‑band (~1530 nm) photons using a cavity‑assisted excitation protocol. The resulting Bell state fidelity exceeds 91 %, and a clear violation of the CHSH Bell inequality (2.455 ± 0.077 > 2) demonstrates quantum nonlocality for this atom–photon system. These results establish a single‑atom telecom emitter capable of long‑distance entanglement distribution in optical fibre networks.
