Qumus is the first AI quantum materials experimentalist, physically embodied within a robotic mini-laboratory as an intelligent, multimodal system designed for creating and nano-processing atomically thin two-dimensional materials and van der Waals structures.   Qumus autonomously navigates the full scientific cycle, from hypothesis generation and protocol planning to multi-step experimental execution, result analysis and reporting.   The system achieved the first AI-driven fabrication of complex atomically thin nanodevices, including a graphene field-effect transistor, without human intervention.
