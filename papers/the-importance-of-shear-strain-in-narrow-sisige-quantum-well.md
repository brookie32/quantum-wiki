arXiv:2611.16015v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Several heterostructure profiles have been proposed for Si/SiGe quantum wells, to improve the operation of quantum-dot spin qubits. These structures share a common goal of generating either of the two wave vectors that govern the conduction-band valley coupling, dubbed k_0 and k_1. Here, we focus on the long-wavelength k_1 term that couples valleys when paired with shear strain, and which governs the Dresselhaus spin-orbit coupling. We show that shear strain induced by metal top gates may be enough to activate the k_1 valley-coupling mechanism, mitigating the need for "ultrasharp" interfaces, as commonly assumed for large valley energy splittings. We propose a scheme featuring narrow quantum wells that does not require ultrasharp interfaces, but should still provide large deterministic valley splittings (when paired with shear strain), and large spin-orbit couplings and g-factor corrections, which are of great interest for experimental implementations.
