The study introduces a quantum‑mechanical “relaxation” framework for modeling temperature and electric‑field induced phase transitions in ferroelectrics and antiferroelectrics.  
Instead of treating the transitions as classical Arrhenius processes, the authors assume (i) relaxational dynamics and (ii) a necessity for quantum treatment, allowing first‑principles simulations that reproduce hysteresis loops more accurately than traditional empirical models.
