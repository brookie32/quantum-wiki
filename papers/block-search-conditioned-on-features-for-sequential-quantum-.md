arXiv:2611.27587v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Sequential quantum data encoding is addressed through the joint selection of input features and encoding blocks within a fixed quantum register. At each step, a new feature, a block of operations, its placement, and its rotation weights are selected together from training data, extending the incremental construction of Q-FLAIR. With direct-loss selection (RAW), nine to twelve features were retained in four qubits across five new Bars and Stripes datasets. Under the same final calibration, mean changes relative to the calibrated Q-FLAIR reference were +5.53 percentage points in balanced accuracy, -15.83% in binary cross-entropy (BCE), and +7.79% in area under the ROC curve (AUC). The prespecified success criterion was met in four of five datasets and for the mean paired differences. These results were obtained by comparing complete procedures under a common cap of 32 operations.
