arXiv:2506.03773v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We show that, as the Choi matrix of a completely positive perturbation direction approaches a singular limit, the shift required for k-positivity can diverge more slowly than that required for complete positivity. For every kgeq 2, we construct a (k-1)-positive map Phi and a completely positive map Gamma on M_{k+1} with singular Choi matrix mathbf{C}_Gamma. Regularizing Gamma by Gamma_arepsilon=Gamma+arepsilonDelta, where Delta(X)=Tr(X)I, we determine the least tgeq 0 for which Phi+tGamma_arepsilon is j-positive. More precisely, s_{j,Gamma_arepsilon}(Phi)=0 for j0 is determined explicitly. The fractional exponent is obtained through singular-value estimates and an explicit construction of rank-k perturbations of a nilpotent Jordan block.
