arXiv:2611.21697v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We construct and solve the three-colored Kitaev model on finite hyperbolic lattices with periodic boundary conditions. We describe a self-contained procedure which uses finite quotients of orientation- preserving triangle groups to generate regular maps on compact genus-g surfaces. From these, one can extract their lattice connectivity, plaquette structure, homology cycles and Wilson sectors while implementing consistent boundary conditions. On the example of {8, 3} and {12, 3} lattices, we enumerate a selection of quotients with up to 72 vertices, which constitute the discrete steps in a finite-size scaling analysis. For the isotropic point, corresponding to the spin-liquid phase in the thermodynamic limit, we identify the ground state superselection sectors, quantify their deviation from the homogeneous {pi} flux sector and show how the selected flux background depends on the quotient geometry. We then discuss how the resulting ground state sector degeneracies are entirely generated by physical lattice symmetries acting on both the plaquette fluxes and non-contractible Wilson loops. Finally, we study the behaviour of the finite-size two-vison gap and matter fermion correlations. We find that the gap does not decrease monotonically with increasing system size, while one still recovers the expected algebraic decay of the correlations at the isotropic point, even for the considered modest system sizes.
