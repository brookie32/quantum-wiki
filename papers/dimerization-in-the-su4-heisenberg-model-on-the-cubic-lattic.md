arXiv:2510.00284v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We study the SU(4)-symmetric Heisenberg model on a cubic lattice with spatially anisotropic magnetic couplings. We utilize several approaches based on the tensor-network representation of many-body wave functions, which enable accurate analysis of ground-state properties of the model in different regimes of spatial anisotropy including the fully isotropic three-dimensional case. Our results point to the persistence of the dimerized color-ordered phase throughout a wide range of magnetic couplings.
