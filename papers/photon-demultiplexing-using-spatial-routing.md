arXiv:2610.19933v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Spatial sectioning of the far-field noncollinear field from spontaneous parametric down-conversion (SPDC) is a promising approach for multiparty quantum communication. Although demonstrations with six pairs of users have been reported, the fundamental limits remain unknown. These include the maximum number of channels, the required degree of channel optimization, and the dependence on input parameters. In this paper, we provide a practical framework that answers these questions by connecting input parameters and invoking a design theorem. For a representative configuration, we realize 12 user-pair channels with routing consistency exceeding 0.9999 at a 12% reduction in photon flux. We further show that sectioning improves the accidental-coincidence budget linearly with the number of channels N, allowing the aggregate key rate to scale with the user count. These results provide a design strategy for spatial-division multiplexing of entangled photons analogous to the channel-spacing standards used in wavelength-division networks.
