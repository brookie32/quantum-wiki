arXiv:2610.34972v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: A state over time (stote) pairs a state rho with a channel E as the Hermitian, generally non-positive operator (rhootimesmathbf{1})star J_E. Characterizing the convex cone widehat{Q} these operators generate was posed as an open problem in Hoogsteder-Riera, Calsamiglia and Winter (2026). We exhibit a linear copy of the bipartite separable cone as an exposed face of widehat{Q}: for output systems of the form B = A'R with A' a copy of the input, there is an explicit witness W_0inwidehat{Q}^* and an explicit injective linear map L with sigmain SEP_{+}(A{:}R)iff L(sigma)inwidehat{Q}(A{:}A'R). Composing with the NP-hardness of separability shows that exact membership, weak membership, and weak optimization are NP-hard for widehat{Q} and its dual. A quantitative stability bound makes the weak-membership reduction valid at inverse-polynomial accuracy. Thus polynomial-time membership tests do not exist unless P=NP. Independently of this complexity assumption, Fawzi's theorem implies that widehat{Q}(A{:}A'R) admits no finite semidefinite representation when d=im Age2, r=im Rge2, and d+r>5.
