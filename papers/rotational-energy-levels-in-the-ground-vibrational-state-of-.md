arXiv:2605.13060v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Methane is a key spherical-top molecule, yet restrictive selection rules for one-photon transitions have prevented determination of its ground state (GS) energies with state-of-the-art kHz-level accuracy. We report the GS rotational energy level differences with kHz-level accuracy from two frequency-comb-referenced Doppler-free methods: optical-optical double-resonance spectroscopy in the {Lambda}-type configuration, and Lamb-dip spectroscopy of allowed and forbidden transitions. A Hamiltonian fit to the data yields GS term values with rotational numbers up to it{J} = 12 with kHz level accuracy.
