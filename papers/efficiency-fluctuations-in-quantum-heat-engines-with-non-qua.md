arXiv:2610.10647v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We have presented an analytical and numerical study of efficiency fluctuations in quantum Carnot and Otto engines with pistons operating under non-quasi-static adiabatic expansion and compression processes at constant speed, along with quasi-static isothermal (for the Carnot engine) or isochoric processes (for the Otto engine). We have considered the two-point measurement scheme during the adiabatic strokes in this regard. By considering each of the pistons as a single quantum particle in a 1-D box with a non-quasi-statically movable boundary, we capture the stochastic nature of the work output resulting from the unitary evolution of the quantum mechanical state of the system during non-equilibrium processes. Furthermore, we have explicitly shown how the piston speed during quantum non-equilibrium strokes introduces additional quantum fluctuations that modify the average efficiency of both the engines.
