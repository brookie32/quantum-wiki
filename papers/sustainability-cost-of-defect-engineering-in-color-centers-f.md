The study assesses the sustainability impact of defect engineering in color centers used for quantum communications by evaluating twenty host‑crystal/defect generation combinations across diamond, SiC, h‑BN, GaN and ZnO using ion implantation, e‑beam irradiation, laser writing and heteroepitaxial growth. A cradle‑to‑gate life‑cycle framework integrating environmental, chemical hazard, human health toxicity and techno‑economic metrics reveals that h‑BN with laser writing delivers the lowest overall burden ( < 20 kg CO₂‑eq per chip, minimal risk profile and cost), while SiC offers a balanced near‑term pathway and ZnO/GaN provide selective benefits for integrated photonics
