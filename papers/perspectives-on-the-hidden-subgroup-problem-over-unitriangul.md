arXiv:2611.23590v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We investigate the quantum Hidden Subgroup Problem (HSP) over the upper unitriangular group U_n(F_p). While efficient quantum algorithms were discovered for some families of non abelian groups and nil-2 p-groups, no efficient algorithms are known for groups of nilpotency class higher than 2. The only techniques known for such groups scale exponentially in the size of the input. We first study the group U_4(F_p), the smallest upper unitriangular group exhibiting nilpotency class three. We analyze the application of the Pretty Good Measurement approach used for Heisenberg groups and show its limits. We then focus on the more general case U_n(F_p) to adapt existing techniques to this particular structure. In particular, we derive an algorithm for cyclic subgroups as a subroutine for a general algorithm, although it is not efficient in the size of the group. Our results highlight the difficulties introduced by the increasing nilpotency class and suggests some directions for an efficient quantum algorithm for the HSP over U_n(F_p).
