arXiv:2611.09653v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: This paper proposes a phase-balanced differential Rydberg atomic quantum receiver (DRAQR) for weak-signal recovery, using local-oscillator phase control to reinforce differential signal responses and a shared squeezed-reservoir model to correlate inter-cell quantum projection noise (QPN) and photon shot noise (PSN). An analytical model incorporating QPN, PSN, intrinsic thermal noise (ITN), and inter-cell correlations quantifies signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) gain, outage-constrained minimum detectable power, and bit-error rate (BER) for square M-QAM. For the evaluated settings, SNR gain reaches approximately 10~dB as PSN correlation approaches unity, with BER decreasing as correlation increases. Under Rician fading, at a 0-dB threshold SNR and maximum outage probability of 0.1, the minimum detectable power decreases from -146.95~dBm at K=1.155 to -159.98~dBm at K=2.4. Analytical results are validated against Monte-Carlo simulations over 150{,}000 data runs per point for receiver-performance plots.
