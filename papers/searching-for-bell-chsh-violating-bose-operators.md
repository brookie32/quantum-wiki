arXiv:2609.05685v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We construct bounded Bell observables for a free scalar field in complementary spacetime wedges using measurable involutions of the spectral variable of a local canonical quadrature. These involutions yield wedge-local Hermitian unitary operators that anticommute with a chosen bounded observable. For a cosine observable, we characterize the admissible translations and reflections and derive exact correlations at finite modular bandwidth. The affine-reflection construction yields a violation of the Bell-CHSH inequality of approximately 2.06.
