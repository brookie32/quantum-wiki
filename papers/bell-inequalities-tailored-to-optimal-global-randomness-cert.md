arXiv:2606.21362v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We present two novel families of bipartite Bell inequalities designed to achieve optimal global randomness certification for an arbitrary number of outputs d. We first use symmetry arguments to argue that their maximal quantum violations certify 2log d random bits. For the first family, we construct a quantum realization using dimes d maximally entangled states which provides a quantum violation that we conjecture to be optimal for any d. It is then numerically shown that the obtained quantum violation certifies optimal global randomness, up to numerical precision, for d=3,4. For the second family, we provide the optimal quantum violation and its quantum realization for any d, again using dimes d maximally entangled states and projective measurements over at least two unbiased bases on one of the parties. We self-test this realization for d=3, which implies the optimal certification of two fully random trits.
