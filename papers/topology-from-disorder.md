In a 2026 *Nature Physics* article, Jens Hertkorn and Botond Oreg report that adding controlled disorder to quantum simulators allows mixed‐state systems to exhibit measurable topological features—something previously observed only in idealized pure states at absolute zero. Two complementary experiments demonstrate how this engineered disorder induces robust topological signatures in otherwise non‑topological regimes.
