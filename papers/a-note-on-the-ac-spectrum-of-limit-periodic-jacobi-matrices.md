arXiv:2610.19416v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We show that limit-periodic Jacobi matrices in the weak exponential class EC(eta) have purely absolutely continuous spectral type when eta is sufficiently large, depending only on the R-boundedness constant. The proof first establishes homogeneity of the spectrum from the weak exponential approximation rate. A closure theorem for reflectionless Jacobi matrices then transfers reflectionlessness from the periodic approximants to the limit, and a theorem of Poltoratski and Remling rules out singular spectrum on a homogeneous set.
