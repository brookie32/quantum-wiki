Using gate‑defined quantum dots fabricated on a natural silicon CMOS foundry platform, the study achieves hole‑spin qubits with single‑qubit gate fidelities of 99.8 % and two‑qubit gates showing a quality factor of 240 (physical fidelity limit ~99.7 %). These results represent the highest performance reported for holes in natural silicon to date and are enabled by fast, all‑electrical exchange pulsing control. The work demonstrates a scalable, high‑speed quantum logic approach that directly leverages industrial semiconductor infrastructure.
