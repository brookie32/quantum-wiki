arXiv:2611.18440v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We present Tessa, a hardware-aware, device-wide scheme to encode classical data on quantum computers. We use it to experimentally encode and decode up to 1568 real values using IBM's Phoenix and Fez quantum computers on a variety of data. Tessa's construction is an extension of QPIXL and QCrank. However, it checkerboards address and data qubits across a coupling graph with neighbouring data qubits sharing only a subset of address qubits. When using the device's coupling graph no SWAP gates or routing is required, but the construction works for any bipartite coupling graph. The shots required for readout are determined by the local coupling degree. As examples, we have used cat states (pixel art of cats), various pixel art, audio, MIDI, DNA sequences and protein amino acid sequences.
