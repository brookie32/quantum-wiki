arXiv:2610.22223v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We investigate the fate of quantum many-body scars in the PXP model subject to Markovian dissipation. We show that when local jump operators respect the same symmetries of the Hamiltonian the full Liouvillian spectrum displays a tower of states with properties analogous to standard quantum scars: large overlaps with Neel states and equally spaced gaps in the imaginary part of the eigenvalues, set by a single frequency. Whether the long-time dynamics is dominated by scars is determined by the real parts of the eigenvalues, which are set by the ratio between the two relaxation rates gamma_{pm} describing spin flips down-to-up or up-to-down respectively. For asymmetric dissipation (gamma_+ > gamma_-) the scarred eigenvalues have the smallest real parts: the resulting enhanced fidelity oscillations are further stabilized by staggered jump operators. In contrast, replacing the full projection operator in the dissipators with a partial one breaks the symmetries of the problem and is seen to destroy dissipative scars altogether.
