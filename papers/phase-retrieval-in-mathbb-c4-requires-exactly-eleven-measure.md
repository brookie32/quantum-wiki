arXiv:2607.27719v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Determining the minimal number of intensity measurements required for phase retrieval in C^4 has been a long-standing open problem. Prior to this work, the best-known results implied that this minimum was either 10 or 11. In this paper, we leverage characteristic classes and cohomology groups from differential topology to prove that no family of 10 vectors in C^4 possesses the phase retrieval property. Combining our lower bound with Vinzant's explicit eleven-vector construction establishes that the exact minimum is 11. Our result yields a significant consequence for pure state quantum tomography, namely, a rank-one POVM on C^4 requires exactly 11 elements to be informationally complete for pure states. This further implies that three orthonormal bases are insufficient to uniquely distinguish all pure states in C^4. Because four orthonormal bases are already known to be sufficient, we conclude that exactly four bases are required, thereby completely resolving the problem left in [C. Carmeli, T. Heinosaari, J. Schultz, A. Toigo, Eur. Phys. J. D].
