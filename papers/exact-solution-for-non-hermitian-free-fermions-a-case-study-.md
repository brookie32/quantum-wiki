arXiv:2605.26813v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We study the non-Hermitian XY spin chain with open boundary conditions and complex anisotropy. We express its quasi-energies and mode eigenvectors in a Chebyshev-polynomial representation using the quasi-energy as the spectral variable. For finite even L, we prove that every exceptional point is of second order. Each Chebyshev sector can contain at most one double root at fixed coupling, and the two sectors cannot become defective simultaneously. Retaining the boundary residual allows the associated generalized eigenvectors to be obtained by differentiation, giving the Jordan structure and the counting of ordinary and generalized many-body eigenstates. For the explicit L=4 case, the energy and eigenvector branches exhibit the characteristic square-root monodromy. The normalized left-right overlap is identified with the phase rigidity, whose vanishing corresponds to a divergent eigenvalue condition number. These results provide an exact treatment of defective eigensystems and their local spectral sensitivity within a quadratic fermionic model.
