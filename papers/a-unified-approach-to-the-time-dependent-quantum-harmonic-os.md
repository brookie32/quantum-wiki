arXiv:2611.24446v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We revisit the time-dependent harmonic oscillator (TDHO) in quantum mechanics based on the notion of equivalence transformations and Lie point symmetries. Within this framework, we give the most general point transformations depending on seven parameters which map from the TDHO to the usual harmonic oscillator (HO) and the other way around. As a byproduct we reconstruct the Schrodinger propagator (Mehler's kernel) for the time-independent harmonic oscillator. We demonstrate, without recourse to the Lewis--Riesenwert (LR) invariant, how Lie algebraic methods enable alternative derivation of the normalized wave functions of the TDHO by constructing solutions invariant under some specific subgroup of SL(2,R) combined with the gauge group. We further show that, on the relevant SL(2,R)-invariant solution family, the Lewis--Riesenfeld operator defines a compatible second-order differential constraint, thereby clarifying its relation to the Lie symmetry structure of the TDHO.
