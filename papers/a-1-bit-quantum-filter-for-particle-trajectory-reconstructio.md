Chiotopoulos et al. (2026) introduce a 1‑bit quantum filter that reformulates high‑luminosity LHC particle tracking as binary ground‑state filtering rather than full matrix inversion, reducing the gate complexity from the standard HHL algorithm to \(O(\sqrt{N}\log N)\) for an \(N\)-dimensional Hamiltonian. The technique achieves track‑finding efficiency comparable to classical state‑of‑the‑art methods on LHCb Monte Carlo events and is benchmarked on a Quantinuum System Model H2 trapped‑ion processor and IBM Heron R3 superconducting device, showing that realistic event topologies can be handled by near‑term quantum hardware under noise‑free simulation conditions.
