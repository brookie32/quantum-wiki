arXiv:2611.32804v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: A one-dimensional model of a Bose-Fermi mixture with contact repulsions is exactly solvable via nested Bethe ansatz. That formal solution is here simplified along a special line where the Bethe-ansatz equations can be unnested. The model solution is expressed in terms of the known solutions for the Lieb-Liniger model and the Yang-Gaudin model of attractive spin-frac{1}{2} fermions in the balanced case. We find explicit analytical results for the ground-state energy, the velocities of excitations, and the Drude weights at weak and strong interactions. At weak interactions, the special line corresponds to the parameter values where the isolated systems of free fermions and weakly interacting bosons have equal velocities.
