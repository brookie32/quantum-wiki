arXiv:2611.17555v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We introduce a model-free method for Hamiltonian tomography based on the operator spreading wavefront. Given two tensor network simulations of the same quantum lattice (one with the nominal Hamiltonian and one with one or more perturbed bonds), the signed cross-entropy of the non-identity Pauli weight field L(v,t) = -ln(n_{rm pert}/n_{rm base}) identifies each perturbed bond by its causal signature: a red-blue sign flip at the two endpoints, visible from the first wavefront crossing. Combining measurements from p strategically placed observation centers via an ensemble log-likelihood sum, a single broken bond is identified with an 82-fold margin over the next candidate on a 7imes7 lattice. Extending to realistic coupling degradation (elta J = -0.20) and up to 8 simultaneous faults, the integrated log-ratio Lambda(v) = ln(B(v)/N(v)) achieves a per-bond detection rate above 90% in a single seismic pass using only 6 simulations. An iterative re-scan strategy progressively resolves multi-bond cases as the signal clears with each recalibration pass. The method requires no model assumptions and no knowledge of the perturbation type or magnitude; the detection is deliberately blind.
