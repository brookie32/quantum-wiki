arXiv:2609.12124v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: A previously unexplored scheme is devised for creating and certifying coherent quantum superposition of distinctly separated states of a spin-tagged massive object passing through a confined uniform magnetic field. This results in entangling the spatial and spin parts of a two-component Pauli wavefunction such that the entailed coherence can be conveniently certified by spin measurements alone. The implementability of this scheme is analysed using NV-centre nanodiamonds of masses sim 10^{10} amu based on the current technological capabilities.
