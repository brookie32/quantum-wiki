arXiv:2611.18136v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: The Higgs boson self-coupling enters the Higgsstrahlung process e^+e^-o ZH at one loop. The three diagrams that carry it project onto two Lorentz structures: the tree-level one, which rescales the rate, and that of the CP-even anomalous coupling b_Z, which changes the polarization of the final-state Z boson. To assess how well quantum state tomography constrains the hZZ anomalous couplings and the trilinear coupling at FCC-ee, we compute the helicity density matrix of the Z boson, for an effective vertex that also includes a CP-odd anomalous coupling ilde b_Z, together with its complete one-loop correction in the Standard Model. With these corrections and no other new physics in the effective vertex, quantum state tomography determines kappa_lambda to pm 3.9, or pm 2.9 with the full angular information, independently of the rate and of the normalization of the vertex, and not far from the limit eltakappa_lambda simeq 1 set by the quantum Fisher information. Without assumptions on the new physics, quantum tomography directly bounds the anomalous couplings at the 10^{-3} level, improving on the existing projections. The density matrix also characterizes the Z boson as a qutrit. At 365 GeV its state in the central region is pure enough to violate the Klyachko--Can--Binicioglu--Shumovsky contextuality inequality, CNTXT_5 = 2.06 at heta = pi/2 against the non-contextual bound 2. We also quantify the purity and the amount of magic of the Z boson state, using the mana, the stabilizer Renyi entropy and the robustness of magic for the latter. At fixed angle, purity, contextuality and magic are robust against anomalous couplings of the size probed by the tomography. The inclusive state, instead, is neither contextual in this test nor magic in the helicity basis.
