Carlo Rovelli, a leading theoretical physicist known for his work on quantum gravity, argues in his 2026 book *On the Equality of All Things* that physics—especially high‑energy and particle theory—has been overstated as the ultimate description of reality. He contends that while fundamental equations reveal deep insights, they do not capture the full complexity of the world and should not be seen as the sole explanatory foundation. Rovelli calls for greater humility in science and questions whether artificial intelligence could ever achieve consciousness.
