arXiv:2609.31872v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We investigate the relation between two complementary descriptions of the Anderson transition on small-world random graphs: finite-size scaling of eigenfunction moments and renormalization-group (RG) flows of multifractal dimensions D_q. The RG flows recover the qualitative structure previously reported for the information dimension D_1, while their extension to other D_q with smaller moment orders q1/2 and small q<1/2 eigenfunction moments, which are associated with distinct critical behaviors, in particular different critical exponents.
