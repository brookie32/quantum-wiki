arXiv:2609.36476v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We derive the finite-temperature characteristic function of work (CFW) for a driven O(N) Gaussian field theory and obtain closed-form expressions for all zero-temperature excess-work cumulants. For gapped protocols, we use adiabatic perturbation theory (APT) to derive the au_Q^{-2} scaling for protocols with a nonzero first derivative at either boundary, where au_Q is the protocol duration; smoother boundaries lead to faster decay. For power-law protocols approaching the critical point with exponent p, we determine the competition between critical excitations and the regular contribution. The nth cumulant's critical contribution follows Kibble--Zurek (KZ) scaling au_Q^{-p(d+n)/(p+2)} for d+n<2p+4, acquires a logarithmic correction au_Q^{-2p}logau_Q at d+n=2p+4, and scales as au_Q^{-2p} above this condition, where d is the spatial dimension. This analytical study of the APT--KZ crossover in a solvable model provides a basis for studying their competition in interacting field theories.
