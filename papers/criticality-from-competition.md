Luisa Eck (2026) reports that conformal field theories (CFTs) capture the universal behaviour at quantum critical points yet few lattice models realize specific CFTs. By analysing competing boundary conditions of topological phases, her work provides a systematic framework for engineering microscopic models whose low‑energy limits correspond to chosen CFTs. The approach offers a practical route to bridge universal field‑theory predictions with concrete quantum systems.
