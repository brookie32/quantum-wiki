arXiv:2608.27202v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We determine the strong converse exponent for the entanglement cost of quantum state merging, showing that it is characterized by the optimized alpha-z conditional Renyi entropies with z=alpha/2in[1/2,1]. This contrasts with the sandwiched conditional Renyi entropies that typically govern strong converse exponents in quantum information theory. As a consequence, we derive the strong converse exponent of the partially smoothed conditional min-entropy in purified distance. This exponent is governed by club-sandwiched conditional entropies, whereas global smoothing leads to a sandwiched expression.
