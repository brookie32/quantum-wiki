arXiv:2610.27098v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We study multi-invariants---a multi-partite generalization of Renyi entropies---of simple stabilizer states, for an arbitrary replica symmetry group. We develop a group theoretic technique for evaluating such invariants, which operates on the replica group and involves counting intersections among its cosets. Our techniques streamline the calculations of multi-invariants for graph states with two GHZ-like nodes and for certain generalizations thereof. The methodology developed here is most suitable when the quantum state is simple but flexibility over the replica group is desired.
