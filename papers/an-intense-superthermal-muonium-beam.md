Researchers Jian Tang and Shihan Zhao report producing an intense superthermal muonium beam whose velocity spread is far narrower than all previously available thermal sources. Muonium—a bound state of a positive muon and an electron—has traditionally been generated with diffuse, low‑intensity beams; the new method yields a tightly collimated, high‑flux stream. This advancement promises higher precision in muonic experiments and could benefit studies of fundamental interactions.
