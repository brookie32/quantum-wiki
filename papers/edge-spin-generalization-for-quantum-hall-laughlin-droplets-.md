arXiv:2611.18377v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: It is known that quasiparticles in the bulk of quantum Hall systems possess an emergent spin, which satisfies a spin-statistics relation. Recent results show that this spin can fractionalize: a quasiparticle spin in the bulk induces an opposite edge spin on the boundary. So far, the edge spin has only been studied in the simplest possible geometries, where the boundary is either circular or straight, without considering that a generic confining potential could give the droplet a non-symmetric shape. Yet, the value of the edge spin is not dictated by symmetry, but by topology: it is expected that the specific shape of the edge should not matter. In this work, we therefore generalize the edge spin to elliptically shaped droplets. We consider two different generalizations of the edge spin and test them on a family of elliptic Laughlin states introduced in the present work. The first, most straightforward, generalization is based on the properties of the single-particle orbitals for the elliptic confining potential. We show that this proposal applies to non-interacting states. The second proposal is based on the emergent properties of the quantum fluid. We show that this proposal also applies to interacting states, making it the suitable definition of the edge spin in the elliptic case. Our work demonstrates that it is possible to define an edge spin for Laughlin fluids with a non-trivial shape.
