arXiv:2510.08267v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We present a low-depth amplitude encoding method for arbitrary quantum state preparation. Building on the foundation of an existing divide-and-conquer algorithm, we propose a method to disentangle the ancilla qubits from the final state. Our method is measurement-based but deterministic, and offers an alternative approach to existing state preparation algorithms. It has circuit depth Theta(n^2) and requires Theta(2^n) ancilla qubits. The resulting spacetime allocation is Theta(2^n), which is optimal. Furthermore, we go beyond scaling arguments to precisely quantify the resources needed for any n. We illustrate our method through detailed worked examples of both a ``dense'' state and a W-state. Finally, we demonstrate how to combine this method with existing methods to construct hybrid algorithms with varying space and circuit depth complexities.
