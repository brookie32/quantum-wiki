arXiv:2611.25051v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We study the spin-1 Kitaev chain and, by analyzing its Hilbert-space connectivity in the Cartesian basis, show that it hosts an exponentially large number of exact zero-energy modes protected by chiral symmetry. For even-length periodic chains, their count is lower-bounded by the Jacobsthal number (2^{L+1}+1)/3, where L is the chain length. We then construct an exponentially large number (2^{L/2}) of zero modes with an analytic expression that have a maximally mixed density matrix for a contiguous bipartition using the operator-state mapping. We further identify an embedded tight-binding structure governing defect dynamics that supports PXP-like quantum many-body scars (QMBS) in multiple sectors previously not reported. The associated forward-scattering approximation (FSA) Hamiltonian takes a universal form across the scarred sectors studied. A simple physical picture of defects propagating between walls provides an intuitive description of these dynamics and a structural guide to identifying sectors with fidelity revivals, while its limitations point to additional scarring mechanisms.
