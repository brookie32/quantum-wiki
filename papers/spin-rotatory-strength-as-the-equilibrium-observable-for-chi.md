arXiv:2608.10697v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We identify the spin rotatory strength---a chirality-odd, finite-frequency spin--dipole cross response---as the equilibrium observable for chiral-induced spin selectivity. Time-reversal symmetry forces the static response to vanish, while nonzero SU(2) Wilson-loop flux is an independent necessary condition. Exact diagonalization of a Kane--Mele--Hubbard model (N{=}4,6,8) reveals that gapped molecules robustly suppress the response, whereas near-degenerate systems show amplification at small N whose persistence at larger sizes remains open.
