arXiv:2609.25462v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We show that 1.4<K_G^{C}<1.404898554746, where K_G^{C} is the complex Grothendieck constant. The upper bound improves on Haagerup's bound of 1.40490913ldots from 1987, and the lower bound improves on Davie's bound of 1.33807 from 1984. The upper bound combines ideas from the real and noncommutative complex cases of Grothendieck's inequality.
