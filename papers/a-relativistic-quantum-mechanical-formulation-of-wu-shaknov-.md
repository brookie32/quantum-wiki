arXiv:2611.24502v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: It is suggested that a spontaneous formation of quantum mechanical entanglements does actually occur between spin, helicity and polarization states involved in creation-annihilation processes. A framework built up earlier that presumably affords a unified relativistic quantum mechanical description of free particle-antiparticle pairs of any spin is used to propose a comprehensive formulation of Wu-Shaknov entanglements. Explicit relationships between amplitudes and Born-like probabilities associated to incoming and outgoing states are obtained thereafter on the basis of certain theoretical assumptions. The behaviours of these relationships under the action of a dynamical group which is taken to supply observational correlations for different frames in spacetime are then specified. It appears that a modified version of some of the conventional conservation laws may afford a set of general selection rules for the formation of relativistic entanglements.
