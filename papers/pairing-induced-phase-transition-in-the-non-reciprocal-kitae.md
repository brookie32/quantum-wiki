The 2026 paper “Pairing induced phase transition in the non reciprocal Kitaev chain” by Brighi and Nunnenkamp demonstrates that reservoir engineering can create non‑reciprocal hopping and pairing in a fermionic Kitaev chain.  They find that the resulting non‑Hermitian Hamiltonian exhibits a pairing‑induced phase transition, with two distinct phases separated by an exceptional point; the non‑reciprocal phase shows directional dynamics and slow relaxation, while strong pairing produces an unexpected density‑wave phase with short relaxation times and altered correlation spreading.  These results reveal how superconducting pairing can fundamentally break non‑reciprocity in quantum matter.
