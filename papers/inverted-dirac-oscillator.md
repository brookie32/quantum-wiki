arXiv:2606.15303v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: The Dirac oscillator is obtained from the Dirac Hamiltonian H^{D} = left( cec{alpha}dot ec{p} + mc^{2}eta right) by modifying the momentum through a non-Hermitian substitution overrightarrow{p} rightarrow overrightarrow{p} pm iomega eta overrightarrow{q}. Despite the non-Hermitian nature of this momentum operator, the full Hamiltonian remains Hermitian due to the presence of the Dirac matrix ec{alpha}. However, if one instead introduces a Hermitian modification of the form ec{p} rightarrow ec{p} pm omega eta overrightarrow{q}, the resulting Hamiltonian is no longer Hermitian. In this case, the system corresponds to an inverted Dirac oscillator H^{r}, where the potential becomes unbounded from below, the energy spectrum becomes continuous, and the eigenfunctions fail to be square-integrable, leading to normalization difficulties. We show that the Hamiltonian H^{r} is a pseudo-PT-symmetric operator, and we introduce an unbounded, non-unitary transformation that establishes a connection between H^{r} and H^{D}. The purpose of this work is to analyze this relativistic quantum system -- known as the Dirac inverted oscillator -- which, despite its various applications, admits an exact analytical solution
