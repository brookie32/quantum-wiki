The article investigates Erwin Schrödinger’s stance on determinism by surveying his writings and correspondence. It finds that, contrary to the common image of him as a reactionary opponent of quantum indeterminism, Schrödinger’s views were more nuanced and, at times, more radical than a straightforward deterministic view. The authors conclude that Schrödinger’s position on determinism was complex, challenging the simplistic portrayal of him as merely anti‑quantum.
