arXiv:2608.02732v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Equipped with the recently recognized symmetry structure of mixed states of matter and "strong-weak spontaneous symmetry breaking" (SW-SSB), we develop a quantum particle-vortex duality of emergent model-F and model-A hydrodynamics of 2d boson/rotor systems. This duality relation demonstrates that classical hydrodynamics of 2d bosons can be described in terms of the charge symmetry U(1)_c, but also equivalently in terms of the dual (emergent) 1-form symmetry U(1)^{(1)}_e, as well as U(1)_v associated with the conservation of vortices. This duality provides a bridge between the hydrodynamics of matter and magnetohydrodynamics.
