Electrons trapped on solid‑neon surfaces form low‑noise charge qubits that can be fully controlled electrically. In this 2026 *Nature Physics* study, the authors demonstrate coherent manipulation of interacting electron‑on‑solid‑neon qubits, achieving strong inter‑qubit coupling and implementing cross‑resonance and bSWAP two‑qubit gates using global microwave drives. These results suggest that engineered confinement on solid neon can enable a compact, scalable quantum computing architecture.
