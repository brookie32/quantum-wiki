arXiv:2611.31340v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Radiation transport is an essential process in many physical simulations, including cosmological codes which track stellar and diffuse radiation. Many present codes utilize ray tracing or moment methods, but these grow increasingly difficult and expensive for large simulations. Quantum lattice Boltzmann methods exponentially reduce complexity by propagating radiation over a lattice, allowing for better spatial resolution without needing to introduce adaptive ray tracing or other optimizations. However, existing formulations result in poor angular resolution for radiation point sources. In this work, we propose an angular redistribution methodology which significantly improves the resolution with minimal increase in complexity. We verify the correctness of the method on both classical processors and quantum simulators and outline how the quantum algorithm can be integrated into existing codes. This method is an important step towards fully-quantum radiation hydrodynamics simulation which could exponentially accelerate existing cosmological codes, enabling simulations at scales currently infeasible.
