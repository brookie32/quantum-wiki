The study reports a hybrid semiconductor–superconductor platform consisting of an InAsSb surface quantum well grown by molecular‑beam epitaxy capped with an in‑situ deposited Nb layer. The low‑effective‑mass, high spin‑orbit InAsSb provides strong coupling to the Nb superconductor (high Tc and large critical field), yielding an induced superconducting gap of 1.3 meV measured in Josephson junctions. Gate‑tunable SQUID interferometry confirms coherent transport through both junctions and the loop, demonstrating a promising material platform for quantum devices.
