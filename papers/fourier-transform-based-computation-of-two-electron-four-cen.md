arXiv:2610.12608v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We present a Fourier-based method for evaluating two-electron four-center Coulomb repulsion integrals over Slater-type orbitals. Spherical Fourier-Bessel coefficients of the convolution factors are precomputed and reused. Since the spherical part of the integral can be evaluated directly, there remains only a one-dimensional quadrature problem in the radial direction. For the ten reference integrals reported here, the largest absolute discrepancy is 1.7imes10^{-9}. A preliminary resident-cache benchmark computes approximately 4.2imes10^5 unique integrals per second on an NVIDIA V100 at the contraction stage.
