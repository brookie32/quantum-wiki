arXiv:2611.27592v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We develop a method for forming Ramsey fringes in atomic interferometers as a difference error signal using the phase-jump technique for Ramsey pulses (phase-jump error signal). In this case, the measurement procedure can be implemented as stabilization (locking) of the frequency chirp rate at the central zero of the error signal. We show analytically that this approach allows suppressing the lineshape-asymmetry-caused shift (LACS). This shift was recently theoretically predicted and experimentally identified in the papers [V. I. Yudin et al., JETP Lett. extbf{122}, 737 (2025); D. N. Kapusta et al., JETP Lett. extbf{123}, 388 (2026)]. A characteristic feature of the LACS is an inverse cubic dependence propto 1/T^3 (where T is the time between adjacent Ramsey pulses), and it can become dominant for interferometers with a short baseline. Therefore, the developed measurement algorithm with a phase-jump error signal will enable significant improvement in the metrological performance of compact quantum sensors for various purposes (atomic gravimeters, accelerometers, gyroscopes).
