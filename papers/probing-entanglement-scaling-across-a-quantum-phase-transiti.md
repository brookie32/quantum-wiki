The article reports the use of a fully‑connected trapped‑ion quantum computer to study entanglement scaling across a continuous quantum phase transition. By implementing a multiscale entanglement renormalization ansatz (MERA) and a holographic tomography scheme, the authors simulate an effectively infinite system with only a small number of qubits, overcoming finite‑size effects that normally hinder critical‑point studies. The experiment observes spontaneous symmetry breaking and maps how bipartite entanglement evolves as the system crosses the phase transition, demonstrating a scalable method for probing many‑body quantum correlations on near‑term hardware.
