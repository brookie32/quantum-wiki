arXiv:2609.34926v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: The Efimov effect manifests discrete scale invariance through a geometric series of three-body bound states. Analogous discrete scale symmetry has been observed in the Efimovian expansion of scale-invariant strongly interacting Fermi gases. However, it is unclear whether strong correlation is necessary. Here we investigate the spontaneous breaking of continuous scale invariance in a driven-dissipative two-level system whose driving and dissipation parameters scale as (1/t). By transforming to logarithmic time, we show that the evolution is governed by a non-Hermitian superoperator whose spectral decomposition reveals the emergence of a pair of eigenmodes with opposite real parts and equal imaginary parts. This signals the spontaneous breaking of continuous scale invariance and gives rise to Efimovian-like oscillations: log-periodic decay oscillations obeying discrete scaling laws. The onset of oscillations coincides with a PT-like-symmetry breaking transition in logarithmic time. Our work implies that the spontaneous breaking of continuous temporal scale symmetry and the emergence of Efimovian-like dynamics can be observed in a minimal driven-dissipative system without requiring strong correlations.
