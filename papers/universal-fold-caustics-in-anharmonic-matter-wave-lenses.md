arXiv:2611.13032v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Caustics provide a universal connection between classical ray focusing and structured wave patterns. We theoretically investigate this connection in non-linear matter waves focused by a short-duration anharmonic lens acting on a Bose-Einstein condensate. A semi-classical description shows how the leading cubic aberration transforms the harmonic focal point into a fold caustic, whose local wave field is described by an Airy canonical integral. Three-dimensional Gross-Pitaevskii simulations show that mean-field interactions modify the near-focus density and longitudinal momentum distribution, producing a secondary momentum-space feature absent from the non-interacting evolution. We quantify the information about the effective interaction strength carried by the 1D distribution using classical Fisher information and maximum-likelihood estimation. We obtain a relative uncertainty of 2.7imes10^{-3} for a sample of 10^5 detected points along z. These results establish a framework for studying interaction-driven departures from single-particle caustic universality and promote matter-wave lenses as a sensitive probe of mean-field interactions.
