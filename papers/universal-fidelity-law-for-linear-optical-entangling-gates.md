arXiv:2609.25861v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Imperfect photon sources degrade the performance of interference-based linear-optical entangling gates. We derive a universal fidelity law for these gates in terms of two standard source metrics: Hong-Ou-Mandel visibility and second-order coherence. We model the multiphoton component as an arbitrary overlap between noise and signal photons. While different values of the signal-noise overlap produce different error structures, associated with different physical platforms, all result in the same fidelity law. We test the model on our actively synchronized CNOT-gate experiment and against published results across different platforms, with no free parameters. This framework turns source characterization into a unified benchmark for photonic quantum computing.
