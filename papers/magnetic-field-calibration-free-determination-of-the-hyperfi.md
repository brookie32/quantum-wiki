arXiv:2609.02572v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Hyperfine constant A is a key parameter of the hyperfine structure and underpins precision spectroscopy and metrology. In this Letter, we develop a magnetic-field-calibration-free method for determining the ground-state hyperfine constant A in an ultracold ^{40}K Fermi gas by utilizing a pair of magnetically insensitive ("clock") transitions. This overcomes the stringent magnetic-field calibration requirements of conventional methods. We measure the transition frequency between these two magnetically insensitive transitions with Hz-level resolution over a range of magnetic fields, and obtain the ground-state hyperfine constant A = -himes 285.730536(2),MHz, corresponding to an absolute uncertainty of about 2,Hz. Our value reduces the uncertainty by nearly three orders of magnitude compared with previous determinations, providing a substantially improved reference for high-precision spectroscopy and metrology with ^{40}K.
