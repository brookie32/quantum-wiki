arXiv:1901.09741v5 Announce Type: replace Abstract: This expanded Urdu-language review introduces classical and quantum game theory with particular attention to Nash equilibrium and evolutionary stability. It adds worked examples of the Prisoner's Dilemma and Hawk--Dove games, a concise introduction to qubits and measurement, quantum protocol construction, noise robustness, multiplayer games, a bilingual glossary, and guided questions for learners. It explains the role of quantum protocols, entanglement, admissible strategy spaces, and payoff equivalence, while distinguishing mathematical game-theoretic conclusions from claims of physical implementation. Detailed new sections distinguish equilibrium from ESS and from stability of payoff-equivalence classes, derive the instability of a classical mixed coordination equilibrium, discuss the Bloch-sphere quotient for a special quantum game, analyze noise and perturbations, and propose a reproducible research framework. The article ends with limitations and open research questions. .
