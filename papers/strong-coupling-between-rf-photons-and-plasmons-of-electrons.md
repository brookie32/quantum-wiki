Strong coupling between radio‑frequency photons and collective electron‑plasmon excitations on liquid helium was experimentally observed using an LC resonator. Time‑resolved measurements show coherent, oscillatory energy exchange between the plasmonic mode of electrons floating on the helium surface and the photonic mode of the resonator, evidencing direct hybridization of the two systems. These results establish a tunable plasmon–photon platform that constitutes a first step toward cavity quantum electrodynamics with flat‑surface electron plasmons.
