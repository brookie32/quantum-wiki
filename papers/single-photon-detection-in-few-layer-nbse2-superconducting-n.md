This work demonstrates single-photon detection in few-layer niobium diselenide (NbSe₂) nanowires, less than 100 nm wide, achieving detection at 780 and 1550 nm wavelengths.   The devices maintain a dark-count rate below 1 Hz and achieve timing jitter below 50 ps, enabling quantum-limited detection integrated into quantum-photonic circuits.
