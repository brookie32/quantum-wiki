arXiv:2611.06480v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Using the heavy-particle QED approach, we derive the last missing alpha,(Zalpha)^5,m^2/M nuclear recoil correction in muonic atoms, which originates from the combination of the (Zalpha)^5,m^2/M recoil with the electron vacuum polarization. Numerical results are presented for the 1S, 2S, and 2P states of muH, muD, mu^3He^+, and mu^4He^+. For the 2P-2S Lamb shift in muonic helium ions, our results are of opposite sign and about four times smaller in magnitude than the previous logarithmic estimates.
