**Summary for AI Knowledge Base**

The paper addresses the long‑standing challenge of producing densely populated, high‑energy (multi‑GeV) positron beams with longitudinal spin polarization in a compact, laser‑driven manner. By directing an ultraintense circularly polarized laser head‑on toward a counterpropagating unpolarized electron beam, a quantum electrodynamics cascade is triggered; during this process positrons are created via the nonlinear Breit–Wheeler mechanism inside the strong laser field. The same laser field then directly accelerates these positrons to multi‑GeV energies while coherently manipulating their spin, yielding a longitudinally polarized, high‑yield positron beam in a single, all‑optical experimental setup.
