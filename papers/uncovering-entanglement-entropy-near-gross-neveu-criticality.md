**Summary (2–3 sentences)**  
The authors introduce an efficient fermionic quantum‑Monte‑Carlo algorithm that employs incremental updates in physical parameters, greatly reducing the computational cost of calculating entanglement entropy while preserving accuracy. Using this method they map out the phase diagram of a two‑dimensional square‑lattice Hubbard model—including its Fermi surface and Goldstone modes—and study Gross–Neveu criticality, finding universal scaling of the entanglement entropy governed by the critical exponent \( \nu \) with a leading coefficient that decreases monotonically rather than peaking at the O(\(N\)) transition.
