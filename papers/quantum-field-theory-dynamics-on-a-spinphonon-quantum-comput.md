A hybrid analogue–digital trapped‑ion quantum computer that encodes qubits in the ions’ internal states and bosons in their motional (phonon) modes was used to simulate the non‑equilibrium dynamics of a one‑plus‑one dimensional Yukawa field theory. By exploiting active bosonic registers rather than large boson‑to‑qubit mappings, the experiment avoided truncation errors and achieved large bosonic occupation numbers from an initially empty state without the significant qubit overhead typical of other approaches. The results reach parameter regimes where classical simulations become intractable, demonstrating a promising route for efficient quantum field theory dynamics on spin–phonon devices.
