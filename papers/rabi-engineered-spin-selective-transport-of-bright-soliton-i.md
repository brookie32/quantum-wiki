arXiv:2611.24112v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Spintronics is usually concentrated on spin-dependent dynamics of single particles such as electrons or holes in solids. Extending this concept to nonlinear matter waves offers an interesting possibility of controlling the transport of collective, particle-like excitations through their spin degrees of freedom. Recent advances in Bose-Einstein condensates have provided a versatile platform for realizing nonlinear spin-1/2 systems that support particle-like objects such as bright solitons, making studies of the motion of these solitons under external fields an interesting and important problem. Here, we study the dynamics of bright solitons with different spin symmetries moving in a coordinate-dependent Rabi field, which acts analogously to a spatially varying magnetic field. In that setup, the velocity perturbed soliton motion possesses very interesting scattering dynamics for both perfect and lifted Manakov symmetry. Lastly, we compare both the velocity-controlled scattering problem with the well-known Rabi quenching protocol. This comparison provides a powerful approach to control the soliton dynamics that may open an avenue to control nonlinear spintronics with solitons as spin carriers.
