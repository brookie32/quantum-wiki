The paper presents a mathematical framework for compiling hardware‑tailored logical Clifford circuits for any stabilizer code. By formulating the task as a discrete optimization that minimizes CZ gates while parameterizing all Clifford stabilizers, the authors achieve scalable solutions (≥ 16 physical qubits) and construct fault‑tolerant flag gadgets—e.g., low‑gate‑count Hadamard circuits on the [[8,3,2]] code. The work supplies both theory and software that replace standard generator decomposition with holistic circuit compilation, yielding significant practical savings.
