arXiv:2611.13934v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In this work, we present a comprehensive analysis of an open quantum system consisting of a collection of N two-level atoms in the thermodynamic limit coupled to bosonic reservoirs. Our results demonstrate that, when the system-bath interactions lacks a metastable minimum, as in the counterterm-free case, the system can undergo a quantum phase transition set by the reservoir structure rather than by coherent internal dynamics. Using exact quantum Langevin equations, valid even in the overdamped regime near criticality, where the loss rates can exceed the resonance frequency of the softening mode, we locate the critical point via the complex excitation spectrum and obtain closed-form reflection and transmission spectra that display the mode softening and an increasingly asymmetric lineshape near the transition.
