arXiv:2609.24490v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: A two-component Bose--Einstein condensate can host a pseudospin Hopf texture while its thermal cloud obeys viscous Navier--Stokes dynamics. Motivated by OpenAI's reported finite-time singular solution, we drive such a texture with a surrogate of the collapsing core, using an effective Madelung--Schrodinger theory with a Faxen-like advective correction. The texture undergoes fold-mediated creation and annihilation of preimage pairs at conserved Hopf charge with the first one-to-three transition confirmed to obey the square-root opening of a Thom A_2
