Raghuram et al. realize a one‑dimensional synthetic lattice by coupling rotational levels of ultracold RbCs molecules, mapping the system onto the Su–Schrieffer–Heeger model. Using microwave‑induced tunnelling and auxiliary‑state spectroscopy they achieve full site‑resolved readout for lattices up to eight sites and observe coherent dynamics over ∼500 tunnelling periods. These long coherence times enable a direct study of the robustness of topologically protected edge states
