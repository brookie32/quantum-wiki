The study introduces a new game based on complement sampling that maximally separates quantum from classical computation using only input and output samples from probability distributions. Restricting the instances to a Bernstein–Vazirani‑style family yields an unconditional, exponentially large violation of classicality without relying on any computational hardness assumptions. The protocol was experimentally validated on Quantinuum System Model H2 trapped‑ion quantum computers with up to 55 qubits, where thousands of distinct circuits produced results consistent with a quantum strategy.
