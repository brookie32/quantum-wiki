arXiv:2508.07500v4 Announce Type: replace Abstract: The optical cycle-averaged ionization rate of Ar, O_2, and N_2 vs. local instantaneous laser intensity I for linear polarized 800 nm light is determined up to approx. 300 TW/cm^2 by numerically inverting published time-of-flight ion spectrometer data. The published Ar^+ collection efficiency of the microchannel plate (MCP) at the end of the spectrometer and its I_0 scale are recalibrated by fitting it to its high I_0 solution. The relative collection efficiencies of the other species are determined by published MCP cathode data. Results for O_2 are consistent with a reevaluation of published data used to determine its cross section sigma _8 in the multiphoton (low I) regime.
