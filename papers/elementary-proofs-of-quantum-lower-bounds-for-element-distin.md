arXiv:2610.32693v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We give elementary proofs of the Omega(n^{2/3}) quantum query lower bound for element distinctness and the Omega(n^{k/(k+1)}) lower bound for the k-sum problem, for fixed k and sufficiently large groups. The proofs use only basic properties of quantum states and linear algebra, analyzing the differences of algorithm states on different inputs.
