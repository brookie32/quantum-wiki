arXiv:2610.12844v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We give a polynomial-time randomized classical algorithm that approximates the largest eigenvalue lambda_{max} of a traceless quartic fermionic Hamiltonian H on n modes. The algorithm achieves energy at least lambda_{max}/O(sqrt n) and outputs either a fermionic Gaussian state or a polynomially sparse state in the Fock basis. This is achieved through a new upper bound on lambda_{max} in terms of the maximum energy of a Gaussian state and sqrt{2^{-n}operatorname{Tr}(H^2)}.
