arXiv:2610.34948v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We demonstrate the ro-vibrational state-selective preparation of CO molecules (v = 0, J = 0 rightarrow v = 1, J = 1) in an internally cold molecular beam using a continuous-wave, narrow-linewidth distributed feedback type quantum cascade laser, with an output wavelength of 4.65 mum. Using a modulation-free wavelength stabilization scheme, we show that population transfer to near saturation levels can be obtained with sufficient long term stability, as needed for state-resolved scattering experiments. We show preliminary results of CO molecules prepared in the v = 1 state, scattering from a clean Cu(100) surface. This study showcases the promise of QCL based excitation for state-selected molecular beam based scattering experiments, especially involving transitions in the mid-infrared domain (lambda > 4.5 mum) which are otherwise difficult to access using conventional sources.
