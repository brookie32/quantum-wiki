In Nature Communications (24 Oct 2026), Vennema et al. demonstrate nuclear magnetic resonance on an individual surface atom using scanning‑tunneling microscopy. They resolve the hyperfine and quadrupole transitions of a single ⁴⁷Ti atom (I = 5/2) via an electron‑nuclear double‑resonance scheme, revealing how weak electron–nuclear coupling enables coherent control of the nuclear spin in a long‑lived quantum state. The work establishes atomic‑scale readout and manipulation techniques crucial for exploiting isolated nuclear spins as qubits.
