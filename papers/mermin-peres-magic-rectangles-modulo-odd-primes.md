arXiv:2609.20746v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The Mermin-Peres magic square provides a simple example of a system of linear equations over Z/2Z which has no classical solutions but does have a finite-dimensional operator solution. For a long time, it was not known how to construct similar examples over Z/dZ with d odd. In this paper, we construct, for every integer dge2, a linear system over Z/dZ that has a finite-dimensional operator solution but no classical solution. For an odd prime p, our operators act on two p-dimensional qudits and generate a finite p-group obtained by adjoining diagonal polynomial phase operators to the generalized Pauli group. Classical inconsistency follows from an elementary linearity argument comparing assignments on abelian subgroups.
