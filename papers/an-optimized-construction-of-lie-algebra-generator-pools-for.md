This study develops a polynomial‑time method to construct minimal Lie–algebra generator pools, avoiding the exponential growth of candidate operators that hampers traditional greedy approaches. Applied to adaptive variational quantum eigensolvers in quantum chemistry, the verified generator sets reduce required qubit and gate resources while improving convergence for strongly correlated molecules, enabling efficient simulations of larger systems than previously possible. The framework also offers potential benefits for broader quantum computing applications such as error correction, machine learning, and hardware control.
