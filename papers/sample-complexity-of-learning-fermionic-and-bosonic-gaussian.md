arXiv:2611.13428v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: With single-copy measurements, every mixed fermionic Gaussian state on m modes, pure modes included, is learned to trace distance epsilon from Theta(m^3/epsilon^2) copies, with no logarithmic factor, against the known Theta(m^2/epsilon^2) for collective measurements. We prove the upper bound, resolving Conjecture 10.1 of arXiv:2609.23189 (lower bound for epsilonle1/3600), and fill most open entries of the sample-complexity table of fermionic and bosonic Gaussian states, for single-copy, k-copy and collective measurements. The single-copy measurement's noise is matched to the metric at every occupation scale. With blocks of k copies, the covariant highest-weight measurement of arXiv:2611.07271, read out through the Weyl-shift estimator, is exactly unbiased for every state; it gives Theta(max{m^3/k,m^2}/epsilon^2) in Frobenius norm on all states and in trace distance on every class at a fixed distance from the pure boundary. The gain 1/k (kle m) away from the pure boundary, against 1/sqrt k on cold number-conserving states, is therefore attained. For bosonic Gaussian states with bounded covariance, any displacement and squeezing, and occupations n_1leotsle n_m, O((m^2+r^3+sum_{i,j>r}1/(n_i+n_j))/epsilon^2) single-copy measurements suffice, r the largest index with r,n_rle2; this is the minimax rate on dyadic-interval classes, with no spectral gap or cold-mode count assumed, and holds with no covariance bound for displaced passive states. With no bound on energy or squeezing, non-Gaussian measurements learn centred one-mode states, mixed included, from O(epsilon^{-2}) and centred pure m-mode states from O(m^2/epsilon^2) single copies, free of the loglog E term of Gaussian measurements, and m-mode states from O(m^3/epsilon^2) copies, entangled only in a first round on blocks of 4m+6.
