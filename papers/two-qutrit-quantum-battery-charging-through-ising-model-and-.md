arXiv:2611.22123v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In this paper, we propose a model of a two-qutrit quantum battery composed of two spin-1 systems coupled to an external field via anisotropic Heisenberg interactions. From the Ising model, we derive an exact analytical expression of ergotropy in the parallel-charging case. However, the collective charging process offers an advantage over the parallel one. Moreover, increasing the collective exchange coupling accelerates the evolution of ergotropy and enhances the maximum average power, as measured by global coherence and mutual information. Conversely, the Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya (DM) interaction primarily acts as a detrimental mechanism, essentially reducing all of these quantities. In the XZ model, the obtained results show that the DM interaction is constructive when it cooperates with the longitudinal anisotropy and when the exchange coupling is sufficiently strong. Therefore, by optimising the maximum reachable ergotropy and maximum average power, we conclude that the Ising model without DM interaction is sufficient to maximise the ergotropy. The anisotropic XZ model allows the DM interaction to be exploited constructively to improve the maximum average power through the joint control of the exchange coupling, longitudinal anisotropy, and chiral interaction.
