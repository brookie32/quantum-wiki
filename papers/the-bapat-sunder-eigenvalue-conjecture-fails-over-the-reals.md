arXiv:2611.23269v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In 1986 Bapat and Sunder asked whether operatorname{per}(A) is the largest eigenvalue of F_A=[a_{i,j}operatorname{per}(A(i,j))] for every positive semidefinite Hermitian A, where A(i,j) deletes row i and column j. Drury disproved this over the complex field in 2018 and conjectured that the property holds for real positive semidefinite matrices. We disprove Drury's conjecture with a rank-4, 32imes32 integer Gram matrix and an integer vector whose Rayleigh quotient exceeds operatorname{per}(A); verification uses integer arithmetic. Together, the two counterexamples resolve the Bapat-Sunder eigenvalue conjecture negatively over both fields. Counterexamples exist of every order nge32. As an application to quantum optics, the violation yields a 36-mode orthogonal interferometer and a direction of relative arrival-time delays among 32 photons with identical-variance Gaussian wave packets along which every sufficiently small nonzero delay increases the probability that all photons are detected within four designated output modes.
