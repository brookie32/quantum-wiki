arXiv:2608.12406v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Quantum-geometric speed and dynamical instability are two natural rates for a driven quantum system, and their relation is unsettled even for exactly solvable dynamics. Here we show that for any multimode quadratic bosonic system referred to the bare-mode vacuum the Fubini-Study speed v_FS is the Frobenius norm of the symmetric, stretching part of the flow. This yields a sharp bound, lambda_max <= sqrt(2) v_FS, saturated by a resonant pure squeezer. The bound is not invertible: in a detuned parametric amplifier we hold either rate fixed while varying the other.
