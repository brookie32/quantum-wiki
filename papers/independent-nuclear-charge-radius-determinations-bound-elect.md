arXiv:2611.09242v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We present an alternative method to determine absolute nuclear charge radii competitively via the bound electron g factor of H-like ions. The method is demonstrated for ^{118}Sn by extracting its radius with a precision of 0.1%. This is the second most precise radius ever determined from an electronic system. We benchmark our result against a state-of-the-art value derived from the spectrum of muonic ^{118}Sn. This comparison reveals a 3.2sigma tension, prompting a careful reexamination of the calculations and calling for additional measurements. Crucially, after decades, we now have two independent methods to measure absolute charge radii with similar precision.
