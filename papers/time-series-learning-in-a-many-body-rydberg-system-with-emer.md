**Summary:**  
In this 2026 Nature Communications study, researchers demonstrated that a laser‑driven vapor of interacting Rydberg atoms can serve as a physical reservoir for time‑series prediction by modulating the driving field’s Rabi frequency to encode input data. They observed that approaching a non‑equilibrium phase transition—where collective amplification of perturbations occurs—significantly enhances the system’s learning capacity, yielding higher accuracy in forecasting future values of the Lorenz–type time series used as a test case. The work highlights the potential of many‑body Rydberg systems for quantum‑enhanced metrology and sensing applications.
