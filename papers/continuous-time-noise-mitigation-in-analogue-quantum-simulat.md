This study presents a continuous‑time framework for mitigating noise in analogue quantum simulators without requiring any active control or measurement during their evolution. By coupling the simulator to a small set of ancillary qubits and performing post‑processing on joint measurements, the protocol achieves exact cancellation of environmental noise while remaining independent of the system Hamiltonian and resilient to realistic ancilla errors. The approach offers a high‑fidelity pathway for quantum many‑body simulations in noisy hardware.
