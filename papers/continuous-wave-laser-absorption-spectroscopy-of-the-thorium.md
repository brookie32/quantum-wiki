Authors demonstrate continuous‑wave excitation and absorption spectroscopy of the ultra‐low‑energy 7.8 eV nuclear transition in ^229Th doped into CaF₂ crystals using a sub‑nanowatt, narrow‑band solid–state laser at 148 nm. By detecting resonant absorption rather than the weak nuclear fluorescence, they eliminate the bottleneck imposed by the long nuclear decay lifetime and achieve rapid signal acquisition. This marks a key step toward a highly stable, fast‑readout solid‑state optical nuclear clock.
