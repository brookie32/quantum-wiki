arXiv:2611.31878v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Momentum-space lattices offer a versatile platform for quantum simulation in particular emulating topological states of matter.In this work, we propose a spectroscopic protocol via utilizing the dynamics of the chiral operator to detect the topological properties of a momentum-space Su-Schrieffer-Heeger chain. We show that the time evolution of the chiral density is directly linked to the local density of state (LDOS). By performing a Fourier transformation of that, we reconstruct both the LDOS and density of states of the system, enabling the unambiguous signature of zero-energy edge modes and the bulk-boundary correspondence. This dynamical approach provides a diagnostic tool for exploring topological phases in synthetic quantum matter.
