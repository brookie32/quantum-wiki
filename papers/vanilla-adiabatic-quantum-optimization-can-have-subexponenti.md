arXiv:2611.24035v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We construct a family of objective functions f olon {0,1}^n o R for which vanilla adiabatic quantum optimization along H(s) = -(1-s)sum_{i=1}^n X_i + ssum_x f(x)lvert xrangle!langle xrvert finds the global minimizer in operatorname{poly}(n) time, whereas any classical algorithm requires exp(n^{Omega(1)}) time to do so in the black-box model. Our result is achieved by replacing the linearization technique in an earlier work of Leng et al. (arXiv:2504.14841), which establishes a similar subexponential quantum speedup using a transverse field Ising mixer rather than -sum_{i=1}^n X_i.
