In this work the authors realize an analogue simulation of a massive relativistic quantum field—the sine‑Gordon model—in two spatial dimensions using coherently coupled spin components in a uniform Bose–Einstein condensate. They measure the long‑wavelength collective excitations, finding a tunable mass gap and a relativistic dispersion that agree with theoretical predictions. In the non‑perturbative regime they observe topological domain walls where the relative phase winds by \(2\pi\), demonstrating the platform’s suitability for studying cosmology‑relevant processes such as preheating and defect dynamics.
