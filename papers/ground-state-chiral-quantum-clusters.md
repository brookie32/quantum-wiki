arXiv:2611.31370v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Quantum time crystals are states of matter that spontaneously break time translation symmetry, extending the notion of crystalline order to time. While their out-of-equilibrium version has been successfully realized by periodic driving, the equilibrium counterpart remains elusive. Here we introduce "ground-state chiral quantum clusters": self-bound many-body ground states supporting a persistent current. We provide a minimal field theory describing these states, and an exact microscopic realization via the attractive anyonic Lieb-Liniger model on a ring. In the latter, the ground-state density-density correlator exhibits the Talbot effect, with a soliton-like profile that periodically disperses and revives while drifting indefinitely along the ring, realizing persistent motion at equilibrium. Possible experimental probes are discussed.
