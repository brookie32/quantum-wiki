arXiv:2611.18037v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We present a comparative analysis of three quantum versions of the kinetic uncertainty relation (KUR), which incorporate coherence-dependent corrections to the classical bound. Using a three-level maser heat engine as a paradigmatic quantum thermal machine, we identify the operating regimes in which each formulation provides the tightest constraint on current fluctuations. We further identify tighter, model-specific bounds for two of these formulations, provide numerical support for both, and establish one of them analytically. Introducing ground-state degeneracy, we then demonstrate that noise-induced coherence provides a tunable resource for relocating, enhancing, or suppressing violations of the standard KUR without requiring an additional coherent drive. In particular, it enables violations in regimes where the three-level engine satisfies the classical bound. Our results clarify how distinct quantum KURs account for coherence and constrain the precision of quantum thermal machines.
