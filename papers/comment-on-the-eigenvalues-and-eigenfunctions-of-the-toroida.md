arXiv:2611.23006v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We show here that the spectrum of the operator hat{T}_3^{(heta)} studied by Anghel and Dolineanu (Phys. Scr. {f 98} (2023) 015223) is continuous, not discrete, contrary to what is claimed by the authors of the paper to which this comment refers. Consequently, there is no splitting of the energy levels corresponding to this operator that could be observed experimentally.
