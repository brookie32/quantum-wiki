arXiv:2608.24502v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The preparation of ground states of Hamiltonians with symmetries that are fundamentally different than those of the underlying device is a central challenge for quantum technologies. Here, we address this challenge with an analog protocol for the iconic generalized cluster Hamiltonian with the translational symmetry resultant from periodic boundary conditions based on a magnetization-preserving interaction on a linear geometry with open boundary conditions. Our results show that this goal can be achieved in a control duration that scales only moderately with the number of spins and Hamiltonian interaction complexity.
