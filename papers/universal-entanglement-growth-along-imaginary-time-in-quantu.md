Two‐dimensional quantum critical points show a universal logarithmic growth of corner contributions to the entanglement entropy along imaginary time, with the prefactor determined solely by the universality class. Large‑scale quantum Monte Carlo simulations confirm this scaling for the interacting Gross–Neveu–Yukawa critical point and uncover significant interaction‑driven deviations from free‑fermion predictions. By extracting high‑precision universal coefficients from the early stages of relaxation, this approach bypasses the computational bottlenecks associated with full equilibrium convergence.
