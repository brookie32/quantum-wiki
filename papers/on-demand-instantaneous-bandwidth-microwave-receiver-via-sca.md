Researchers fabricated a scalable Rydberg‑atom microwave receiver that uses a Stark‑field comb to spatially multiplex an array of vapor cells, giving each cell the role of an independent frequency‑selective heterodyne sensor. The prototype operated over a 210 MHz instantaneous band while retaining a sensitivity of 326.6 nV cm⁻¹ Hz⁻¹/² even when the carrier resonance was tuned by 160 MHz. Full simultaneous reception across the bandwidth requires parallel readout of all channels, preserving both high bandwidth and high sensitivity.
