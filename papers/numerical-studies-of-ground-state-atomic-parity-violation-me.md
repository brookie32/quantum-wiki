arXiv:2611.33610v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We report results of numerical simulations of an ongoing measurement designed to measure the electric dipole (E1) transition moment between hyperfine components of the ground state of atomic cesium. This E1 moment is non-zero solely due to nuclear weak interactions between the nucleons, leading to a nuclear anapole moment of the cesium nucleus. Through these simulations, we explore the effect on the microwave spectrum of a static transverse magnetic field, a spatial offset of the atomic beam from the axis of the radio-frequency (rf) cavity, the angular tilt of the rf cavity, and the ac Stark shift by the Raman-coupling beams used to generate the initial superposition state between ground state components. The results of these simulations help place limits on the experimental parameters, and help guide the future experimental procedures.
