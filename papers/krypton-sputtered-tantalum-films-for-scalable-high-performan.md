Krypton‐sputtered tantalum films enable crystalline bcc Ta on silicon at temperatures as low as ~200 °C—substantially lower than the >400 °C required for argon deposition—and fit within standard back‑end‑of‑line processes.  These low‑temperature films exhibit uniformly high‑quality coplanar waveguide resonators, and when incorporated into compact (20 µm capacitor gap) transmon qubits they achieve quality factors up to 16.9 million; higher‑temperature growth increases microwave losses due to enhanced tantalum–silicon intermixing.
