arXiv:2610.35797v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We describe a family of convex continuous functions E_{F,arepsilon}, arepsilonin(0,1], on the set of states of a bipartite quantum system (consisting of finite-dimensional or infinite-dimensional subsystems) obtained by applying the Mora-Piani-Briegel construction of arepsilon-measure (proposed in [Phys. Rev. A 109, 052413]) to the Entanglement of Formation (EoF) with the use of the trace norm distance in the role of basic metric. These functions are non-increasing in arepsilon and converge pointwise to the EoF as arepsilono0^+. We conducted a detailed analysis of properties of the functions E_{F,arepsilon}, using (among others) the relationship between the family {E_{F,arepsilon}}_{arepsilonin[0,1]} and the family {E^lambda_F(rho)}_{lambda>0} of Moreau-Yosida approximations of the EoF explored in [arXiv:2609.30246]. We obtain a variational expression for the functions E_{F,arepsilon} (which for arepsilon=0 turns into the standard variational expression for the EoF). We also describe the weak subadditivity property of the functions E_{F,arepsilon} and analyse their continuity properties. The semicontinuity bounds for the EoF (presented in [Lob.J.Math., 46, 2632-2658],[arXiv:2404.07978]) allow us to obtain easily computable upper bounds on the rate of uniform convergence of the function E_{F,arepsilon} to the EoF as arepsilono0^+ on the sets of states with bounded rank/energy of one of the marginal states. Applicability of the obtained results to other entanglement measures, in particular, to the relative entropy of entanglement, is discussed. As an auxiliary result, it is shown that for any state of an infinite-dimensional bipartite quantum system, there exists a nearest separable state w.r.t. the trace norm distance. The non-uniqueness of such a state is also shown.
