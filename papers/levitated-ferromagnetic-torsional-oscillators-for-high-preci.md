This research demonstrates a levitated ferromagnetic torsion oscillator operating as a compact room-temperature magnetometer with a sensor volume of (2.5 mm)³ and magnetic sensitivity of 391 ± 59 fT·Hz⁻¹/².   The device's compact sensing volume and magnetic sensitivity make it suitable for probing short-range spin-dependent interactions through pseudomagnetic-field detection, establishing a compact approach to precision magnetometry and providing a basis for future searches for exotic interactions.
