arXiv:2611.08518v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In Heo and Kiem (2019), the authors raised a problem relating integral zeros of binary Krawtchouk polynomials, parametrized by quadruples (m,n,k,ell) with mle n and kle ell, to the existence of pairs of subspaces satisfying the range criterion in quantum information theory, and settled the symmetric case k=ell. In this paper, we solve the problem for the following three asymmetric families: (m,n,k,ell)=(3,t^2-1,t(t-1)/2,t(t+1)/2), tge2; (m,n,k,ell)=(4,3t^2+2t-1,(3t^2-t)/2,(3t^2+5t+2)/2), tge2; (m,n,k,ell)=(4,3s^2+4s,(3s^2+s)/2,(3s^2+7s+4)/2), sge1. The remaining cases are sporadic zeros, with only finitely many for each mge5.
