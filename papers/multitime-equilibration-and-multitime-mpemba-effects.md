arXiv:2611.17749v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Equilibration of a quantum state leaves open whether its response to later interventions has also equilibrated. We establish multitime equilibration for a continuous process on a fixed observation window, retaining the autonomous system--environment dynamics and all internal time differences. A finite-time mean-square bound controls the full continuous-process norm through the effective energy dimension and energy-gap correlations. A factorial temporal-order bound lifts fixed-sector convergence to the full process, while a process-visible spectral measure distinguishes mean-square from pointwise equilibration. This framework defines multitime Mpemba effects by comparing preparations relative to one stationary process. In a semicircle-spectrum model, a preparation filter changes the decay exponent from 3/2 to 5/2 in every fixed sector and in the summed norm, despite a larger initial distance. A fixed unitary control and terminal measurement exhibit the same exponent improvement in their output probabilities. Noncommuting baths and a many-body spin environment separate this all-sector mechanism from selective finite-time acceleration.
