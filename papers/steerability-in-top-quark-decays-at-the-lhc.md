arXiv:2611.23368v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Quantum information observables in top quark pair production have opened a new experimental window on non-classical correlations of particle interactions at colliders. Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen (EPR) steering occupies a distinguished place in the quantum hierarchy, lying between entanglement and Bell nonlocality. We present a detailed collider analysis of steerability for top quark pairs at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC). In particular, we utilize both the decay and kinematic methods to estimate the expected sensitivity of the LHC and High Luminosity LHC (HL-LHC) to detect steerable states. We find that with the current LHC dataset of 139~fb^{-1} the steerability of the tar t state in the boosted region can be established at 7.7sigma (1.8sigma) in the semileptonic (dileptonic) channel with the decay method, corresponding to a measurement of the steerability integral at a precision of 1.5% (5.2%), while the kinematic method reaches a precision of 0.05% in both channels. At the HL-LHC the decay-method precision improves to 0.3% (1.1%). Near threshold, where the inclusive selections used for entanglement and discord yield a state at the steerability boundary, an optimized selection M_{tar{t}}lesssim360--400~GeV isolates a steerable singlet-dominated state, where the sensitivity is projected to reach 7sigma (3.4sigma). We also provide a detailed discussion on the interpretation of these results with respect to fictitious quantum states, and argue for the robust nature of the steerability measurement at colliders.
