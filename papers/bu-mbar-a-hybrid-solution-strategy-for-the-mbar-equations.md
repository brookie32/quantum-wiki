arXiv:2609.03541v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: The multi-state Bennett acceptance ratio (MBAR) equations combine the data collected under different thermodynamic conditions in a statistically optimal way. Due to their practical importance, several solution strategies have been devised to optimize the convergence of the resulting set of coupled equations. However, even with graphics processing unit (GPU) acceleration, the convergence of these methods can still be either unstable or slow. We propose a new approach where the equations are interpreted as the limit of infinitesimal bin width of the respective weighted histogram analysis method (WHAM). In the proposed binned-to-unbinned MBAR (BU-MBAR) method, the bin width is adapted dynamically to ensure a stable and efficient convergence to the asymptotic solution.
