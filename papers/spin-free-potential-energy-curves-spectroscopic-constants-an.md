arXiv:2611.22544v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We report ab initio potential-energy curves and permanent dipole-moment functions for all 18 spin-free adiabatic electronic states of ^{12}C^{16}O correlating with the lowest dissociation limit, C(^3P)+O(^3P). Our state-averaged CASSCF/icMRCI(+Q) calculations with the aug-cc-pV5Z basis cover the complete set of singlet, triplet, and quintet manifolds. For the ground state, we examine basis-set convergence using the aug-cc-pVnZ series (n=T,Q,5,6) and extrapolate to the complete-basis-set limit. The resulting potential-energy curves reproduce the established topology of the low-lying CO states, including the double-minimum structure of the 2^{1}Sigma^{+} state and pronounced adiabatic mixing in several excited states. We determine rovibrational levels and spectroscopic constants for bound states and selected local minima, assess uncertainties, and compare our results with experiment and previous calculations. We find good agreement with experiment for the ground state and well-characterized low-lying singlet and triplet states. For the 1^1Sigma^+ ground state, R_e=1.131~r{A} and omega_e=2161.04~cm^{-1} are both within 0.5% of experiment. The dissociation energy is D_0=89473pm141~cm^{-1}. The most strongly bound quintet, 1^5Pi, has D_e=6401~cm^{-1} and omega_e=982~cm^{-1}. The 1^5Sigma^+ state has a shallow long-range well with D_e=849cm^{-1}, while 2^5Sigma^+, 2^5Pi, and 1^5Delta are predominantly repulsive. Variations in the permanent dipole moments with bond length indicate changes in electronic character and charge redistribution, particularly where adiabatic states interact strongly. These data provide a basis for interpreting rovibrational spectra and for future studies of nonadiabatic effects, spin--orbit coupling, and collision dynamics.
