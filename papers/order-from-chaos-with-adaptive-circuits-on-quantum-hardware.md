In 2026 Pokharel et al. demonstrated adaptive monitored quantum circuits that use local mid‑circuit measurements and resets to apply conditional feedback, thereby steering the evolution of a chaotic quantum system towards a fixed point. The experiment, performed on an IBM superconducting processor with up to 100 qubits, simulated a quantum Bernoulli map while implementing ~5 000 entangling gates and ~5 000 non‑unitary operations. The resulting competition between unitary scrambling and measurement‑induced steering produced an experimentally observed dynamical phase transition between quantum and classical behavior, which the authors characterized through noisy simulations, matrix‑product‑state analysis, and mappings to statistical‑mechanics models.
