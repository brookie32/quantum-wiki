**Optimal quantum channel tomography in diamond distance (Mele et al., 2026)**  
The authors establish the precise query complexity required to learn an unknown quantum channel to a fixed constant accuracy measured by the diamond distance, showing that both upper and lower bounds have matching dependence on the input/output Hilbert space dimensions and the Kraus rank. Their optimal algorithm performs tomography on the Choi-state representation of the process, reducing channel learning to the well‑studied problem of pure‑state tomography. Consequently, the framework simultaneously gives optimal query limits for states, unitaries, and measurements as special cases of quantum channels.
