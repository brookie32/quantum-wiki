arXiv:2608.28719v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We study the nonequilibrium response of a one-dimensional Bose-Hubbard medium to a two-magnon bound state propagating along an attractive XXZ chain. A Holstein-type displacement coupling makes the magnon density act both as a local chemical-potential perturbation and as a source of bosons. We derive a long-wavelength hydrodynamic description of the density and phase fluctuations and test it against matrix-product-state simulations of the full coupled dynamics. The theory predicts a comoving near-field deformation together with retarded density waves confined to a sound cone. In the Mott regime, the deformation remains localized around the moving pair. In the compressible regime, two counterpropagating fronts detach and approach the semiclassical sound velocity, recovered from the equilibrium density with no fitted parameters. The coupling also induces a dynamical backreaction that slows the bound state and broadens its magnon-density profile. The comparison delimits where hydrodynamics stays quantitative once the probe reacts back on the medium.
