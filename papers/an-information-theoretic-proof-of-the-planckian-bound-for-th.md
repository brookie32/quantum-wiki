Paolo Abiuso et al. provide a model‑independent, information‑theoretic proof that quantum thermalization cannot occur faster than the Planckian timescale \( \tau_{\mathrm{Pl}}=\hbar/(k_{B}T) \). By treating equilibration as estimation of distinct Hamiltonians and applying quantum metrology bounds, they show a universal lower bound
\[
\tau \;\ge\; \tfrac12\,\tau_{\mathrm{Pl}}\quad(\text{finite }T),
\]
which reduces to a gap‑dependent limit \( \tau \gtrsim \hbar/\Delta \) in the low‑temperature regime. These results establish fundamental, parameter‑free limits on how quickly quantum systems can thermalize.
