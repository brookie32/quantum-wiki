A hybrid nanophotonic trap that substitutes the conventional red‑detuned laser attraction with Casimir–Polder and electrostatic atom–surface forces has been demonstrated, achieving record‑long storage and coherence times for cold atoms coupled to a nanofibre surface. This design reduces decoherence from optical fields while maintaining strong confinement, improving performance for quantum memory and sensing applications. The results indicate that purely surface‑based trapping can outperform traditional laser‑driven traps in terms of atom lifetime and stability.
