arXiv:2611.23965v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Three-centre two-electron Coulomb and hybrid integrals over exponential-type functions involve semi-infinite radial integrals containing oscillatory spherical Bessel functions. We investigate the combination of the S transformation with Fourier-type double-exponential (DE) quadrature for their accurate and efficient numerical evaluation. The S transformation transfers derivatives from the spherical-Bessel factor to the radial amplitude, converting the integral into a sine representation particularly well suited to Fourier-type DE quadrature. Numerical comparisons demonstrate that the combined S+DE approach can substantially reduce the number of quadrature nodes required to achieve high accuracy compared with direct DE. We further identify a geometry-dependent oscillation-to-damping ratio and establish a convergent moment expansion in the weakly oscillatory regime. Exploiting this complementary representation markedly enhances the numerical reliability of the algorithm in regimes where direct oscillatory quadrature becomes inefficient. The method is examined through semi-infinite radial integrals and complete H_2O and C_2H_4 molecular integrals over Slater-type functions, with comparisons against complete S-series evaluations and values reported in the literature.
