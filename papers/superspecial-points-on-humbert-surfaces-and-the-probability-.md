Let pequiv3pmod4 be a prime and let Ngeq2 be an integer. We determine, to first order in 1/p and with an explicit error bound, the proportion of superspecial principally polarized abelian surfaces over overline{F}_p that are optimally (N,N)-split. The surfaces are weighted by the reciprocals of their automorphism orders, which is the stationary measure of the Richelot random walk. The main theorem states that for pnmid N the expected number of optimal (N,N)-splittings of a surface is exactly 5NJ_2(N)(p-1)/(p^2+1), where J_2 is Jordan's totient; that for p>2N^2 the second factorial moment is c_N/(p^2+1) with c_N independent of p; and consequently that, for fixed N, the proportion of split surfaces is 5NJ_2(N)/p+O_N(p^{-2}). The constants c_N are determined explicitly for 2leq Nleq7. This proportion is the stationary target density of the splitting-detection attack on the superspecial isogeny problem in dimension two. It was previously known only from experiments or from a one-sided bound. The method is to show that the superspecial surfaces sample the genus of their refined Humbert invariants with Siegel's weights, which makes Siegel's theorem on genus averages available. The same method gives the first moment of primitive representations of every discriminant prime to p, mixed moments for several levels, an exact probability at level 2, and a proof that the geometric upper bound of earlier work has the correct leading term. All results are proved; the enumerations enter only through the values of c_N, and every formula was additionally confirmed by exact computation at twelve primes.
