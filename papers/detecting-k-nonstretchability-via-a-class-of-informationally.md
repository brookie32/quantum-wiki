arXiv:2508.12817v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Characterizing multipartite entanglement is a fundamental problem in quantum information theory. The concept of k-stretchability provides a framework for characterizing the structure of multipartite entanglement. We investigate k-nonstretchability using informationally complete (s,t)-positive operator-valued measures ((s,t)-POVMs) and derive two families of criteria. These criteria identify classes of k-nonstretchable states, and we demonstrate their applicability and advantages through explicit examples.
