arXiv:2610.13622v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Exceptional points (EPs) are usually introduced as defective degeneracies of non-Hermitian Hamiltonians and, in fully open quantum dynamics, of Liouvillian generators. Here we formulate an operational theory of exceptional points that does not invoke a Hamiltonian or a master equation. The primitive dynamical object is a completely positive trace-preserving (CPTP) quantum channel Phi, applied an integer number n of times, while physical data are probabilities p_n^{mathbf A}(x|rho) associated with states rho and observables mathbf A having measurements results x. We define exceptional channels through nontrivial Jordan chains of Phi and show that a Jordan block of order m generates discrete polynomial-exponential terms with maximal degree m-1. This places the physics of exceptional points in contact with what can be directly measured in experiments.
