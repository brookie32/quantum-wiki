arXiv:2611.09549v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In analogy to the Kramers-Henneberger transformation in non-relativistic quantum mechanics, we propose and investigate a spinor transformation for the Dirac equation which allows us to transform space-time dependent vector potentials A(t,x)oldsymbol{e}_x away. In the mass-less case, we exactly arrive at the free Dirac equation, while for massive Dirac fields, the transformation results in a non-trivial mass matrix containing space-time dependent phases. Special emphasis is placed on the phenomenon of fermion pair creation in the external field A(t,x)oldsymbol{e}_x where the spinor transformation allows us to conceptually disentangle the different contributions to pair creation. It can also be used to reformulate the WKB approach and the Riccati equation (which can be advantageous in certain cases) and to obtain additional insight into the mechanism of dynamically assisted pair creation.
