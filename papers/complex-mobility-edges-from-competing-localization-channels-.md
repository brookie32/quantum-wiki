arXiv:2611.22336v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Non-Hermitian localization transitions often accompany multiple mobility edges, but their physical origin can be ambiguous. To address the issue, we introduce an extension of the Ganeshan-Pixley-Das Sarma model with both asymmetric hopping and complex onsite potential, thereby incorporating a broad range of non-Hermitian quasiperiodic chains. Its solution reveals two energy-dependent localization contributions manifested as distinct Lyapunov growth scales, whose competition determines the structure of mobility edges. We derive exact Lyapunov exponents via Avila's global theory, obtain thresholds of the two channels on complex-energy plane, and formulate a selection rule that distinguishes physical mobility edges from suppressed analytical candidates. The channel that uniquely controls localization can switch with energy, producing complex mobility edges of non-Hermitian origins. Nonreciprocal hopping further shifts the thresholds and alters the bulk energy patterns. Numerical spectra and finite-size localization diagnostics confirm the theory. Our work thus provides a unified framework for characterizing phase transitions and mobility edges under competing localization mechanisms in non-Hermitian quasicrystals.
