arXiv:2608.11523v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: The spontaneous emission factor - often known as the eta} factor - is an important quantity in the description of quantum well lasers, influencing both the threshold power as well as the general shape of the light in-light out (L-L) curve. Past work on modelling multiple quantum well (MQW) nanowire laser devices has typically assumed that the eta} factor is a constant parameter that can either be estimated or fit in a post-hoc manner. However, the eta} factor can be derived from the transitions between valence and conduction bands in semiconductor quantum wells, together with knowledge of the cavity modes. Here we investigate the dynamic nature of the eta} factor for MQW nanowire lasers, and show how it can be computed. We also examine the dependence of the spontaneous emission rate and spontaneous emission factor eta} on the charge carrier density, quantum well thickness, and composition, and discuss the impact on laser threshold and operation.
