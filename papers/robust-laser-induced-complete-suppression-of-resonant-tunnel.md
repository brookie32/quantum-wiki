Researchers show that a laser field can generate an exceptional point—a non‑Hermitian degeneracy—in a double‑barrier structure modelling a resonant‑tunnelling diode, thereby completely extinguishing the resonance peak in electron transmission. They derive an analytic expression for this quantum‑exceptional‑point condition and confirm through numerical simulations that it is robust across a wide range of laser frequencies and intensities. The results furnish design guidelines for dynamically controlling tunneling currents, pointing toward fast, tunable quantum switches based on resonant‑tunnelling technology.
