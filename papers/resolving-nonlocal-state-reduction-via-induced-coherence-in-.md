arXiv:2610.27602v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: How macroscopic definite outcomes and nonlocal correlations emerge from entangled superpositions across spacelike-separated regions without superluminal action or {it ad hoc} wavefunction collapse remains a defining open question in the foundations of quantum mechanics. Here, we resolve this century-long paradox within extit{linear} relativistic quantum electrodynamics by developing an open-system and measurement-defined framework realized in dual, spacelike-separated nonlinear crystals driven synchronously by a multi-mode optical frequency comb and seeded by an ultra-narrow coherent laser. Expanding the transverse electromagnetic vector potential into discrete single-photon frequency-comb supermodes, we show that tracing out the unmeasured continuous-wave idler field yields an exact spatial dephasing Lindblad master equation whose decoherence rate is governed by the non-orthogonal state overlap of single-photon-added coherent states. Connecting this open-system map to free-vector-space quotient measurement theory, we demonstrate that single-particle interference arises from the geometric projection of the composite state onto detector-defined conditional cosets rather than remote collapse, establishing strict operational equivalence across pump, seed, and signal phase scans. The resulting wave-particle-entanglement triality equality P^2 + E^2 + V^2 = 1 is saturated across the complete parameter space, while the atomic-clock-referenced comb structure anchors continuous-variable time-frequency displacement stabilizers directly at the hardware layer. Our work unifies induced coherence, Bohr's complementarity, and quotient measurement theory, providing a microscopic pathway toward fault-tolerant bosonic quantum information processing.
