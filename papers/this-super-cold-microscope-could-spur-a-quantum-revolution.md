GAIA is a scanning‑transmission electron microscope fitted with a liquid‑helium cooling system that keeps samples at temperatures close to absolute zero for extended periods. The extreme cold suppresses atomic thermal motion, producing sharper images and enabling the observation of emergent quantum behaviors in materials. It will soon be shipped to a handful of research labs where scientists expect it to accelerate investigations into quantum phenomena.
