arXiv:2610.36007v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In this paper, we show that the optical depths of any OH Lambda-doublet quartet obey one exact linear relation in every velocity channel. No approximation is needed. The only exception is the ^{2}Pi_{1/2} J{=}1/2 level, which has three transitions only, because its F{=}0{leftrightarrow}0 line is strictly forbidden. The relation is au_{S_1}(v)/mu_{S_1}^{2}+au_{S_2}(v)/mu_{S_2}^{2}=au_{M_1}(v)/(amu_{M_1}^{2})+au_{M_2}(v)/(bmu_{M_2}^{2}), where a=2J^{2}+J-1, b=2J^{2}+3J, and the mu_i^{2} are reduced transition dipole moments derived from the catalogued Einstein A coefficients. It holds equally for gas in local thermodynamic equilibrium (LTE), for non-LTE gas, for masers and for conjugate gas. We suggest that it is a powerful tool for measuring the variation of fundamental physical constants.
