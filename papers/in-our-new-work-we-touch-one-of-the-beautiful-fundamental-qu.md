In our new work, we touch one of the beautiful fundamental questions at the heart of quantum physics: when does classical behavior emerge from quantum systems? We study this question through the lens of learning bosonic Gaussian states, and find a precise quantum-to-classical crossover in their learnability as thermal fluctuations increase. Cold Gaussian states are intrinsically quantum to learn, while sufficiently warm ones can be learned with the same sample complexity as classical Gaussian distributions, using operations that are just as simple. Thanks a lot to my collaborators @CSenrui, @FrancescoMeleAn, and @preskill for all their great work! I'm also grateful for my visit to Caltech in March, where I had the nice opportunity to brainstorm this project in person with them! ArXiv link: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.26705
