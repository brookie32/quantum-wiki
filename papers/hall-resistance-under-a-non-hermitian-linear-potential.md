arXiv:2502.14579v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We investigate how a non-Hermitian linear perturbation modifies Landau states and Hall transport, combining an exact continuum solution with numerical tight-binding calculations on a finite square lattice. The analytical solution yields energy branches with a complex linear Stark shift. A conserved non-Hermitian guiding-center operator identifies coherent states and determines the orbit center through its eigenvalue. The continuum model admits states with balanced gain and loss whose energies remain real at any perturbation strength. Finite-lattice calculations support the Landau-level structure and exhibit eigenvalue coalescences followed by the emergence of imaginary parts, consistent with exceptional points. Finally, transport calculations at a fixed injection energy show a shift of the Hall resistance plateaus moving them linearly from the von Klitzing constant.
