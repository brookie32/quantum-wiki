arXiv:2611.24354v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Supersolids on curved surfaces are becoming experimentally accessible. We develop a Galilean-covariant effective field theory of supersolids on curved surfaces and show that gradients of Gaussian curvature couple lattice shear to compression with a strength fixed by the shear modulus and the curvature gradient. As a result, shear drives superflow. On spheroids this yields geometric selection rules and multiplet splittings; near mode crossings, weak deformations produce hybrids in which superfluid and lattice carry comparable kinetic energy, and shear becomes visible as a density response.
