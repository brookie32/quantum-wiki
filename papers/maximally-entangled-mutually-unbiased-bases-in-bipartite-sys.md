arXiv:2611.18232v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Mutually unbiased bases with prescribed entanglement properties are of fundamental interest in quantum information theory. In the interesting case of a bipartite Hilbert space of unequal prime dimensions, H_potimesH_q, with p<q prime, the standard solution provides p+1 product bases. For any such dimension d=pq we construct p+1 bases, such that p of them are represented by complex Hadamard matrices, each equivalent to a Fourier matrix F_d, with all vectors maximally entangled. Our explicit construction is based on eigenvectors of suitable Weyl--Heisenberg operators, the Chinese Remainder Theorem and quadratic Gaussian sums. The obtained result establishes a lower bound for the number of maximally entangled mutually unbiased bases, M(p,q)geq p.
