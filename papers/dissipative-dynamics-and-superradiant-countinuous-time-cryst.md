arXiv:2604.18119v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: The interplay between many-body interactions and controlled dissipation provides a rich setting for nonequilibrium quantum phases. We explore the superradiant phase transitions in a Rydberg-interacting open Dicke model with cavity loss and local dissipation. We find that the nonlinear feedback from atomic interactions stabilizes the atomic collective excited state and induces resonant collective motion, which can be further converted into robust superradiant oscillations with the inclusion of local dissipation. Beyond the mean-field approximation, we prove this system can support the finite-size local dissipative temporal order, i.e., continuous time crystal, from evidences of the decreasing damping of a finite-frequency Liouvillian mode and the linear-size scaling of quantum fluctuations. The correlation between the atomic colletive dynamics and the cavity photon number makes our findings easy to detect from output optical signal. By bridging interacting atomic matter and driven-dissipative cavity QED, our Rydberg-interacting Dicke model provides a unique spin-1/2 platform for exploring dynamical phase transitions and macroscopic temporal order in open quantum matter.
