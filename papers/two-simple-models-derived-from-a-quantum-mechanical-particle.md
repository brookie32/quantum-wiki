arXiv:2512.09905v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We analyze two simple models derived from a quantum-mechanical particle on an elliptical path. The first Hamiltonian operator is non-Hermitian but equivalent to an Hermitian operator. It exhibits the same two-fold degeneracy as the particle on a circular path. More precisely, the spectrum is E_{n}=n^{2}E_{1}, n=0,pm 1,pm 2,ldots , E_{1}>0. The second Hamiltonian operator is Hermitian and does not exhibit such degeneracy. In this case the nth excited energy level splits at the nth order of perturbation theory. Both models can be described in terms of symmetry point groups with one-dimensional irreducible representations but the non-Hermitian example exhibits an additional symmetry that explains the two-fold degeneracy. The Schrodinger equation for the first example is exactly solvable.
