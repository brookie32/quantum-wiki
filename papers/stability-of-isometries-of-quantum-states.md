arXiv:2609.37133v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We prove dimension-independent Hyers-Ulam stability for surjective approximate isometries of the positive trace-class cone in both the trace and Bures metrics. Here arepsilongeq0 is a uniform upper bound on the additive error in preserving distances. Such an approximate isometry is uniformly approximated by a Wigner symmetry (a unitary or antiunitary conjugation), with error at most 9arepsilon/2 in the trace metric and 2sqrt2arepsilon in the Bures metric, even if it does not fix zero. For maps fixing zero, the trace bound improves to 3arepsilon even under the hypothesis of elta-surjectivity, independently of elta. In infinite dimensions, we construct bijections of the full state space, including mixed states, whose distortions tend to zero in both metrics but which stay a fixed uniform distance from every Wigner symmetry. For state spaces, stability holds in each fixed finite dimension with a dimension-dependent modulus tending to zero as arepsilono0, but no such modulus can be chosen independently of dimension.
