arXiv:2610.27338v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Given an open quantum system S with environment E, we establish a one-to-one correspondence between completely positive trace-preserving (CPTP) maps from S to E and assignment maps initializing the composite system SE in a possibly correlated state prior to unitary evolution. We show that every possible joint state rho_{SE} can be realized by such assignment maps, which are inherently Hermiticity-preserving and trace-preserving (HPTP). While these maps sacrifice global positivity, we derive a simple necessary and sufficient condition for any local state of S to belong to their domain of validity. Furthermore, we prove that the geometry of this domain is governed by the joint state assigned to the maximally mixed input, ensuring that a non-zero volume of admissible local states can readily coexist with initial system-environment entanglement. Finally, we provide an operational protocol for witnessing such limits of validity.
