arXiv:2610.01940v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Given oracle access to an unknown unitary U=e^{iH} , the fractional query problem asks how many queries are required to implement a noninteger power U^t=e^{itH}, 0<t<1, when the spectrum is separated from the branch cut by a gap elta. Quantum singular value transformation gives an upper bound of O!left(frac{1}{elta}logfrac{1}{arepsilon}right) queries for approximation error arepsilon. We prove a matching lower bound for arbitrary query algorithms. Our argument reduces any N-query circuit to the approximation of e^{itheta} by a trigonometric polynomial with degree bounded by O(N), together with Remez inequality. This allows us to establish the lower bound of Omega_au!left(frac{1}{elta}logfrac{1}{arepsilon}right). Consequently, the optimal query complexity for fractional query problem is Theta_{au}!left(frac{1}{elta}logfrac{1}{arepsilon}right), showing that the known QSVT construction is asymptotically optimal. We also give an alternative lower bound proof based on constructing a linear functional that annihilates the approximant space, yielding a Omega_{au}!left(logfrac{1}{arepsilon}right) bound uniform to elta.
