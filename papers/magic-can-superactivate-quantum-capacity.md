arXiv:2611.09594v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Environmental magic can enable two channels with zero quantum capacity to transmit quantum information jointly. We establish this superactivation within a family of discrete quantum convolution channels with fixed Clifford interactions. For the couplings considered, stabilizer-mixture environments induce entanglement-breaking channels. We construct a mixed magic environment whose induced channel has positive private capacity and a Choi state with positive partial transpose, hence zero quantum capacity. A half-erasure auxiliary activates this channel through the Smith--Yard mechanism. Finite-block encoding and decoding over a self-complementary convolution auxiliary then realize a higher-dimensional erasure channel that still permits activation. We also bound the activated capacity by the modified relative entropy of magic of the main environment. Replacing either environment of the convolution pair by any stabilizer mixture makes the joint quantum capacity vanish, identifying environmental magic as a necessary resource on both sides for this realization.
