arXiv:2611.22046v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We establish a unified ground-state phase diagram for the one-dimensional Z_N quantum torus chain, which is connected to dipolar cluster and dipolar Ising chains via noninvertible dualities. Combining tensor-network calculations with bosonization and perturbation theory, we establish a rich phase diagram governed by the local Hilbert-space dimension N and its parity. We identify phases with coexisting X and Z long-range orders (C phase), and dipolar Luttinger liquids (dLL_{rm I} and dLL_{rm II}) separating coexisting phases from single-order phases. Gapless dipolar excitations coexist with long-range order in dLLs. Both the C and dLL phases appear at N geq 5, but only the C phases persist as No infty. The stability of the dLL phase is understood by an emergent Luttinger liquid theory, with Berezinskii-Kosterlitz-Thouless transitions out of it driven by competing umklapp and clock anisotropy perturbations. For odd N geq 5, a third dLL phase (dLL_{rm III}) emerges near heta = pi due to the low-energy dynamics inside the exponentially degenerate ground-state manifold that maps onto an effective XXZ chain. Duality considerations suggest a possible gapless dipolar symmetry-protected topological phase and spontaneous dipole-symmetry breaking in dipolar cluster chains.
