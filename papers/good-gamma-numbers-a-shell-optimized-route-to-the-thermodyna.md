arXiv:2610.27761v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Single-particle finite-size errors remain a central obstacle in quantum Monte Carlo (QMC) simulations of periodic systems, conventionally reduced via twist averaging (TA). Here we introduce Good-Gamma (GG) particle numbers as system sizes N at which the Gamma-point Hartree--Fock (HF) energy of the high-density two-dimensional uniform electron gas (2D UEG) lies anomalously close to its thermodynamic-limit (TL) value. All results in this work concern the 2D UEG in its high-density Fermi-liquid regime, r_s=1--4. We systematically investigate the kinetic- and exchange-energy errors, Delta KE(N) and Delta EX(N), that underlie this one-body finite-size-error cancellation across four densities and both spin polarizations. We then compare diffusion Monte Carlo (DMC) energies extrapolated to the TL from GG-N sequences against twist-averaged extrapolations at the same densities and polarizations, finding agreement to within a fraction of a millihartree in every case, independent of whether a 1/N or an N^{-5/4} extrapolation form is used.
