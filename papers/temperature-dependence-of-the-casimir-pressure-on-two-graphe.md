arXiv:2609.27864v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The dispersion interaction between two infinite sheets of graphene located on a substrate is considered based on the Lifshitz formula, taking temperature into account. The result is obtained in the form of a series that is easy to calculate. The influence of the interband conductivity of graphene, which turned out to be small, is examined.
