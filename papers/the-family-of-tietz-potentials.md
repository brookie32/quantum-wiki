arXiv:2610.28126v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We show that several potential-energy functions based on the exponential function e^{-alpha r}, alpha > 0, are either equivalent or identical to the Tietz potential. Some of those potentials exhibit redundant parameters that can be removed without affecting their performance for the description of the vibration-rotation spectra of diatomic molecules.
