arXiv:2611.07462v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: For classical-quantum multiple access channels (cq-MACs) with two senders, we give several general expressions for the capacity region, valid for arbitrary sequences of channels. In the case of asymptotically vanishing error (arepsilon=0), the capacity region is expressed in terms of spectral inf-mutual information rates. For every 0learepsilon<1, the arepsilon-capacity region is expressed in terms of a quantity defined through a linear combination of three alternative hypotheses. In particular, the outer bound of Kubo and Nagaoka (2015) is tight. Our results include the quantum counterpart of the general formula of Han (1998) for the classical MAC. The most important ingredient is the one-shot inner bound of Sen (2021), which we apply to each member of an arbitrary channel sequence.
