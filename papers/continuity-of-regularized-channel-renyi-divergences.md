arXiv:2609.28635v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We prove that the regularized, stabilized sandwiched Renyi divergence of finite-dimensional quantum channels converges to their regularized relative entropy as the Renyi order tends to one. The key tool is the channel hockey-stick divergence: Gour's Stinespring approximation bound and a Schatten norm estimate amplify an asymptotic bound below one into exponential decay at higher threshold rates. For channel pairs with finite max-relative entropy, known operational connections then give exponential strong converses for parallel and adaptive discrimination, a sharp zero--one testing law, and the subchannel asymptotic equipartition property.
