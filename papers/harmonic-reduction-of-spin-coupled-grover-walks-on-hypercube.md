arXiv:2610.14467v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We consider spin-coupled Grover walks on hypercubes within the framework of quantum Bernoulli noises (QBN). We identify parity-dependent harmonic subspaces generated by the central Hamming layers: in even dimension they give a fixed-point subspace, whereas in odd dimension the walk reduces on the harmonic subspace to an explicit eight-dimensional reduced dynamics. Using symmetric-group representation theory, we characterize the corresponding isotypic structure and determine the reduced spectrum. The harmonic reduction also yields explicit long-time probability behavior and shows that the resulting states may occupy an asymptotically vanishing fraction of the hypercube while still spanning its full Hamming diameter.
