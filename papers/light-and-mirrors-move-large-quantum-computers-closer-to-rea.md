Scientists have developed an enhanced photon‑collecting system that uses mirrors, lenses and other optical components to guide photons emitted from optical cavities containing neutral-atom qubits. The improved device can link multiple quantum‑computing arrays in a scalable way, potentially enabling neutral‑atom quantum computers with thousands of qubits. This advancement brings large‑scale neutral‑atom quantum systems closer to practical realization.
