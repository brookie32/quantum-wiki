The authors present a sublattice polarimeter that reconstructs full Bloch wavefunctions in bipartite photonic lattices by applying controlled phase shifts and attenuation via a spatial light modulator to each sublattice. This technique yields the amplitude and phase of crystal orbitals across the Brillouin zone, even near band degeneracies, allowing direct experimental measurement of the Bloch Hamiltonian, quantum geometric tensor, and band Chern numbers. It can be extended to lattices with more internal degrees of freedom, paving the way for probing advanced topological phenomena such as trigonal warping, Chern insulators, and Euler curvature in multiband systems.
