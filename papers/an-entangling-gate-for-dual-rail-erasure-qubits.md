Researchers engineered a high‑speed two‑qubit entangling gate for dual‑rail cavity qubits, a type of erasure qubit encoded in superconducting microwave cavities. The experimentally demonstrated gate runs in approximately 500 ns and maintains the desired error hierarchy: it produces about 0.5 % leakage (erasure) per operation while keeping residual Pauli errors below 0.1 % and eliminating bit‑flip errors to a 10⁻⁶ level. These performance metrics, supported by surface‑code simulations, suggest an accelerated pathway toward scalable error‑corrected quantum computing.
