arXiv:2604.13172v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We establish a rigorous operator-space condition for the thermalization of typical initial states. We introduce a concept of simple slow operators (SSOs), defined as operators that evolve slowly under Heisenberg-picture dynamics and have significant small-sized components. We show that the non-thermalization of typical initial states drawn from a weakly entangled state ensemble implies the existence of SSOs; equivalently, the absence of SSOs implies that typical initial states thermalize. We establish these results by introducing the concept of an ensemble variance norm of an operator, defined as the typical magnitude of the expectation value of that operator with respect to states in the ensemble. For low-entanglement ensembles, the norm is related to operator sizes, allowing us to establish a direct link between operator dynamics and thermalization.
