Zapata Quantum and the University of Maryland have launched ForShor.ai, a formal verification challenge combining formal theorem proving with AI to optimize quantum factoring subroutines. The initiative invites researchers to submit recursive arithmetic schedules, which are then verified for mathematical correctness and asymptotic gate complexity. This aims to ensure that AI-discovered optimizations are inspectable and reusable in fault-tolerant quantum software.
