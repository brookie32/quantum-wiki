arXiv:2611.17543v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We resolve the Abrikosov coefficient conjecture for rapidly rotating two-dimensional Bose--Einstein condensates within the variational framework of Gamma-convergence. For a harmonic trap, we establish the Gamma-convergence of the rescaled Gross--Pitaevskii energy functionals in the fast-rotation regime, through weak L^2 convergence of the rescaled densities, and identify the effective interaction coefficient eta_* with b_riangle, the Abrikosov ratio associated with the triangular vortex lattice. The limiting Thomas--Fermi functional determines the first correction to the minimum energy and the macroscopic density of almost minimizers. For the auxiliary rescaled model and its lowest-Landau-level restriction, we also compute a strictly positive limiting L^2 distance between minimizing densities and the Thomas--Fermi profile. The identification of the effective interaction coefficient eta_* with the triangular Abrikosov ratio b_riangle relies on the sharp Gaussian-minorant theorem established in a recent OpenAI preprint~ite[Theorem~1.2]{Atomic}. A Fourier representation of the quartic energy and a weighted Poisson identity yield a lower bound for arbitrary magnetic-periodic lowest-Landau-level states. The sign conditions on the minorant and its Fourier transform give the bound, while the lattice contact conditions identify its coefficient with b_riangle. Cutoff and magnetic projection transfer this estimate to local holomorphic states without boundary conditions or periodicity assumptions. Consequently, the free-cell coefficients converge to b_riangle with error O(L^{-1/5}) on squares of side length L.
