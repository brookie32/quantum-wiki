arXiv:2610.19936v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Entropy estimation of discrete probability distributions is a fundamental problem in statistical inference and quantum property estimation. In this paper, we study the problem of estimating Renyi entropy H_alpha(mathbf{p}) for any distribution mathbf{p} = (p_i)_{i=1}^n given its purified quantum query-access. For non-integer alpha > 1, our algorithm estimates H_alpha(mathbf{p}) to within additive error epsilon using only widetilde{O}ig(n^{max{1/2,1-1/alpha}}/epsilonig) queries, which improves the best known upper bound for dependence on both n and 1/epsilon. For 0<alpha<1, our algorithm has query complexity widetilde{O}ig(n^{1/(2alpha)}/epsilon^{max{1,1/(2alpha)}}ig), which improves the dependence on 1/epsilon of the current best upper bound without incresing its n dependence. Our approach adapts the multi-level estimation framework to relative approximation of the power sums and leverage binary search to remove the requirement of prior knowledge of them. We also strengthen the precision-dependent lower bound to Omegaig(1/epsilon^{max{1,1/(2alpha)}}ig) for 0<alpha<1, which identifies the optimal precision dependence of our algorithm.
