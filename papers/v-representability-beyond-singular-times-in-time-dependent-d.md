arXiv:2610.33837v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We study the global V-representability problem in time-dependent density functional theory (TDDFT) for the two-particle Hubbard dimer model in the spin-singlet sector. In particular, we investigate whether the time-dependent density of the interacting system obtained for a given one-body external potential u (the tunable control variable) can be reproduced by a non-interacting Kohn--Sham system beyond the singular times at which the usual inversion formulas break down. We deal with both the conventional formulation of TDDFT based on some time-dependent variational principle and the recently introduced geometric formulation of TDDFT (Phys. Rev. Lett. 136 (2026) 256401, Phys. Rev. A 113 (2026) 062222). For both principles, we prove global-in-time V-representability, show that singular times induce non-uniqueness of the time-dependent effective potential, and classify the set of solutions.
