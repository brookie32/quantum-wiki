Researchers demonstrated a nonvolatile method for tuning the surface chemistry of H‑terminated diamond nanopillars using laser-induced oxidation, which in turn controls the charge states of embedded silicon‑vacancy (SiV⁻) color centers. By correlating SiV⁻ photoluminescence with photon flux and energy, they showed that defect‑mediated charge cycling supplies carriers for a photocatalytic oxygen reduction reaction that oxidizes the surface. The approach offers a general strategy for precise surface engineering and charge‑state control in solid‑state quantum devices.
