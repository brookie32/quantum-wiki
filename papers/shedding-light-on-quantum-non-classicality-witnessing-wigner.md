arXiv:2611.24834v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Certifying quantum non-classicality like Wigner negativity typically demands full optical state tomography. This work introduces a noise-resilient framework for witnessing optical Wigner negativity using phase-resolved quantum photon-induced near-field electron microscopy (QPINEM). By mapping electron coherences directly to symmetrically ordered optical characteristic functions within a coupling-limited phase-space region, the protocol bypasses full optical reconstruction. A post-measurement witness matrix is constructed via Bochner's theorem from recorded electron spectra, where negative eigenvalues certify non-classicality. To ensure reliability under realistic experimental conditions, a noise-aware selection procedure decouples discovery from statistical validation. Benchmarked across various optical preparations-including vacuum-Fock superpositions, odd cat states, and GKP states-the protocol provides a statistically bounded pathway for probing quantum features with free electrons.
