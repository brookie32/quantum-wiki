Using ¹⁶⁷Er dopants embedded in Y₂SiO₅ inside a cryogenic Fabry–Perot cavity (65 MHz linewidth, ≪ the 0.9 GHz hyperfine splitting), researchers achieved frequency‑selective emission enhancement that enables single‑shot optical readout of individual nuclear spin qubits with 91 % fidelity. A large magnetic field freezes paramagnetic impurities, extending nuclear‑spin coherence times to over 0.2 s. The resulting system offers telecom‑band, lifetime‑limited photon emission and frequency‑multiplexed addressing, establishing ¹⁶⁷Er as a leading platform for long‑range fiber quantum networks.
