arXiv:2611.33699v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: How thin must a layer of spins be to act as a two-dimensional bath? For nitrogen spins around an NV^- centre in diamond, a cluster expansion with flip-flopping outer spins gives a crossover near the mean spin spacing. Thin layers follow two-dimensional scaling with a prefactor six to seven times that of bulk. Without hyperfine disorder the crossover remains but the step is under three: the disorder leaves each spin almost no resonant partner. Flip-flops allow several milliseconds, so another process must set the measured 0.5 ms; nitrogen spin-lattice relaxation is consistent.
