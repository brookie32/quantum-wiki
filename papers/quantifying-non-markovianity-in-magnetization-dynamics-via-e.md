The article investigates magnetization dynamics beyond the standard stochastic Landau‑Lifshitz‑Gilbert (LLG) equation, which on picosecond scales requires inertial or open‑system extensions that make the dynamics non‑Markovian. By evaluating entropy production rates with established measures, the authors show analytically and numerically that while the classical LLG yields strictly positive rates, its extended versions exhibit temporarily negative rates—signatures of non‑Markovian behavior. These results indicate that experiments necessitating open‑system LLG dynamics are intrinsically more non‑Markovian than those described by either classical or inertial LLG models.
