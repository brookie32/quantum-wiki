arXiv:2611.09668v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: In this work, we try to understand the question of how to address the survival of band topology in the presence of dephasing. We investigate this question in the Kane-Mele Z_2 topological insulator. To determine the stability of the topological invariant we construct a single particle Hamiltonian that also provides identical correlation at the single particle level. The phase transition from topological to trivial is determined by the gap closing of this correlation Hamiltonian. This gap closing is related to purity gap. As a diagnostics we calculated the invariant directly and as a complementary diagnostic, coherent two-terminal transport through the auxiliary correlation Hamiltonian exhibits quantized conductance in the nontrivial region. This does not establish physical conductance under dephasing. Our results identify a predictive local criterion for the survival of single-particle topological structure under a specified noise channel.
