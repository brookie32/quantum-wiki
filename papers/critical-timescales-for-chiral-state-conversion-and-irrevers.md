arXiv:2604.01918v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Non-Hermitian systems driven along slow parametric loops undergo non-adiabatic transitions whose outcome depends sensitively on the driving speed, yet no explicit formula has been available for the critical timescale T_{cr} at which these transitions develop. Using a 2imes 2 Hamiltonian with circular parameter trajectories, we derive T_{cr} = G,ln(1/|Delta|) in closed form for non-encircling loops, phase-shifted loops, offset loops, and loops encircling exceptional points, where G is a geometry-dependent growth factor and Delta is the instability seed. This formula sharply separates the regime where the system remains in the averagely dominant eigenstate (T T_{cr}), resolving the apparent tension between the previous literature. We identify two competing seeds: a geometric Stokes multiplier and the finite-precision floor. When the geometric seed vanishes, precision alone governs the transition, yielding T_{cr} propto mlneta, linear in the number m of digits in base eta (bits for eta=2). For loops encircling an exceptional point, the Stokes seed only sets an intermediate plateau; the final transition is again seeded by the precision floor, and T_{cr} remains linear in m. This provides a purely forward-evolution manifestation of precision-induced irreversibility [Phys. Rev. A 114, 012207 (2026)], demonstrating that the fundamental limit identified through echo protocols also controls the outcome of slow non-Hermitian dynamics without requiring time reversal. For PT-symmetric energy spectra, T_{cr} additionally determines the onset of chirality: the dynamics is non-chiral for T T_{cr}.
