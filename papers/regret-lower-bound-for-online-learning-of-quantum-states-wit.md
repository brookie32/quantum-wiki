arXiv:2610.21273v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We study the problem of online learning of quantum states with logarithmic loss, which is a quantum generalization of online portfolio selection and has applications to quantum state tomography, the task of estimating an unknown quantum state from measurement outcomes. We first establish a regret lower bound, showing that the regret rates of Exponential Weights and the algorithm of Tseng et al. (2025) have optimal polynomial dependence on the dimension. Our analysis leads to two further results. First, our lower-bound proof leads to a quantum state tomography algorithm that is sample optimal up to polylogarithmic factors in the sample size. Second, we develop an algorithm for constructing confidence sequences for the mean of a matrix-valued random variable. A numerical experiment shows that the latter algorithm is competitive with existing methods.
