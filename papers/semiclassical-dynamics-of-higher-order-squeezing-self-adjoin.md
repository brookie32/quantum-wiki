arXiv:2610.33378v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Higher-order squeezing Hamiltonians have several self-adjoint realizations, while their classical trajectories reach infinity in finite time. We relate these extension problems through boundary amplitudes and the semiclassical propagation of coherent states. A discrete Fourier transformation gives the sink-to-source ray matrix of each quantum realization. After the first passage through infinity, the full Husimi probability measure converges to point masses on outgoing classical branches, with weights equal to the squared moduli of the matrix entries. The proof combines finite-order approximate tail solutions satisfying the exact extension boundary condition with full-norm Gaussian matching and a square-summable residual estimate. For positive energy, we determine the limit after any fixed number of passages: amplitudes evolve by powers of the unitary ray matrix, not by iterations of its squared-modulus matrix. We also realize these limits by concentrated complex amplitudes for an energy-preserving Liouville extension, distinguishing deterministic classical gluings from coherent boundary mixing.
