arXiv:2607.15221v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We demonstrate a method to measure energy shifts of the top level in a four-level ladder setup induced by atom interactions in thermal vapors. It utilizes the observation of two transmission minima corresponding to a split electromagnetically induced absorption (EIA) effect. We apply this method to measure mean Rydberg atom interactions in a hot vapor. We believe this approach could provide a valuable tool for accurately modeling mean-field Rydberg atom interactions, as well as sensing the occurrence of strong interactions.
