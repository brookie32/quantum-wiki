Researchers experimentally detected third‑ and fourth‑order photon scattering from a single two‑level atom in cavity QED, revealing intrinsic multiphoton entanglement in resonance fluorescence. The three‑photon component was shown to be genuinely energy–time entangled by violating Svetlichny’s inequality, and the emitted photons were used to implement a quantum secret‑sharing protocol. This work extends the theoretical description of resonance fluorescence and provides a straightforward method for generating multiphoton entangled states for quantum information tasks.
