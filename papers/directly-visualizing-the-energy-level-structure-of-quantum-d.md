They demonstrate a spectroscopic method that maps the full energy level spectrum of a silicon double‑quantum dot as a function of detuning, inter‑dot tunnel coupling, and magnetic field. In the single‑electron regime it resolves the transition from isolated atom‑like levels to molecular bonding‑antibonding states and measures Zeeman splitting of ground and excited valley states; in the two‑electron regime it reveals the detuning‑dependent singlet–triplet splitting. The approach offers a direct means to extract key energy gaps and could be extended to other systems with strong spin‑orbit coupling or proximitized qubits.
