arXiv:2610.18875v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We propose a symmetry-preserving Boson-informed neural network (BINN) for the variational Monte Carlo computation of interacting-boson ground states in continuous space. The framework explicitly separates the two dominant physical scales of the system. At the microscopic interaction scale, a cell Jastrow factor absorbs the leading near-contact stiffness, while at the macroscopic scale, a potential-aware envelope encodes the external confinement, leaving the neural network to capture a smoother many-body residual. Across both two- and three-dimensional tests, the BINN approaches the Hartree and Gross-Pitaevskii limits with an empirical O(N^{-1}) finite-size trend in both energy and density. The energies agree with diffusion Monte Carlo to within 0.06% for the particle number tested up to 350 in the three-dimensional shrinking-range case. Applications are made under various trapping scenarios, justifying robust performance of BINN.
