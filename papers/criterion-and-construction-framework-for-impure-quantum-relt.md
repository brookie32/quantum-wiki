arXiv:2611.27626v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: This paper aims to uncover new phenomena and provide new tools for quantum ((r,elta))-LRCs derived from optimal ((r,elta))-LRCs. The initial motivation is to identify the main reason for the existence of impure cases in such quantum codes. Our main result reveals that whether (d = r+1) is the key factor governing the impurity of quantum ((r,elta))-LRCs. We prove that, when (d neq r+1), impure quantum codes derived from optimal ((r,elta))-LRCs have bounded lengths. Conversely, when (d = r+1), we construct impure quantum ((r,elta))-LRCs with unbounded lengths. The central tool developed in this work is a criterion for identifying impure quantum codes derived from optimal ((r,elta))-LRCs. The main results include a criterion for impurity in quantum ((r,elta))-LRCs (Theorem 3.2), a construction framework for impure quantum ((r,elta))-LRCs (Theorem 4.4), and two families of impure quantum ((r,elta))-LRCs (Theorems 4.8 and 4.11).
