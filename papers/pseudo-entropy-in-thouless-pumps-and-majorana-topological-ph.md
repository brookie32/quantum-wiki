arXiv:2611.27846v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We study the average excess pseudo entropy, Delta S_{12}, in the Rice-Mele model under Thouless pumping. We compute it for two driving schemes, the control-freak and smooth pumps, under open and periodic boundary conditions. We find that Delta S_{12} distinguishes the trivial, monomer, and topological stages of the pump, and that it depends on the boundary condition, the driving scheme, and the reference time. We also find that a nonzero staggered potential breaks chiral symmetry and gaps out the SSH critical point, thereby flattening the pseudo-entropy peak at the phase boundary. We also study reference-state dependence in the open Kitaev chain, comparing a fixed even-parity target with five reference families: even free-fermion, pairing-only, vacuum, and two fixed gapped states. We find that the pseudo-entropy response depends on the reference and on the parity of the subsystem size, even when the reference remains gapped. At mu=0, the pairing-only reference gives the exact result S_{pr}=ln 2 for L=4m+2, while the L=4m sequence has analytically determined finite-size corrections. These results show that, in both models, pseudo entropy is a property of the reference-target pair and the subsystem partition, rather than a reference-independent signature of the topological phase.
