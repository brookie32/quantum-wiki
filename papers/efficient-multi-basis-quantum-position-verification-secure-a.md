The paper presents a robust quantum position verification (QPV) protocol whose verifier state preparation is independent of channel loss, enhancing reliability under real‑world conditions. It refines security analysis techniques to increase resilience against experimental imperfections and removes implicit assumptions that previously limited theoretical guarantees. Additionally, the authors demonstrate how the protocol can be adapted for authenticating classical communication in quantum key distribution.
