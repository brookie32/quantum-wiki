Moth Quantum has developed Tessa, a hardware-native data encoding scheme for quantum processors that significantly reduces overhead by eliminating SWAP gates and ancilla qubits. This method, detailed in a technical preprint, allows for encoding and retrieval of a large number of real-valued parameters across qubits, outperforming previous methods. Tessa is now integrated into Moth's Atlas platform, offering quantum-driven creative tools for various media.
