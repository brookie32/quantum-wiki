Researchers have demonstrated a functional nuclear clock using the low‑lying isomeric transition in ^229Th, converting this highly stable nuclear frequency into a practical timing discriminator. The device offers precision that rivals or surpasses current optical clocks while providing enhanced robustness and sensitivity to fundamental physics tests. This work marks a significant step toward compact, high‑accuracy timekeeping based on nuclear transitions.
