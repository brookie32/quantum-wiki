The study demonstrates how machine‑learning “foundation” models of interatomic forces can be rigorously evaluated and fine‑tuned for predicting experimentally relevant thermomechanical properties. By coupling the Wigner formulation of heat transport with Grüneisen theory, the authors establish standards that link quantum‑accurate atomic dynamics to measurable thermal conductivity and thermal expansion, achieving first‑principles accuracy across a database of 103 diverse solids. This framework overcomes key limitations of earlier approaches—high computational cost, poor transferability, and lack of physical interpretability—facilitating accelerated design of high‑performance heat‑management materials.
