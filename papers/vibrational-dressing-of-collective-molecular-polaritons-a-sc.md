arXiv:2610.20912v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We develop a scalable Green's-function theory of vibrationally dressed molecular-polariton absorption in the Holstein--Tavis--Cummings model. Within the self-consistent Born approximation (SCBA), the ground-state linear response of N identical molecules coupled to one cavity mode follows from a 2imes2 cavity--bright matrix, while scattering through the N-1 dark states remains in the molecular self-energy. The number of self-consistent equations is independent of N, allowing large ensembles to be treated without explicitly resolving the dark manifold. Benchmarks against hierarchical equations of motion show that the theory captures collective linewidth narrowing and an N-dependent minimum in the lower-polariton linewidth as its exciton fraction varies, beyond a Hopfield-weighted linewidth estimate. Comparisons with exact diagonalization for discrete local vibrations show weaker vibronic progressions and better overall SCBA agreement as N increases at fixed single-molecule coupling. The resulting framework connects microscopic vibrational scattering to collective absorption spectra without introducing fitted polariton linewidths.
