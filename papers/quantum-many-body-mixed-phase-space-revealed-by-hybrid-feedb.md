Quantum many‑body systems can transition between ordered and chaotic regimes, but signatures of a mixed phase space have been elusive.  
In this work the authors develop a hybrid quantum–classical feedback protocol that repeatedly applies short quantum evolution on a superconducting processor followed by classical optimization projecting onto a low‑entanglement variational manifold; this autonomously discovers and stabilizes long‑lived regular trajectories.  
The resulting dynamics expose clear evidence of a quantum many‑body mixed phase space emerging from nonlinear variational dynamics—behaviour not found in few‑body quantum systems with a small number of effective degrees of freedom.
