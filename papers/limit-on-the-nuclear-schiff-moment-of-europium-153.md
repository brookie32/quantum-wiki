arXiv:2606.12084v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: The Schiff moment of a nucleus is a symmetry-violating nuclear moment that indicates new physics beyond the Standard Model. We place the limit, |mathscr{S}({}^{153}Eu)| < 1.7 imes 10^{-8} e,fm^3 (95% confidence), on the Schiff moment of the ^{153}Eu nucleus, using nuclear spin resonances in two ensembles of oppositely-polarized ^{153}Eu^{3+} ions in a Y{}_2SiO{}_5 crystal. This measurement using octupolar nuclei in a mm-scale crystal constrains new physics at the TeV energy-scale.
