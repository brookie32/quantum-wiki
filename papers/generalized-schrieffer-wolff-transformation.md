arXiv:2611.22014v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The Schrieffer-Wolff transformation (SWT) is a cornerstone of effective-Hamiltonian theory. However, the SWT is predominantly used for weak-coupling problems, while a systematic counterpart for strongly coupled regimes remains largely unexplored. A general closed-form SWT generator and a clear physical principle for gauge fixing remain elusive, obscuring the transformation's underlying structure. We introduce the dual SWT to deal with the strong-coupling problems. The generalized SWT (gSWT) is formulated for any Hermitian operator and block partition. We establish the geodesic principle for its canonical gauge. Furthermore, we derive an exact closed-form solution of the gSWT generator at every order as a finite nested-commutator sum whose only input is the active gap set. Both the geometric and algebraic aspects of the SWT physics are revealed. The gSWT framework is illustrated in two quantum many-body models, the collective spin model and the transverse-field Ising model.
