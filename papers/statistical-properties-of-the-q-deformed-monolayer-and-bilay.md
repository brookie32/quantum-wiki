arXiv:2611.21675v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We investigate the statistical properties of the q-deformed Dirac oscillator for monolayer and bilayer graphene in the presence of a constant external magnetic field. Using q-deformed creation and annihilation operators, we obtain the corresponding energy spectra and eigenfunctions, as well as their probability current densities, recovering the conventional Landau-level structures in the undeformed limit. For bilayer graphene, the lowest Landau levels are explicitly constructed within the q-deformed framework. The partition functions for both systems are derived using zeta-function techniques and the Euler-Maclaurin formula, allowing us to determine their main thermodynamic quantities for small values of the deformation parameter. Our results show that the q-deformation modifies the energy spectra, the behavior of the probability current density components, and the thermodynamic response of the systems. A comparison between monolayer and bilayer graphene reveals distinct thermal behaviors, reflecting their different Landau-level structures and highlighting the influence of the q-deformation on their statistical properties.
