arXiv:2602.07788v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We present a comprehensive phase diagram of quantum correlations in a three-mode Gaussian state generated by a tritter, driven by a two-mode squeezed vacuum and a coherent state. The coherent amplitude does not affect the correlation structure, which is solely governed by the initial squeezing. By systematically analyzing five physically relevant asymmetric loss configurations, we map out the exact resilience thresholds for entanglement and Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen (EPR) steering under all bipartite partitions. We reveal that EPR steering exhibits a pronounced directional asymmetry under loss, and its survival can be maintained over a much wider range of loss by strategically protecting a single channel. This tunable fragility provides practical guidance for one-sided device-independent quantum protocols in noisy asymmetric networks. We further confirm the limitations of Renyi-2 entropy in the quantification of entanglement and steering. Our results transform the abstract correlation hierarchy into a calculable, experimentally relevant guide for engineering robust quantum resources.
