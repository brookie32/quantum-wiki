arXiv:2603.17200v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We present a p-adic version of the Jackiw-Rebbi model, in which the real line is replaced by the p-adic numbers and the Dirac Hamiltonian by a non-local operator built from the twisted Vladimirov operator. We show that p-adic quantum mechanics, although non-local, yields a physical picture that agrees with the Archimedean one, realized through mechanisms with no Archimedean counterpart. The agreement is specific: for a constant mass the p-adic model is gapped, with the same gap; it has a chiral symmetry, so its spectrum is symmetric about zero energy; a change of sign of the mass binds states at an interface; and at low energies its spectrum cannot be distinguished from the Archimedean continuous spectrum. The mechanisms are not: the p-adic free Hamiltonian is diagonalized by compactly supported wavelets, so every stationary state is strictly localized; momentum takes values on a geometric ladder of powers of the prime; and a zero-energy interface state exists only for masses commensurate with that ladder. The standard Dirac equation does not admit space-localized solutions because it contradicts Einstein's causality. The model is realizable at every finite resolution, as a continuous-time quantum walk, and the topological protection of the Archimedean zero mode is lost.
