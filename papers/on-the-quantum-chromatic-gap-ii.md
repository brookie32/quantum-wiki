arXiv:2610.40571v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We confirm a conjecture of the first author [SODA'26] on a strong form of pseudo-telepathy for Khot's d-to-1 games: for every dgeq 4, every epsilon>0, and every kinmathbb N, there is a d-to-1 label-cover game of classical value less than epsilon that admits a perfect quantum strategy using finite-dimensional shared entanglement with measurement contexts of radius at least k. As a consequence, there exist graphs whose quantum chromatic number hi_q is 3 and whose classical chromatic number hi is arbitrarily large. This is the first super-exponential hi_q vs. hi gap, and it improves on the exponential lower bounds of Brassard, Cleve, and Tapp [Phys. Rev. Lett. '99], who produced graphs for which higeq 1.1397^{hi_q}, and Lalonde, who recently achieved higeq 1.1546^{hi_q}. To prove the conjecture, we proceed as follows. We start with a ``stretched'' version of the Mermin-Peres magic square, obtained by spacing out its equations and linking them with additional long equation wires. This operation increases the radius of the initial measurement contexts of a perfect quantum strategy for the game, while preserving classical unsatisfiability. We then apply parallel repetition to make the classical value small, and we turn the system into a pseudo-telepathic multi-layered Kneser hypergraph via a PCP construction of Dinur, Guruswami, Khot, and Regev [STOC'03]. Finally, we feed the resulting hypergraph into the reduction of Fei, Minzer, and Wang from hypergraph colouring to 4-to-1 games, we establish quantum completeness of this reduction, and we show that it preserves context radius.
