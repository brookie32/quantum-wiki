arXiv:2611.24054v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quantum information can be reliably transmitted at rates below the quantum capacity with an error that vanishes as the number of channel uses grows. The reliability function characterizes the optimal exponential decay rate of this error. For quantum erasure channels with a prime input dimension, we determine the reliability function exactly at all positive communication rates below capacity that meet or exceed an explicit critical rate. Notably, stabilizer coding strategies attain this optimal reliability throughout this regime.
