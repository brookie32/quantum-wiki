arXiv:2611.33852v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Bright sources of high-dimensional spatially entangled photon pairs are increasingly required for the rapid advancement of quantum technologies. However, simultaneously achieving high brightness and high-dimensional spatial entanglement through spontaneous parametric down-conversion (SPDC) remains challenging. Here, we demonstrate a bright source of high-dimensional spatially entangled photon pairs using beam-like Type-II SPDC in a eta-barium borate crystal. Compared with a conventional collinear Type-I source, it exhibits a sim1.56-fold enhancement in the detected single-photon count rate and a sim1.45-fold enhancement in the coincidence count rate. Measurements of position correlations and momentum anti-correlations certify at least 62-dimensional spatial entanglement within a 121-dimensional basis. These correlations are further exploited to demonstrate quantum imaging in both the position and momentum domains. These characteristics make the source a practical resource for high-dimensional photonic quantum technologies.
