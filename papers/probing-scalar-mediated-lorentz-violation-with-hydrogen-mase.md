arXiv:2611.21810v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We propose using hydrogen maser spectroscopy and molecular spin-precession experiments, traditionally employed in precision frequency measurements and searches for the electron electric dipole moment, as complementary probes of light scalar-mediated, spin-dependent fifth forces. We investigate their sensitivity to Lorentz-violating interactions mediated by scalars with masses ranging from O(10^{-4}~mueV) to O(10~keV). While hydrogen maser spectroscopy and molecular spin-precession measurements probe different combinations of Lorentz-violating couplings, we find that the strongest projected sensitivities can be achieved using spin-precession of ultracold molecules, benefiting from their extended coherence times. These experimental approaches provide access to previously unexplored regions of parameter space, probing interaction ranges extending from centimeter to sub-angstrom scales. Our proposals highlight the potential of precision atomic and molecular experiments to search for new spin-dependent scalar-mediated interactions and violations of fundamental spacetime symmetries.
