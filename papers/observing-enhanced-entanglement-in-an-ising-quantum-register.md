arXiv:2611.18278v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Measurements often yield a disentangled quantum state which is the most probable measurement outcome while the measurement outcomes with enhanced entanglement could be exponentially less probable or absent. Higher probability in measurement outcomes with enhanced entanglement is virtue in experimental observation of enhance entanglement in post-selected or Born-rule measurements with less resources in noisy quantum devices. We develop a periodic measurement sampling(PMS) protocol in the framework of uniform matrix product states. Using PMS, we identify a peak enhancement of entanglement in a uniform post-measurement state whose probabilistic outcome can be maximized by optimizing a measurement super-cell. A measurement super-cell is the smallest repeating building block of qubits defined by the measurement density, external field strength and exchange interactions between quantum register and ancilla qubits. The above prediction based on a classical description of the many-body quantum state is demonstrated in a quantum computing experiment in a loop of quantum register qubits with Ising interactions in the presence of transverse magnetic field. Further, we show the scalability of predictions of the periodic measurement sampling protocol in the thermodynamic limit to finite size systems. Especially, we present a rescaling ansatz which would enable predicting the possibility of observing enhanced entanglement in finite size systems.
