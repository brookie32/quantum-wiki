arXiv:2610.22983v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Estimating statistical properties of probability distributions is a fundamental problem in statistics and theoretical computer science. We develop quantum algorithms for estimating the q-Tsallis entropy H_q^{Tsa}(P) =frac{sum_{i=1}^n p_i^q-1}{1-q} when 0<q<1, given purified quantum query access to a distribution P on [n]. When 0<qleq frac{1}{2}, we provide a quantum algorithm with query complexity widetilde{O}rbra{left(frac{n}{arepsilon}right)^{frac{1}{2q}}}, which is optimal up to polylogarithmic factors. When frac{1}{2}<q<1, we introduce a two-stage simultaneous probability estimation procedure, yielding a quantum algorithm with query complexity widetilde{O}left(frac{n^{frac12+frac{1}{2q}}}{arepsilon^{frac{1}{2q}}}+frac{n^{1-q}}{arepsilon}right), which improves the result given by Chen et al. (ICALP 2026) when arepsilon=Orbra{n^{1-2q}}.
