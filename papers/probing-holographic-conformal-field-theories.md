arXiv:2602.07895v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We embed relativistic quantum information protocols in AdS/CFT: an Unruh-DeWitt detector coupled to a local primary of a holographic CFT has a reduced state fixed by the universal boundary Wightman function. We find that the mana generated in a qutrit probe reads off the boundary condition of the dual bulk scalar, de-excitation spectroscopy tracks the double-trace flow through the lowest cylinder gap, and a local boundary detector is inequivalent to the HKLL representation of a bulk-local one.
