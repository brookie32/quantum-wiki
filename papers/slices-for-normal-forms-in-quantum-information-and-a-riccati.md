arXiv:2611.09645v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: It is known that the normal space to an orbit of a compact group linear action meets each orbit. We show that this yields known normal forms for quantum states. We compute the gradient flow converging to the normal form and find it gives a Riccati type matrix-valued ODE.
