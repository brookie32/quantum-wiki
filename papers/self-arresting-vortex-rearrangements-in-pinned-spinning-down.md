arXiv:2611.33103v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Superfluids, in a spinning-down container with pinning centres, exhibit sudden collective migration of quantized vortices. The onset, progress, and termination of this process remain poorly understood. Here, we show that: 1) the first exodus of vortices occurs when a sector of the vortex array, having a minimum opening angle, is vacated close to the wall; 2) driven by this exodus, the superfluid flow near the boundary continually decreases in strength and arrests the exit; and 3) the resultant depletion region has well-defined dimensions---governed primarily by the critical unpinning velocity, the container size, and the initial rotation rate---that limit the angular momentum transferred to the container. We conclude by discussing the relevance to pulsar glitches.
