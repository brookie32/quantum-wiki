arXiv:2606.26770v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We present a Hermitian squeezing-Dicke model as a minimal closed-system setting for non-Hermitian many-body physics. Squeezing generates an effective parity-time (PT) symmetry and exchanges gainy and lossy eigenmodes without dissipation. We identify an anomalous dynamical superradiant phase (DSRP) that exhibits by a mean-field superradiant order in the PT symmetry-breaking regime, accompanied by a Z_2-symmetry-breaking ground-state quantum phase transition. Additionally, it undergoes a PT-breaking dynamical phase transition at an exceptional point (EP). The excitation energy vanishes near both boundaries, giving critical slowing down. The vacuum photon number is divergent at the ground-state transition but is finite and nonanalytic at the EP, distinguishing the DSRP from the conventional superradiant phase and from a standard dynamical instability. Squeezing causes the coupling phase to act as an artificial magnetic flux, which yields flux-controlled nonreciprocal quantum amplification in the DSRP. Our results offer an alternative pathway to non-Hermitian many-body physics and nonreciprocal quantum amplification in closed Hermitian systems.
