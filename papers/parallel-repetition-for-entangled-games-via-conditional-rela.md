arXiv:2610.21410v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We give a proof of the cubic parallel-repetition bound for finite two-player entangled games using quantum conditional relative entropy. For a game with entangled value 1-arepsilon<1 and answer alphabets mathcal A,mathcal B, its n-fold repeated value is at most [ exp[-Omega(arepsilon^3n/L)],qquad L:=max{1,log(|mathcal A||mathcal B|)}, ] for every nge1. This recovers Song's cubic rate. We also prove a threshold bound: the probability of winning a fraction at least 1-arepsilon+elta is at most exp[-Omega(elta^5n/(arepsilon^2L))] for 0<eltalearepsilon. Our information charge is the decrease in relative entropy when a question reference is discarded. Our comparison states preserve the original conditional question law. A chain rule bounds the coordinate charges using the cost of conditioning on checked wins and retaining checked answers. Our main lemma converts small charges into nearby pure-state descriptions and local corrections, each depending only on one player's question. Together with Song's sampler, these enable approximate local preparation of the conditioned workspace.
