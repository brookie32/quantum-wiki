arXiv:2608.29874v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We investigate the behavior of a homogeneous one-dimensional Bose gas composed of N identical bosons experiencing attractive two-body and repulsive three-body interactions. Utilizing intermediate Hartree theory, we rigorously derive the homogeneous cubic-quintic nonlinear Schrodinger functional as the mean-field limit of the many-body Hamiltonian. Particular attention is given to the impact of the repulsive three-body term on ground state energy estimates and mass concentration properties
