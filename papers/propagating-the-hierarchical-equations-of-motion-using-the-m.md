arXiv:2611.09637v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The hierarchical equations of motion (HEOM) are a powerful, numerically exact approach for simulating the time evolution of open quantum systems. Recently, several tensor-train- and tensor-network-based approaches have been suggested and realized to extend the range of applicability of the HEOM approach to larger and more realistic systems. In this paper, we build upon the existing twin-space formulation of the HEOM and present a novel approach to propagate the fermionic HEOM efficiently using the multi-configurational time-dependent Hartree method (MCTDH). We benchmark the resulting HEOM+MCTDH method with electron transport calculations for a Holstein-type nanojunction model. Furthermore, we demonstrate the capabilities of the HEOM+MCTDH method by applying it to a more complex vibronic coupling nanojunction model with strong molecule-electrode coupling for which fully quantum simulations have not been feasible previously.
