arXiv:2604.19426v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We study how hardware noise changes the p=1 Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) landscape for cardinality-constrained portfolio problems on ibm_fez (IBM Heron r2): seven instances from n=6 to 16 qubits, each on a 7x7 parameter grid at 4,096 and 8,192 shots per point. The first version of this paper proposed Landscape Span Compression (LSC), the fractional loss of energy range across the grid, as a device benchmark, based on stable values of 0.24-0.30 at n=0.9993) are uninformative and the only effect noise can remove is a raised feasible fraction. We withdraw the benchmark and noise-model-gap claims of the first version, correct its solution-quality figures, and describe a protocol that separates feasibility loss from solution quality.
