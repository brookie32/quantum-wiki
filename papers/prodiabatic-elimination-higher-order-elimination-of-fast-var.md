arXiv:2602.21896v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Adiabatic elimination plays a central role in cavity quantum electrodynamics by removing fast cavity degrees of freedom, but at the cost of losing their finite-time response in multi-time correlations. We introduce prodiabatic elimination, a systematic expansion of the quantum Langevin equations that retains vacuum-noise contributions alongside higher-order corrections to the eliminated cavity field. This provides controlled access to arbitrary time- and normally ordered multi-time correlation functions, including photodetection observables, beyond what can be recovered from effective atomic dynamics alone. In the driven-dissipative Jaynes-Cummings model, we identify a vacuum-mediated effective repulsion between detected photons that suppresses short-delay bunching. For a single-photon switch, we analytically resolve a finite heralded response time arising from the cavity field's delayed adjustment to the atomic transfer.
