arXiv:2609.17574v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We compare four local geometries of centered one-mode bosonic Gaussian covariances while keeping their operational meanings distinct. In a common trace-orthonormal normalization, the Bures and covariance Fisher--Rao cometric determinants cross intrinsically at (x^2=arphi). After introducing a constant action-matching parameter (eta), the Bures--Wasserstein determinant joins them only at ((x,eta)=(sqrtarphi,sqrtarphi)). Equal determinant density does not mean tensor equality: relative to Fisher--Rao, the Bures cometric at the junction is [ Q_{rm gold}=arphi^{-2}Pi_0+arphiPi_2, qquad et Q_{rm gold}=1, ] so its inverse has the exact scalar-to-weight-two quadratic penalty ratio (arphi^3). The normalized Bogoliubov--Kubo--Mori metric supplies an independent relative-entropy diagnostic. It agrees radially with Bures but has shape cometric eigenvalue (4hbar^2x/log[(x+1)/(x-1)]); it therefore does not form a fourth determinant junction and becomes tangentially stiff at the pure-state boundary. Operationally, weight-two motion is generated by a system-only Gaussian unitary, whereas radial motion requires a Gaussian channel or ancillary dilation. The known Uhlmann--Ruan purification quotient realizes the local Bures state metric, but an explicit (mathfrak{sp}(4,mathbb R)) test shows that an unweighted right-invariant Frobenius gate norm does not reproduce the golden penalty. Conditional branch selection and finite-rate Bures--Wasserstein cost separation are presented as secondary statements, not as dynamical phase transitions.
