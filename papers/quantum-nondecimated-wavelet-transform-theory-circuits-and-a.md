arXiv:2512.21478v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: The quantum nondecimated wavelet transform uses a shift register to apply circular shifts to an amplitude encoded signal before a single orthogonal wavelet transform. We show that tracing out the shift register gives level probabilities equal to the normalized nondecimated wavelet energies, invariant under circular shifts. Its diagonal gives moving averages of squared coefficients. We derive a multinomial model for the level counts and show how to read the autocovariance at lag one for all but the coarsest detail level. For these global statistics, direct Fourier basis measurement of the input is at least as precise and uses a simpler circuit. For denoising, we insert a block encoded shrinkage operator between the transform and its inverse. Postselection on the shift register implements the cycle spinning average exactly as a linear combination of unitaries. Weights shared within each level give a translation equivariant filter with zero phase. Local weights also preserve translation equivariance when indexed by the nondecimated translate. The circuit provides access to individual decimated branches and combines them coherently. We illustrate the method with James Stein shrinkage, local empirical Wiener filtering, and a scaling analysis of turbulent wind velocity. Gate simulations verify the construction, and we report state preparation, weight estimation, and output costs separately.
