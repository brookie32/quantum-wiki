arXiv:2609.31319v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: In a recent paper Nahum argued on the basis of a renormalization-group analysis that, contrary to standard lore, ground states of 1D spin chains with short-range interactions can spontaneously break U(1) ``easy-plane'' spin rotation symmetry. True long-range order of (S^x,S^y) arises at the phase transition between two quasi-long-range-ordered phases. Here we present detailed numerical results for an anisotropic spin-1 chain model. We find a magnetization exponent etaapprox0.29, consistent with the epsilon-expansion prediction etaapprox0.28, a divergence of the Luttinger parameter on approaching the transition from both sides with an exponent that we relate to Nahum's transition, and finite-size spectra consistent with a dynamical critical exponent zapprox2. Our results support the critical behavior predicted by Nahum's field theory.
