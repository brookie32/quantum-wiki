arXiv:2611.17896v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: An optimized commutator bound can lie strictly below the quantum infimum even when its minimizing mean is realized by a quantum state. We study hybrid uncertainty, defined as the sum of pairwise products of standard deviations. For any spanning list of nonzero real linear combinations of the generators in a positive discrete series representation of mathfrak{su}(1,1), the commutator bound has a unique minimizing mean, realized by a Perelomov coherent state. We prove that the optimized bound equals the infimum over states with finite second moments if and only if the symmetrized covariance matrix of the prescribed observables is diagonal in this state. When this condition fails, the quantum infimum is strictly greater than the bound; for a nonorthogonal list, we obtain an explicit positive lower bound on this gap. For irreducible spin-j representations of mathfrak{su}(2), we derive the exact minimum of the spectral hybrid uncertainty over states for any spanning list of spin observables. For a fixed state, we compare separate measurements made before a random target is disclosed with direct measurement of the target known in advance. For these protocols, the excess mean squared error coefficient equals twice the listed hybrid uncertainty, and its minimum over orthogonal combinations of the observables equals twice the spectral hybrid uncertainty.
