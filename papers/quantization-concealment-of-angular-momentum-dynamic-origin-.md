arXiv:2611.33267v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Since their introduction, spins of elementary particles remain a phenomenological addition to modern quantum theories. While relatively recent studies on Dirac and other linearized equations of motion have revealed the spatial current nature of the spin magnetic moment (instead of the commonly pictured self-rotation), the dynamical origin of this circulating current remains unresolved. Here, we expose a long-overlooked generic shortcoming of wave mechanics ("quantization concealment of angular momentum") that undesirably reduces the canonical angular momentum of bound eigen-modes and renders incomplete the minimal coupling to magnetic fields. Recovering the kinetic orbital angular momentum resolves 1s-orbital's counterintuitive "zero" angular momentum and reproduces all planar modes' anomalous Zeeman splitting. Regarding spins as effective remedy to the concealment offers natural explanations to their main puzzles: 1) dynamical origin of spin magnetic moment and 2) frame invariance of spin angular momentum. This implies absence of spin magnetic moment for free particles, a strong prediction verifiable through Stern-Gerlach experiments on electrons.
