arXiv:2608.28569v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quantum Fourier transforms (QFT) for general groups were recognized to be fundamental already early in the field. A canonical example of non-abelian QFT for the symmetric group was outlined by Beals (1997). Later, a more detailed analysis of this algorithm was carried out by Kawano and Sekigawa (2016). In this paper, we revisit that construction. After a careful analysis, we revise their gate complexity to widetilde{O}(n^{3.5}) and circuit depth to widetilde{O}(n^3). Moreover, we observe that their construction is not optimal in the choice of transversal elements, so we propose simpler realization of the symmetric group QFT.
