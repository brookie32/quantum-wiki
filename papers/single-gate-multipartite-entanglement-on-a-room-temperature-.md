The study demonstrates a parallel multi‑qubit entangling operation on a room‑temperature nitrogen‑vacancy (NV) centre in diamond, producing a four‑qubit Greenberger–Horne–Zeilinger state in 14.8 µs—approximately ten times faster than sequential two‑qubit gates—and achieving a fidelity of 0.92(4). Parallel three‑qubit gates were also realized on all‑nuclear-qubit subsets, and entanglement was verified through multiple quantum coherences. The technique surpasses the performance of conventional gate sequences (fidelity = 0.69(3)) and offers a scalable pathway for generating multipartite entanglement in solid‑state quantum registers.
