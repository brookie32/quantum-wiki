arXiv:2610.30887v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We first prove a sharp Lieb-Thirring inequality with semi-classical constant and gradient correction in one dimension. In the periodic setting, the corresponding inequality also includes a parity dependent shell-correction term, which appears to be new. Moreover, we also characterize the optimizers of these inequalities. In particular, the optimizing density profiles form a two-dimensional manifold, which we call here the Poincare density manifold. We then apply these results to study the Thomas--Fermi--von Weizsacker (TFW) approximation of the kinetic energy in Density Functional Theory. More precisely, we first obtain an explicit and non-asymptotic bound on the global and local error made by the TFW approximation. These bounds are controlled by the distance of the normalized density profile from the Poincare density manifold times the number of particles. In particular, these bounds show that the TFW approximation is exact on the Poincare manifold. We then show that, in the periodic setting, the distance between the Kohn-Sham potential and the inverse TFW potential in the natural dual Sobolev space can also be controlled by explicit density dependent constants times the distance to the Poincare manifold. As a corollary, we prove that for a fixed density profile, the Kohn-Sham potential converges towards the inverse Thomas--Fermi potential in the high-density limit N rightarrow infty with asymptotic rate of order N^{-2}. This rate is shown to be optimal.
