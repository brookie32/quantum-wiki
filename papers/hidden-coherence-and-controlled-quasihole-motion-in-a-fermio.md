arXiv:2611.10687v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Fractional quantum Hall (FQH) states support fractionalized excitations and geometric phases, yet experimentally resolving the many-body coherence encoded in their wave functions remains challenging. Here, we prepare a three-electron nu=1/3 Laughlin state on the Haldane sphere using seven superconducting qubits and reconstruct its many-body density matrix. Through Read's composite-boson construction, we reveal vortex-dressed coherence that remains appreciable over a larger separation range than electron coherence. The extended profile is suppressed in a Fock-diagonal reference constructed from the measured populations, demonstrating its dependence on off-diagonal many-body coherence. We further prepare a one-flux-added quasihole and characterize its fractional charge response against finite-size benchmarks. Controlled quasihole motion yields an effective geometric-phase signature from the reconstructed dominant-eigenstate trajectory. These complementary measurements provide microscopic access to the coherent structure and quasihole response of a finite fermionic Laughlin state, demonstrating an experimental route to probing strongly correlated FQH wave functions on programmable quantum processors.
