arXiv:2611.25019v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Entanglement entropies of pure multipartite states can be constrained by organizing subsystem cuts as edges (hyperedges) of graphs (hypergraphs). Starting from the one-party entanglement polygon inequality, we derive these constraints from strong subadditivity and pure-state complementarity, with optimal coefficients and explicit equality conditions. Our central result determines, for a distinguished two-party cut and an arbitrary weighted family of cuts, the optimal polygon coefficient: the smallest total weight of the cuts separating a member of the distinguished pair from a remaining party. A refinement involving three mutual information terms gives the exact minimum of the weighted sum at fixed entropies of the two parties and of their union, attained by at most three independent bipartite pure states. This criterion recovers the cycle and complete-graph inequalities and shows that uniform incidence parity, which we prove sufficient for polygon inequalities on hypergraphs, is not necessary. Averaging strong subadditivity over both sides of a bipartition gives a sharp k-marginal inequality with coefficient 2inom{n-2}{k-1}, and we determine uniformly optimal coefficients for collective hyperstar inequalities. For paths and overlapping hyperchains, the deficits are exact sums of conditional mutual information terms; genuinely multipartite entangled cluster states saturate the hyperchain inequality while the chain inequality remains strict. We characterize pure chain equality states, bound the trace distance to cycle equality states independently of the number of parties and local dimensions, and obtain bond-dimension bounds for matrix product states and sequential-recovery error estimates.
