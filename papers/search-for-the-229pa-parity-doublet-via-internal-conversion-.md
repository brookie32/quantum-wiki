arXiv:2611.05494v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: The radioisotope ^{229}Pa is predicted to possess a nearly degenerate ground-state parity doublet. Previous gamma-ray and conversion-electron spectroscopy suggests a splitting of Delta E = (60pm 50)~eV, although the doublet's existence remains unconfirmed. Such a small splitting would give ^{229}Pa an exceptionally large enhancement of symmetry-violating nuclear properties. However, the splitting, and with it the sensitivity of ^{229}Pa to CP-violating physics, remains uncertain by orders of magnitude. We propose an experiment to measure this splitting with sub-eV precision within a few days of measurement time by using delayed-coincidence internal-conversion spectroscopy of ^{229}Pa ions produced by the electron-capture decay of ^{229}U^{+} ions stored in a Penning trap at the Facility for Rare Isotope Beams (FRIB). This measurement would provide the key nuclear-structure input needed to establish ^{229}Pa as a leading candidate for next-generation atomic and molecular electric-dipole-moment (EDM) experiments.
