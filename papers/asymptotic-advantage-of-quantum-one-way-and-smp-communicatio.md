arXiv:2611.07318v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We exhibit a total Boolean function on n input bits whose bounded-error quantum simultaneous-message (and hence one-way) communication complexity is in O(sqrt n), whereas its randomised one-way (and hence simultaneous-message) communication complexity is in Omega(n). The same function admits a classical simultaneous-message protocol of cost O(sqrt n) when the players share O(sqrt n) EPR pairs.
