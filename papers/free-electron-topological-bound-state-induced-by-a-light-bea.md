The study reports that a low‑energy free electron exposed to a spatiotemporally twisted laser field can form a Jackiw–Rebbi type topological bound state with charge e/2, which propagates without dispersion due to its topological protection.  This “flying” bound state demonstrates topological confinement and pair generation entirely in free space, extending topological phenomena beyond conventional solids and photonic systems.  The work was published in *Nature Communications* (August 2026) by a collaboration of researchers from China and the USA.
