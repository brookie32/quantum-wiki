arXiv:2611.29415v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We prove the generalized second law (GSL) for black-hole exteriors undergoing unitary driving and relaxation on a fixed semifinite algebra. Wall's influential proof [arXiv:1105.3445] assumes semiclassical geometry and restricts fixed parent states to algebras of successive horizon cuts. We instead evolve the state on a fixed dressed exterior algebra. With complete characteristic data, the same semiclassical exterior supports both descriptions, analogous to the Heisenberg and Schrodinger pictures through state-operator duality. The asymptotic horizon charge is the momentum of a quantum clock; its conjugate gives the reading. The modular crossed product admits density operators whose entropy with respect to its trace reproduces generalized entropy up to clock contributions. Our approach parallels thermodynamics in regulated field theory and formulates the GSL through a quantum channel fluctuation theorem (FT) for the open exterior. We develop three complementary formulations under specified dynamical assumptions. Separated unitary driving and relaxation combine modular work, the physical-process first law relating horizon absorption to area gain, and relative-entropy contraction through data processing. A time-local description accommodates simultaneous unitary driving and dissipation. A two-time measurement of surprisal, the negative logarithm of the density operator, gives an FT for the complete exterior channel. It identifies the correction for a nonunital channel and gives a GSL bound for unital evolution without resolving intermediate dynamics. Relaxation that preserves every dressed-charge sector and the full faithful dressed reference state is unital. Unitality is sufficient for this FT proof but is not necessary for the GSL along a particular evolution. The bounds establish the GSL in the slow-clock limit when the errors vanish and quantify what is needed at finite clock width.
