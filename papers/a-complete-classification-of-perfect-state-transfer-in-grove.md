arXiv:2610.21946v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We completely classify connected Cayley graphs over finite abelian groups that exhibit perfect state transfer in Grover walks. First, we show that a Cayley graph over any finite group exhibiting perfect state transfer has valency 1, 2, or 4. For valency 4, we prove that an abelian Cayley graph operatorname{Cay}(Gamma,S) exhibits perfect state transfer if and only if S={a,-a,a+h,-a+h}, where h has order 2, and 4nmid |Gamma|/2. Combining this characterization with the valency-one and valency-two cases, we explicitly determine all pairs (Gamma,S) for which operatorname{Cay}(Gamma,S) exhibits perfect state transfer. Our results extend the classification of circulant graphs of valency at most four obtained by Kubota and Yoshino [Circulant graphs with valency up to 4 that admit perfect state transfer in Grover walks, J. Comb. Theory Ser. A., 216 (2025) 106064] to Cayley graphs over arbitrary finite abelian groups and to all valencies.
