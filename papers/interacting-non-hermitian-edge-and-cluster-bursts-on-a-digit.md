This research investigates non-Hermitian edge and cluster bursts in lossy quantum systems, uncovering interacting many-body extensions of edge bursts that are spatially extended and patterned, as well as cluster bursts occurring away from boundaries.   The researchers realized these phenomena in an interacting quantum ladder model on a superconducting quantum processor using a novel digital quantum simulation approach.   Experiments demonstrated clear edge-burst signatures in systems of up to 64 unit cells.
