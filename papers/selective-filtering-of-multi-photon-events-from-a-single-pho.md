**Summary:**  
The study investigates why single‑photon emitters exhibit residual multi‑photon errors caused by spontaneous re‑excitation during pulsed driving. It demonstrates that photons emitted within a short drive pulse possess a broader spectral bandwidth than the emitter’s natural linewidth, enabling selective rejection of these photons with narrowband filters. This filtering technique reduces the zero‑delay second‑order coherence \(g^{(2)}(0)\) by nearly an order of magnitude while preserving the desired single‑photon emission rate.
