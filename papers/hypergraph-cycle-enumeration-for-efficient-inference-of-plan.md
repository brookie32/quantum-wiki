arXiv:2610.40941v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We introduce hypergraph cycle enumeration (HCE), a simple spectral framework for detecting and recovering planted signals in noisy hypergraph inference problems. We study HCE on one representative problem from this class, namely planted noisy k-XOR, also known as sparse Learning Parity with Noise (LPN). Both theoretically and empirically, HCE achieves comparable recovery behavior to Kikuchi method, matching Kikuchi hierarchy. We identify parameter regimes where HCE outperforms Kikuchi method, showing that neither algorithm is universally better. We also show a subquadratic quantum speedup for HCE by replacing one of the algorithm's steps with a quantum walk. Our classical and quantum algorithms do not refute the quartic quantum speedup of quantum Kikuchi method, which remains valid for the practically relevant regime of large size and high noise strength.
