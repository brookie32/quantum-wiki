arXiv:2611.33704v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Photonic orbital angular momentum (OAM) provides a naturally discrete and unbounded Hilbert space for high-dimensional encoding and has emerged as a powerful degree of freedom for advanced information processing. On-demand tailoring of both the amplitude and phase profiles of high-dimensional OAM states is essential for numerous applications, particularly in quantum information science. Here, we propose and experimentally demonstrate an efficient method for achieving such on-demand tailoring using a purely phase-only modulation scheme. The measured fidelity remains above 80% even for a 128-dimensional state, demonstrating the scalability of the approach. Compared with previous techniques, our method achieves significantly higher generation efficiency, and this advantage becomes more pronounced as the dimensionality increases. Moreover, our approach substantially reduces experimental complexity while maintaining precise control, thereby offering a practical and scalable solution for high-dimensional OAM state engineering in both classical and quantum information processing.
