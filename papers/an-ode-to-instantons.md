arXiv:2603.06575v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We review a formalism for semiclassical time evolution in quantum mechanics, identifying complex saddle points in real time, real saddle points in complex time, and complex saddle points in complex time that reproduce known answers to classic problems. For the decay of a metastable state, we find finite-time and finite-energy analogs of the "bounce" which have no zero or negative modes. The one-loop phase of the wave function and the multiplicity of bounce solutions at late times are discussed. The motivation for this work is to learn how to compute decay rates in quantum field theory in situations with interesting time dependence, by first taking a humble step back to the fascinating world of quantum mechanics.
