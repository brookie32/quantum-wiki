arXiv:2611.09773v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Aaronson and Ambainis conjectured that every bounded polynomial of low degree on the discrete cube has an influential variable. They showed that the conjecture implies that the acceptance probability of every quantum algorithm making T queries can be approximated on most inputs by a classical algorithm making poly(T) queries. We prove their conjecture: every function folon{-1,1}^no[-1,1] of degree at most dge1 has a variable of influence at least c,operatorname{Var}(f)^{5/2}/d^{6}, where c>0 is an absolute constant. In the original formulation, for polynomials of degree at most d with values in [0,1] on {0,1}^n and variance at least arepsilon, this gives a variable of influence at least (arepsilon/d)^{7}. The proof is elementary, with steps inspired by works of others. Thus, as Aaronson and Ambainis anticipated, superpolynomial quantum speedups in the query model require structure, such as a promise on the inputs.
