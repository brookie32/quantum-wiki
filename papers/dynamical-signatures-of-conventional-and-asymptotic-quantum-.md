This study demonstrates that asymptotic quantum many‑body scars (AQMBS), which become increasingly stable with larger system sizes, can be engineered in a 2‑local spin model by linking gapless excitations of reference Hamiltonians to scar states. Using the all‑to‑all connectivity of the Quantinuum H1‑1 trapped‑ion quantum processor, these conventional and asymptotic scar states are prepared in logarithmic circuit depth and exposed to Floquet circuit dynamics with up to 418 entangling \(ZZ\) gates. Experimental simulations reveal progressively longer thermalisation times as system size grows, providing clear dynamical signatures of AQMBS.
