arXiv:2611.14376v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We implement reference renewal to reconsider retained physical quantum-service results under a prespecified protocol. Twelve transitions retained six results each; ten completed renewal, eight met the prespecified endpoint, and five succeeded using retained results alone. The exact one-sided p=0.0546875 does not establish the recovery hypothesis. Incomplete references, binding failures and sequential waits expose operational limits. Offline reconstruction recovers decisions from preserved counts. Retrospective binding-plus-oracle checking accepts all 60 usable-transition results without renewal. With the exact oracle also supplying calibration scores, the study does not demonstrate incremental numerical-correctness utility of renewal.
