arXiv:2607.19105v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We propose programmable spin conversion in ultracold gases as gradient quantum matter, whose spin-dependent self-energy varies in space. Quantum kinetic theory shows that a dissipative self-energy curvature turns a force-driven scalar anisotropy into a spin source with mixed longitudinal-transverse momentum parity. Spin-resolved time-of-flight imaging can reveal a transverse spin texture that changes sign when either the drive or programmed curvature is reversed. Ultracold gases thereby offer a controllable spin source for gradient quantum matter.
