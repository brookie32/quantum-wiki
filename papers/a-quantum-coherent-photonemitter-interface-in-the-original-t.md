This paper demonstrates waveguide-integrated quantum dots that realize a fully quantum-coherent photon-emitter interface operating in the original telecommunication band (O-band).   The device produces 41.7 MHz of highly coherent single photons with efficiency exceeding state-of-the-art for telecom emitters.   The quantum dots exhibit transform-limited linewidths and demonstrate Hong-Ou-Mandel indistinguishability currently limited only by experimental instrumentation rather than fundamental dephasing.
