arXiv:2609.38091v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Suppose a Hamiltonian H has a unique ground state |psi_0rangle with an eigenvalue E_0, and we have an estimate ilde{E}_0 such that |ilde{E}_0-E_0|leqelta, and there is a gap of at least 3elta between E_0 and all other eigenvalues. Suppose we have a unitary A available that can produce a "guiding state'" A|0rangle that has overlap at least gamma with |psi_0rangle. We show how to obtain an arepsilon-approximation of |psi_0rangle using O(log(1/arepsilon)/gammaelta) applications of U=e^{iH} and A, and their inverses. We give two different algorithms, one based on interleaving amplitude amplification and error-reduction in the style of [HMdW03], and one using the composition of transducers. This paper is the state-preparation follow-up to our two recent ground-state-energy estimation papers [JW26, SdW26]. Combined, our results show an optimal O(log(1/arepsilon)/gammaelta) upper bound for ground state preparation.
