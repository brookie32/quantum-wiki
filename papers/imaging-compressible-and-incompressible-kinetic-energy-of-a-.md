arXiv:2611.31502v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We introduce a method based on velocity-sensitive two-photon transitions to reconstruct the two-dimensional velocity field as well as the local atomic density of a Bose-Einstein condensate in a single realization. We quantitatively benchmark the method on a quantized vortex. Applied to a condensate far from equilibrium, the method reveals the contrasting behaviors of the system's flow kinetic energy components, where the compressible component quickly decays while the incompressible one remains, dominating the long-time dynamics.
