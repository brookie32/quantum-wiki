arXiv:2610.23573v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We open the quantum baker map with a position window of width w and center q_c. Classical pruning tiles the (q_c,w) plane into diamonds where the T leading resonances form root-of-unity multiplets. At w=1/2 one periodic orbit survives for almost every q_c, and the multiplets are quantized without free parameters: their modulus 2^{-1/2} saturates the pressure bound and their phase is the orbit action. They form cycling multicomponent cat states, and the opening becomes one mid-circuit measurement, enabling spectroscopy with sim10^2 shots on quantum processors.
