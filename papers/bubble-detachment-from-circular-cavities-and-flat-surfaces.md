arXiv:2609.24342v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We determine the maximum stable volume of bubbles attached to flat surfaces as a function of contact radius, contact angle, and capillary length. By solving the Young-Laplace equation using a shooting method, we calculate equilibrium bubble shapes and determine when bubbles detach through three distinct modes: necking at the cavity, sideways instability, and spreading followed by necking on the surrounding surface. Our predicted detachment volumes agree well with experimental measurements of electrolytic, boiling, and carbonated bubbles, as well as pendant drops. These results bring together and extend previous analytical estimates and, by symmetry, also apply directly to pendant drops.
