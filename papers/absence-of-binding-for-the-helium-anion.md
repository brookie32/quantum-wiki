arXiv:2611.19370v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We study the helium anion in the nonrelativistic Coulomb model with an infinitely massive nucleus and spin-frac12 electrons. We prove the absence of binding, with E^{F}(3,2)=E^{F}(2,2) and no three-electron ground state. A decomposition relative to the helium ground state and completion of the square reduce the problem to a one-dimensional variational inequality.
