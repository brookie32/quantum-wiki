The paper demonstrates that quantum linear optics can be extended beyond its usual bounds by using an adaptive boson‑sampling architecture, where the photonic evolution is progressively modified conditioned on intermediate measurement outcomes. Through quantitative analysis they show that this adaptive approach can generate photonic state dynamics that violate the limits imposed by purely passive linear optics, indicating a route toward nonlinear, resource‑efficient quantum computing. The study provides the first systematic method to evaluate the emergence of nonlinear dynamics in near‑term photonic devices.
