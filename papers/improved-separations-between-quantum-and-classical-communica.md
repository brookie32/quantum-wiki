arXiv:2609.16726v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We refine Gavinsky's framework (arXiv:2608.18784) for exponential separations between quantum and randomized communication complexity of total functions and obtain larger separations: polylogarithmic quantum communication versus ildeOmega(sqrt n) randomized communication with two quantum messages, and versus Omega(n^{1-arepsilon}) for every fixed 0<arepsilon<1 with more quantum messages.
