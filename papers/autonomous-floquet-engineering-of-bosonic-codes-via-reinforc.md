Autonomous Floquet engineering applies reinforcement learning to design control waveforms that prepare and stabilize bosonic codes for continuous‑variable quantum error correction. By optimizing Floquet‑driving parameters, the method reduces execution time by over two orders of magnitude—requiring only ~1 % of the duration needed in conventional adiabatic schemes—while maintaining high‑fidelity state creation even under significant dissipation and dephasing, thereby offering a scalable, experimentally feasible route to fault‑tolerant bosonic quantum computing.
