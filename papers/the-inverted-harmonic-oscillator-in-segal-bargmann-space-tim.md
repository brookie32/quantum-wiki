arXiv:2611.27458v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We investigate the real-time dynamics and resonance quantization of the inverted harmonic oscillator (IHO). Its self-adjoint Hamiltonian on L^2(R) has a real continuous spectrum, while generalized resonance states outside L^2(R) have purely imaginary energies pm ihbaromega(n+1/2). In coordinate space, these states are expressed in terms of complex Hermite polynomials and are biorthonormal under the Fresnel Gaussian pairing. Adapting Moriconi's coordinate-time transformation to the real-time path integral, we derive the exact Feynman propagator. Using Mehler's formula, we show that each discrete expansion alone reconstructs the full unitary kernel on its time half-axis, with the decaying Gamow-Siegert branch converging for T>0 and the growing antiresonant branch converging for T<0. In the complex-scaled Segal-Bargmann representation, the two Hamiltonians take the form pm ihbaromega(z(d/dz)+1/2), and their monomial eigenfunctions map back to the dual complex Hermite states. The two convergence domains correspond to one-sided contraction semigroups of dilations. Finally, we construct bi-coherent states whose biorthogonal position and momentum second cumulants are purely imaginary and complex conjugate, with product hbar^2/4. The corresponding Heisenberg expectation values follow the exact classical hyperbolic trajectories for arbitrary real initial positions and momenta.
