arXiv:2611.14377v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Tensor network states such as tree tensor network states (TTNS) or matrix product states (MPS) allow very compact representations of high-dimensional wave functions. Using them efficiently relies on a compact representation of the Hamiltonian, and many methods are limited to the sum-of-products (SOP) form. General potential energy surfaces (PESs) do not have this form. To overcome this issue, a stochastic discrete variable representation (SDVR) is presented. The matrix elements of the potential operator in the node-local basis are estimated stochastically by importance sampling of grid configurations. The estimator of the matrix elements is unbiased, and the PES is only evaluated pointwise. The variance is reduced by representing parts of the potential with a 2-mode representation and only sampling the remainder. Sampling noise biases the eigenvalues. A noise-control scheme mitigates this bias. Final energies are obtained from an unbiased variational Monte Carlo estimator. The method is demonstrated by calculating vibrational states of CH3 (6D) and acetonitrile (12D), as well as the zero-point vibrational energy of uracil (30D). For all three systems, the energies agree with the exact references to sub-wavenumber accuracy.
