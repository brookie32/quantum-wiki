arXiv:2608.20151v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We explore quantum Turing pattern zoology, where the same Lindblad equation supports a morphology atlas of stripes, spots, holes, labyrinths, and defects. The stable stripe species provides a quantitatively controlled case in which morphology and Gaussian witness loss separate parametrically. In particular, visible Turing stripes can remain after two Gaussian witness margins associated with the same k_* mode cross zero in a completely positive Lindblad lattice. The witness thresholds on the exact shell fall as N^{-1}. The stripe nematic threshold tends to a nonzero value at fixed lattice size, time window, and morphology criterion. The ratio of the morphology threshold to either witness threshold therefore grows with N. Imaging and momentum-resolved covariance measurements probe these sectors separately.
