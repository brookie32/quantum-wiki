arXiv:2611.27295v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quite comprehensive iterative algorithms based on consecutive supersymmetric quantum mechanics (SUSYQM) have been constructed heuristically, for resolving the eigenspectra of the Legendre operator and its associates parametrized in various coordinates, without receiving any input from traditional analytical methods. On building these algorithms, the two symmetries ext{--} supersymmetry and shape invariance symmetry ext{--} hidden in these operators are brought to the fore as the only two governing principles, with the aids of the newly devised algebraic techniques such as the active supersymmetrization transformations, momentum operator maps as well as plain and higher-order spin matrix maps. In view of their algebraic simplicity and elegance, high efficiency, as well as independence of traditional methods, some of these algorithms become the promising candidates to supplant the popular power series approach to the eigenspectra of Legendre-type operators.
