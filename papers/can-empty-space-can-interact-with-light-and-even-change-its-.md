Recent observations of a magnetar have shown that an ultra‑strong magnetic field can polarize light passing through the surrounding vacuum, providing the first direct confirmation of quantum electrodynamics’ prediction of vacuum birefringence. The study reported increased linear polarization of photons emitted by the neutron star as they traversed its intense magnetic environment. This marks a milestone in demonstrating how empty space itself can modify photon properties under extreme astrophysical conditions.
