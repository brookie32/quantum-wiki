arXiv:2610.37991v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Braiding Majorana zero modes does not by itself provide a universal set of quantum gates. Additional operations can be supplied through ancillary states, whose preparation need not have the same protection as braiding. Here, we consider purification of the non-Gaussian ancilla used for an entangling gate in Bravyi's scheme. We keep his elementary braid-and-fusion comparison and change how its outputs are combined. An additional layer compares the product of the three stabilizer signs. The first accepted errors giving an incorrect output then require three faulty input blocks rather than two. As a result, the preparation error decreases cubically instead of quadratically, and the asymptotic raw-copy overhead is reduced. We also construct a preliminary sequence in which a processed block is paired with a fresh copy from the same noisy source. For independent inputs supported in the even-parity sector and ideal purification operations, this sequence establishes a sufficient distillability fidelity of 59.95 percent after free Gaussian twirling. At a common source fidelity, it reduces the expected cost of preparing an input for the recurrence by more than seven orders of magnitude relative to the balanced construction studied here. Finally, we examine imperfect purification operations in an effective syndrome-noise model. The additional layer leaves the leading operation-error coefficient unchanged, although a nonzero error floor remains.
