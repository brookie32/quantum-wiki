arXiv:2507.13067v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We consider the geometry of quantum states associated with random matrix Hamiltonians belonging to ensembles that exhibit an integrable-to-chaotic transition in terms of the nearest-neighbour energy level spacing distribution, focusing on the eta-Gaussian ensembles with generic Dyson index. For the tridiagonal Gaussian eta-ensemble, which shows chaos-to-integrability transition with varying Dyson index, we first calculate an analytical expression for the ensemble-averaged fidelity susceptibility for a two-by-two matrix representation of the Hamiltonian for generic values of the Dyson index, and show that it diverges as the total Hamiltonian, which is the sum of a diagonal matrix with independent elements and a tridiagonal eta-matrix, goes over to the integrable phase. Next, for large-dimensional matrices, we numerically compute the fidelity susceptibility and the quantum metric tensor, respectively, for a one-parameter and a two-parameter class of Hamiltonians that we construct from the eta-ensemble, and find scaling relations of these quantities for chaotic and integrable phases. We also consider variations of the eta-ensemble, such as the one that preserves the rotational invariance of the ensemble, and compute the relevant ensemble-averaged fidelity susceptibility to show that it has similar features as the tridiagonal ensemble in the integrable as well as the chaotic phase, thereby establishing the universality of these properties. Finally, the presence of non-vanishing non-diagonal elements, which arises due to the rotational non-invariance of the eta-ensembles, is a specific feature of the corresponding metric tensor, and we use this to illuminate the difference between the quantum state space geometry of these ensembles and the rotationally invariant ones, such as the classical Gaussian ensembles.
