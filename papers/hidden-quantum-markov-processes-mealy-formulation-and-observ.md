arXiv:2611.18040v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We formulate hidden quantum Markov processes in a Mealy architecture using a single completely positive unital transition expectation coupling the present hidden system, the emitted output, and the next hidden system. Together with a boundary state, it generates a quasi-local, finitely correlated hidden--output state; for the commutative generator associated with a classical Mealy kernel, this state is the full joint law of the classical Mealy model. Moore realizations correspond to transition expectations that factor on the prescribed hidden algebra. We establish a rank obstruction to such factorization and prove its sufficiency for isometric generators: factorization holds exactly when the induced hidden-memory channel has Kraus rank at most the hidden Hilbert-space dimension. The Kraus rank also gives the minimal intermediate Hilbert-space dimension. When the hidden and output dimensions are at least two, the factorizable isometric generators form a closed subset of measure zero. For any fixed projective readout, a Mealy generator induces an operational hidden quantum Markov model in the sense of Monras, Beige and Wiesner; conversely, every finite-dimensional operational model admits a Mealy lift on the same memory space with a rank-one readout. For their cluster-state instrument, two isometric, Moore-factorizable lifts with the same instrument and initial memory state yield identical measured output statistics but different quantum output states: one has marginals entangled across 1|2dots n for ngeq3, with three-output negativity frac12, whereas the other has fully separable marginals. Thus operational equivalence does not determine output entanglement.
