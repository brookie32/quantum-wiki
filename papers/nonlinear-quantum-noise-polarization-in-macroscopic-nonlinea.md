arXiv:2610.31406v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We construct a quantum theory of light in nonlinearly responding materials with dispersion and absorption and, in particular, show the emergence of non-Gaussian noise associated with nonlinear absorption processes. Our theory is based on a path-integral formulation of the microscopic Huttner-Barnett model including cubic matter self-interaction required for hi^{(2)} nonlinear processes. We show that the noise polarization emerges naturally from a decoupling of the stochastic weights of the bath response which, in case of cubic self-interactions, leads to a decoupling via Airy functions. As the theory is, by construction, consistent with the nonlinear fluctuation-dissipation theorem, it constitutes a viable quantum theory of light in nonlinear media.
