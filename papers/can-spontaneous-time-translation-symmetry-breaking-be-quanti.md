arXiv:2610.32030v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Can classically branched dynamics associated with spontaneous time-translation-symmetry breaking be quantized without explicitly retaining the branches of the Hamiltonian? We investigate this question using a reduced phase space. formulation in which the Hamiltonian is single-valued, while the critical structure of the classical Legendre map is encoded in a singular, momentum-dependent symplectic geometry. Quantization of the reduced Poisson algebra yields a deformed position-momentum commutator whose singularities determine distinct regular momentum sectors. Using the positive measure induced by the reduced symplectic structure, we identify a bounded central sector and two unbounded outer sectors. In the central sector, states with vanishing mean momentum obey a nonzero lower bound on position uncertainty, subject to the operator-domain assumptions of the representation. We further identify a possible field-theoretic connection with k-essence, where nonlinear scalar-field kinetics can produce finite-velocity degeneracies of the Legendre map. The relationship between the resulting reduced quantum description and branch-matching quantization remains unresolved and requires a comparison of the corresponding Hilbert spaces and operator domains.
