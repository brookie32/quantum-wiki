arXiv:2610.31571v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We propose a new method for proving L^2 and modified logarithmic Sobolev inequalities with constants independent of system size for quantum Gibbs samplers whose Lindblad generators satisfy Kubo-Martin-Schwinger (KMS) detailed balance. We first establish the L^2 logarithmic Sobolev inequality by transporting it along a temperature-dependent path from the infinite-temperature product state to the target Gibbs state while maintaining a size-independent decay rate for the sandwiched Renyi-2 divergence. A tensor-power argument, combined with the quantum asymptotic equipartition property, then transfers this decay bound to relative entropy, yielding the modified logarithmic Sobolev inequality. This approach provides a general framework for proving size-independent logarithmic Sobolev inequalities for quantum Gibbs samplers. We apply it to two regimes: one-dimensional quantum spin chains at every finite temperature, and finite-range quantum spin systems at sufficiently high temperature in any fixed dimension.
