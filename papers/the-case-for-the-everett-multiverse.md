arXiv:2608.07706v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: I give a non-technical presentation of the argument that quantum mechanics, in the form in which it is currently used, needs to be understood in many-worlds (Everettian) terms; the alternatives that have been discussed are at present not able to reproduce the full empirical content of the theory. This is a draft of a chapter for the forthcoming Blackwell Companion to Philosophy and the Multiverse (Klaas Kraay and Daniel Rubio, eds.)
