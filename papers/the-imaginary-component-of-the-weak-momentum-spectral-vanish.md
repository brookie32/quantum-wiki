arXiv:2610.12112v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We establish a rigorous functional-analytic framework for the imaginary component p_I of the complex weak momentum p_Q = p_R + ip_I = frac{hbar}{i}nablalnpsi. For every wavefunction in the Sobolev space H^1(Omega), including those with nodal sets, we prove that the expectation value of p_I vanishes identically, as a consequence of the regularity of rho = |psi|^2 and without appeal to the polar form psi = Re^{iS/hbar}. We further show that extnormal{(i)} the Bohm quantum potential arises entirely from p_I and satisfies langle V_{qu}rangle = langle p_I^2rangle/2m, from which the known Fisher information relation follows as a corollary; extnormal{(ii)} the group velocity of a Bloch band is the density-weighted average of p_R/m, with no contribution from p_I; extnormal{(iii)} the decomposition is gauge-invariant under magnetic minimal coupling, the vector potential acting only on p_R; and extnormal{(iv)} the results extend to curved Riemannian manifolds.
