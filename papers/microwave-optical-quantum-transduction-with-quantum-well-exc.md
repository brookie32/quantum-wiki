arXiv:2611.21785v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Linking superconducting quantum processors over optical fiber requires microwave-optical quantum transducers that are efficient, high-bandwidth, and low-noise. This combination has been out of reach because of the weak nonlinearities of conventional electro-optic (EO) materials. We propose a transducer with an engineered, strong EO nonlinearity using the quantum-confined Stark effect (QCSE) of quantum-well excitons. The QCSE transducer leverages the strong light-matter interactions and extreme polarizability of quantum well excitons. Strong coupling to an optical cavity forms exciton-polaritons whose vacuum EO coupling is up to five orders of magnitude larger than in a typical thin-film lithium niobate device. We describe a GaAs/AlGaAs stepped-quantum-well transducer design with predicted transduction efficiencies above 60% and fewer than 0.01 added noise quanta over a sim60 MHz bandwidth, using pump powers below 10 muW and loaded optical and microwave quality factors of only 3imes10^4 and 10^3. We further show that the cavity protection of the polariton provides robustness against inhomogeneous broadening of the quantum well excitons.
