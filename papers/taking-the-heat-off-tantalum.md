The authors report a krypton‑assisted deposition process that yields high‑quality α‑phase tantalum thin films on silicon while staying within standard back‑end‑of‑line thermal budgets. These low‑loss films enable superconducting qubits whose coherence times match state‑of‑the‑art performance, indicating the method is compatible with large‑scale quantum processor integration.
