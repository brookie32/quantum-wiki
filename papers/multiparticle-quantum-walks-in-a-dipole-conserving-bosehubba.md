In a one‑dimensional Bose–Hubbard model with strict dipole conservation, the authors use ultracold bosons in an optical lattice and site‑resolved potentials to create deterministic hole‑doublon bound states (dipoles and antidipoles).  They track these mobile excitations as they perform coherent quantum walks and collide, demonstrating the kinetic constraints that give rise to fractonic behaviour.  This experiment provides a bottom‑up platform for probing low‑energy phases of dipole‑conserving systems and the dynamics of correlated hopping processes.
