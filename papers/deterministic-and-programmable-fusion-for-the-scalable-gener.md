The study presents a deterministic fusion protocol implemented on a superconducting circuit that enables scalable synthesis of photonic graph states. By non‑destructively projecting pairs of flying microwave photons into Bell states and actively resetting qubits to mitigate decoherence, the scheme links on‑demand time‑bin encoded cluster states into larger reconfigurable graphs. The authors demonstrate genuine multipartite entanglement over 13 photonic qubits, establishing a framework that surpasses probabilistic fusion’s hardware overhead for quantum‑information applications.
