Four independent research teams report significant advances in spin-qubit quantum computers, with error rates reduced to around 0.2% compared to the previous state-of-the-art of 4% , while  spin qubits have been considered underdogs in the quantum computing race compared to front-running superconducting and ion-based systems .  Recent Nature papers from HRL Laboratories and QuTech describe systems containing 18 and 53 spin qubits respectively, demonstrating substantial progress toward practical quantum computing .
