arXiv:2406.06530v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: In the extended Lagrange formalism of classical point dynamics, the system's dynamics is parametrized along a system evolution parameter s, and the physical time t is treated as a dependent variable t(s) on equal footing with all other configuration space variables q^{i}(s). In the action principle, the conventional classical action L dt is then replaced by the generalized action L_{e}ds. A direct reparametrization of the conventional action yields an extended Lagrangian that is homogeneous of degree one in the velocities. A regular extended Lagrangian that is not homogeneous of degree one can also reproduce the conventional dynamics on a suitably selected invariant hypersurface. With this representation, the physical motion takes place on a hypersurface within a symplectic extended phase space of even dimension. The formal similarity of conventional and extended Lagrange formalisms provides a direct connection with the proper-time path integral approach to relativistic quantum physics. To provide an example, the non-homogeneous extended Lagrangian L_{e} of a classical relativistic spinless point particle in an external electromagnetic field will be presented. This extended Lagrangian is a quadratic function in the velocities, and its selected zero-energy hypersurface is the classical mass shell. With this L_{e}, the generalized path integral first yields an evolution equation in s. Imposing the corresponding quantum mass-shell condition then yields the Klein-Gordon equation as the physical wave equation. This establishes the relation between the classical extended Lagrangian description and proper-time quantization for a charged spin-zero particle.
