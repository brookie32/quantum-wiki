arXiv:2611.23612v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We study extreme-value phenomena for random samples in partially ordered sets through their meets and joins, the order-theoretic analogues of minima and maxima. In this paper we consider the probability simplex equipped with either UT-majorisation or majorisation, with samples drawn uniformly from the corresponding simplex. We analyse these extrema both at fixed dimension and as the dimension grows, identifying almost-sure collapse to the bottom or top elements and critical scalings with nondegenerate limiting laws.
