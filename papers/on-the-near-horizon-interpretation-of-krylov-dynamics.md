arXiv:2602.11627v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: The Lanczos recursion maps operator growth onto the motion of a wave function on a semi-infinite chain, and for linearly growing Lanczos coefficients the deep region of the chain behaves like the near-horizon region of a black hole. We develop this correspondence in the time domain and determine how far it extends. Writing the Krylov equation as an exactly skew-adjoint difference operator, we derive a unitary continuum limit whose corrections are suppressed by n^{-2} once the origin of the chain is fixed by the offset of the Lanczos coefficients. For b_nsimeqalpha n the Krylov evolution is chiral transport in ln n, which coincides with infalling waves near the horizon of an AdS_2 black hole when the Krylov depth is identified with the blueshift of an infalling particle; this fixes alpha=pi T, while staggered configurations of the chain carry the outgoing chirality. For b_n=alphasqrt{n(n+2h-1)} the correspondence is exact: the Krylov basis is the discrete series of SL(2,R) carried by a bulk scalar of mass m^2L^2=h(h-1), the Liouvillian is the boost generator of the black hole at temperature T=alpha/pi, and positivity of b_n^2 reproduces the Breitenlohner--Freedman bound, saturated by strictly linear coefficients. Beyond leading order we prove that, for every Lanczos sequence with a smooth large-n expansion, the ingoing solutions of the Krylov equation have coefficients polynomial in frequency to all orders in 1/n, whereas a massive bulk field has a pole at the first special point omega=-2pi iT with residue proportional to m^2. Smooth subleading Lanczos data therefore cannot encode the bulk mass locally. The obstruction persists for the BTZ black hole, for which we obtain the Lanczos coefficients in closed form.
