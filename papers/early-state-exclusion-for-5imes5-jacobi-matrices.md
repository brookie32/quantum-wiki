arXiv:2611.13315v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In this paper, we present a problem that arose from quantum computing, when the quantum state to be transferred completely vanishes before the first occurrence of the perfect state transfer. It was posed as an open problem for 1D quantum chains with n of qubits, where n>4 is an odd number. We present the solution for n=5. The proofs are accessible to students who studied trigonometry and calculus. As for the set-up the knowledge of eigentheory and inner products from linear algebra is needed.
