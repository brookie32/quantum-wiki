Researchers used an attosecond X‑ray free‑electron laser to directly image the quantum‑mechanical motion of electrons in a molecule immediately after a high‑energy photon, electron, or ion impact. The experiment captured the ultrafast dynamics occurring within the first femtoseconds, revealing how electron motion initiates bond breaking. This work delivers the first real‑time view of the earliest stages of collision‑induced molecular dissociation.
