arXiv:2611.21889v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quantum Fisher information (QFI) is a fundamental quantum metrological quantity arising from the second order term in a Taylor expansion of a smooth divergence evaluated at nearby quantum states. The alpha-z information matrix is a QFI measure arising from the alpha-z Renyi relative entropy, and QFI measures corresponding to various underlying smooth divergences may be recovered from the alpha-z information matrix, including the standard symmetric logarithmic derivative (SLD) QFI. Bosonic Gaussian states are common probe states for quantum metrological tasks and admit a complete characterization in terms of their first and second moments. In this work, we consider the quantum metrological task of estimating parameters that characterize a Gaussian unitary time evolution of a faithful bosonic Gaussian thermal state. We establish a formula for the alpha-z information matrix of faithful bosonic Gaussian thermal states that have undergone a Gaussian unitary time evolution. As an example, we calculate entries of the alpha-z information matrix for a two-mode Gaussian thermal state that has undergone a beam splitting operation. For a parametrized family of time-evolved bosonic Gaussian states, we also derive a formula for the SLD operator, which is known to provide a measurement scheme that saturates the quantum Cramer-Rao bound and thus achieves the ultimate precision for single parameter estimation.
