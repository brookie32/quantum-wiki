The paper introduces surrogate‑enabled zero‑noise extrapolation (S‑ZNE), a quantum error‑mitigation method that replaces repeated noisy measurements of many circuit instances with predictions from classical learning surrogates. By shifting all extrapolations to the classical side, S‑ZNE reduces the measurement cost for an entire family of parameterized circuits from linear to constant while maintaining accuracy comparable to conventional ZNE in practical settings. Numerical tests on up to 100‑qubit ground‑state energy estimation and quantum metrology illustrate its effectiveness and suggest that the surrogate framework can be adapted to other error‑mitigation protocols.
