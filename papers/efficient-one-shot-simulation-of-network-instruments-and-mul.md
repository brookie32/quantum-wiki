arXiv:2611.09641v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We undertake an information theoretic study of instrument compression problems (ICPs) in {multiple network} configurations, wherein the goal is to design and analyze protocols that simulate specific quantum classical (QC) correlation amidst distributed parties - some holding components of the quantum state and the rest holding classical registers - with minimum communication. We present a extit{unified framework} of protocols based on likelihood instruments whose analysis yields new inner bounds in the one-shot and asymptotic regimes for the different network configurations we study. Our framework comprises of a common template for designing the instrument simulation protocol and analyzing its information theoretic performance. We also address a related problem of multi-party purity distillation wherein three distributed parties have to distil pure states efficiently via local operations and minimal communication. In regards to the tools, in addition to one-shot purity concentration protocol of ite{202203arXiv_ChaPadSen} and one-shot quantum simultaneous decoder of Sen ite{202102SAD_Sen}, we leverage Sen's fully smooth multi-partite covering ite{202504arXiv_Sen} for all of our covering bounds.
