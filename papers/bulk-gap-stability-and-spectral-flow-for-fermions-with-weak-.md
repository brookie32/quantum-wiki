arXiv:2610.40299v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We prove that weak long-range many-body interactions preserve the bulk gap of a free fermion system and that a quasi-local spectral flow transports the free ground state to ground states of the interacting system. Let Phi_h be a free fermionic interaction, possibly including pairing terms, whose infinite-volume Majorana coefficient operator h has no spectrum in (-g,g), and let mathcal V be an even self-adjoint many-body interaction. For sufficiently small |lambda|, we construct pure ground states omega_{lambda} of the interacting bulk system Phi_{lambda}=Phi_h+lambdamathcal V and quasi-local automorphisms eta_{0,lambda} such that omega_{lambda}=omega_0irceta_{0,lambda}, where omega_0 is the free ground state of Phi_h. Each omega_lambda has a GNS Hamiltonian with gap at least g-C_*|lambda| |mathcal V|_*, where |dot|_* is the relevant interaction norm. Our result allows polynomial decay of both the free coefficients and the many-body interaction. For two-site interactions on a uniformly discrete point set GammasubsetR^d, a sufficient condition is decay as (1+|x-y|)^{-arpi} with arpi>2d, for both the hopping and pairing coefficients and the interaction terms. The spectral-flow generator preserves the spatial exponent of the polynomial interaction norm, and its automorphisms satisfy locality bounds at that exponent. If h and mathcal V have stretched-exponential decay, so does the spectral-flow generator. The proof constructs the spectral flow and establishes the gap through an excitation-number comparison, using polynomial propagation bounds for evolved interactions proved in a companion paper.
