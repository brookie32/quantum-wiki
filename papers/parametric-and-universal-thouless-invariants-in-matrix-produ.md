arXiv:2611.21744v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We investigate the classification of continuous families of translation-invariant injective matrix product states on an infinite chain, parametrized by a paracompact Hausdorff space X and subject to a compact Hausdorff symmetry G. We construct an invariant for phases of such families, valued in heck{H}^1(X;widehat{G}), that generalizes the known widehat{G} phase invariant for G-symmetric Thouless pumps. The phase invariant is constructed as the pullback to X of a single universal Cech cohomology class on a classifying space of G-symmetric translation-invariant injective matrix product states. Several examples are given over various parameter spaces where this invariant is nontrivial.
