This Nature paper (2026) presents an experimental demonstration of device‑independent quantum position verification (QPV) based on a Bell-test protocol that relies only on observed correlations from a loophole‑free Bell test across a quantum network. It establishes security against adversaries without requiring trusted quantum hardware or bounds on computational power, thereby overcoming the spoofing vulnerabilities inherent in classical position‑verification schemes. The authors implement the protocol against adversaries who can prepare strategies before each trial, illustrating its practical feasibility for secure position authentication.
