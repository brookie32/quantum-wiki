A superinductor was realised entirely within a commercial 22‑nm fully depleted silicon‑on‑insulator CMOS process by exploiting the high kinetic inductance (~1 nH µm⁻²) native to thin TiN films in the fabrication line. The device incorporates this nano‑scale superinductor into an on‑chip resonator that is coupled to a silicon quantum dot, forming a radio‑frequency single‑electron transistor sensor with only 18 µm² of area. Its low parasitics and strongly nonlinear impedance yield a power signal‑to‑noise ratio of 4,500 when measuring electron tunnelling events in the integrated circuit.
