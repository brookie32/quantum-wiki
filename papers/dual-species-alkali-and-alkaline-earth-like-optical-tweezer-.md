arXiv:2608.13646v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We have developed a dual-species optical tweezer array apparatus combining ^{87}ext{Rb} and ^{174}ext{Yb} atoms with a permanent hybrid Twyman-Green--Fizeau interferometer, which provides precision co-alignment of two opposing 0.6-numerical aperture (NA) objectives and the high NA beams that pass through the system. This technique is extensible to other tweezer platforms operating at high numerical aperture across widely-separated wavelengths. Here we present simultaneous trapping and single-site resolved imaging of both species in co-aligned tweezer arrays with ^{87}ext{Rb} confined at 840 rm{nm} and ^{174}ext{Yb} at 532 rm{nm}. The platform provides a foundation for hybrid quantum register operation, including mid-circuit measurements and asymmetric intra- and inter-species interactions, greatly expanding the capabilities of neutral atom arrays.
