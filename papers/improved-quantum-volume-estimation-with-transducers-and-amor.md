arXiv:2609.40217v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The volume estimation problem is a classic task in computational geometry. The development of randomized algorithms for this problem spurred the development of many influential algorithmic techniques related to Markov Chain Monte Carlo and simulated annealing, and the problem connects to several important geometrical results, like the recently-resolved KLS conjecture. In this work, we quantize the state-of-the-art widetilde{O}(d^{3.5}+d^3/arepsilon^2)-query randomized algorithm developed by Cousins and Vempala, and obtain a widetilde{O}(d^{3.5} + d^{1.75}/arepsilon)-query quantum algorithm, improving over the widetilde{O}(d^{3.5} + d^{2.25}/arepsilon) state-of-the-art bound. Our key technical contribution is a framework for amortizing the cost of a quantum walk. The framework is based on the recent transducer toolkit introduced by Belovs, Jeffery and Yolcu. It is this amortized quantum walk framework that allows us to exploit the amortized analysis of the ball walk by Cousins and Vempala, thus overcoming the key barrier that previously barred its quantum implementation.
