arXiv:2611.33744v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We study late-time out-of-time-order correlators (OTOCs) of semi-degenerate primary fields in two-dimensional A_{N-1} Toda conformal field theory. In Liouville theory (N=2), we find that the OTOC decays as t^{-3/2} when the continuum edge dominates, correcting a previously reported constant limit. For N=3, a transverse contour pinch reduces the spectral integral to one dimension. Assuming that the reduced expansion is complete, in the continuous phase the regulated edge behaves as a discrete state, and the OTOC approaches a pure braiding phase. As we check numerically, the edge block is a free-field correlator in a charge-neutral Weyl frame; this explains the pure phase and is consistent with crossing symmetry. For both N=2 and N=3, a dominant discrete residue gives a constant late-time limit. For Ngeq4, the same one-dimensional reduction violates a crossing bound on the lowest intermediate weight of a positive diagonal expansion. Hence the N=2,3 phase diagram does not extend to higher rank without additional contributions below the reduced continuum edge or outside a positive diagonal expansion.
