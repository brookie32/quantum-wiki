We show that the recent construction of witness encryption (Hair and Sahai, arXiv:2609.18275v1) is a public-coin extractable witness encryption scheme under the Knowledge of Orthogonality Assumption, or KOALA for short (Buellens-Wee, PKC 2019). As an immediate application, we obtain public-coin differing-input obfuscation (diO) from KOALA and indistinguishability obfuscation (iO).
