The paper investigates quantum spin systems with non‑invertible symmetries and duality defects, showing that wavepackets crossing topological interfaces always transmit perfectly while converting the particle into a non‑local string‐like excitation. It presents a systematic construction of these defects by identifying their Hilbert space with the virtual bond dimension of an associated matrix‑product operator. This framework offers a lattice analogue to the resolution of the Callan–Rubakov monopole paradox in quantum field theory.
