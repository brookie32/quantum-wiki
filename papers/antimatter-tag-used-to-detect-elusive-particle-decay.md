A BESIII experiment used quantum‑entangled antihyperons to “tag” the decay of λ hyperons, enabling measurement of an otherwise inaccessible strange‑quark transition. The measured λ → pπ⁻ decay parameter matches Standard Model predictions, contradicting previous experimental values. This demonstrates that entanglement‑based tagging can probe elusive particle decays and provides a new stringent test of conventional physics theory.
