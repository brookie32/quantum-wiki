arXiv:2609.15040v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Entanglement between two local multi-photon multi-mode systems can be generated by swapping a pair of modes between the two local multi-mode systems. In this presentation, we consider possible strategies for the efficient generation of entanglement between systems with three or more modes. It is shown that the choice of photon number inputs in the local systems adds a new degree of freedom to the non-local interference effects observed in the output photon number statistics.
