Silicon-based single‑photon emitters are demonstrated by nanometer‑precise positioning of a single W‑center defect inside a circular Bragg grating cavity, producing emission at the zero‑phonon line (1217 nm).  Under above‑gap continuous‑wave excitation, a saturated photon rate of \(1.29\times10^6\) counts s\(^{-1}\) is achieved with strong antibunching \((g^{(2)}(0)=0.06\pm0.02)\); weak pulsed excitation yields 93 ± 2 % single‑photon purity, although saturation‑induced repumping reduces purity.  These results provide a key step toward on‑demand indistinguishable photon sources in silicon photonic quantum technologies.
