arXiv:2604.23420v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We identify a boundary-robust topological signature of open Floquet lattices: although nonadiabatic boundaries strongly reshape the transmission lineshape, the integrated left--right transmission asymmetry saturates to a plateau set by the bulk Floquet winding number. Its origin is a deep-bulk branch-population principle: in the long-sample limit, each propagating Floquet--Bloch branch is generically populated with unit weight, since true Floquet bound states are nongeneric. The robust observable is therefore the cumulative transmission imbalance rather than the boundary-sensitive transmission profile. We propose direct detection by cold-atom transmission spectroscopy. For electronic transport, the same asymmetry admits contact-model-dependent electrical readouts: a coherent Floquet--Landauer--Buttiker interpretation predicts a near-(2ef) response in weak SAW devices, whereas a blocking-factor post-processing yields a qualitatively different signal.
