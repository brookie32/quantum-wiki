arXiv:2610.34079v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We give a direct analytic proof that every total Boolean function computable exactly with at most two quantum queries depends on at most six input variables. This bound is sharp. A previous derivation relied on a numerical classification of Boolean functions on at most four variables; to the best of our knowledge, ours is the first unconditional proof. The main difficulty beyond the one-query case is that query-path vectors need not be mutually orthogonal, so interference prevents the scalar weight-counting argument from applying. We overcome this difficulty by establishing a rigid support-and-branch decomposition: after the coordinates in the first-query support are fixed, the second query induces a family of exact one-query branches. This structural description may be of independent interest.
