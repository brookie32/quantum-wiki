arXiv:2609.36203v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We consider the problem of simulating dynamics under geometrically local Lindbladians acting on a lattice of qudits. We present an algorithm to approximate the time evolution under these Lindbladians for evolution time t and on N qudits to diamond norm error arepsilon using geometrically local circuits of depth O(t polylog(Nt/arepsilon)). Our results thus achieve a performance comparable to that of the Haah-Hastings-Kothari-Low (HHKL) algorithm for lattice Hamiltonian simulation.
