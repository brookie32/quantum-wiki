arXiv:2608.06562v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Loschmidt echoes are a well-established method to characterize dynamical properties such as quantum chaos and sensitivity to perturbations. Here we present a straightforward generalization that identifies non-classical dynamical behavior. The generalized echoes involve four forward-backward time propagators. If these propagators do not commute, one can readily identify signatures of this property in the relations between echoes. As an example, detection of intrinsic non-commuting quantum features is analyzed in critical dynamics of a spin chain. Interestingly, this phenomenon is also observed for Gaussian dynamics and high temperature limits which are generally regarded as classical.
