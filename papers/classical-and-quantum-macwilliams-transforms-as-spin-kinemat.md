arXiv:2607.27173v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Spin is the hidden engine behind the zoo of MacWilliams transforms in weight enumerator theories - not only for qubits and qudits, but even for classical codes. From nothing more than a split into trivial and nontrivial errors, we kinematically derive the MacWilliams transform as a Wigner-D rotation between two canonical bases. Within each classical and quantum theory, changing the length n leaves the rotation untouched: the same element simply reappears at spin n/2. And at fixed n, changing the rotation axis simply moves between the various classical and quantum theories.
