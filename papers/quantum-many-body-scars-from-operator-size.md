arXiv:2611.27982v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We present a general construction of approximate many-body scar states in two copies of a system using a bilocal size interaction that couples one site to its partner on the other copy. For an uncoupled two-sided Hamiltonian whose terms have fixed size in an operator basis, we identify polynomially many Krylov basis states that it annihilates and that, in the thermodynamic limit, become concentrated on a single size sector. The size operator splits them into a tower of equally spaced levels that gives rise to coherent revivals characterized by collective oscillations of energy across the two copies. We provide a concrete realization of this construction in two copies of a general q-body nearest-neighbor spin chain, and the SYK model.
