arXiv:2611.21790v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We present a multicomponent Selective Configuration Interaction (SCI) method based on the deterministic Adaptive Sampling CI (ASCI) algorithm for computing energies and wavefunction properties of molecular systems containing leptons of variable masses, or quantum nuclei. Near-FCI accuracy is achieved by incorporating second-order Epstein-Nesbet perturbation theory or by dressing the Hamiltonian via Davidson's and co-workers shifted-Bk method. We validate the approach through benchmark positronic (e^+LiH, e^+OH^-, e^+Mg) and quantum nuclear systems (HCN), establishing a novel protocol for accurate convergence, based on the iterative adaptation of natural orbitals starting from those obtained by considering only cross-particle CISD excitations, to those obtained from SCI variational space, surpassing the accuracy of previous SCI studies. Overall, we show that the present multi-component SCI framework offers a simple, systematically improvable, and cost-effective alternative to traditional CI approaches.
