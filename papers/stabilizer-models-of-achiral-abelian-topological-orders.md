arXiv:2611.24105v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We construct continuous-variable stabilizer codes realizing every achiral 2D abelian topological order, both bosonic and fermionic. Starting from an arbitrary signature-zero integer K-matrix, we show that the corresponding topological order can be obtained by condensing a lattice of dyons with trivial pairwise mutual braiding in an R^N gauge theory. We implement this condensation microscopically by gauging a non-on-site 1-form symmetry of the R^N toric code.
