The authors extend majorization theory to quasiprobability distributions—functions that can take negative values and are defined on infinite spaces—by proving four equivalent characterisations that reduce to the classical Hardy–Littlewood–Pólya theorem in finite settings. They further generalise the framework relative to any positive distribution, creating new resource monotones and state‑conversion constraints useful in quantum thermodynamics and optics. The theory is illustrated through analytic and numerical studies of Wigner and Husimi functions.
