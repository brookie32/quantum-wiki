arXiv announced it will ban researchers from posting manuscripts on the platform for one year if submissions contain references hallucinated by AI tools.   The ban applies to submissions with "incontrovertible evidence" of inappropriate language, plagiarized content, biased content, errors, or misleading content written by large language models.   After the one-year ban, any subsequent submissions must first be accepted by a peer-reviewed venue.
