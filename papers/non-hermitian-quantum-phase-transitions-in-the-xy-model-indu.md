arXiv:2609.23721v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We investigate non-Hermitian quantum phase transitions driven by staggered imaginary Dzyaloshinskii--Moriya (DM) interactions in a transverse-field XY chain. By virtue of a staggered nonunitary transformation, we exactly diagonalize the Hamiltonian. For D1 regime falls into the RT-symmetry-broken phase containing two distinct z-ferromagnetic phases. We examine common quantum-information probes and show that conventional measures, such as entanglement entropy, quantum discord, and quantum coherence, fail to capture the RT symmetry-breaking transition. To address this deficiency, we propose a novel coherence measure widetilde{QC}_{max}^{rm LR} based on RT symmetry and complex-conjugate eigenpair correlations. Embedding intrinsic non-Hermitian information of left and right eigenstates, widetilde{QC}_{max}^{rm LR} reliably identifies the RT symmetry-breaking transition and accurately resolves all magnetic phase boundaries.
