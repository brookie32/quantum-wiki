arXiv:2610.21071v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We study zero-error communication and exact simulation of quantum channels assisted by quantum no-signalling correlations. For a channel with Kraus operator space K, we introduce a semidefinite programming parameter Gamma(K) that is exactly multiplicative under tensor products and upper-bounds Theta_{rm NS}(K), where log_2Theta_{rm NS}(K)=C_{0,rm NS}(K) and C_{0,rm NS}(K) denotes the asymptotic quantum no-signalling-assisted zero-error capacity. Among known upper bounds on Theta_{rm NS} given by semidefinite programs, Gamma is the tightest. We also prove that the previously introduced simulation lower bound Sigma^{-}(K) is exactly multiplicative and satisfies Theta_{rm NS}(K)leqGamma(K)leqSigma^{-}(K)leqGamma(K)^2. Using this multiplicativity, we show that neither S_{0,rm NS}(K)=log_2Sigma^{-}(K) nor its regularized extension S_{0,rm NS}(K)=limnolimits_{noinfty}n^{-1}log_2Sigma^{-}(K^{otimes n}) holds in general. This answers an open question about the asymptotic simulation cost S_{0,rm NS}(K). For classical-quantum channels, we obtain sharp bounds. If r is the effective output dimension, then Theta_{rm NS}leqGammaleq(1+r^{1/2})Theta_{rm NS}/2 and GammaleqSigma^{-}leq(1+r^{1/2})Gamma/2. If k is the minimum rank of the output support projections, then Sigma^{-}/Theta_{rm NS}leq[(1+r^{1/2})/(1+k^{1/2})]^2, and this bound is sharp for every 1leq kleq r. Thus, we obtain the sharp bound S_{0,rm NS}-C_{0,rm NS}leq2log_2[(1+r^{1/2})/(1+k^{1/2})].
