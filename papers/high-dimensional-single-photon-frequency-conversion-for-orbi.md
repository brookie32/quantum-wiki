arXiv:2611.33642v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The orbital angular momentum (OAM) degree of freedom provides an important resource for constructing high-dimensional optical quantum information systems. However, the generation of high-dimensional single-photon OAM comb state and its high-fidelity frequency conversion remain challenging due to limited spatial modulation resolution, imperfect mode matching, and finite nonlinear conversion efficiency. Here, we proposed and experimentally demonstrated a scalable scheme for the generation and frequency conversion of high-dimensional single-photon OAM comb state. We prepared a single-photon OAM comb state with a dimension of up to 128, achieving a fidelity of 0.787 between the reconstructed and target states and an effective dimension of 100. Furthermore, using the sum-frequency generation, we demonstrated the frequency upconversion of single-photon high-dimensional OAM comb state from the communication band to a visible wavelength of 520 nm, while verifying the preservation of both the OAM mode distribution and phase information through density matrix reconstruction. Our work established a versatile platform for the generation, manipulation, and characterization of high-dimensional OAM comb states, with potential applications in cross-band quantum information transfer, high-capacity quantum communication, and high-dimensional quantum networks.
