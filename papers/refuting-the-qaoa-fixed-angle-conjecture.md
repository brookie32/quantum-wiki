arXiv:2609.27703v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The fixed-angle conjecture for the quantum approximate optimisation algorithm (QAOA) is shown to be false for depth-2 QAOA on 9-regular graphs, and proven to be correct for depth-1 on any regular graph, as well, as for any depth on all 2-regular graphs.
