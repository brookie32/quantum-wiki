arXiv:2611.23756v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: One of the greatest merits of cavity magnonics (CM) is the ability to explore rich coupled-mode physics, driven by the large extit{in situ} flux tunability of magnonic resonances and magnon--photon coupling. Meanwhile, van der Waals (vdW) magnets offer an ultra-compact and highly integrated implementation of CM without complex fabrication processes. Nonetheless, the substantial magnon losses significantly hinder the combination of all these advantages. Here, we realize a hierarchical anti-crossing structure in a tripartite vdW CM with CrCl3 and realize an effective coupled-dual-mode Hamiltonian. We achieve an order-of-magnitude reduction in loss while maintaining a strong coupling condition. Additionally, we theoretically confirm the feasibility of simultaneous and extit{in situ} tuning of both loss and coupling by varying the magnetic field direction and strength. Featuring a micrometer-scale thickness and a width of a few millimeters, our device is significantly smaller than those based on bulk crystals or crystal arrays. Our results establish vdW cavity magnonics as versatile platform for investigating intriguing phenomena predicted in coupled-mode systems.
