arXiv:2610.39387v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We derive analytic expressions for observable dynamics under the fully quantized trilinear Hamiltonian, governing quantum three-wave mixing and parametric amplification. Starting from a coherent pump and vacuum signal and idler, the calculation reorganizes the Heisenberg expansion into resummed depletion and return profiles, yielding directly evaluable expressions for all 1st-, 2nd- and 3rd-order correlations. The construction uses no parametric approximation or fitted parameters. Representative numerical benchmarks show errors of order 10^{-12} throughout the entire pump depletion, and below 10^{-3} across dozens of depletion-and-back-conversion cycles, up to the first total collapse of the oscillation. The first-depletion treatment additionally provides 4th-order correlations, accommodates bright squeezed-pump inputs and reveals phenomena important to future optical quantum technologies. The framework can be extended to higher moments.
