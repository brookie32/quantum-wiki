arXiv:2611.27466v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Cliff metrics are right invariant Riemannian metrics on the unitary group U(2^N) introduced by Nielsen and his collaborators in the geometric approach for gate complexity in quantum computations. In this paper, we compute the exponential growth rate for the diameter of U(2^N) equipped with cliff metrics as N rightarrow infty. We prove that if the norm of most generalized Pauli matrices are sufficiently large, this growth rate coincides with the growth rate of maximal gate complexity obtained by Knill.
