arXiv:2611.33944v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: In the two-dimensional (2D) Gross-Pitaevskii model for a Bose gas, the naive dimensional non-equilibrium wave-turbulence power-law prediction for the direct energy cascade coincides with thermal equilibrium and therefore carries no energy flux. We resolve this degeneracy by deriving a logarithmically corrected spectrum that supports a positive, constant energy flux toward small scales. A systematic asymptotic expansion of the wave-kinetic collision integral determines the logarithmic exponent to be 1/3, while the small-scale dissipation scale sets the upper cutoff in the logarithmic correction. Regularization of a marginal infrared divergence at the forcing scale yields an estimate of the spectral prefactor for asymptotically well-separated forcing and dissipation scales. Simulations of the Gross-Pitaevskii and wave-kinetic equations support the predicted spectral shape. Over finite spectral ranges, the correction produces apparent power laws steeper than the dimensional prediction, offering a possible explanation for the steep spectra observed in 2D Bose gas experiments. Our approach provides a systematic framework for constructing wave turbulent cascade spectra when the power-law prediction is degenerate with thermodynamic equilibrium.
