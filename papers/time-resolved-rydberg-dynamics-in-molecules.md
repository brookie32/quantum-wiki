arXiv:2610.27286v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We develop a general theoretical framework for the ultrafast dynamics of coherently populated autoionizing Rydberg states in molecules, whose coherent decay results in asymmetric Fano line shapes produced by interference between resonant autoionization and direct ionization. The asymmetric line shapes form in the photoelectron continuum during the autoionization lifetime of the decreasing Rydberg population. By parameterizing autoionization resonances and solving the time-dependent Schrodinger equation, we model the buildup of the Fano profile together with the decline of the coherently excited Rydberg-state population. We compare our calculated photoelectron yields with the XUV-pump--IR-probe photoemission experiment of Biswas et al., Phys. Rev. A 110, 043106 (2024), in which neutral ground-state CO_2 molecules are excited by an XUV pulse to the Henning sharp and diffuse Rydberg series and subsequently ionized by the delayed IR pulse. Based on our numerically modeled photoelectron yields, we analyze two complementary processes: (i) photoemission into the X^2Pi_g continuum of residual CO_2^+ ions, which exposes interferences between direct photoionization and excited-state autoionization, and (ii) IR-induced photoemission into the CO_2^+[B^2Sigma_u^+] continuum, which reveals quantum beats between Rydberg states. Our close-coupling model provides a versatile, extendable framework for tracking molecular Rydberg-state relaxation and, more generally, light-driven coherent dynamics in complex quantum systems.
