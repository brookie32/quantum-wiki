A thorium‑229 optical nuclear clock was realized by locking a continuous‑wave laser to the 148 nm nuclear transition in solid‑state thorium. Rapid feedback based on absorption detection keeps the laser’s frequency stable at the level needed for high‑precision timing, offering performance that could surpass current electronic‐shell atomic clocks. The scheme is expected to be far less sensitive to external perturbations and to enable more stringent tests of fundamental physics using clock–based constraints.
