arXiv:2611.17936v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We construct projected entangled-pair state (PEPS) approximations to various many-body states in 2D lattices with global error at most epsilon and bond dimension polynomial in the system size and 1/epsilon. They include critical free-fermion models in two dimensions, as well as chiral topological states. For the two-dimensional nearest-neighbor tight-binding model at half filling, our construction combines Gaussian wavelet transformations with matrix product state approximations of the one-dimensional chains, as well as selected fermionic swap operations. For arbitrary uniformly gapped, finite-range quadratic Hamiltonians, we give a different construction based on repeated one-particle filtering, which yields Gaussian PEPS in any spatial dimension. Those include, for instance, p+ip models or a Chern-insulator in a square lattice, for which we give an explicit construction. Finally, we prove that Gutzwiller projections of a fixed number of identical Gaussian PEPS approximations to free-fermion states at arbitrary filling do not amplify considerably the approximation error. This provides a polynomial-bond PEPS approximations to the resulting states, including familiar lattice chiral-spin-liquid states.
