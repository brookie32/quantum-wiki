arXiv:2609.09608v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We study the structure of Gibbs states in weakly perturbed interacting fermionic systems. First, for a sparse Hamiltonian H=H_0+V with a quadratic term H_0 and a non-quadratic perturbation V of scale epsilon, we show that the Gibbs state rho_{eta} decomposes into a convex combination of Gaussian states whenever the inverse temperature satisfies eta le O(log(1/epsilon)). Moreover, we prove that this bound is asymptotically tight by establishing that eta le Theta(log(1/epsilon)) is necessary for certain sparse Hamiltonians. This general framework applies directly to the weak-coupling (small-ert{}Uert{}) regime of the Fermi--Hubbard model with hopping t and on-site interaction U on any graph of maximum degree D. Complementarily, in the strong-coupling (small-ert{}tert{}) regime, we show that the Gibbs state remains convex-Gaussian up to eta le Oig(ert{}Uert{}^{-1}log(ert{}Uert{}/(Dert{}tert{}))ig), revealing a mechanism for convex-Gaussianity distinct from the weak-coupling setting.
