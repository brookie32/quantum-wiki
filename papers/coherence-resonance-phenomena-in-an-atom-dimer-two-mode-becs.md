arXiv:2608.27947v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We investigate the non-equilibrium dynamics of atomic-molecular Bose-Einstein condensates coupled via a Feshbach resonance, with Gaussian white noise acting on both the coupling strength and the detuning. Using the bosonic Josephson-junction and Bloch-sphere formalisms, we examine how coherence (coupling) noise and imbalance (detuning) noise modify the coherence-resonance point as functions of the initial polarization and the Feshbach detuning. Noise plays a more coherent role when its characteristic timescale matches the intrinsic timescales of the dynamics, leading to extrema in the time-averaged purity and the Husimi-Q distribution.
