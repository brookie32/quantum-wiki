arXiv:2611.33150v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We propose Boseformer, a universal attention-based neural-network architecture for representing the wavefunctions of particles with bosonic quantum statistics, including superfluid and crystal phases. The model is trained to generate the wavefunction as a function of a Hamiltonian parameter across the quantum phase transition between these phases. Once trained with variational Monte Carlo optimization, it can serve as a foundation model describing the phase transition without the need to retrain it for individual parameter values. We demonstrate our method on a system of two-dimensional bosons with 1/r^3 repulsive interaction.
