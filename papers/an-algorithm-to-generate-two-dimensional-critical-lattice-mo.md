The authors present a “conformal field theory factory,” an algorithm that constructs two‑dimensional lattice models whose critical points are governed by conformal field theories (CFTs). By engineering boundary conditions for three‑dimensional string‑net topological orders and inducing commensurate condensation of non‑commuting anyons, the method yields an infinite family of critical lattices, reproduces known CFTs with Haagerup symmetry, and predicts three new candidate theories. The critical couplings are explicitly given by algebraic data from the underlying string‑net models, offering a systematic route to discover and classify CFTs.
