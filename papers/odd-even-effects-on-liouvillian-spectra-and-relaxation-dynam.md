arXiv:2611.24053v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We investigate odd-even effects on Liouvillian spectra and relaxation dynamics in boundary-driven XY and cluster chains. With the bulk couplings and boundary dissipation rule fixed, the parity of chain length determines whether non-decaying oscillatory modes exist. Consequently, odd- and even-sized chains exhibit qualitatively different dynamical behaviors. We trace this mechanism to the coupling between bulk Majorana paths and the boundary jump operators. An isolated path supports undamped modes, and products of an even number of its Majorana operators span a non?decaying subspace of even operators. This structure yields nonzero purely imaginary eigenvalues of the Liouvillian and persistent contributions to bond correlations or magnetization after preparation in product states. The mechanism extends to cluster interactions of even range under a fixed boundary dissipation rule. The existence and frequencies of the persistent modes are independent of the magnitudes of the positive boundary dissipation rates. Our results unveil how chain parity controls the existence of persistent dynamics.
