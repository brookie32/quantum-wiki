arXiv:2611.23956v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: By intertwining ideas from sequential and adiabatic quantum computing, we propose a quantum algorithm for the problem class Exact Cover 3, which is a sub-class of 3-satisfiability (3-SAT) problems and NP (nondeterministic polynomial time) complete. We find a quantum run-time which is significantly faster than the square-root run-time No N^{1/2} of the Grover algorithm. Depending on the structure of the problem (e.g., connectivity of clauses), the run-time scaling may range from approximately third root No N^{1/3} to nearly fourth root No N^{1/4}, possibly even faster.
