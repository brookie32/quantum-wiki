arXiv:2510.26326v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We prove Kantorovich duality for a linearized version of a recently proposed non-quadratic quantum optimal transport problem, where quantum channels realize the transport. As an application, we determine optimal solutions of both the primal and the dual problem using this duality in the case of quantum bits and distinguished cost operators, with certain restrictions on the states involved. Finally, keeping the same restrictions regarding the states involved, we use this information on optimal solutions to give an analytical proof of the triangle inequality even for the square of the induced quantum Wasserstein divergences.
