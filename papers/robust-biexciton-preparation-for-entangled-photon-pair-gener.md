The study presents a new robust protocol for preparing exciton–biexciton states in quantum emitters using ultrafast single‑shot Floquet driving. By applying two strongly detuned but symmetric optical pulses that form a stroboscopic Hamiltonian, the scheme directly couples the ground state to the biexciton while a pulse delay introduces an effective magnetic field, yielding >96 % biexciton occupation and 93.4 % entangled‑photon fidelity. This method reduces experimental complexity and enhances scalability for deterministic entangled photon pair generation in photonic quantum technologies.
