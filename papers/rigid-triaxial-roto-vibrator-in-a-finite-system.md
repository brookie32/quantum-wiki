arXiv:2611.26071v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We investigate the emergence of rigid triaxiality in a finite system, within the framework of the interacting boson model of nuclei. Appropriate Hamiltonians for a maximally-triaxial rigid shape are constructed. Closed expressions for wave functions, energies and E2 rates for states of the ground band are derived. Classical and quantum analyses reveal that for a fixed Hamiltonian, the system evolves from a gamma-soft SO(6) structure for small N, to a gamma-rigid structure for large N. Attention is paid to symmetry and spectral properties of both rotational and vibrational states.
