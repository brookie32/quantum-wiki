Researchers grew molecular Haldane chains composed of antiferromagnetically coupled spin‑1 triangulene units on a proximitized Au(111)/Nb(110) superconductor surface. Scanning tunneling spectroscopy revealed that chain parity determines coupling to the superconducting condensate: odd‑length chains host Yu–Shiba–Rusinov bound states originating from a global S = 1 ground state, whereas even‑length chains form an S = 0 ground state decoupled from the superconductor. A two‑site model attributing this alternation to inter‑edge spin interactions is corroborated by extra‑gap spin excitations observed in the tunneling spectra.
