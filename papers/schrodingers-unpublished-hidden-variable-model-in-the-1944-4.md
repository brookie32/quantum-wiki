arXiv:2610.20752v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: In the mid-1940s, the question of whether quantum mechanics could be supplemented by deterministic hidden variables resurfaced in a debate in ^textit{Nature}. I show that Erwin Schrodinger drafted a hitherto unpublished response to this controversy. In it, Schrodinger provided an explicit hidden-variable model, thereby showing that such models could not be ruled out. I reconstruct the historical context of this forgotten manuscript and publish here the first transcription and analysis. Importantly, Schrodinger's proposal should be understood as a proof of principle rather than as evidence of his commitment to determinism.
