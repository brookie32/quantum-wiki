arXiv:2607.21798v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The mean-field Heisenberg ferromagnet is a quantum spin model on the complete graph with isotropic spin-1/2 interactions. This non-commuting Hamiltonian is permutation and mathsf{SU}(2) invariant, and its Gibbs states undergo an mathsf{SU}(2) symmetry breaking phase transition at inverse temperature eta=2. We consider the associated Davies generator, a canonical model of open-system thermalization, and prove tight asymptotic estimates for its spectral gap at all noncritical temperatures. For fixed eta2 the gap is Theta(n^{-1}). The matching upper bound of the spectral gap is witnessed by the total magnetization order parameter, suggesting that the low-temperature (eta>2) slowdown is associated with broken continuous symmetry. Two key ingredients in our approach are a comparison argument, which introduces auxiliary generators to bound dissipation on nontrivial representations of the symmetry groups mathsf{SU}(2) and mathsf{S}_n, and a decomposition of the space of observables into spherical tensor operators to reveal a form of monotonicity.
