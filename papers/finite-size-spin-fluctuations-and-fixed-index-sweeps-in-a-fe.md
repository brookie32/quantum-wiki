arXiv:2610.28163v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We find a dip in the optimized rotation quantum Fisher information of a zero-magnetization ferromagnetic spin-1 Bose-Einstein condensate near the excited-state quantum phase transition. Its displacement from epsilon_c=-q decreases on an inverse-size scale. At q=0.6,0.8,1.0,1.2, the dip value of F_{max} equals 2langle S^2rangle for N up to 3840, linking it to total-spin fluctuations. A semiclassical orbit average normalized by the classical density of states describes the dip over N=120--3840 with maximum relative residual below 0.028; a small power law fits comparably. Inserting the measured energy offsets directly into the semiclassical formula leaves residuals of 17--30%, so kappa acts as an effective cutoff. Among retained states, spin-nematic squeezing occurs at the spectral edges. Separately, fixed-index excited-state sweeps have half-excitation times between 11.2 and 13.1 times their ground-state counterparts, with local size exponents increasing across the four sizes.
