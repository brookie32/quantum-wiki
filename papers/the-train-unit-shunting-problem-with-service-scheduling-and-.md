arXiv:2611.17987v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Efficient railway rolling stock management requires the coordinated planning of train-unit assignment, shunting movements, track occupation, service operations, and workforce allocation. These interdependent decisions give rise to a challenging combinatorial optimization problem, particularly as the size of the fleet and the planning horizon increase. This paper introduces the Train Unit Shunting Problem with Service Scheduling and Workforce Assignment, a mixed-integer linear programming formulation that integrates these operational decisions within a unified optimization framework. To improve computational tractability, a preprocessing procedure is developed to reduce the size of the resulting model. The proposed approach is evaluated through computational experiments on instances of increasing size and under different objective-function weight and cost configurations. Classical optimization experiments using Gurobi show that filtering method reduces the number of decision variables and constraints, while its impact on computational time varies across instances. In addition, a constrained quadratic model representation is investigated using D-Wave's hybrid quantum-classical optimization solver.
