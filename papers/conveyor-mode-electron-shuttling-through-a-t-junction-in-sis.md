Conveyor‑mode shuttling in gated Si/SiGe quantum‑dot arrays allows adiabatic transfer of single electrons across several microns with only four control channels per conveyor belt. The authors fabricated a T‑junction linking two independently driven shuttle lanes and achieved an inter‑lane charge‑transfer fidelity exceeding 99.99999 % at a velocity of 270 mm s⁻¹, while simultaneously populating 54 quantum dots with programmable atomic pulses that can swap electron patterns—a key step toward native spin‑qubit SWAP operations.
