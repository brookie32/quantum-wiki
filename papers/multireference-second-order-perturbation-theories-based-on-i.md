arXiv:2610.37221v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Under the assumption that the static correlation can well be described by selected configuration interaction, to the extent that the residual dynamic correlation can be accounted for accurately by a second-order perturbation theory (PT2), a family of multireference PT2s (iCI-MRPT2) based on the iterative configuration interaction with selection are proposed and compared. The first step is to partition a large complete active space (CAS) P into the direct sum of a primary (P_m) and a secondary (P_s) subspace via a parameter C_{min}. The former serves as the actual reference space, whereas the latter is treated via Epstein-Nesbet PT2. An MRPT2 is then invoked to account for the dynamic correction of the Q=1-P space to the P_m space. Different MRPT2s are distinguished by the underlying zeroth-order Hamiltonians. To circumvent the prohibitive computational cost and memory footprint associated with high-order reduced density matrices in the fully internally contracted scheme and the exponential growth of external functions (XF) in the uncontracted scheme, a partial contraction strategy is employed for constructing the XFs: those involving one or two inactive (core or virtual) orbitals are kept uncontracted, whereas those involving three or four inactive orbitals are internally contracted. They span the uncontracted (Q_u) and contracted (Q_c) portions of Q, respectively. The former is further split into two subspaces Q_u^c and Q_u^u via a parameter Q_{min}, which are to be treated in coupled and uncoupled manners, respectively. Extrapolations to zero Q_{min} and C_{min} can be performed, so as to approach CAS-based MRPT2. Calculations with active spaces as large as CAS(56e,58o) can now be performed. The size-consistency of the iCI-MRPT2s is also analyzed in detail.
