The authors report an “encapsulation epitaxy” technique that produces > 1‑inch, air‑stable monolayer niobium diselenide (NbSe₂) films with atomically flat interfaces and high crystallinity. This method overcomes the typical oxidation problem of 2D superconductors, enabling large‑scale synthesis suitable for compact lumped‑element devices in superconducting quantum circuits. The resulting material serves as a platform for both strongly correlated physics studies and quantum information applications.
