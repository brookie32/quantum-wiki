This work introduces a scalable photonic quantum computing architecture that combines programmable, high‑fidelity linear‑optical networks with integrated nonlinear modules to implement a universal gate set, overcoming the long‑standing lack of strong nonlinearity in optical platforms. The authors achieve quasi‑deterministic generation of Gottesman–Kitaev–Preskill (GKP) states—critical resources for bosonic error correction—which had previously been produced only probabilistically. They also demonstrate the platform’s ability to simulate complex many‑body quantum dynamics, such as the Bose–Hubbard model, illustrating its versatility for both computation and simulation tasks.
