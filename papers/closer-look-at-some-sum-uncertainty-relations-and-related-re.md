arXiv:2504.09122v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We analyze the weak and critical points of some state--dependent uncertainty relations that follow from the inequalities for the norms of vectors in the Hilbert space of states of a quantum system. Uncertainty relations for sums of standard deviations, for sums of variances, and other relations between standard deviations or variances are studied. The obtained results are compared with the conclusions obtained in similar cases using the standard Heisenberg--Robertson uncertainty relation. We also show that, in critical cases, the right--hand sides of sum and stronger sum uncertainty relations provide similar information regarding lower bounds on the left--hand sides of these relations as the Heisenberg--Robertson uncertainty relations do. New reverse uncertainty relations for a sum of variances are derived.
