arXiv:2609.08591v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Nowadays, the realization of quantum computations and communications based on continuous variables has attracted significant attention due to a substantial expansion of the system dimensionality. The main progress in this area is attributed to the implementation of multimode systems based on squeezed states of light. One of the simplest ways to generate such states relies upon their producing in a single-pass optical parametric amplifier (OPA) under ultrafast pumping. However, for homodyne detection of such multimode states, the profile of the local oscillator (LO) must perfectly match the profile of the measured mode. Usually, this is not the case; therefore, a proper treatment of multimode squeezing is required. In this work, we study both theoretically and experimentally the multimode squeezed light generated in type-0 and type-II OPA. We characterize such sources using the Schmidt-mode representation and investigate the degree of squeezing depending on the LO spectral profile, employing a pulse shaping technique. In addition, we investigate cluster states that can be generated under the given experimental conditions. This work might have a significant impact on the realization of multimode quantum protocols.
