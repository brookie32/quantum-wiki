The authors demonstrate a single‑molecule spin–photon interface using a triplet‐ground‑state carbene molecule hosted in a structurally matched crystal. The emitter shows bright, narrow zero‑phonon‑line fluorescence with spectral stability exceeding an hour, supports spin‑selective optical transitions, and enables optically detected magnetic resonance at the single‑molecule level. Coherent dynamical decoupling extends electron‑spin coherence to millisecond timescales while longitudinal relaxation remains on the order of tens of milliseconds at 4.5 K.
