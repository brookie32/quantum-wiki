arXiv:2610.29791v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We show that the concept of particles and antiparticles is related to the Weyl-Heisenberg group W!H_3. Its generators are the position operators hat x^a, the momentum operators hat p_a, and the central element Q_{sf{v}}, which defines a subgroup U(1)_{sf v} of W!H_3. We show that the wave functions have a charge relative to the group U(1)_{sf{v}} equal to either q_+=1/hbar (particle psi_+) or q_-=-1/hbar (antiparticle psi_-). We call these eigenvalues of the operator Q_{sf{v}} quantum charges: Q_{sf{v}}psi_pm^{}=q_pm^{}psi_pm^{}. The wave functions psi_+ and psi_- belong to the Hilbert spaces H_+ and H_-, on which unitarily inequivalent representations pi_hbar and pi_{-hbar} of the Weyl-Heisenberg group W!H_3 are defined. These representations are related to each other by the antilinear charge conjugation operator hat C, which maps H_+, q_+ to H_-, q_-. Charges q_pm^{} are intrinsic characteristics of the wave functions psi_pm^{}, similar to spin and electric charge. The wave function possesses one of these charges, q_+ or q_-, prior to the specification of any equations. We show that negative probabilities/energies and the Klein paradox in Klein-Gordon and Dirac theories are consequences of the violation of U(1)_{sf v}-invariance in these theories. Restoring this invariance separates particle and antiparticles and eliminates all paradoxes at the level of first-quantized relativistic theories.
