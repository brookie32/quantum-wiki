arXiv:2611.17433v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We show that a strong form of correlation decay in quantum Gibbs states has far-reaching dynamical consequences. For a smooth path of geometrically local commuting Hamiltonians whose Gibbs states satisfy strong decay of correlations, we construct O(1)-time quasi-local Lindbladian evolutions exactly connecting these states in both directions, together with O(1)-time quasi-local Hamiltonian evolutions exactly connecting their canonical purifications. These quasi-local connections strengthen previously known quantum-channel circuit constructions based on the decay of connected correlation functions [R. Ma, V. Khemani, and S. Sang, PRX Quantum 7, 033052 (2026)], which approximately connect states within the same phase but have depths that grow logarithmically with system size. Furthermore, if the Gibbs-state path contains a product state, we prove that the single-site Glauber dynamics has a Omega(1) spectral gap independent of the system size throughout the path. This establishes Glauber-dynamics fast mixing for models that may violate the strong spatial mixing condition used in prior proofs, including (1) the q-state Potts model throughout the high-temperature phase for any q and any finite dimension; (2) the Ising model with a longitudinal field at low temperature, with frustrating perturbations that destroy monotonicity of the dynamics; and (3) the three-dimensional Z_2 lattice gauge theory at low temperatures comparing to a field perturbation, even though the model supports classical memories at intermediate temperatures. Intriguingly, we establish fast mixing of these classical Markov chains using tools from quantum physics like Lieb-Robinson bounds.
