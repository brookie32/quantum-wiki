arXiv:2611.27975v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We establish nonperturbative bounds on the ground-state energy of lattice quantum electrodynamics with naive fermions in Coulomb gauge, within the Gaussian photon approximation. Our central result is a spectral comparison theorem showing that, in the thermodynamic limit, the minimum fermionic energy density over all link configurations coincides with that of the trivial gauge configuration. The proof exploits a logarithmic determinant representation and dimensional reduction, without relying on reflection positivity, and applies to lattices of either parity. Combining this result with the positivity of the electromagnetic and Coulomb Hamiltonians, we derive an explicit lower bound on the interacting ground-state energy. Finite-lattice counterexamples show that the thermodynamic limit is essential.
