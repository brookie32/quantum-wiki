arXiv:2610.19286v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: The mutual information of measurement of a local operator between two spatial regions characterizes the reduction in the uncertainty of measurement outcomes in one region given the outcome in the other. We study the Renyi-Shannon mutual information of measurement in conformal field theories (CFTs). Explicit calculations for scalar primaries and descendants in large N CFTs are performed in the two extreme limits of the intervals being adjacent or largely separated. The results depend on only the cross ratio and the scaling dimension Delta of the operator being measured, with striking similarities to the mutual information of entanglement. We find a surprising quadratic dependence as (Delta-1/2)^2 for adjacent intervals. This dependence superficially seems to violate the information processing inequality, which we discuss and resolve. We also offer a holographic interpretation of the mutual information of measurement.
