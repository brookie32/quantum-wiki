The paper reports the first experimental implementation of a quantum analogue‑to‑digital converter (QADC) for phase estimation. Using a reconfigurable photonic integrated circuit and highly indistinguishable photons from a quantum‑dot source, the protocol converts a continuous phase into an output bit string by employing various entangled state resources; it achieves higher mutual information than classical analog methods. Experimental imperfections are mitigated through machine‑learning–based noise deconvolution, demonstrating a practical digital quantum metrology benchmark that redefines precision limits such as the Heisenberg bound.
