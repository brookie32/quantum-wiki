arXiv:2609.27129v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We prove quantum soundness of the total-degree diagonal line-vs-point test using the individual-degree soundness theorem of Ji, Natarajan, Vidick, Wright, and Yuen. A random change of coordinates yields projective polynomial decoders of total degree at most md. The uniform-line slice of the test bounds the weight of outcomes of degree greater than d, which are removed by a common relabeling. This reduction does not yield a dimension-independent soundness bound: the operatorname{poly}(m) dependence of the individual-degree theorem persists, as discussed in Section 1.2 of arXiv:2009.12982.
