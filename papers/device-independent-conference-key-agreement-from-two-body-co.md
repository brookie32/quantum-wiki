arXiv:2610.41712v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Device-independent conference key agreement (DI-CKA) enables N > 2 distant parties to establish a common secret key without detailed characterization of the source or measurement devices. Many existing protocols rely on full-body Bell correlators, whose measurement can be demanding for a large number of parties. Here, we show that two-body correlators alone suffice for DI-CKA using balanced Dicke states for every even N geq 4. We construct two-body Bell inequalities and derive a tight analytical lower bound on the conditional von Neumann entropy by comparing their Bell operators with those of asymmetric Clauser-Horne-Shimony-Holt inequalities. In the ideal setting, certified key rates can approach 1-h[(N-2)/(4(N-1))], where h is the binary entropy function. This rate remains bounded away from zero as N rightarrow infty, tending to 1-h(1/4) approx 0.189 bits per key-generation round. We show that the honest implementation of our protocol is invariant under collective phase noise. Moreover, a complementary full-body Bell construction certifies positive conference-key rates from correlations arbitrarily close to the local set.
