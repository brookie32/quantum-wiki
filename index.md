# Quantum Wiki

Living knowledge base tracking quantum computing and quantum information science
across hardware, research, industry, applications and tools.

## Categories

- [[papers]] — arXiv preprints, peer-reviewed results
- [[research]] — lab updates, theory, conference talks
- [[hardware]] — chip generations, qubit milestones, fab
- [[industry]] — company news, funding, partnerships
- [[applications]] — chemistry, optimization, ML, finance
- [[tools]] — SDKs, frameworks, simulators
- [[breakthroughs]] — claimed advantage / supremacy / fault-tolerance milestones
- [[syntheses]] — cross-cutting reports
- [[concepts]] — auto-generated entity indexes (people, modalities, papers)
- [[inbox]] — unprocessed captures
- [[raw]] — original source files
